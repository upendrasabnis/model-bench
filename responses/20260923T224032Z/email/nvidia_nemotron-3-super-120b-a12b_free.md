**Auspicing arrangement**  
The CRG expressly permits applications from unincorporated community groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local government council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. Please attach a signed auspice letter from the council confirming their support and outlining the scope of their oversight.

**Grant size**  
For this funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your project scope, a request in the $15,000‑$25,000 band would be typical, but you may propose any figure within the allowable limits that reflects the true cost of the works.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting tanks, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related site works—is expressly listed as an eligible expense under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” category. Operational costs (e.g., stall fees, membership administration) are not fundable, but the capital components you described are fully permissible.
