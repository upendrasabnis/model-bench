**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines expressly state that unincorporated community groups may apply provided they are formally auspiced—or “sponsored”—by a legal entity that can assume financial and legal responsibility for the project. In your case, the local council’s involvement, including holding the necessary insurances, satisfies this requirement. Consequently, your group is eligible to apply, even though you are not yet a registered charity. The council’s auspice will be documented in the application’s “Organizational Structure” section, and we will need a brief letter from the council confirming its support and its role in managing risk‑related matters.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award. Funding levels are determined on a case‑by‑case basis, reflecting the scope of the proposed work, the demonstrated community benefit, and the proportion of the total project cost that can be covered. Typical awards for infrastructure projects of the size you describe range from $15,000 to $45,000, but we have approved grants well beyond this band for larger‑scale initiatives. Please be aware that the grant can fund up to 80 % of eligible project costs, with the remainder expected to be met through in‑kind contributions or cash donations.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs – rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Both rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible expenses under the “Climate‑Resilient Infrastructure” category of the grant. The key is that the items must be directly related to the project’s resilience objectives—in your case, enhancing the garden’s ability to operate during hot summer periods. We require detailed specifications, cost breakdowns, and evidence that the installations will be permanent (i.e., anchored, not portable). If you can demonstrate how these assets will reduce water consumption and provide heat‑mitigation benefits, they will comfortably satisfy the eligibility criteria.  

**Supporting documents we recommend preparing**  
1. **Project Proposal Form** – Completed using the official template (available on our grants portal).  
2. **Organizational Profile** – Include a brief history, mission statement, and the council’s auspice letter.  
3. **Budget and Narrative** – A line‑item budget that separates eligible from ineligible costs, accompanied by a concise narrative describing the problem, solution, and expected outcomes.  
4. **Proof of Financial Capacity** – Recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for small groups, a simple cash‑flow statement) demonstrating your ability to manage funds responsibly.  
5. **Risk Management and Insurance Documentation** – The council’s insurance certificate and any additional safety plans required for the installation work.  
6. **Community Support** – Letters of support from local businesses, resident groups, or other stakeholders, and, where available, attendance figures from your produce stall.  
7. **Site Plans and Technical Specifications** – Scaled drawings or photographs of the garden site, together with technical datasheets for the proposed tanks and shade structure.
