Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. The council would assume full legal and financial accountability for the grant, including acquittal responsibilities. We will require a formal letter from the council confirming their auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and evidence of their current public liability and workers’ compensation insurance coverage.

Your annual turnover of approximately $40,000 falls well within the program’s thresholds for small community organisations. There is no minimum grant amount for this round; however, the maximum request is capped at $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project—involving the installation of rainwater tanks and a shade structure—aligns directly with the program’s objective of building climate resilience for community assets. Capital infrastructure of this nature is considered an eligible cost, provided it is fixed, permanent, and located on land where you have secure tenure (e.g., a lease, licence, or letter of support from the landowner/council for a minimum of five years).

In terms of supporting documentation, you should prepare the following for a complete application:
1.  The signed auspicing agreement letter from your council.
2.  Evidence of land tenure or a landowner consent letter.
3.  Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
4.  A simple project plan with a timeline, risk assessment, and budget (showing GST-exclusive figures).
5.  Your most recent annual financial statement or treasurer’s report.
6.  Letters of community support demonstrating the garden’s reach and volunteer base.

Please note that applications must be submitted via our online portal by 5:00 PM AEST on the closing date next month.
