**2. Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of **$5,000** to a maximum of **$50,000** per successful applicant. The amount awarded is based on the scope, impact, and budget of the proposed project, as demonstrated in the application. Given your estimated turnover and the nature of the works you describe, a request in the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be typical, but you are free to propose any amount within the stated limits that reflects the true cost of the rainwater tanks and shade structure.

**3. Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, water‑efficient irrigation, and related site works—is an eligible expense. Both the purchase and installation costs, as well as any necessary engineering or design fees, can be included in the budget. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., stall‑running expenses) are not fundable under this grant, but they may be referenced in the project narrative to illustrate the broader benefit.
