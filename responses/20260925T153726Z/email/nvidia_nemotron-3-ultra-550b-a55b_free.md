Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. It is encouraging to hear about the valuable work your community garden is doing in a regional setting, and I am happy to provide clarification on the points you have raised to assist with your planning.

Firstly, regarding organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible entity—such as a local government authority—are eligible to apply. Your arrangement with the local council, whereby they hold the relevant insurances and assume legal and financial responsibility for the grant, meets our eligibility criteria. You will need to provide a signed auspicing agreement or letter from the council confirming their willingness to act as the legal applicant and outlining the terms of the arrangement.

In terms of funding parameters, the Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Given your described project scope and annual turnover of approximately $40,000, your request would likely fall well within this range. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some capacity for contribution, whether in-kind (volunteer labour, donated materials) or cash, though this is assessed relative to organisational size and capacity.

The infrastructure you describe—rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure—is considered an eligible capital expenditure under the "Community Infrastructure and Asset Development" stream, provided the assets are fixed, serve a clear community resilience purpose, and are located on land where you have secure tenure (e.g., a lease, licence, or letter of support from the landowner). As you operate on council land, a letter from the council confirming ongoing access for the asset’s useful life will be required.

Regarding supporting documentation, please prepare the following for a complete application:
- A completed application form (available on our portal from the opening date).
- The council auspicing agreement/letter.
- Land tenure confirmation or landowner consent.
- Your most recent annual financial statement or treasurer’s report (unaudited is acceptable for groups of your size).
- A project budget using the provided template, showing all income (including the grant request) and expenditure, with quotes for the tanks and shade structure.
- A project plan/timeline demonstrating delivery within the 12-month funding agreement period.
- Evidence of community need and benefit (letters of support, usage data, climate risk assessment for the region).
- Proof of public liability insurance (provided by the council under the auspicing arrangement).

I strongly recommend attending one of our online applicant information sessions scheduled over the next two weeks; dates are on our website. These sessions cover the assessment criteria in detail and allow time for questions.
