**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our Community Resilience Grant is open to a broad range of applicants, including unincorporated community groups, provided that the group operates under the formal auspice of a local government or other legally‑constituted entity that can assume fiscal and legal responsibility for the grant. In your case, because your garden is already supported by the council—who holds the relevant insurances and can act as the contracting party—your group should meet the eligibility criteria. If you decide to proceed with an application, the council will be required to sign the grant agreement and to certify that it will manage the funds and reporting on behalf of the garden.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant program does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award; rather, the amount awarded is determined by the scope, cost‑effectiveness, and overall merit of the proposal in relation to the funding available for the round. Based on recent cycles, awards have ranged from approximately $5,000 to $75,000, though we have approved projects of both smaller and larger value depending on the demonstrated need and budget discipline. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should be realistic and fully justified in order to align with the funder’s expectations.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is expressly permitted under the “Resilience and Climate Adaptation” component of the grant. These items are considered eligible because they directly enhance the garden’s ability to operate during extreme heat events, thereby strengthening community food security and reducing reliance on municipal water supplies. We do encourage you to provide a clear breakdown of each item’s cost, including installation, permits (if required), and any ongoing maintenance expenses.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend compiling the following materials:

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem you are addressing, the proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – line‑item costs for the tanks, shade structure, and any ancillary expenses (e.g., permits, labour, contingency).  
3. **Proof of Council Auspice** – a letter from the council confirming its support, its role as fiscal sponsor, and that it holds the necessary insurance coverage.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of the garden’s history, volunteer base, and community impact (membership numbers, participant demographics, etc.).  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for an unincorporated group, a set of simplified accounts) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover and the sources of revenue.  
6. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, schools, or community organisations that highlight the project’s relevance.  
7. **Risk Assessment and Insurance Certificate** – confirmation that the council’s insurance policy covers the activities that will be conducted using the new infrastructure.  
8. **Site Plans or Sketches** – visual representations of where the tanks and shade structure will be located, indicating any constraints or site‑specific considerations.  

We would be happy to review a draft of your application should you wish to share it in advance of the submission deadline.
