**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant programme is designed to support projects that deliver measurable community benefit, and we do accept applications from unincorporated groups provided they have a suitable auspice arrangement. An auspice organisation—such as your local council—must be able to administer the grant funds, be responsible for compliance with reporting requirements, and hold any necessary insurance or liability coverage on behalf of the project. Because your council already holds the relevant insurances and can act as the fiscal host, your group should meet this criterion. We will, however, need a formal letter of support from the council confirming that it will act as the primary grantee and that it agrees to manage the funds and reporting on your behalf.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award amount. Instead, the funding level is determined by the scope, impact, and cost‑effectiveness of the proposed project. Preliminary estimates suggest that projects of the scale you describe—water‑harvesting infrastructure and a permanent shade structure—typically fall within the $15,000‑$40,000 range, but we will evaluate your full budget against the available funding pool for the round. If your total project cost exceeds the funds we can allocate, we can discuss options such as phased implementation or complementary financing.

**Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Both rainwater tanks and a shade structure are explicitly listed as eligible capital costs under the “Climate‑Resilient Infrastructure” component of this grant. The programme seeks to fund physical assets that enhance community resilience to climate‑related challenges, and your project directly addresses increased summer temperatures and water security for the garden and its users. We will, of course, need detailed specifications, cost breakdowns, and evidence that the assets will be installed on land for which you have secure access.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To streamline the review process, we recommend assembling the following documentation:

1. **Project Proposal Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, objectives, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – itemised costs for equipment, installation, labour, contingency, and any in‑kind contributions.  
3. **Letters of Support** – from the council (confirming auspice status), local businesses, partner organisations, and at least three community members who will benefit from the project.  
4. **Proof of Insurance** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming coverage for the project period.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for a small volunteer group, a simple statement of revenue and expenses for the last two years).  
6. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your group’s mission, activities, and volunteer base, even if you are not a registered charity.  
7. **Site Plans or Maps** – showing the garden layout, proposed tank locations, and shade structure placement.  
8. **Risk Assessment and Safety Plan** – outlining how the installation will be managed safely, particularly given the volunteer nature of the team.
