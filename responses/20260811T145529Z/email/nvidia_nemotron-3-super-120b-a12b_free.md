Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you and your volunteers are doing to strengthen the community garden’s resilience. I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant and to outline the documentation you will need to prepare.

**Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The grant program does accept applications from unincorporated community groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity—in this case, your local council. The council must act as the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and assume responsibility for financial reporting and acquittal. As long as the council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming its support, insurance coverage, and willingness to manage the funds, your garden can proceed with the application.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Applications are assessed on a competitive basis, and the requested amount should be justified by a detailed budget that aligns with the project scope.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures—is expressly eligible, provided the items are new, essential to the project, and not already funded through other sources. Operational costs (e.g., stall fees, membership administration) are not covered, but you may include a modest proportion (up to 10 % of the total budget) for project‑related coordination or volunteer training if it is integral to the infrastructure installation.

**Supporting documentation**  
To substantiate your application, please prepare the following:

1. **Letter of auspice** from the local council (on council letterhead) confirming:
   - Their role as the legal applicant and fund manager  
   - Confirmation of existing public liability and volunteer insurance covering the garden activities  
   - Any in‑kind support they will provide (e.g., site access, utilities)

2. **Project plan** (max 2 pages) describing:
   - The specific rainwater tank capacity and shade structure design  
   - How these interventions will mitigate heat‑related risks and ensure year‑round operation  
   - A timeline for procurement, installation, and commissioning

3. **Detailed budget** (using the template provided in the grant guidelines) showing:
   - Itemised costs for tanks, pumps, fittings, shade‑structure materials, labour, and any required permits  
   - Quotations or indicative prices from at least two suppliers for each major item  
   - Confirmation of any co‑funding or in‑kind contributions (e.g., volunteer labour valued at an hourly rate)

4. **Governance documents** (if available) such as:
   - Minutes from a recent garden committee meeting endorsing the project  
   - A list of current volunteers and their roles (to demonstrate community support)  
   - Any existing partnership agreements with the council or local organisations

5. **Proof of turnover** (e.g., a simple profit‑and‑loss statement or bank statements) showing the approximate $40,000 annual revenue, to confirm the organisation’s scale.

Once these documents are assembled, you can submit the application through the online portal before the closing date. Should you encounter any difficulty obtaining a specific item (e.g., multiple quotes), please let me know as soon as possible—I can advise on acceptable alternatives or provide a waiver request template.
