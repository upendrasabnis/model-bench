I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing Arrangements & Eligibility**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your council) is a legally constituted entity that meets the program’s eligibility criteria—typically an incorporated association, company limited by guarantee, or statutory body. The council must sign the grant agreement, accept legal and financial responsibility for the funds, and hold the required insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation, etc.) on your behalf. Please ensure you have a current, signed Memorandum of Understanding or Auspicing Agreement that clearly outlines roles, responsibilities, and financial accountability; this document will be required at application stage.

**Turnover & Financial Viability**
An annual turnover of approximately $40,000 derived from a produce stall and membership fees is well within the program’s parameters for small community organisations. You will need to provide your most recent financial statements (income and expenditure statement and balance sheet) or a treasurer’s report certified by the council’s finance officer if formal audited accounts are not available.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your project scope—rainwater tanks and a shade structure—suggests a budget that would comfortably sit within this range. Please note that grants are awarded on a competitive basis; requests should reflect genuine project costs supported by quotes.

**Eligible Infrastructure Costs**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate impacts is an eligible expenditure category. Rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures that extend safe operating hours during extreme heat events are explicitly listed as eligible in the program guidelines. Costs must be for purchase and installation only; ongoing maintenance, operational salaries, and recurrent utility bills are not funded.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Signed auspicing agreement with council (including council’s ABN, insurance certificates of currency, and a letter of support on council letterhead).
2. Your group’s governance documentation (constitution, rules, or terms of reference; minutes confirming the decision to apply).
3. Latest financial statements or certified treasurer’s report.
4. Two written quotes for each major capital item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
5. Project plan with timeline, risk management matrix, and clear outcomes linked to the grant objectives (community resilience, climate adaptation, food security).
6. Evidence of community need (e.g., letters of support from local health service, school, or neighbourhood house; photos of current site conditions).
7. Quotes or evidence of any co-contributions (cash or in-kind) from your group or partners.
