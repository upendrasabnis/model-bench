Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Community Resilience Grant so you can decide whether to proceed with a full submission.

**Auspice arrangements**  
The grant is open to not‑for‑profit groups that may not be incorporated in their own right, provided they operate under a formal auspice agreement with an eligible sponsoring organisation—in this case, your local council. The council must be able to confirm that it will assume legal and financial responsibility for the grant funds, hold the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance, and provide a signed letter of auspice outlining the relationship, the council’s ABN, and the period of coverage. As long as you can supply that documentation, your group meets the auspice eligibility requirement.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is **$5,000** and the maximum is **$50,000** (GST exclusive). Applications are assessed on the scale and impact of the proposed project, so you may request any amount within that band that reflects the true cost of your rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure works.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate‑related stresses is an eligible expense. This includes the purchase and installation of rainwater harvesting tanks, associated plumbing, and shade structures (e.g., pergolas, shade sails, or solid roofing) that protect volunteers, visitors, and produce from extreme heat. Operational costs such as ongoing maintenance, utilities, or staff salaries are not covered, but you may include reasonable project‑management fees if they are directly tied to the delivery of the infrastructure works.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
1. **Auspice letter** from the council (signed, dated, on council letterhead) confirming the arrangement, insurance coverage, and willingness to administer the grant.  
2. **Proof of the council’s ABN** and, if applicable, a copy of their public liability insurance certificate.  
3. **Project plan** (max two pages) describing the current site, the specific works to be undertaken, timelines, and how the infrastructure will increase resilience to hot summers.  
4. **Detailed budget** with itemised quotes (minimum two quotes for each major component—tanks and shade structure) and a clear total that matches the amount requested.  
5. **Financial summary** of your group (e.g., a simple income‑and‑expense statement for the past 12 months) to demonstrate the $40,000 annual turnover you mentioned.  
6. **Letters of support** (optional but helpful) from regular users of the garden, local schools, or other community organisations that will benefit from the improved facilities.  
7. **Risk‑management outline** addressing any OH&S considerations related to the installation (e.g., working at height, water storage safety).

Once you have assembled these items, you can submit your application via the online portal before the closing date (30 September 2025).
