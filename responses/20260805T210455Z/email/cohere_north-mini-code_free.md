**Eligibility of unincorporated, council‑auspiced groups**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑focused entities, including unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of a local government. The key requirement is that the applicant can demonstrate a clear point of accountability—typically the council in your case—and that the council holds the necessary insurances and can enter into contractual arrangements on the group’s behalf. Consequently, your current structure should satisfy the eligibility criteria, provided you can provide a formal letter of support from the council confirming its endorsement of the project and its willingness to act as the fiscal agent if the grant is awarded.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
We do not set rigid minimum or maximum grant values; each award is determined by the merit of the proposal, the total project budget, and the available funding for the round. Historically, grants in this program range from roughly $5,000 to $50,000, but applications for amounts outside this band are also considered on their individual merits. When you develop your budget, aim to align the requested amount with the proportion of total project costs that the grant will cover, while retaining a realistic contribution from other sources (e.g., council funding, in‑kind volunteer labour).

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital assets such as rainwater tanks and shade structures are expressly permitted as eligible expenditures, provided they directly support the project’s resilience objectives and will remain the property of the community garden after installation. We recommend budgeting for the purchase, delivery, and installation of these items, as well as any ancillary works (e.g., foundation preparation, plumbing connections) that are essential to the project’s functionality.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest assembling the following materials:

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem you are addressing, the proposed solution, expected outcomes, and a timeline with milestones.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – itemised costs for each component (including contingency), clearly distinguishing grant‑requested funds from other contributions.  
3. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) demonstrating your $40,000 turnover.  
4. **Council Endorsement Letter** – a formal letter from your local council confirming that it auspices your group, holds the required insurances, and supports the grant application.  
5. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from community members, partner organisations, or user groups illustrating local buy‑in.  
6. **Risk Assessment and Insurance Proof** – a summary of safety measures and confirmation that insurance coverage is in place (your council’s policy should satisfy this requirement).
