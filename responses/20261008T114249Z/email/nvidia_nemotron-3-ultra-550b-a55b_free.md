Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application—this is exactly the right approach. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing Arrangements & Eligibility**
Unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for administering organisations. The council must hold an Australian Business Number (ABN), be registered for GST, and maintain current public liability insurance of at least $20 million that covers the project activities. Since you have confirmed the council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf, this requirement appears to be satisfied. You will need a formal letter of support from the council confirming their willingness to act as auspice, including their ABN, insurance details, and a statement that they accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant.

**Grant Amounts**
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding between **$5,000 and $50,000** (GST-exclusive). There is no requirement to request the maximum; you should apply for the amount genuinely required to deliver the project. Given your described scope—rainwater tanks and a shade structure—a request in the mid-range would be typical, but the final figure should reflect a detailed budget.

**Eligible Costs**
Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures fall squarely within this definition, provided they are fixed assets that remain with the garden site. Eligible expenditure includes purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation (e.g., earthworks, plumbing connections). Ineligible costs include ongoing operational expenses (water bills, maintenance), retrospective costs incurred before a funding agreement is executed, and any items not directly tied to the approved project scope.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. **Auspice Agreement** – signed letter from the council (as above).
2. **Governance Evidence** – your group’s constitution, rules, or terms of reference; minutes from the most recent annual general meeting; and a current office-bearer list.
3. **Financial Information** – a simple profit-and-loss statement for the last financial year and a current balance sheet (or treasurer’s report) showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
4. **Project Plan** – a one-page timeline with key milestones (procurement, installation, commissioning) and risk mitigation strategies.
5. **Budget** – itemised quote(s) from suppliers for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary works, clearly separating GST.
6. **Site Evidence** – proof of tenure (lease, licence, or letter from the landowner/council) confirming the garden’s right to occupy the land for at least five years post-completion.
7. **Insurance Certificates** – copies of the council’s public liability and, if applicable, professional indemnity policies.
8. **Letters of Support** – at least two from community stakeholders (e.g., local school, neighbourhood house, CFA brigade) demonstrating broader community benefit.

**Additional Considerations**
Because your turnover is under $150,000, you are not required to be GST-registered; however, the auspicing council will receive the funds and must account for GST appropriately in their acquittal. Please ensure your budget states whether each line item is GST-inclusive or exclusive.
