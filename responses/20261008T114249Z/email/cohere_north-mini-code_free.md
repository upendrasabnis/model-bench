Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed information you have already shared. I’m pleased to provide the following clarification on the points you raised, so you can decide confidently whether to proceed with a full application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our Community Resilience Grant is open to a broad spectrum of applicants, including unincorporated community groups, provided they are formally **auspiced or sponsored by a legal entity** such as a local council, incorporated association, or other not‑for‑profit organisation. In your case, the fact that your council holds the necessary insurances and can act as the contracting party means you meet the structural eligibility criteria. However, we do require a **letter of support or formal auspice agreement** from the council confirming that it will assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds if your group is awarded the funding. Please ensure this document is signed and dated.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant programme does not prescribe a strict minimum award; applications requesting as little as $2,000 are considered. The upper limit for any single application is **$75,000**, and projects that request amounts above this threshold are not eligible under this round. Most successful applications fall within the $5,000‑$30,000 range, reflecting the scale of infrastructure and community impact.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes, capital infrastructure such as **rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures** is an eligible cost category. These items must be directly linked to the project’s resilience objectives—in your case, enhancing water security and reducing heat‑related operational risks. We do require detailed quotations, specifications, and evidence that the items are not already owned by the group.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend compiling the following:  

1. **Project plan and narrative** outlining the problem, proposed solution, timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed budget** with line‑item costs, including contingency (typically 10 %).  
3. **Quotations** for the rainwater tanks and shade structure, with specifications.  
4. **Council auspice letter** confirming insurance coverage and financial responsibility.  
5. **Financial statements** for the most recent year (or a simplified income/expenditure summary given your modest turnover).  
6. **Letters of support** from stakeholders such as local businesses, community members, or partner organisations.  
7. **Proof of membership/volunteer base** (e.g., a roster or sign‑up sheet) to demonstrate community engagement.
