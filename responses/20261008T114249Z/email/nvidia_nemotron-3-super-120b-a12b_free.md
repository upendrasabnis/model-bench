**Auspicing Arrangements**  
Unincorporated groups that operate under a formal auspicing agreement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided that the auspicing body (in your case, the council) signs the grant agreement and assumes legal and financial responsibility for the funds. You will need to submit a signed Auspicing Agreement that outlines the council’s role, the extent of insurance coverage, and the delegation of reporting obligations.

**Grant Size**  
The Community Resilience Grant programme offers awards ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per project. Given your estimated turnover of $40,000, you may request an amount that reflects the scale of the proposed rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure installation, up to the ceiling of $50,000 if justified by a detailed budget.

**Eligible Costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related site‑works—is explicitly listed as an eligible expense. Operational costs (e.g., staff wages, routine maintenance) are not covered unless they are integral to the delivery of the funded infrastructure (for example, installation labour or project management fees). Please ensure that any requested items are clearly itemised and accompanied by quotes or cost estimates.

**Supporting Documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following:

1. **Auspicing Agreement** – signed by an authorised council representative.  
2. **Project Plan** – a concise description (max 2 pages) outlining the need, objectives, timeline, and expected resilience outcomes.  
3. **Detailed Budget** – broken down by line item (tanks, shading, plumbing, labour, contingencies) with attached supplier quotes.  
4. **Letters of Support** – from the council, local residents, or partner organisations demonstrating community backing.  
5. **Proof of Insurance** – a certificate showing the council’s public liability coverage that extends to the garden’s activities.  
6. **Financial Summary** – a brief statement of your annual turnover and sources of income (produce stall, membership fees) to confirm financial viability.
