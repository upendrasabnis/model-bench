SECTION 1: RESEARCHING FUNDERS  
UNDERSTAND THE LANDSCAPE OF POTENTIAL FUNDERS BEFORE YOU WRITE A SINGLE WORD  
IDENTIFY FUNDERS WHO ALIGN WITH YOUR MISSION AND GEOGRAPHIC FOCUS  
USE DATABASES SUCH AS FOUNDATION CENTER, GUIDESTAR, AND GRANTSTATION TO BUILD A MASTER LIST  
TRACK KEY METRICS INCLUDING AVERAGE GRANT SIZE, FUNDING PRIORITIES, APPLICATION DEADLINES, AND REPORTING REQUIREMENTS  
CREATE A SPREADSHEET WITH COLUMNS FOR FUNDER NAME, CONTACT PERSON, WEBSITE, PRIORITY AREAS, DEADLINE, AND NOTES  
EXAMPLE: A COMMUNITY HEALTH NONPROFIT TARGETS FOUNDATIONS THAT FOCUS ON MATERNAL HEALTH IN THE MIDWEST  
CHECKLIST FOR FUNDER RESEARCH  
- DEFINE YOUR PROGRAM’S CORE OUTCOMES AND MATCH THEM TO FUNDER PRIORITIES  
- VERIFY ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA SUCH AS 501(C)(3) STATUS, GEOGRAPHIC RESTRICTIONS, AND PROJECT TYPE  
- REVIEW PAST GRANTS AWARDED TO SIMILAR ORGANIZATIONS FOR INSIGHT INTO SUCCESSFUL PROPOSALS  
- NOTE ANY REQUIRED LETTERS OF SUPPORT OR MATCHING FUND REQUIREMENTS  
- SET REMINDERS FOR DEADLINES AT LEAST 30 DAYS IN ADVANCE  

SECTION 2: BUILDING RELATIONSHIPS WITH FUNDERS  
ESTABLISH CONTACT EARLY THROUGH NETWORKING EVENTS, WEBINARS, OR PERSONALIZED EMAILS  
REQUEST A MEETING TO DISCUSS FUNDER’S CURRENT INTERESTS AND ANY SHIFTS IN STRATEGY  
SHARE A ONE‑PAGE IMPACT SUMMARY THAT HIGHLIGHTS YOUR ORGANIZATION’S TRACK RECORD  
FOLLOW UP WITH A THANK‑YOU NOTE AND ANY ADDITIONAL INFORMATION REQUESTED  
MAINTAIN A CONTACT LOG WITH DATES, TOPICS DISCUSSED, AND NEXT STEPS  

SECTION 3: CRAFTING A COMPELLING NEEDS STATEMENT  
DEFINE THE PROBLEM WITH SPECIFIC DATA POINTS AND LOCAL CONTEXT  
USE QUANTITATIVE EVIDENCE SUCH AS INCIDENCE RATES, SURVEY RESULTS, OR CENSUS FIGURES  
INCORPORATE QUALITATIVE STORIES THAT HUMANIZE THE DATA  
EXAMPLE: IN COUNTY X, 27 PERCENT OF CHILDREN UNDER FIVE EXPERIENCE FOOD INSECURITY, COMPARED TO A STATE AVERAGE OF 15 PERCENT  
FRAME THE NEED AS A GAP THAT YOUR PROGRAM IS UNIQUELY POSITIONED TO FILL  
AVOID JARGON; WRITE IN CLEAR, ACTIVE LANGUAGE THAT A NON‑EXPERT CAN UNDERSTAND  
CHECKLIST FOR NEEDS STATEMENT  
- STATE THE PROBLEM IN ONE SENTENCE  
- PROVIDE AT LEAST THREE CREDIBLE DATA SOURCES  
- INCLUDE A PERSONAL NARRATIVE OR CASE STUDY  
- EXPLAIN WHY CURRENT SOLUTIONS ARE INSUFFICIENT  
- CONNECT THE NEED DIRECTLY TO YOUR PROPOSED ACTIVITIES  

SECTION 4: DEVELOPING PROGRAM GOALS, OBJECTIVES, AND OUTCOMES  
DISTINGUISH BETWEEN GOALS (BROAD, LONG‑TERM) AND OBJECTIVES (SPECIFIC, MEASURABLE, TIME‑BOUND)  
USE THE SMART FRAMEWORK: SPECIFIC, MEASURABLE, ACHIEVABLE, RELEVANT, TIME‑BOUND  
EXAMPLE OBJECTIVE: INCREASE THE NUMBER OF FAMILIES RECEIVING NUTRITION EDUCATION FROM 120 TO 200 BY DECEMBER 2026  
LINK EACH OBJECTIVE TO A CORRESPONDING OUTCOME INDICATOR  
DEFINE BOTH OUTPUT INDICATORS (ACTIVITIES COMPLETED) AND OUTCOME INDICATORS (CHANGES IN TARGET POPULATION)  

SECTION 5: DESIGNING A LOGIC MODEL  
CREATE A VISUAL MAP THAT CONNECTS INPUTS, ACTIVITIES, OUTPUTS, OUTCOMES, AND IMPACT  
INPUTS: STAFF, VOLUNTEERS, FUNDING, PARTNERSHIPS, FACILITIES  
ACTIVITIES: WORKSHOPS, HOME VISITS, MOBILE CLINIC DAYS, COMMUNITY EVENTS  
OUTPUTS: NUMBER OF WORKSHOPS HELD, FAMILIES SERVED, MATERIALS DISTRIBUTED  
SHORT‑TERM OUTCOMES: INCREASED KNOWLEDGE OF NUTRITION, IMPROVED ACCESS TO HEALTHY FOOD  
LONG‑TERM IMPACT: REDUCED CHILDHOOD OBESITY RATES IN TARGET COMMUNITY  
USE A TABLE FORMAT FOR EASY READING IN THE PROPOSAL  
CHECKLIST FOR LOGIC MODEL  
- LIST ALL INPUTS WITH QUANTITIES  
- MAP EACH ACTIVITY TO AT LEAST ONE OUTPUT  
- ENSURE EVERY OUTPUT FEEDS INTO A MEASURABLE OUTCOME  
- INDICATE DATA COLLECTION METHODS FOR EACH INDICATOR  
- VALIDATE THE MODEL WITH PROGRAM STAFF AND EXTERNAL REVIEWERS  

SECTION 6: WRITING THE PROJECT NARRATIVE  
STRUCTURE THE NARRATIVE AROUND THE LOGIC MODEL FLOW  
BEGIN WITH AN EXECUTIVE SUMMARY THAT CAPTURES THE PROBLEM, SOLUTION, AND REQUESTED AMOUNT  
FOLLOW WITH DETAILED SECTIONS: NEEDS STATEMENT, PROGRAM DESIGN, IMPLEMENTATION PLAN, EVALUATION, SUSTAINABILITY  
USE HEADINGS THAT MATCH THE FUNDER’S GUIDELINES  
INCLUDE TIMELINES WITH MILESTONES AND RESPONSIBLE PARTIES  
PROVIDE EVIDENCE OF ORGANIZATIONAL CAPACITY: STAFF BIOS, PAST SUCCESSES, GOVERNANCE STRUCTURE  
EXAMPLE TIMELINE ENTRY: MONTH 1‑2 – RECRUIT AND TRAIN 5 COMMUNITY HEALTH WORKERS; RESPONSIBLE: PROGRAM DIRECTOR  

SECTION 7: BUDGET DEVELOPMENT AND JUSTIFICATION  
BUILD A LINE‑ITEM BUDGET THAT MIRRORS THE LOGIC MODEL ACTIVITIES  
CATEGORIES: PERSONNEL, FRINGE BENEFITS, TRAVEL, EQUIPMENT, SUPPLIES, CONTRACTUAL, INDIRECT COSTS  
USE REALISTIC UNIT COSTS AND PROVIDE SOURCE DOCUMENTATION (e.g., SALARY SURVEYS, VENDOR QUOTES)  
INCLUDE A BUDGET NARRATIVE THAT EXPLAINS EACH LINE ITEM AND ITS RELEVANCE TO PROJECT GOALS  
EXAMPLE: PERSONNEL – PROGRAM COORDINATOR 0.5 FTE AT $45,000 ANNUAL SALARY = $22,500 FOR 12 MONTHS  
CHECKLIST FOR BUDGET  
- TOTAL REQUESTED AMOUNT MATCHES FUNDER’S MAXIMUM AWARD  
- ALL COSTS ARE ALLOWABLE PER FUNDER’S COST PRINCIPLES  
- INDIRECT COST RATE IS APPROVED OR JUSTIFIED  
- CONTINGENCY LINE (MAX 5 PERCENT) IF PERMITTED  
- BUDGET RECONCILES WITH FINANCIAL STATEMENTS AND AUDIT FINDINGS  

SECTION 8: EVALUATION PLAN  
DEFINE BOTH FORMATIVE (PROCESS) AND SUMMATIVE (OUTCOME) EVALUATION COMPONENTS  
SPECIFY DATA SOURCES, COLLECTION TOOLS, FREQUENCY, AND RESPONSIBLE STAFF  
USE BASELINE DATA FROM NEEDS STATEMENT TO MEASURE CHANGE  
EXAMPLE: PRE‑ AND POST‑WORKSHOP SURVEYS TO ASSESS KNOWLEDGE GAIN; ADMINISTERED BY EVALUATION COORDINATOR  
INCLUDE A DATA ANALYSIS PLAN: STATISTICAL TESTS, QUALITATIVE CODING, REPORTING FORMAT  
ADDRESS HOW FINDINGS WILL BE USED FOR CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT AND REPORTING TO FUNDER  

SECTION 9: SUSTAINABILITY AND LEVERAGING RESOURCES  
ARTICULATE A CLEAR PLAN FOR CONTINUING THE PROGRAM AFTER GRANT ENDS  
IDENTIFY POTENTIAL REVENUE STREAMS: INDIVIDUAL DONORS, CORPORATE SPONSORS, FEE‑FOR‑SERVICE, GOVERNMENT CONTRACTS  
DESCRIBE PARTNERSHIPS THAT PROVIDE IN‑KIND SUPPORT OR SHARED STAFFING  
EXAMPLE: PARTNERSHIP WITH LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENT PROVIDES CLINIC SPACE VALUED AT $12,000 PER YEAR  
INCLUDE A SUSTAINABILITY TIMELINE SHOWING MILESTONES FOR FUNDING DIVERSIFICATION  

SECTION 10: PREPARING SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS  
GATHER REQUIRED ATTACHMENTS: IRS DETERMINATION LETTER, AUDITED FINANCIALS, BOARD LIST, ORGANIZATIONAL CHART  
PREPARE LETTERS OF SUPPORT FROM KEY PARTNERS WITH SPECIFIC COMMITMENTS  
ENSURE ALL DOCUMENTS ARE CURRENT, SIGNED, AND FORMATTED PER FUNDER SPECIFICATIONS  
CREATE A MASTER CHECKLIST TO VERIFY EVERY REQUIRED ITEM BEFORE SUBMISSION  

SECTION 11: SUBMISSION BEST PRACTICES  
SUBMIT EARLY TO AVOID TECHNICAL ISSUES; KEEP CONFIRMATION RECEIPTS  
USE THE FUNDER’S ONLINE PORTAL OR EMAIL ADDRESS EXACTLY AS INSTRUCTED  
DOUBLE‑CHECK FILE NAMING CONVENTIONS AND PAGE LIMITS  
ARCHIVE A COMPLETE COPY OF THE SUBMITTED PACKAGE FOR YOUR RECORDS  

SECTION 12: POST‑AWARD MANAGEMENT  
SET UP A GRANT MANAGEMENT CALENDAR WITH ALL REPORTING DEADLINES  
ASSIGN A GRANT OFFICER OR PROGRAM MANAGER AS PRIMARY POINT OF CONTACT  
ESTABLISH INTERNAL CONTROLS FOR EXPENDITURE TRACKING AGAINST THE APPROVED BUDGET  
CONDUCT MONTHLY FINANCIAL RECONCILIATIONS AND QUARTERLY PROGRAMMATIC REVIEWS  
PREPARE PROGRESS REPORTS THAT FOLLOW THE FUNDER’S TEMPLATE AND INCLUDE:  
- NARRATIVE UPDATE ON ACTIVITIES AND OUTCOMES  
- QUANTITATIVE DATA AGAINST INDICATORS  
- CHALLENGES AND MITIGATION STRATEGIES  
- FINANCIAL EXPENDITURE SUMMARY  

SECTION 13: COMPLIANCE AND AUDIT READINESS  
MAINTAIN ORGANIZED FILES FOR ALL RECEIPTS, CONTRACTS, TIME SHEETS, AND CORRESPONDENCE  
FOLLOW FUNDER’S SPECIFIC COMPLIANCE REQUIREMENTS (e.g., OMB CIRCULAR A‑133, STATE REGULATIONS)  
SCHEDULE AN INTERNAL AUDIT AT LEAST ONCE PER GRANT CYCLE  
ADDRESS ANY FINDINGS PROMPTLY AND DOCUMENT CORRECTIVE ACTIONS

COLLECT TESTIMONIALS, PHOTOS, AND IMPACT STORIES THROUGHOUT THE PROJECT  
CREATE A ONE‑PAGE IMPACT BRIEF FOR USE IN FUTURE PROPOSALS AND DONOR COMMUNICATIONS  
SHARE RESULTS WITH CURRENT FUNDER THROUGH A FINAL REPORT AND THANK‑YOU EVENT  
USE LESSONS LEARNED TO REFINE LOGIC MODELS, BUDGETS, AND EVALUATION PLANS FOR NEXT CYCLE
