Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application—this is exactly the kind of due diligence we encourage. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing Arrangements & Eligibility:**  
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., incorporated not-for-profit, local government authority, or other prescribed entity). The council must sign the grant agreement, hold the insurance policies you mentioned, and accept legal and financial responsibility for the project. Please ensure your auspicing agreement clearly outlines the council’s obligations regarding fund management, reporting, and acquittal.

**Grant Amount Parameters:**  
For this round, the minimum request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST-exclusive). Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. If the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you demonstrate confirmed co-contributions (cash or in-kind) for the balance.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:**  
Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is an eligible expense category. Costs must be for purchase and installation only; ongoing maintenance, operational salaries, and recurrent utilities are not funded. Quotes must be from licensed contractors and itemised.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**  
1. Signed auspicing agreement on council letterhead.  
2. Council’s current ABN, incorporation/registration evidence, and insurance certificates (public liability, professional indemnity, workers’ compensation).  
3. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item.  
4. Project plan with timeline, risk management, and sustainability strategy.  
5. Most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover.  
6. Letters of community support (minimum three).  
7. Evidence of land tenure or council permission for the installation site.

Applications close 5:00 pm AEST on 28 June. I recommend starting the draft in our online portal early so you can flag any technical questions well before the deadline. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you need further clarification.
