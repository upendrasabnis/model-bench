**1. Eligibility of groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to incorporated not‑for‑profit organisations and to unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by a local government authority, provided the auspicing body holds the requisite insurance and can demonstrate that it will act as the grant recipient on your behalf. Because your garden operates under the umbrella of the municipal council and the council retains the appropriate public liability coverage, you meet the eligibility criteria for this grant stream.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
For this funding round, the grant offers tiered awards ranging from **$5,000 to $25,000**. Projects that request less than $5,000 are ineligible, while those seeking more than $25,000 will be considered only if they demonstrate a phased implementation plan that aligns with the maximum award. Your proposed work on rainwater tanks and a shade structure comfortably fits within the $5,000–$25,000 bracket.

**3. Eligible project costs**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances climate‑resilience — such as rainwater harvesting systems, shading structures, solar‑powered irrigation, and related civil works — is expressly eligible under the grant’s cost‑eligibility rules. Eligible expenses include design and engineering fees, materials, labour, and project‑management overhead, provided they are documented as being incurred for the purpose of improving community resilience to extreme weather events.

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To complete a robust application, please prepare the following items:  

- **Letter of endorsement** from the council confirming its auspicing role and confirming that the council’s insurance covers the proposed works.  
- **Project budget** that breaks down each cost category (materials, labour, professional services, etc.) and aligns with the maximum award you are seeking.  
- **Detailed work plan** outlining timelines, milestones, and how the project will be monitored for resilience outcomes.  
- **Risk assessment and mitigation strategy** that demonstrates how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will reduce vulnerability to heatwaves and water scarcity.  
- **Proof of non‑profit status** (e.g., council registration, constitution, or equivalent governing document).  
- **Evidence of community support**, such as letters of support from local residents, schools, or other stakeholders.
