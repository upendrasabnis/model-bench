Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful description of your community garden’s activities and plans. I appreciate the opportunity to clarify the eligibility requirements for the Community Resilience Grant before you invest further time in preparing an application.

**Auspicing arrangements**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or another incorporated organisation. As long as the council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will administer the funds on your behalf and assumes responsibility for reporting and acquittal, your group meets the auspicing criterion. Please ensure that the letter includes the council’s ABN, contact details, and a statement of willingness to act as the legal entity for the grant.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated project scope, a request within the $10,000–$20,000 band would be well‑aligned with typical awards for similar infrastructure upgrades.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance resilience—such as the installation of rainwater harvesting tanks, shade structures, irrigation improvements, or other climate‑adaptation measures—are explicitly listed as eligible expenditures. Both the purchase of equipment and reasonable installation labour are allowable, provided that quotes are obtained and the total cost does not exceed the requested grant amount. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., stall fees, membership administration) are not covered.

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:  

1. **Auspice letter** from the local council (signed, on council letterhead).  
2. **Project plan** detailing the objectives, timeline, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will increase resilience to extreme heat.  
3. **Detailed budget** with itemised costs, including at least two written quotes for each major component (tanks, shade structure, plumbing, labour).  
4. **Evidence of community support**, such as letters of endorsement from residents, the council, or local organisations.  
5. **Financial summary** showing your current annual turnover (~$40,000) and a brief statement of how the grant will complement existing revenue streams.  
6. **Insurance confirmation** from the council confirming that public liability and volunteer coverage are in place for the garden’s activities.
