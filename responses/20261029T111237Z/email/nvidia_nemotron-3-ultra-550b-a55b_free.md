First, regarding organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible entity—such as a local government authority—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., is an incorporated legal entity with an ABN, holds appropriate insurances, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant). Your council’s willingness to hold insurances on your behalf is a positive indicator; we will require a signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming their role and responsibilities.

Second, the current round offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000. There is no minimum project size, but the total grant request must fall within this band. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears well within these limits.

Third, capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as water security assets and heat-mitigation structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category in the program guidelines. Installation, materials, and necessary site preparation are all allowable, provided they are essential to the project and represent value for money. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not eligible.

Finally, for supporting documentation you should prepare: (1) the council’s auspicing confirmation letter; (2) a simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and maintenance plan; (3) two competitive quotes for each major infrastructure item; (4) your most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover; (5) evidence of land tenure or council permission for the garden site; and (6) letters of community support demonstrating the garden’s reach and volunteer base.

Please consult the full Program Guidelines on our website for the complete eligibility checklist and assessment criteria.
