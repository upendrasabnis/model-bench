**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to incorporated not‑for‑profit organisations, registered charities, and *unincorporated* groups that are formally auspiced by an eligible entity such as a local government council, a state‑based peak body, or another incorporated organisation that holds the necessary legal and insurance responsibilities. Since your garden operates under the auspices of your regional council—which provides the required public liability and volunteer insurance—you meet this criterion. You will need to include a letter of auspice from the council confirming their support and detailing the insurance coverage they provide on your behalf.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is **$5,000** and the maximum is **$50,000** (GST exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your projected budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, you should be able to tailor your request within these limits.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate‑related stresses is an eligible expense. This includes the purchase and installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related site works. Operational costs (e.g., staff salaries, ongoing utilities) are not funded under this stream, but the capital outlay you described fits squarely within the program’s intent.
