Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local council are eligible to apply, provided the council acts as the legal entity and enters into the funding agreement on your behalf. The council must hold an ABN, be registered for GST, and provide a letter of support confirming their willingness to auspice the project, manage the funds, and assume legal and financial responsibility. As your council already holds the relevant insurances, this is a positive indicator.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure—falls well within this range. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some level of co-contribution (cash or in-kind), though this is not mandatory for requests under $20,000.

**Eligible Costs:** Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience and climate adaptation—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible expenditure in the program guidelines. Costs must be for purchase and installation only; ongoing maintenance or operational expenses are not covered.

**Supporting Documents:** At a minimum, you should prepare:
1. A signed auspicing agreement or letter from the council.
2. The council’s Certificate of Currency for Public Liability and Workers’ Compensation insurance.
3. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
4. A simple project plan with timeline, risk management, and volunteer coordination details.
5. Your most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover.
6. Evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site.

I strongly recommend reviewing the full Program Guidelines and Frequently Asked Questions on our website, as they contain detailed assessment criteria and definitions of “community resilience.” If you would like, I can arrange a brief phone call next week to discuss your project concept before you finalise the application.
