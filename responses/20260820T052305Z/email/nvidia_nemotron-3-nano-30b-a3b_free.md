**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our funding guidelines allow organisations that are not formally incorporated but are operating under the auspices of a local government authority to apply, provided they can demonstrate a clear governance structure and that the council’s insurance and financial controls cover the activities in question. Since your garden operates under the council’s umbrella and the council holds the necessary public‑liability insurance, you meet the eligibility requirement. We will, however, require a copy of the council’s endorsement letter confirming this arrangement and a brief governance statement outlining who holds decision‑making authority for the garden.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers awards ranging from **$5,000 to $25,000**. Projects that focus on climate‑adaptation infrastructure—such as water storage or shade structures—typically fall within this band. There is no separate “minimum” threshold beyond the $5,000 floor, but the amount awarded is contingent on the scope, budget, and impact of the proposed work.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Both rain‑water harvesting systems and shade structures are classified as eligible capital expenditures under the grant, as they directly enhance the community’s ability to maintain essential services during extreme weather events. Costs for design, procurement, installation, and associated site preparation are all fundable, provided they are reasonable, itemised, and supported by quotes or quotations from suppliers/contractors.

- Evidence of community need, such as a brief needs assessment or letters of support from local residents or council officers.
