Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. The council would assume full legal and financial responsibility for the grant, including acquittal and reporting obligations. We will require a formal letter from the council confirming their willingness to act as auspice, along with a copy of their current public liability insurance certificate of currency.

Your annual turnover of approximately $40,000 falls well within the program’s parameters for small community organisations. There is no minimum grant amount; however, the maximum request for this round is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure—aligns directly with the program’s objectives of building climate resilience and ensuring the continuity of essential community services during extreme weather events. Capital infrastructure of this nature is an eligible cost category, provided the assets remain the property of the auspicing council or are governed by a formal asset management agreement.

In terms of supporting documentation, please prepare the following: the council auspice letter and insurance certificate mentioned above; your group’s most recent annual financial statement (or a simple income/expenditure summary signed by your treasurer); written landowner consent for the garden site; two quotes for each major infrastructure item; a brief project plan with timeline and risk assessment; and letters of support demonstrating community need and volunteer capacity.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I strongly encourage you to start the online application early so that any technical questions can be resolved well before the deadline. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require further clarification on any of these points.
