**Auspicing Arrangements & Eligibility**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that they are a legal entity with an ABN, hold appropriate public liability insurance, and can enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. You will need to provide a signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming they accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant, including acquittal obligations.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. If the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you must demonstrate confirmed co-contributions or alternative funding sources for the balance.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts and ensure continued operation during extreme weather events align squarely with the program’s objectives. Eligible expenditure includes purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation or plumbing/electrical work certified by a licensed tradesperson. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Signed auspicing agreement or council letter of support (on letterhead) detailing the arrangement.
2. Council’s Certificate of Currency for Public Liability Insurance (minimum $20M).
3. Your group’s most recent financial statement or a simple income/expenditure summary showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
4. Quotes (minimum two per major item) for the rainwater tanks and shade structure, including installation.
5. Evidence of land tenure or a council letter confirming permission to install infrastructure on the site.
6. A brief project plan with timeline, risk management, and volunteer capacity statement.
7. Letters of community support or partnership (optional but strengthened).
