Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application—this is exactly the kind of due diligence that strengthens the assessment process. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) holds an Australian Business Number (ABN), maintains current public liability insurance, and is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. You will need to supply a signed letter from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, the council’s ABN, and a copy of their current certificate of currency for public liability insurance.

Second, the grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure falls well within this range. Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as water security and heat mitigation assets—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category in the program guidelines, provided the items are permanently affixed, remain the property of the auspicing council for the duration of the asset’s useful life, and are not funded through any other Commonwealth or state grant for the same purpose.

Third, in terms of supporting documentation, please prepare the following: (1) the council auspicing letter and insurance certificate mentioned above; (2) a simple project plan outlining scope, timeline, risk management, and maintenance arrangements; (3) two written quotes for each major infrastructure item; (4) your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report demonstrating the $40,000 annual turnover; (5) evidence of community support (e.g., letters from members, partner organisations, or the council); and (6) a completed budget template (available on the grant portal) showing total project cost, grant amount sought, and any in-kind or cash contributions.

Finally, I strongly recommend you attend the optional applicant webinar scheduled for 10 June, where the assessment criteria and common application pitfalls are discussed in detail. Registration details are on the program webpage. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require further clarification on any of these points.
