**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
The grant guidelines state that unincorporated community groups are eligible provided they have a formal auspice arrangement with a recognised entity—most commonly a local government council. In your case, operating under the council’s umbrella and having the council hold the necessary insurances satisfies this requirement. The council’s involvement also counts toward the administrative capacity criteria we assess, so you should be able to proceed with confidence.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
Our grant round does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award. Instead, we evaluate each proposal against the overall budget available for the round and the scope of the requested funding. Typical awards for infrastructure projects of the size you describe range from $15,000 to $40,000, but we are happy to consider any amount that reflects the true cost of your rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure installation.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes—capital infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible expenditures under the “Resilience Enhancements” category. These items directly support the garden’s ability to operate during extreme heat events, which is a core aim of the grant.

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend preparing the following:  

1. **Project plan** – a concise description of the installation, timelines, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget breakdown** – itemized costs for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any contingency.  
3. **Site plans** – a simple diagram showing where the tanks and shade will be placed.  
4. **Proof of auspice** – a letter from the council confirming its support and insurance coverage.  
5. **Financial statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or a set of cash‑flow statements given your $40 k turnover).  
6. **Letters of support** – endorsements from local businesses, community members, or other stakeholders.  
7. **Risk assessment** – a brief outline of how you will manage safety and maintenance.
