Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, hold the grant agreement, and assume financial and reporting responsibility. As your council already holds the relevant insurances, they would need to submit the application on your behalf and provide a letter of support confirming the auspicing arrangement, including their ABN and proof of incorporation.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project appears to fall comfortably within this range. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some level of co-contribution (cash or in-kind), though this is not a strict requirement for grants under $20,000.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts are both considered eligible, provided they are fixed assets that remain with the garden site. Items such as portable equipment, ongoing operational costs, or retrospective expenditure are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:** To streamline the process, the council (as applicant) should have ready:
1. A signed auspicing agreement or letter of support on council letterhead.
2. Council’s Certificate of Incorporation / ABN confirmation and current public liability insurance certificate.
3. Your group’s simple governance document (e.g., terms of reference or meeting minutes showing office bearers).
4. A project budget with at least two quotes for the tanks and shade structure.
5. Evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site (council letter or lease).
6. Your most recent annual financial statement (even if unaudited) showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
7. A brief project plan with timeline, risk management, and volunteer capacity statement.

The application portal opens on 1 June and closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I strongly recommend starting the draft in the portal early so any technical issues can be resolved before the deadline.
