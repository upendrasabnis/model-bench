**Eligibility of unincorporated groups under council auspices**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from unincorporated community groups that are auspiced by a local government entity, provided the auspicing organisation signs a formal letter of support confirming that it will assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds. In your case, the local council’s endorsement would satisfy this requirement, and you would be eligible to apply as long as the council agrees to act as the auspicing body.

**Grant amount range**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications are assessed on a competitive basis, and the requested amount should reflect the scale and scope of the proposed activities while demonstrating clear value for money.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works such as the installation of rainwater tanks and shade structures are considered eligible expenditures, provided they are directly linked to the stated resilience outcomes (e.g., ensuring continued operation during extreme heat events) and are not primarily for private benefit. You will need to include a detailed budget that itemises each component, quotes from suppliers, and a justification showing how the infrastructure enhances the garden’s capacity to withstand climate‑related stresses.

4. **Itemised budget** with at least two written quotes for each major item (tanks, shade structure, plumbing, labour) and a clear total cost.  
5. **Financial statements** (or a simple income‑expenditure summary) showing the garden’s annual turnover of approximately $40,000, to demonstrate financial viability and the need for external funding.
