Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for legal entities—specifically, that they are an Australian local government body with an ABN and appropriate insurance coverage. The council must be willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf and assume full financial and legal responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a signed letter from the council’s Chief Executive Officer or authorised delegate confirming the auspicing arrangement, the council’s acceptance of these obligations, and details of the insurance policies that cover your activities.

Second, the current grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 (GST exclusive). Given your projected project scope, your request would likely fall comfortably within this range. Please note that grants are typically awarded on a co-contribution basis; while in-kind contributions (such as volunteer labour) are valued, a cash or confirmed in-kind contribution from the applicant or auspice body of at least 10 per cent of the total project cost is generally required.

Third, the infrastructure you describe—rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure—is considered eligible capital expenditure under the “Community Infrastructure” stream, provided the assets are fixed, depreciable, and directly support the garden’s ongoing operation and climate resilience. Items such as portable shade sails or non-permanent storage tanks may be classified as equipment rather than infrastructure; if you are unsure about the classification of specific items, please include detailed specifications and quotes in your application so we can assess them accurately.

Finally, to streamline your preparation, I recommend gathering the following supporting documents now:
- The council auspicing letter described above.
- Your group’s most recent financial statement or a simple income-and-expenditure summary for the last financial year (showing the ~$40,000 turnover).
- Two competitive quotes for each major capital item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
- A project plan with a realistic timeline, risk assessment, and maintenance plan for the new assets.
- Evidence of community support (letters from members, partner organisations, or the council).
- A copy of the council’s public liability and workers’ compensation certificates of currency.

The application portal opens on 1 June and closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I encourage you to draft your responses in the program guidelines’ template well before the deadline.
