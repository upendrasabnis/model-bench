Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. The council would act as the legal entity receiving the funds and would assume full financial and reporting accountability for the project. We require a formal letter from the council confirming their auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and their agreement to manage the grant finances and acquittal processes.

Your annual turnover of approximately $40,000 falls well within the program’s thresholds for small community organisations. There is no minimum grant amount for this round; however, the maximum request is capped at $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project—involving the installation of rainwater tanks and a shade structure—aligns directly with the program’s objective of building climate resilience for community assets. Capital infrastructure of this nature is considered an eligible cost, provided it is fixed, permanent, and directly supports the organisation’s ongoing operations. Please note that we cannot fund portable equipment, routine maintenance, or operational costs such as utilities or wages.

In terms of supporting documentation, you should prepare the following to attach to your application:
1. The aforementioned auspicing confirmation letter from the council (on council letterhead).
2. A copy of the council’s Certificate of Currency for Public Liability Insurance (minimum $20 million) covering the garden’s activities.
3. Your group’s most recent annual financial statement or a simple income and expenditure report for the last financial year.
4. Two written quotes for the rainwater tanks and shade structure from licensed contractors.
5. A brief project plan outlining timelines, risk management, and how the infrastructure will be maintained post-completion.
6. Letters of support from community stakeholders or partner organisations, if available.

Applications close at 5:00 PM AEST on the date advertised on our website. I strongly encourage you to draft your responses in a separate document first, as the online portal does not auto-save. If you would like, I can arrange a brief 15-minute phone call next week to review a draft budget or discuss any aspect of the guidelines in more detail.

We look forward to receiving an application from your community garden.
