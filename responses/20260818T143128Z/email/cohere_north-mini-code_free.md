Thank you for reaching out and for the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria for our Community Resilience Grant. I’m pleased to confirm that your organization meets several key requirements, and I will address each of your questions in turn.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program explicitly welcomes applications from unincorporated community groups that are formally auspiced—or “hosted”—by a local government entity. When a council provides the necessary legal and financial backing (including insurance coverage, risk management, and accountability mechanisms), the group is considered a eligible applicant. In your case, the fact that the council holds the relevant insurances on your behalf satisfies this condition. Please note that you will still need to submit a “Host Organisation Agreement” signed by both your group’s leadership and the council representative, confirming that the council will act as the primary point of contact for grant administration and reporting.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, we assess each proposal against the program’s strategic priorities and the total cost of the project. Typical grants in recent rounds have ranged from $5,000 to $50,000, but we are prepared to consider larger requests where the proposed activities demonstrate strong community impact and sustainable outcomes. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appear to align well with this flexible funding model.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure assets such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are expressly listed as eligible expenses under the “Climate‑Resilient Infrastructure” category of our grant. These items must be directly related to the project’s resilience objectives—in your case, ensuring continued operation during hot summer periods. We recommend that you provide detailed quotations, specifications, and installation timelines for each component, as these will be reviewed to confirm that the costs are reasonable and that the assets will be owned and maintained by the community group (or the council, as appropriate).

**4. Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest assembling the following documentation:

- **Project Plan**: A clear description of the objectives, timeline, and expected outcomes, including how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be used.  
- **Budget Table**: Itemised costs with quotations for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary expenses (e.g., permits, contingency).  
- **Host Organisation Agreement**: A formal letter of support from your local council, confirming insurance coverage and oversight.  
- **Organizational Profile**: A short constitution or set of operating guidelines for your volunteer group, even if unincorporated.  
- **Financial Statements**: Most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or a simplified profit‑and‑loss statement and cash flow summary) demonstrating the $40,000 annual turnover you mentioned.  
- **Risk Assessment and Management Plan**: Documentation outlining how you will manage safety, maintenance, and potential environmental impacts.  
- **Community Support**: Letters of endorsement from local residents, businesses, or other stakeholder groups, as well as any preliminary sign‑ups from members who will benefit from the project.  

We also recommend reviewing the full grant guidelines on our website (Section III – Eligibility and Section IV – Required Documentation) to ensure that all formatting and submission requirements are met. The application portal will open for the upcoming round in two weeks, and we strongly advise submitting a draft well in advance of the closing date to allow time for any clarification requests.
