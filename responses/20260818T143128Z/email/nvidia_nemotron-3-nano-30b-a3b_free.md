Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed overview of your community garden project. I appreciate the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria and procedural requirements for the upcoming Community Resilience Grant round. Below you will find answers to each of the points you raised, together with recommendations on the documentation you may wish to prepare.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant program explicitly permits applications from unincorporated community groups that are formally auspiced by a local government authority, provided that the auspicing council holds the requisite insurances and can demonstrate its support for the project. In practice, this means that your garden, operating under the council’s umbrella, meets the eligibility threshold. However, the application must include a letter of support from the council confirming its willingness to act as the primary sponsor and to assume any necessary insurance responsibilities.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
Grant awards in this stream range from **$5,000 to $50,000**. Projects that propose a total budget within this band are considered eligible for funding. Should your proposed infrastructure (rainwater tanks and shade structure) fall below the $5,000 threshold, you may still apply, but the grant amount will be limited to the maximum allowable figure for the project’s scope. Conversely, if your budget exceeds $50,000, you would need to consider either a phased approach or a different funding stream.

**3. Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Both rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures are classified as **eligible capital expenditures** under the grant’s “Infrastructure and Climate‑Adaptation” category. The funding can cover design, procurement, installation, and associated site works, provided that the primary purpose is to enhance the community’s resilience to climate‑related stressors (e.g., extreme heat or water scarcity). It is advisable to articulate how the tanks will reduce dependence on municipal water and how the shade structure will mitigate heat‑related health risks for volunteers and visitors.

**4. Supporting documentation to submit**  
To substantiate your application, please include the following items:  

- A signed **letter of support** from the local council confirming the auspicing arrangement and insurance coverage.  
- A **detailed project budget** that delineates costs for equipment, materials, labour, and any contingency items, ensuring that the total requested amount does not exceed the $50,000 ceiling.  
- **Quotes or pro‑forma invoices** from reputable suppliers for the rainwater tanks, filtration components, and shade structure, to demonstrate market‑rate pricing.  
- A **project timeline** outlining key milestones, from procurement to installation, and a brief risk‑mitigation plan addressing potential delays.  
- Evidence of **community need**, such as demographic data, usage statistics from the weekly produce stall, or letters of endorsement from local residents or partner organisations.  
- Your **constitution or governing documents** (if any) and a concise description of the group’s objectives, governance structure, and volunteer base.  

**Additional guidance**  
When drafting the narrative section, emphasise how the proposed works will contribute directly to climate resilience—specifically by securing a sustainable water supply and providing a safe, habitable space during heat events. Highlight any plans for community education (e.g., workshops on water conservation) that could amplify the project’s impact.
