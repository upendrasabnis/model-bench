Subject: Re: Eligibility Inquiry – Community Resilience Grant  

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the valuable work you are doing with your community garden. I am happy to clarify the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Community Resilience Grant so you can determine whether to proceed with a full submission.

**1. Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from unincorporated community groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, incorporated association, or registered charity. As long as the council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds (including reporting and acquittal), your garden meets this requirement. Please attach that letter to your application; a simple email endorsement is insufficient.

**2. Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum award of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Funding is intended to cover the total eligible costs of the proposed activity, and applicants are encouraged to request an amount that closely matches the budgeted expenses. There is no requirement to contribute matching funds, although co‑funding can strengthen an application.

**3. Eligible infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that enhance climate resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related site preparations—are explicitly listed as eligible expenses. Both the purchase and installation of the tanks and shade structure, as well as any necessary engineering or design fees, can be included in the budget. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., stall utilities, membership administration) are not eligible unless they are directly tied to the delivery of the grant‑funded activity.

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To facilitate assessment, please prepare the following items:  

- **Auspice letter** from the local council on official letterhead, signed by an authorised officer, stating the council’s willingness to manage the grant and confirming insurance coverage.  
- **Project plan** (max two pages) outlining the objectives, timeline, milestones, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will improve resilience to extreme heat.  
- **Detailed budget** (using the template provided in the application guidelines) showing itemised costs, quotes or estimates for the tanks and shade structure, and any in‑kind contributions (e.g., volunteer labour).  
- **Proof of incorporation or council auspice** (if the council is not the applicant, a copy of the council’s ABN/ACN).  
- **Recent financial statement** (a simple profit‑and‑loss summary for the past 12 months) to demonstrate the $40,000 annual turnover you mentioned.  
- **Letters of support** (optional but recommended) from community members, local schools, or other stakeholders that attest to the garden’s impact and need for the proposed upgrades.
