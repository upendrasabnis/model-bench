Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in detail.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under the auspices of an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., is an incorporated not-for-profit, local government body, or other eligible entity) and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. Your arrangement with the council, including their holding of relevant insurances, satisfies this requirement. In your application, you will need to provide a formal letter of support from the council confirming their willingness to auspice the project, along with their ABN, incorporation status, and current public liability insurance certificate.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Given your project scope and annual turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within this band would be appropriate. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some capacity for co-contribution (cash or in-kind), although this is not mandatory for organisations with turnover under $100,000.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience—such as rainwater tanks and shade structures to mitigate heat impacts—is an eligible cost category. Eligible expenses include purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation or plumbing/electrical work. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not funded. You should obtain at least two quotes for each major component to demonstrate value for money.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
In addition to the council auspicing letter and insurance certificate, please prepare:
- Your group’s governance document (e.g., constitution, rules, or terms of reference)
- Most recent annual financial statement or treasurer’s report
- Project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and budget (using the template provided in the guidelines)
- Two quotes per major expenditure item
- Evidence of landowner consent for the installation site (if not council-owned)
- Letters of community support (minimum two) demonstrating the garden’s broader benefit

I encourage you to review the full Program Guidelines and Frequently Asked Questions on our website, as they contain further detail on assessment criteria and the online application portal. If you would like, I can arrange a brief pre-application phone call to discuss your project plan once you have drafted it.
