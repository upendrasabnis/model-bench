Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you and your volunteers are doing to strengthen your community garden. I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant and outline the information you will need to prepare a competitive application.

**Auspice arrangements**  
The grant program does accept applications from unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. In your case, operating under the auspices of your regional council satisfies this requirement, provided the council agrees to act as the legal applicant and to accept responsibility for the grant funds, reporting, and any contractual obligations. You will need to obtain a formal letter of support (or auspice agreement) from the council that confirms their willingness to administer the grant on your behalf and outlines any insurance or risk‑management arrangements they have in place.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of **$5,000** to a maximum of **$50,000** per successful applicant. There is no prescribed “average” award; the amount is determined by the scope, budget, and demonstrated need of the project. Given your estimated turnover of $40,000 and the nature of the proposed infrastructure, a request in the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be typical, but you should tailor the budget to the actual costs of the rainwater tanks and shade structure.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience of community‑based activities is an eligible expense. This includes the purchase and installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related site works (e.g., foundations, plumbing connections, and basic landscaping). Ongoing operational costs (e.g., stall fees, membership administration) are not covered, but you may include a modest contingency (up to 10 % of the total project cost) for unforeseen expenses related to the infrastructure build.

**Supporting documentation**  
To substantiate your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice letter** – signed by an authorised officer of the council, confirming their role as the legal applicant and outlining any insurance coverage they provide.  
2. **Project plan** – a clear description of the rainwater tank and shade structure works, including timelines, milestones, and how the infrastructure will improve resilience to hot summers.  
3. **Detailed budget** – itemised costs (equipment, delivery, installation, labour, permits, contingency) and any co‑funding or in‑kind contributions (e.g., volunteer labour, council‑provided site preparation).  
4. **Evidence of need** – brief data or anecdotal information showing increasing heat stress on the garden (e.g., temperature records, member feedback, reduced yields).  
5. **Organisational information** – a short profile of the volunteer group, its activities, and the council’s relationship to the group (meeting minutes, MOU, or similar).  
6. **Financial statements** – although you are not a registered charity, a simple statement of income and expenditure for the past 12 months (showing the $40,000 turnover) helps demonstrate financial viability.
