**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines permit applications from unincorporated community groups provided that the group is formally *auspiced* by a municipal or regional council. The council must act as the legal conduit for the project, holding any necessary insurances, licences, and tax‑exempt status on behalf of the group. In your case, because the local council already holds the relevant insurances and provides administrative support, you should be considered eligible. We do require a formal *Auspice Agreement* (or memorandum of understanding) between your group and the council that outlines the council’s responsibilities for financial management, risk management, and reporting. This document should be included with your application.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant program does not prescribe a fixed award size; rather, funding is allocated based on the overall merit of the proposal, the demonstrated need, and the total project budget. Historically, awards for projects of the nature you describe have ranged from approximately **$5,000 to $75,000**, but this is not a limiting factor. If your total eligible costs are lower than the minimum, you may still apply and receive a proportionate award. Conversely, if your project requires a larger investment, you may wish to discuss a phased‑funding approach or explore complementary funding sources.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs (rainwater tanks and shade structures)**  
Infrastructure of this type is explicitly listed as an eligible cost under the “Climate‑Resilient Community Assets” category of the grant. Both rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures that directly support year‑round garden operations qualify, provided they are:  

1. **Purchased from reputable suppliers** with warranty and installation details.  
2. **Installed in accordance with local building codes and council regulations**.  
3. **Integrated into a broader project plan** that demonstrates how the assets will achieve measurable resilience outcomes (e.g., reduced water consumption, increased crop yields during heat events).  

We recommend attaching detailed quotations or purchase orders for each item, along with a brief technical specification sheet.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To streamline the review process, we suggest you assemble the following materials well in advance of the submission deadline:

|----------|----------|
| **Project Narrative** (max 2 pages) | Clear description of the problem, objectives, methods, timeline, and expected outcomes. |
| **Budget Table** (itemised) | Breaks down all eligible costs, including contingency (typically 5‑10 %). |
| **Auspice Agreement** | Formal confirmation from the council that it will act as the applicant, manage funds, and provide required insurances. |
| **Insurance Certificate** | Proof that the council holds public liability and any professional indemnity coverage. |
| **Organizational Profile** | Brief overview of your group’s volunteer base, governance (if any), and community impact. |

| **Site Plans / Photographs** | Visual representation of the current garden layout and proposed installation locations. |
| **Letters of Support** | Endorsements from local businesses, schools, or community organisations. |
| **Previous Financial Statements** (optional) | For groups with an established track record, this can strengthen credibility. |
