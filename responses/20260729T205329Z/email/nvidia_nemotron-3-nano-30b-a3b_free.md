Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful description of your community‑garden project. I’m pleased to clarify the points you raised regarding the Community Resilience Grant (CRG) and to provide some practical guidance for your upcoming application.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The CRG accepts applications from entities that are *not* formally incorporated, provided they are operating under the auspices of a local government authority that holds the necessary insurances and can demonstrate formal oversight. Because your garden is administered by the regional council and the council’s insurance covers the activities, you meet the eligibility criteria for an unincorporated group in this situation. However, the application must be submitted on the council’s official letterhead and include a signed declaration from the council confirming its ongoing support and responsibility for the project.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
For the current funding round, the allowable grant size ranges from **$5,000 to $25,000**. Projects that request less than $5,000 may be considered only if they demonstrate exceptional community impact and a clear need for a smaller investment. Conversely, applications seeking more than $25,000 will not be accepted under this round. Your proposed works—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure—fit comfortably within the $5,000–$25,000 band, especially given the modest budget you outlined ($40,000 annual turnover).

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the capacity of a community organisation to respond to climate‑related stresses is an eligible cost under the CRG. The rainwater tanks and shade structure you plan to install are specifically listed as permissible expenditures, provided they are directly tied to resilience outcomes (e.g., water security during heatwaves or extended operating periods). Be sure to itemise these costs in your budget and include any quotations or pro‑forma invoices you have received.

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To accompany your application, we recommend the following documents:  

- A council‑signed letter of support confirming the auspice arrangement and insurance coverage.  
- A detailed project plan outlining objectives, timelines, and expected outcomes.  
- Itemised quotes or tenders for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, and any associated installation works.  
- Evidence of your current financial statements (the $40,000 turnover) and proof of any existing funding sources.  
- A risk‑management or sustainability plan that demonstrates how the project will be maintained after the grant period.
