Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you and your volunteers are doing to strengthen community resilience through the garden. I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant and provide guidance on the supporting documentation you will need to prepare.

**Auspice Arrangements**  
Unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by a local government entity (such as a council) are eligible to apply, provided the auspice agreement is in place at the time of submission and the council agrees to act as the legal and financial sponsor for the grant. The council must be able to receive the funds, administer any reporting requirements, and hold the necessary insurances. Please attach a signed auspice letter from the council that outlines the scope of their responsibility, the duration of the arrangement, and confirmation that they hold public liability and volunteer insurance covering the garden’s activities.

**Grant Size**  
The Community Resilience Grant program offers funding ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per project. Applications are assessed on a competitive basis, and the award amount is determined by the scale of the proposed activities, the demonstrated need, and the alignment with the program’s objectives. Your requested budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure would fall comfortably within this range, assuming the total cost does not exceed the $50,000 ceiling.

**Eligible Costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience of community‑based activities—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related fittings—is explicitly listed as an eligible expense. Costs for design, procurement, installation, and associated professional fees (e.g., engineer or contractor quotes) can be included. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water usage fees, routine maintenance) are not covered, but you may allocate a modest portion of the grant for initial commissioning and testing.

**Supporting Documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice Agreement** – signed letter or memorandum of understanding from the local council confirming their role as sponsor and detailing insurance coverage.  
2. **Project Plan** – a clear description of the rainwater tank and shade structure works, including site maps, dimensions, proposed materials, and a timeline for completion.  
3. **Budget Breakdown** – itemised costs (equipment, delivery, installation, contingency) with quotes or estimates from at least two suppliers where possible.  
4. **Organisational Information** – a brief profile of the garden (mission, volunteer structure, activities, and any existing partnerships) and a summary of your annual turnover ($40,000) with evidence of income sources (e.g., stall receipts, membership registers).  
5. **Letters of Support** – optional but valuable endorsements from neighbours, local schools, or the council highlighting the garden’s community impact.  
6. **Risk Management Summary** – outline of any safety considerations (e.g., tank installation, shade structure anchoring) and how they will be mitigated.
