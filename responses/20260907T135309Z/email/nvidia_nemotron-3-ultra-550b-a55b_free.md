Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are indeed eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and assume financial and reporting responsibility for the grant. Your council will therefore need to submit the application on your behalf, and we will require a formal letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, its willingness to hold the funds, and its current public liability insurance coverage that extends to your garden’s activities.

Regarding grant amounts, this round offers a minimum of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure falls well within this range, and capital infrastructure of this nature is explicitly listed as an eligible cost under the “Climate Adaptation and Community Infrastructure” stream, provided the assets remain the property of the auspicing organisation or the community group and are accessible to the broader public.

In terms of supporting documents, please prepare the following for submission with the application:
1. A signed auspicing agreement or memorandum of understanding between the garden group and the council.
2. The council’s current certificate of currency for public liability insurance (minimum $20 million).
3. A detailed project budget with at least two quotes for each major cost item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
4. Evidence of land tenure or a licence to occupy the site for a minimum of five years post-completion.
5. Your group’s most recent financial statement (even if unaudited) showing the $40,000 turnover.
6. Letters of community support demonstrating broader benefit beyond garden members.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November 2024. I strongly recommend drafting your responses in our online portal at least a week before the deadline to allow time for the council’s authorised signatory to review and submit. If you would like, I can arrange a brief 15-minute video call next week to walk through the portal and answer any follow-up questions.

We look forward to receiving a strong application from your community garden.
