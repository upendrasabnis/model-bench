Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in an application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

Firstly, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) holds an Australian Business Number (ABN), maintains appropriate public liability insurance, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a signed letter of support from the council confirming this arrangement, along with a copy of their Certificate of Currency for public liability insurance.

In terms of funding parameters, the Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Given your annual turnover of approximately $40,000, your organisation falls well within the program’s focus on small-to-medium community entities. The infrastructure you describe—rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure—is considered an eligible capital expenditure under the “Climate Adaptation and Community Infrastructure” stream, provided the assets are fixed, remain the property of the auspicing council or the community group, and directly support the garden’s ongoing operation during extreme heat events.

For supporting documentation, please prepare the following: the aforementioned council auspicing letter and insurance certificate; a simple project plan outlining timelines, risk management, and maintenance arrangements; two written quotes for each major infrastructure item; a current bank statement or financial summary demonstrating the $40,000 turnover; and letters of support from at least two community stakeholders (e.g., local schools, health services, or neighbourhood houses) evidencing the garden’s broader community benefit.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November. I strongly recommend starting the draft in our online portal at least two weeks prior to allow time for the council’s finance team to review the budget. If you would like, I can arrange a brief 15‑minute video call next week to walk through the application form or answer any follow‑up questions.

We look forward to potentially supporting your valuable work in building community resilience.
