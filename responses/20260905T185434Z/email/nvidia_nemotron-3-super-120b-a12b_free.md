Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant and for taking the time to clarify the eligibility requirements before preparing a full application. I have addressed each of your questions in turn below.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from not‑for‑profit community groups that are not incorporated, provided they are auspiced by an eligible legal entity such as a local council, incorporated association, or registered charity. In practice, the auspicing organisation must be willing to act as the grant recipient, assume financial and reporting responsibilities, and provide a formal letter of auspice that outlines the arrangement, the council’s acceptance of liability, and confirmation that the necessary insurances (public liability, volunteer insurance, etc.) are in place. If your council can supply such a letter, your garden would meet the eligibility criterion.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
For the current funding round, the Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum award of **$5,000** and a maximum award of **$50,000** per successful applicant. Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your project scope, a request in the $15,000–$30,000 band would be typical, but you should tailor the budget to reflect the actual quoted costs of the rainwater tanks and shade structure.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that enhance the resilience of community facilities—including the installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related site works—are explicitly listed as eligible expenditures. The grant can cover the purchase of materials, contractor labour, design fees, and any necessary permits or approvals. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water usage, maintenance) are not eligible, but you may include a modest contingency (usually up to 10 % of the total project cost) to accommodate unforeseen expenses during installation.

**4. Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please assemble the following items:

- **Auspice letter** from the local council on official letterhead, confirming the auspice arrangement, acceptance of financial responsibility, and verification of existing insurances.  
- **Proof of council endorsement** (e.g., a resolution or email from the relevant department) showing that the council supports the garden’s resilience project.  
- **Financial information** for the group: a simple income‑and‑expenditure statement for the past 12 months (showing the ~$40,000 turnover) and a projected budget for the grant period.  
- **Detailed project plan** outlining objectives, timeline (including key milestones), and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will improve resilience to heat and water scarcity.  
- **Itemised quotes** from at least two reputable suppliers/contractors for the tanks, shade structure, footings, plumbing, and any associated works.  
- **Site plan or schematic** indicating where the infrastructure will be placed, with any relevant council planning approvals or confirmation that no additional permits are required.  
- **Letters of support** (optional but recommended) from regular users of the garden, local schools, or neighbouring community groups that demonstrate broad community benefit.  
- **Insurance certificates** (public liability and volunteer coverage) held by the council, or a statement that the council’s policy extends to the auspiced group.

Once you have compiled these documents, you can submit your application via the online grants portal before the closing date (please verify the exact deadline on the grant website). Should you encounter any difficulties or require clarification on specific items, feel free to contact me directly at grants@agency.gov.au or on 02 1234 5678.
