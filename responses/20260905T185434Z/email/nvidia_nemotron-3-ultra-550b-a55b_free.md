First, regarding organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your council) is a legal entity that can enter into a funding agreement on your behalf. The council must hold the required public liability insurance and be willing to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a formal letter from the council confirming their auspicing arrangement, including their ABN and a statement that they accept the grant’s terms and conditions.

Second, the current grant round offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 per project. There is no minimum turnover threshold, so your annual revenue of approximately $40,000 does not affect eligibility. However, you will need to demonstrate financial viability through a simple income and expenditure statement for the last financial year.

Third, infrastructure costs such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are considered eligible capital expenditure, provided they are directly linked to the project’s resilience outcomes—namely, maintaining operations during extreme heat and water scarcity. Quotes from at least two suppliers for each item must be included.

Finally, key supporting documents to prepare include: the council auspicing letter; your group’s governance rules or terms of reference; a project plan with timeline and risk management; two supplier quotes per capital item; a detailed budget showing grant funds and any in-kind or cash contributions; and evidence of community support (letters, petition, or meeting minutes).
