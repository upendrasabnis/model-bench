Thank you for reaching out and for the clear description of your project and its potential impact on the community. I’m pleased to provide the following information regarding the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the current Community Resilience Grant round.  

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant programme welcomes applications from both incorporated not‑for‑profit entities and unincorporated groups, provided the group operates under a formal auspice arrangement with a municipal or governmental body. In your case, the fact that your local council holds the necessary insurances and acts as your auspice organisation satisfies the “organisational structure” requirement. However, we do require a written auspice agreement (or a letter from the council confirming its support and responsibility for liability) to be included with the application.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant fund does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award; rather, the amount awarded is determined by the scope of the proposed project, its alignment with programme objectives, and the overall available budget for the round. Past applications for infrastructure such as water‑harvesting and shading facilities have received between $15,000 and $75,000, but we encourage you to submit a realistic budget that reflects the true cost of your rainwater tanks and shade structure.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes, capital infrastructure that enhances environmental sustainability and community resilience is an eligible cost category. This includes, but is not limited to, rainwater tanks, permeable paving, solar‑powered lighting, and shade structures that protect both garden produce and participants from extreme heat. We do expect a clear link between each proposed item and the overall goal of “building resilience to climate‑related challenges,” which your project clearly meets.  

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documentation:  

1. **Auspice Agreement / Council Letter** – a formal statement confirming that the council will act as the legal sponsor, including liability insurance coverage.  
2. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem you are addressing, the proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
3. **Detailed Budget** – itemised costs for equipment, installation, labour, and any contingency funds, with sources for each line item.  
4. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed statements (or, for a small volunteer operation, a simple cash‑flow summary) demonstrating an annual turnover of approximately $40,000.  
5. **Proof of Insurance** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming that it covers your activities, or a letter confirming that coverage will extend to the new infrastructure.  
6. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, community groups, or other stakeholders that highlight the broader community benefit.  
7. **Site Plans or Drawings** – schematic layouts showing where the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be placed.  
8. **Evaluation Plan** – a brief outline of how you will measure success (e.g., water‑usage data, increased growing days, participant satisfaction).  

We strongly advise that you review the full grant guidelines on our website (particularly the “Eligibility” and “Eligible Projects” sections) before submitting your application.
