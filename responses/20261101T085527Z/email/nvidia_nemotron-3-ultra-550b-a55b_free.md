Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to clarify eligibility requirements before investing time in a full application—this is exactly the kind of due diligence we encourage. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing Arrangements:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) holds an Australian Business Number (ABN), maintains current public liability insurance covering the project activities, and signs the grant agreement as the legal entity responsible for financial accountability and reporting. Please ensure the auspicing agreement explicitly covers the proposed project scope and duration.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your projected budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to fall comfortably within this range.

**Eligible Costs:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is listed as an eligible expenditure category in the program guidelines. Costs must be for purchase and installation only; ongoing maintenance, operational wages, and retrospective expenses are excluded.

**Supporting Documents:** To streamline your application, please prepare the following:
1. A signed letter from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, insurance coverage, and willingness to enter into the grant agreement.
2. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report (showing the ~$40,000 turnover).
3. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item (tanks, shade structure).
4. A simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and evidence of community need (e.g., temperature data or council heat-mapping reports).
5. Proof of land tenure or a letter of consent from the landowner for the installation site.

The guidelines, application form, and a sample budget template are available on our website. Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. If you would like a brief pre-application phone consultation, please reply with a few preferred times next week.
