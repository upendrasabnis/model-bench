**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our Community Resilience Grant programme welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. Unincorporated groups are eligible provided they have a reputable auspicing body that can assume legal responsibility for the grant funds and any reporting obligations. In your case, the local council’s role as an auspice satisfies this requirement, as the council already holds the necessary insurances and can act as the primary applicant. If you decide to register as a charity in the future, that status will not be a prerequisite for this round; however, having an auspice that can sign the grant agreement is essential.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant programme does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award. Instead, funding levels are determined by the scope, impact, and budget of each proposal, typically ranging from $5,000 to $50,000 for projects of the size you describe. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure fall well within this band, and we would expect a grant award of roughly $20,000‑$30,000, assuming the remaining budget is sourced from other contributions.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes – capital infrastructure that directly supports the ongoing operation and climate‑adaptation goals of a community garden is an eligible cost. Specifically, the purchase and installation of rainwater harvesting systems, durable shade structures, and associated site works are covered under the “Environmental Resilience” category of the grant. We do require that the items be new, purpose‑built, and demonstrably necessary for the project’s objectives.  

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documentation:  

1. **Project Narrative** – a clear description of the problem (heat stress, water scarcity), the proposed solution, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – line‑item costs for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any contingency funds, indicating which portions you plan to fund yourself versus seeking from the grant.  
3. **Letter of Support from the Council** – confirming that the council will act as the legal auspice, will hold the insurances, and will oversee financial reporting.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your group’s composition, volunteer base, and how long you have been operating the garden.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed statements (or, for a small volunteer group, a simple set of cash‑flow statements and bank statements) demonstrating an annual turnover of around $40,000.  
6. **Insurance Certificate** – a copy of the council’s insurance policy confirming coverage for public liability and property damage relevant to the project.  
7. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, schools, or community members that attest to the project’s community benefit.  
8. **Site Plans and Technical Specifications** – drawings or sketches of the proposed tank and shade structure placement, along with supplier quotes or provisional quotations.  

We strongly advise that you also review the full grant guidelines on our website (the “Community Resilience Grant – Guidelines” PDF) to ensure you meet any additional procedural requirements, such as the need for a project timeline and measurable evaluation metrics.
