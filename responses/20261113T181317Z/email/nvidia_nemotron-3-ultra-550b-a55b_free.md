Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in detail.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are eligible to apply. The council must be the legal applicant and sign the funding agreement, but your group can be named as the "project delivery partner." You will need to provide a signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming they accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant, hold the required insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation/volunteer insurance), and will administer the funds on your behalf.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project scope appears to fall comfortably within this band.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts and ensure continued operation during extreme weather are considered eligible, provided they are fixed assets on land where you have documented tenure (e.g., a lease, licence, or letter of permission from the landowner/council for the project duration plus a minimum of five years). Costs for installation, plumbing, engineering certification, and necessary permits are also claimable. Ongoing operational costs, consumables, and retrospective expenses are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:** To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Council auspicing agreement/letter (on letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).
2. Evidence of land tenure/permission for the garden site.
3. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
4. Quotes (minimum two per major item) for the tanks, shade structure, and installation.
5. A simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and volunteer management strategy.
6. Letters of community support or partnership (optional but strengthen the case).
7. Council’s current certificates of currency for public liability and volunteer/workers’ compensation insurance.
