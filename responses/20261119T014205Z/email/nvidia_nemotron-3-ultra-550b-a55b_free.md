First, regarding organisational eligibility: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local government authority—are absolutely eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and assume financial and reporting accountability for the grant. Your council’s willingness to hold insurances and act as auspice is a strong foundation; we will require a signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council’s authorised delegate confirming this arrangement at the application stage.

Second, the current round offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000. There is no minimum project size, but the requested amount must fall within this band and be proportionate to the scale of activities described. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears well within these parameters.

Third, capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as water security assets and heat-mitigation structures—is an eligible cost category. Eligible expenditure includes purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation or permitting fees. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., utilities, maintenance) are not covered, so please ensure the budget isolates capital items only.

Finally, supporting documents you should prepare include: (1) the council’s auspicing letter on letterhead; (2) your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report demonstrating the $40,000 turnover; (3) a simple project plan with timeline, risk register, and volunteer capacity statement; (4) two quotes for each major infrastructure item; (5) evidence of land tenure or council permission for the garden site; and (6) a brief community benefit statement outlining how the project addresses climate resilience for your regional town.
