Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria for our upcoming Community Resilience Grant round. I’m pleased to share that we do consider applications from unincorporated community groups, provided they are formally auspiced by a legal entity such as your local council. When a council acts as the fiscal sponsor, it can sign the grant agreement, hold any awarded funds, and assume responsibility for compliance reporting. In your case, the fact that the council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf should satisfy the liability and risk‑management requirements we typically require.

**Grant size** – Our grant program does not prescribe a minimum or maximum award; rather, we evaluate each proposal against its scope, impact, and budget. Applications have been successful with requested amounts ranging from roughly $5,000 to $75,000, but we are open to reviewing any well‑justified request that aligns with the program’s objectives. If your total project cost is lower than the typical range, please feel free to propose a proportionate request.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs** – Yes, capital assets such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are considered eligible expenses, provided they directly support the resilience outcomes you are seeking (e.g., water security, heat‑stress mitigation). We do require that the items be essential to the project’s success, have a useful life of at least five years, and be clearly documented in the budget.

**Supporting documents** – To streamline the review process, we recommend preparing the following:

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, objectives, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – itemizing all costs, including equipment, installation, and any contingency.  
3. **Proof of Auspice** – a letter from your council confirming the sponsorship arrangement, insurance coverage, and authority to sign grant agreements.  
4. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for a small operation, a simple cash‑flow summary) demonstrating your $40,000 turnover.  
5. **Organizational Profile** – a brief constitution or charter outlining your group’s purpose and volunteer structure.  
6. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, community members, or other stakeholders.  
7. **Site Plans or Sketches** – illustrating where the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be located.

We would be happy to review any draft documents you prepare before you submit a full application. Please forward them at your convenience, and we will provide feedback within five business days. If you have any additional questions about reporting requirements, matching funds, or the application portal, let us know—our grants team is here to support you throughout the process.

I look forward to seeing your proposal and to collaborating on a project that will truly enhance community resilience in the region.
