Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in an application—this is exactly the kind of due diligence we encourage. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing and eligibility:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspice arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation (in this case, your council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria—specifically, that it is an incorporated legal entity with current public liability insurance and an ABN. The council would need to sign the grant agreement on your behalf and assume legal and financial responsibility for the project. Please ensure you have a written auspice agreement or letter of support from the council confirming this arrangement, including their agreement to hold insurances and manage funds.

**Grant amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project—rainwater tanks and a shade structure—falls squarely within this range. Please note that we cannot fund more than 80 % of total project costs; the remaining 20 % may be met through in-kind contributions (volunteer labour, donated materials) or cash co-contributions.

**Eligible costs:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate impacts is an eligible cost category. Rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible in the program guidelines, provided they are fixed assets that remain with the garden site. Costs for site preparation, plumbing, engineering certification, and installation labour are also claimable. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water bills, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting documents to prepare:**
1. Council auspice agreement/letter of support on letterhead, signed by an authorised officer.
2. Council’s current public liability insurance certificate of currency ($20M minimum).
3. Your group’s simple governance statement (committee members, meeting frequency, decision-making process).
4. Project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and itemised budget (quotes for tanks, structure, installation).
5. Evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site (lease, licence, or council letter).
6. Most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
7. Letters of community support (at least two) demonstrating broader benefit.

**Next steps:** I recommend downloading the full Program Guidelines and Application Form from our website; the guidelines contain the assessment criteria and a checklist that mirrors the list above.
