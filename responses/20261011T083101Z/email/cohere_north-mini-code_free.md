**Eligibility of unincorporated, council‑auspiced groups**  
Our grant guidelines state that applicants must be either a registered charitable entity or an unincorporated group that is formally auspiced by a public‑sector body such as a local council. The council’s role must be documented through a formal agreement (often referred to as an “auspice agreement”) that confirms the council will maintain liability insurance, oversight of finances, and responsibility for any compliance reporting. If you can provide a copy of such an agreement—or a letter from the council confirming its auspice status—we will be able to consider your group eligible under the “unincorporated group” pathway.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, the funding envelope is determined by the scope, impact, and budget of the proposed project. Historically, awards have ranged from $5,000 to $75,000, but the final amount will be based on the cost‑effectiveness of your proposal and the availability of funds in the round. Our expectation is that the total project budget will be proportionate to the requested grant; we therefore encourage you to prepare a detailed budget that reflects the actual costs of the rainwater tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary expenses.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is expressly permitted under the “Environmental Resilience” component of the grant. These items are considered eligible because they directly support the continuity of community operations during periods of extreme weather, align with sustainability objectives, and generate long‑term cost savings. Be sure to itemise each component (e.g., tank capacity, materials for the shade structure, labour, permits) so we can assess their relevance and value.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend compiling the following documents:

1. **Auspice Agreement / Council Letter** – a formal statement confirming that the council will act as the fiscal host, provide insurance coverage, and assume legal responsibility for the project.  
2. ** organisational Profile** – a brief overview of your group’s mission, volunteer base, and any past community projects.  
3. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) showing the $40,000 turnover and sources of income.  
4. **Budget and Cash‑Flow Projection** – a line‑item budget for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, and any other costs, together with a 12‑month cash‑flow forecast.  
5. **Project Plan** – a timeline, deliverables, and milestones, including installation phases and expected operational dates.  
6. **Community Impact Statement** – evidence of need (e.g., climate data for the region, letters from local residents, or attendance figures from the produce stall) and how the project will benefit the wider community.  
7. **Risk Assessment and Mitigation** – a brief outline of potential health, safety, and environmental risks, together with the measures you will take to manage them.  
8. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from the council, local businesses, or community organisations that demonstrate broad backing for the initiative.  

If you can provide these documents, our review panel will be able to assess your proposal comprehensively and address any gaps well before the submission deadline.
