Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful description of your community garden’s activities and plans. I appreciate the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant before you invest further time in preparing an application.

**Auspicing arrangements**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. As long as your council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds (including reporting and acquittal), your garden can proceed with an application. The auspicing letter should be on council letterhead, signed by an authorised officer, and include the council’s ABN and contact details.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Projects requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within the $10,000–$30,000 band would be competitive, though the final amount will be assessed against the project budget and demonstrated need.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience—such as rainwater harvesting tanks, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related site works—is explicitly listed as an eligible expense. Costs must be itemised, justified, and supported by quotes or vendor estimates. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., staff wages, routine maintenance) are not covered unless they are integral to the delivery of the infrastructure component (for example, a short‑term project manager overseeing the installation).

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice agreement** – a signed letter from the local council confirming auspice, including the council’s ABN and contact person.  
2. **Project plan** – a concise description (max 500 words) outlining the problem (increasing heat and water scarcity), the proposed solution (rainwater tanks + shade structure), timelines, and expected outcomes (e.g., water savings, extended growing season, community participation metrics).  
3. **Detailed budget** – a table showing each cost item, unit cost, quantity, and total, with at least two written quotes for major items (tanks, shade structure, footings, installation).  
4. **Evidence of community support** – letters of support from council officers, local residents, or partner organisations, and any existing volunteer registers or membership lists that demonstrate the garden’s active engagement.  
5. **Financial statements** – a simple profit‑and‑loss summary for the last 12 months (showing the $40,000 turnover) and a current bank statement to verify financial management capacity.  
6. **Risk management outline** – brief notes on insurance coverage (council‑held), work‑health‑safety considerations for installation, and any permits required (e.g., building approval for the shade structure).
