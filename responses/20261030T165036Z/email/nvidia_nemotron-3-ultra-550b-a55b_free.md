Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in detail.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for legal entities. The council must be willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf, accept financial accountability for the grant, and maintain the required insurances—including public liability and workers’ compensation—which you have confirmed are already in place. You will need to submit a signed letter from the council’s Chief Executive Officer or authorised delegate confirming the auspicing arrangement, their acceptance of the grant obligations, and their ABN.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project scope appears to fall comfortably within this band. Please note that we cannot fund 100 per cent of project costs; a minimum co-contribution of 20 per cent (cash or in-kind) is required, which volunteer labour valued at $30 per hour can satisfy.

**Eligible Costs**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks, associated plumbing, and a permanent shade structure designed to mitigate heat stress for volunteers and preserve crops are explicitly listed as eligible in the program guidelines. Site preparation, engineering certification, and installation labour are also claimable. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water rates, stall consumables) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
In addition to the council auspicing letter, please gather:
1. Your group’s constitution or rules of operation (even if informal).
2. A simple profit-and-loss statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 annual turnover.
3. Two written quotes for each major cost item (tanks, structure, installation).
4. A project plan with timeline, risk management, and volunteer safety procedures.
5. Evidence of land tenure or council permission for the infrastructure.
6. Letters of support from at least two community stakeholders.
