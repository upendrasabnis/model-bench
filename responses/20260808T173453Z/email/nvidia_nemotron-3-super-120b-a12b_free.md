Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with the community garden. I appreciate you taking the time to confirm eligibility before preparing a full application, and I am happy to provide the details you requested.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to a broad range of applicants, including incorporated associations, registered charities, and unincorporated groups that operate under an auspicing arrangement with a local government entity. As long as your garden’s activities are formally endorsed by the council and the council agrees to act as the legal and financial auspice for the grant, your group is eligible to apply. You will need to include a signed auspice agreement or a letter of support from the council confirming that they will hold the grant funds, manage reporting, and assume any liability associated with the project.

**Grant amount range**  
For this funding round, the minimum grant award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated project costs, you may wish to prepare a budget that falls comfortably within these limits; if your total exceeds $50,000, consider phasing the works or seeking co‑funding from other sources to bring the grant‑requested amount into the allowable band.

**Eligibility of proposed infrastructure**  
Capital works that enhance the resilience of community facilities—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures—are expressly listed as eligible costs under the “infrastructure improvement” category. Both items qualify, provided they are directly linked to the stated objective of maintaining garden operations during hotter periods and are not primarily for commercial gain. Ensure that the budget clearly itemises each component (e.g., tank purchase, installation, shade‑cloth, supporting posts) and includes any associated labour or professional‑services fees.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please gather the following items:

1. **Auspice agreement or council letter of support** – confirming the council’s role as legal auspice and acknowledging insurance coverage.  
2. **Project plan** – a brief description (max 500 words) outlining the need, objectives, implementation timeline, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will improve resilience.  
3. **Detailed budget** – itemised costs, quotes (or indicative pricing) for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any contingency (typically 10 %).  
4. **Evidence of community benefit** – photographs of the garden, membership records, or a summary of weekly stall turnover and volunteer hours to demonstrate the $40,000 annual turnover and community engagement.  
5. **Governance documents** – even though you are not a registered charity, provide any existing governance framework (e.g., terms of reference, meeting minutes) that shows how decisions are made and how funds will be managed.  
6. **Insurance confirmation** – a copy of the council’s public liability policy that covers the garden’s activities, or a statement from the council verifying coverage.

Once these documents are assembled, you can proceed to the online application portal. Should you have any further questions—particularly regarding the auspice arrangement or budget formatting—please do not hesitate to contact me directly at grants@resilience.gov.au or on 02 1234 5678.
