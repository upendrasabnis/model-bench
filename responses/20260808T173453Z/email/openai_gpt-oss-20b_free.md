**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to a broad range of community organisations, including unincorporated groups, cooperatives, and clubs that are *not* registered charities. The key requirement is that the applicant must have a clear governance structure and the ability to enter into a contractual relationship with the funder. Because your garden operates under the auspices of the local council, you can submit an application on behalf of the garden. The council will need to provide a formal letter of support confirming that it holds the relevant insurances, licences, and other statutory obligations on your behalf. In addition, the council will need to sign an endorsement form indicating that it agrees to the terms of the grant and to any responsibilities that arise (e.g., reporting, compliance).

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amount**  
For this round, the grant ranges from **$5,000 to $25,000**. The exact amount awarded will be determined by the assessment panel based on the strength of the proposal, the impact of the project, and the sustainability of the outcomes. We recommend preparing a budget that includes a line item for infrastructure (rainwater tanks, shade structure, associated fittings) and ensuring that the total proposal falls within the stated range.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that directly supports the resilience and sustainability of the garden is fully eligible. This includes:

- Rainwater harvesting systems (tanks, filtration, storage)
- Shade and shelter structures (permanent or semi‑permanent)
- Site improvements that reduce vulnerability to extreme heat (e.g., planting shade trees, mulching)
- Energy‑efficient lighting or solar power for the produce stall

All infrastructure items should be fully costed, with supplier quotations and, where possible, evidence of procurement timelines.

| Document | Purpose |
