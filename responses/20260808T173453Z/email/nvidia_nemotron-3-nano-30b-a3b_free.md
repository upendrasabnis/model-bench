Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful description of your community garden project. I’m pleased to clarify the points you raised regarding eligibility, grant limits, allowable costs, and required documentation.

**1. Eligibility of groups auspiced by a council**  
Our funding guidelines permit applications from unincorporated groups provided they are formally *auspiced* by an eligible host organisation. A local council that holds appropriate public liability insurance on behalf of the group satisfies this requirement, provided the council is listed as the primary contact and signatory on the application. Please ensure that the council’s letter of support explicitly states its role as the auspicing body and confirms that it has the authority to act on your behalf.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers awards ranging from **$5,000 to $25,000**. Projects that demonstrate a clear cost‑benefit analysis and a realistic budget are more likely to be funded at the higher end of the scale. Your proposed rainwater tanks and shade structure appear well‑suited to the $10,000‑$20,000 bracket, assuming the total project cost falls within that range.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Both rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures are classified as *eligible capital works* under the program, provided they directly enhance the garden’s capacity to respond to climate‑related stressors (e.g., water scarcity and extreme heat). Costs must be *eligible, reasonable, and directly attributable* to the project. In‑kind contributions, such as volunteer labour, are permissible but must be documented separately.

**4. Supporting documentation to prepare**  
- A signed **Letter of Support** from the council confirming its auspicing role and insurance coverage.  
- A **Detailed Project Budget** outlining all expected expenditures, including a line‑item breakdown for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary works.  
- **Proof of Income** (e.g., recent financial statements or cash‑flow summaries) demonstrating that the garden’s annual turnover is approximately $40,000.  
- **Site plans or schematics** showing the proposed locations of the tanks and shade structure.  
- **Risk Management Plan** addressing how the infrastructure will improve resilience to heat and water shortages.
