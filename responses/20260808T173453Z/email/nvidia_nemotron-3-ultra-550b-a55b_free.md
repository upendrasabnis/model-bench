First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups that operate under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are indeed eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., it is an incorporated not-for-profit or a local government authority) and is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. The council would need to provide a letter of support confirming their willingness to act as auspice, their ABN, and evidence of their current public liability insurance that covers your activities.

Second, the Community Resilience Grant offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 per project. There is no minimum project size, but the requested amount must fall within this range. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to sit comfortably within these limits.

Third, capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate impacts—such as water security installations and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category in the current guidelines. Costs must be for the purchase and installation of fixed assets; ongoing maintenance or operational expenses are not eligible.
