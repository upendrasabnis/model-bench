Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are eligible to apply. The council must be the legal applicant and sign the funding agreement on your behalf. They will also bear the legal and financial accountability for the grant. Please ensure you have a written auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming this arrangement, their willingness to hold the funds, and their current public liability insurance coverage that extends to your activities.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your project scope appears to fall comfortably within this range. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some level of co-contribution (cash or in-kind), though this is not a strict eligibility criterion for grants under $20,000.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience is a priority for this program. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts are considered eligible capital expenditure. However, ongoing operational costs (e.g., water bills, maintenance contracts) are not eligible. You must provide quotes from licensed contractors for both components.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**
1.  **Auspicing Agreement / Council Letter:** On council letterhead, confirming the arrangement, insurance coverage, and authorisation for the CEO or delegate to sign the grant agreement.
2.  **Project Budget:** A detailed budget using our template (attached), showing GST-exclusive costs, the grant amount sought, and any co-contributions.
3.  **Quotes:** At least two written quotes for the tanks and shade structure from licensed suppliers/installers.
4.  **Governance Evidence:** Minutes from a recent garden meeting endorsing the application and the auspicing arrangement.
5.  **Financials:** A simple income and expenditure statement for the last financial year (showing the ~$40k turnover) and a current bank statement.
6.  **Land Tenure:** Evidence of your legal right to occupy the land (e.g., lease, licence, or letter from the landowner/council) for a period exceeding the project delivery timeline by at least 12 months.
7.  **Risk Management Plan:** A brief plan addressing installation risks, extreme weather, and volunteer safety.
