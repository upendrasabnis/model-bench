Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed description of your community garden’s vision. I’m pleased to provide clarification on the points you raised, and I hope this information will help you decide whether to proceed with a full application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our Community Resilience Grant is open to both incorporated not‑for‑profit organisations and unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by a municipal or governmental entity. An auspice arrangement is considered acceptable when the council holds the required insurances, holds the assets, and assumes fiduciary responsibility for the grant‑related activities. Therefore, your garden’s arrangement should satisfy the eligibility requirement regarding organisational status. A short letter from the council confirming the auspice relationship and outlining the insurance coverage we hold on your behalf is usually sufficient as an annex to the application.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant programme does not prescribe a fixed minimum; successful applications typically request between $5,000 and $50,000, depending on the scale and scope of the proposed work. Your anticipated costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure fall comfortably within this range. If you wish to request a smaller amount (e.g., $3,000–$4,000) we can certainly consider that, provided the budget aligns with the full cost of the infrastructure.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital assets such as rainwater tanks, permanent shade structures, and associated installation work are expressly listed as eligible expenses under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” category of the guidelines. These items must be essential to the project’s resilience objectives, directly support the garden’s ongoing operations, and have a useful life of at least five years. Photomicro‑graphs of the proposed layout and quotations for the tanks and structure will be required to demonstrate that the costs are realistic and directly related to the grant’s purpose.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application we recommend the following documentation:  

1. **Project narrative** – a concise description of the problem you are addressing (heat stress), the proposed solution, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget table** – detailed line items for tanks, shade structure, installation, permits, and any ancillary costs, with quoted invoices where possible.  
3. **Letters of support** – from the council (confirming auspice and insurance) and from any partner organisations or community members.  
4. **Financial statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed statements (or a set of basic cash‑flow statements for an unincorporated group) to verify turnover and financial stability.  
5. **Insurance certificate** – a copy of the council’s liability policy confirming coverage for the garden’s activities.  
6. **Site plans or sketches** – showing where the tanks and shade structure will be located.  

We also advise reviewing the full grant guidelines (available on our website) to ensure that all other criteria—such as alignment with the grant’s thematic priorities and demonstration of community involvement—are met. Should you decide to move forward, we are happy to provide a brief pre‑application review once you have compiled the documents listed above.

Please feel free to contact me directly if any aspect of the eligibility criteria remains unclear, or if you would like to arrange a brief telephone conversation to walk through the application process. We wish your garden every success in building resilience for the coming summers.
