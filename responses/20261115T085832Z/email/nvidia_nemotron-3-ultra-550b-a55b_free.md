Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarification on each of your questions.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local council are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria—specifically, that they are a legal entity with an ABN, hold appropriate public liability insurance, and are willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. You will need to provide a signed letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their acceptance of financial and legal responsibility for the grant, and evidence of their current insurance certificates.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the Community Resilience Grant offers funding between **$5,000 and $50,000** (GST exclusive). There is no requirement for matched funding, though applications demonstrating co-contributions—whether cash, in-kind, or volunteer labour—are viewed favourably during assessment. Given your project scope, your request should fall comfortably within this range.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience is an eligible expenditure category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts and ensure continued operation during extreme weather events align squarely with the program’s objectives. Eligible costs include purchase of materials, contractor labour for installation, necessary permits, and associated engineering or design fees. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. **Auspicing agreement letter** from the council (as described above).
2. **Project plan** with timeline, risk management, and clear outcomes linked to community resilience.
3. **Detailed budget** (GST exclusive) with at least two quotes for major items (tanks, shade structure, installation).
4. **Evidence of community need** (e.g., letters of support from garden members, local health service, or council).
5. **Governance documentation** for your group (constitution, rules, or minutes showing decision to apply).
6. **Financial records** for the last financial year (income/expense statement) to demonstrate viability.
7. **Insurance certificates** (public liability, volunteer/worker’s compensation) held by the council.
8. **Land tenure evidence** – lease, licence, or letter from the landowner permitting the infrastructure.
