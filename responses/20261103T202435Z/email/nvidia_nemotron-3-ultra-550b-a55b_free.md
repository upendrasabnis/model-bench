Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarification on each of your questions.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and assume financial and reporting accountability for the grant. Your council will need to provide a letter of support confirming the auspicing arrangement, including their ABN, insurance coverage details, and a statement accepting legal and financial responsibility for the project.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Given your projected project scope, your request would need to fall within this band. Please note that we cannot fund 100 per cent of project costs; a minimum co-contribution of 20 per cent (cash or in-kind) is required, which can include volunteer labour valued at the current Australian Bureau of Statistics volunteer rate.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure qualify, provided they are fixed assets that remain with the garden site. Costs for site preparation, plumbing, engineering certification, and council permits are also eligible. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water bills, maintenance) are not covered.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Council auspicing letter (as described above).
2. Quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure item.
3. A simple project plan with timeline, risk register, and maintenance plan.
4. Evidence of community need (e.g., letters of support, usage statistics, climate data for your region).
5. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover.
6. Proof of land tenure or a licence/lease agreement for the garden site.
7. Insurance certificates of currency held by the council (public liability, volunteer workers).

Once you have these documents drafted, you are welcome to send a brief summary for a pre-application check. The round closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November, and late submissions cannot be accepted.
