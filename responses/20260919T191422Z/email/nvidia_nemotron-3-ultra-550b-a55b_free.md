Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions below.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria—specifically, that it is a legally constituted entity with an ABN and holds appropriate public liability insurance. As the council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf, this arrangement satisfies our risk-management requirements. You will need to submit a signed Auspicing Agreement or a letter from the council confirming their willingness to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. If the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you demonstrate confirmed co-contributions (cash or in-kind) for the balance.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure that enable continued operation during extreme heat events align squarely with the program’s objectives. Please note that site preparation, installation, and associated plumbing or electrical work are also eligible, whereas ongoing maintenance costs are not.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Council auspicing letter/Agreement (as above).
2. Evidence of the council’s current public liability insurance certificate.
3. Your group’s most recent financial statement or a simple income-and-expenditure summary showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
4. Quotes from at least two suppliers for the tanks and shade structure.
5. A brief project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and volunteer capacity statement.
6. Letters of support from community stakeholders (e.g., local Landcare group, neighbourhood house).

**Next Steps**
The guidelines and application form are available on our website, and I strongly recommend reviewing the assessment criteria—particularly the sections on community need, climate adaptation outcomes, and sustainability beyond the grant period. The round closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November; late submissions cannot be accepted.

If you would like feedback on a draft budget or project narrative before final submission, please feel free to email them to me at least five business days before the deadline.
