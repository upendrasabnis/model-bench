**Auspicing arrangements**  
The grant programme expressly permits applications from unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible entity, such as a local government authority. Your council’s role as the auspicing body satisfies the requirement that the applicant have a legal entity capable of entering into a funding agreement and managing acquittal reporting. Consequently, your garden is eligible to apply provided the council signs the auspice agreement and assumes responsibility for the grant funds and any associated reporting obligations.

**Grant size**  
For this funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Projects requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000– $30,000) would be well‑aligned with the programme’s intent to support scalable, community‑driven resilience initiatives.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances a community’s ability to withstand climate‑related stresses is an eligible expense. Rainwater harvesting tanks and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat are therefore permissible, provided they are clearly linked to the stated resilience outcomes and are not primarily for general amenity improvement. Operational costs (e.g., staff wages, routine maintenance) may be included only up to a maximum of 20 % of the total budget.

**Supporting documentation**  
To substantiate your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice agreement** – a signed letter or memorandum of understanding from the local council confirming they will act as the legal recipient of the grant and will oversee financial management and reporting.  
2. **Project plan** – a concise description (max 2 pages) outlining the objectives, activities, timeline, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will improve resilience to hot summers.  
3. **Budget spreadsheet** – itemised costs for equipment, installation, any required permits, and a clear indication of the proportion allocated to capital versus operational expenses (capped at 20 %).  
4. **Evidence of community support** – letters of endorsement from at least two community stakeholders (e.g., neighbouring residents, local schools, or the council’s community services unit).  
5. **Financial summary** – a brief statement of your garden’s annual turnover and sources of income (produce stall receipts, membership fees) to demonstrate financial viability and the need for external funding.  
6. **Insurance details** – confirmation from the council that the existing public liability and volunteer insurance cover the proposed works; if additional coverage is required, outline how it will be obtained.
