**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program is open to a broad spectrum of applicants, including unincorporated community groups, provided that the group operates under a formal auspice agreement with a local government or other recognised entity. The council’s involvement is viewed favourably because it can supply the required public liability insurance, governance oversight, and a clear point of contact for any compliance matters. To demonstrate eligibility, we typically require:  

* A written auspice agreement (or memorandum of understanding) that outlines the council’s responsibilities, including insurance coverage, financial oversight, and the authority for the group to administer grant‑funded activities.  
* A letter from the council confirming that it holds the relevant insurances on behalf of the group and that it consents to the group acting as the primary applicant.  

If you are able to provide these documents, an unincorporated, council‑auspiced group will satisfy the eligibility criteria.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant is designed to fund projects ranging from modest improvements to larger‑scale infrastructure initiatives. The current funding band is **$5,000 – $150,000** per application. Projects that are primarily capital‑intensive—such as the installation of rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure—often fall within the upper half of this range, but the exact amount will depend on the overall budget, the proportion of eligible costs, and the merit of the proposed outcomes.  

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs (rainwater tanks and shade structure)**  
Both rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible capital expenses under the “Climate‑Resilient Infrastructure” category of our grant guidelines. Eligible costs include the purchase, delivery, and installation of the equipment, as well as any ancillary works required to integrate the infrastructure with existing site utilities. Please note that routine maintenance or consumable items (e.g., water filters, temporary awnings) are not covered.  

**4. Recommended supporting documents**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest preparing the following materials:  

* **Project Plan & Timeline** – a clear description of the work, objectives, milestones, and completion dates.  
* **Detailed Budget** – a line‑item breakdown that distinguishes between capital costs (tanks, shade structure, foundations, labour) and any allowable overheads. Include quotes or invoices where possible.  
* **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts, or, for a small volunteer‑run operation, a simple income‑and‑expense statement for the past fiscal year.  
* **Insurance Certificate** – a copy of the council’s public liability policy confirming coverage for the group’s activities.  
* **Auspice Agreement** – as noted above, the formal arrangement with the council.  
* **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, community organisations, or residents that attest to the project’s community value.  
* **Site Plans / Drawings** – schematic layouts showing where the tanks and shade structure will be positioned, together with any site‑specific constraints.
