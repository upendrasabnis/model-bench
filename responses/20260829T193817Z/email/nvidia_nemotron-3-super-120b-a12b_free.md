Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you and your volunteers are doing with the community garden. I appreciate your diligence in confirming eligibility before preparing a full application, and I am happy to address each of the points you raised.

**1. Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The Community Resilience Grant expressly allows applications from organisations that are not incorporated or registered as charities, provided they are auspiced by an eligible legal entity—in this case, your local council. The council must act as the grant recipient and will be responsible for the financial administration, reporting, and compliance with any audit requirements. To satisfy this requirement, you will need to submit a formal letter of support from the council that confirms:  
- Their agreement to auspice the project,  
- That they hold the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance covering the garden’s activities, and  
- Their willingness to accept the grant funds on behalf of the garden and to acquit the expenditure in accordance with the grant conditions.

**2. Grant amount range**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this band will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated project cost for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, you should aim to develop a budget that falls within this range; if the total exceeds $50,000, you may consider phasing the works or seeking co‑funding from other sources to bring the grant‑requested component into the allowable band.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that enhance the resilience of community facilities—such as the installation of rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures—are explicitly listed as eligible expenditures under the “Infrastructure Adaptation” category of the grant. The funds can cover:  
- Purchase and delivery of tanks, fittings, and associated plumbing,  
- Design and engineering fees (if required),  
- Construction or installation of the shade structure, including materials and labour, and  
- Related site preparation (e.g., levelling, foundation work).  
Operating costs, ongoing maintenance, or staff salaries are not covered, so please ensure your budget separates these items clearly.

**4. Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please assemble the following documents:  
- **Council auspice letter** (as described above).  
- **Proof of insurance** held by the council (certificate of currency).  
- **Financial statements** for the past 12 months (even if informal, such as a simple income‑expenditure summary showing the ≈$40,000 turnover).  
- **Project plan** outlining the objectives, timeline, milestones, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will increase resilience to hot summers.  
- **Detailed budget** with itemised costs, quotes from at least two suppliers for each major component (tanks and shade structure), and any in‑kind contributions (e.g., volunteer labour).  
- **Risk management plan** addressing safety during installation and ongoing use.  
- **Letters of support** from community members, local schools, or other stakeholders that demonstrate broad benefit and need.  
- **Evidence of need** (e.g., recent temperature data, water usage records, or anecdotal reports of heat‑related stress on plants and volunteers).

Once you have these items ready, you can submit your application through the online grants portal before the closing date (please verify the exact deadline on the grant webpage, as late submissions cannot be accepted).
