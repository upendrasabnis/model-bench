Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I appreciate your diligence in confirming eligibility before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide the information you requested.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant expressly permits applications from not‑for‑profit organisations that are either incorporated associations, registered charities, or unincorporated groups operating under an eligible auspicing body. A local government council qualifies as an approved auspice, provided it can furnish a letter of support confirming that it will assume legal and financial responsibility for the grant funds and any associated reporting obligations. Therefore, your garden’s arrangement with the council satisfies the eligibility criterion, and you may proceed with an application on that basis.

**Grant amount range**  
For this funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this band will be deemed ineligible. Given your projected turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be well‑aligned with the program’s intent to support scalable, community‑driven resilience projects.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance a community’s ability to withstand climate‑related stresses are explicitly listed as eligible expenses. This includes the installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related site‑works that improve water security and heat mitigation. Consequently, both the rainwater tanks and the shade structure you propose are permissible cost items, provided they are clearly linked to the stated resilience outcomes and are supported by quotes or cost estimates.

**Suggested supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice agreement** – a signed letter from the local council confirming its role as auspice, outlining insurance coverage, and stating its willingness to manage the grant funds.  
2. **Project plan** – a concise description (max 2 pages) of the garden’s current activities, the specific climate challenges faced, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will mitigate those challenges.  
3. **Budget** – a detailed, itemised budget showing requested grant funds, any co‑contributions (in‑kind or cash), and unit costs for each infrastructure component, accompanied by at least two written quotes for major items.  
4. **Governance documentation** – minutes or a resolution from your volunteer committee endorsing the project and confirming the auspice arrangement.  
5. **Evidence of community support** – letters of endorsement from regular stallholders, members, or neighbouring residents, and any relevant council strategic plans that reference the garden.  
6. **Financial statements** – a simple profit‑and‑loss statement or bank statement for the past 12 months to demonstrate the $40,000 turnover figure you mentioned.
