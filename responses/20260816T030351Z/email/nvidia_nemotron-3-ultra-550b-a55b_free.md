Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry and for the valuable work your community garden does in supporting local food security and social connection in your region. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn to help you determine whether an application is the right next step for your group.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local government authority—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., it is an incorporated not-for-profit or a local council) and is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. The council would be the contracted party and would assume legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a signed letter from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and evidence of their current public liability insurance.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the Community Resilience Grant offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 (GST exclusive). There is no requirement for matched funding, though demonstrated in-kind or cash contributions are viewed favourably during assessment. Given your project scope, your request would likely fall comfortably within this range.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly supports climate adaptation and ongoing community use is an eligible expenditure category. Rainwater harvesting systems (tanks, pumps, plumbing) and permanent shade structures (engineered, compliant with relevant Australian Standards) are both explicitly listed as eligible in the program guidelines. Costs for site preparation, professional installation, and necessary permits are also claimable. However, ongoing operational costs (e.g., water bills, maintenance contracts) and portable or temporary structures are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:** To streamline your application, I recommend gathering the following now:
1.  **Auspicing Agreement:** A draft or signed Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) between your garden group and the council outlining roles, responsibilities, and financial management.
2.  **Project Plan:** A one-page summary including objectives, timeline (Gantt chart preferred), risk management plan, and a clear explanation of how the infrastructure builds resilience against extreme heat and water scarcity.
3.  **Budget:** A detailed, itemised budget (GST exclusive) with at least two quotes for major items (tanks, shade structure supply/install).
4.  **Governance Evidence:** Your group’s constitution or rules of operation, a current list of office bearers, and minutes from a recent meeting endorsing the application.
5.  **Financials:** A simple Income & Expenditure statement for the last financial year and a current bank statement (council’s if funds are held by them).
6.  **Land Tenure:** Evidence of permission to install infrastructure on the site (e.g., lease, licence, or letter from the landowner/council).
7.  **Letters of Support:** At least two letters from community stakeholders (e.g., local school, neighbourhood house, health service) demonstrating broader community benefit.
