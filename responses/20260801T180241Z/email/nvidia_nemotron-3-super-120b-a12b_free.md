Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I appreciate the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant before you invest time in preparing a full application.

**Auspicing Arrangements**  
Unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by a local government entity (such as a council) are eligible to apply, provided that the auspicing organisation signs the grant agreement and assumes legal and financial responsibility for the funds. The council must therefore be listed as the legal applicant on the application form, while your garden can be named as the project‑delivery partner. Please ensure that a signed auspice agreement (or a letter of support from the council outlining their role) is included with your submission.

**Grant Size**  
The Community Resilience Grant programme has a minimum award of $5,000 and a maximum award of $50,000 per project. Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated project scope, a request in the $15,000–$25,000 band would be typical, but you are free to propose any amount within the stated limits that reflects the true cost of the works.

**Eligible Expenditure**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate‑related stresses—such as rainwater harvesting tanks, shade structures, and associated fittings—is expressly listed as an eligible cost under the “Infrastructure Adaptation” category. Both the purchase and installation of the tanks and the shade structure (including any necessary site preparation, engineering advice, and labour) can be included in the budget. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., water bills, routine maintenance) are not eligible, but you may allocate a modest portion of the grant for commissioning and initial testing if justified in the project plan.

**Supporting Documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice Agreement / Council Letter of Support** – confirming the council’s legal responsibility and endorsing the project.  
2. **Project Plan** – a clear description of the objectives, timeline (including milestones for tank procurement, installation, and shade‑structure erection), and how the works will improve resilience during hot summers.  
3. **Detailed Budget** – itemised costs for tanks, pumps, fittings, shade‑structure materials, labour, permits, and any professional services; indicate any in‑kind contributions or volunteer labour.  
4. **Quotations** – at least two written quotes for each major cost item (tanks and shade structure) to demonstrate value for money.  
5. **Evidence of Community Need** – brief data on local temperature trends, water‑use statistics from your produce stall, and letters or signatures from garden members or nearby residents supporting the initiative.  
6. **Organisational Information** – although you are not a registered charity, provide a brief outline of your governance structure, meeting minutes showing decision‑making to apply for the grant, and confirmation of the council’s insurance coverage for the site.
