**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program explicitly accepts applications from unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of a local council, provided the council holds the necessary insurance and governance arrangements on their behalf. The key requirement is that the applicant can demonstrate a clear, written agreement with the council that outlines the group’s purpose, financial oversight, and reporting obligations. If you have a formal memorandum of understanding or a council‑issued letter confirming this arrangement, it will satisfy the eligibility criteria.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The current funding round offers grants ranging from **$5,000 up to $25,000**. Applications that request amounts outside this band will be considered ineligible, although the council may consider co‑funding or in‑kind contributions to support larger projects. We recommend aiming for a project cost that falls within this range to maximise the likelihood of a successful submission.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience of the garden — such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related water‑storage or cooling equipment — are eligible costs. These items must be integral to the project’s ability to maintain operations under extreme weather conditions. Capital purchases that are purely decorative or unrelated to resilience are not eligible.

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please include the following:  

1. A copy of the council’s endorsement letter or formal agreement confirming your auspiced status.  
2. A detailed project budget that breaks down the requested grant amount, any matched funding, and in‑kind contributions.  
3. Quotations or pro‑forma invoices for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, and any associated installation work.  
4. A concise project plan outlining objectives, timelines, and measurable outcomes.  
5. Evidence of your current financial statements and the $40,000 annual turnover, demonstrating fiscal transparency.  
6. Any relevant insurance certificates held by the council that cover the garden’s activities.
