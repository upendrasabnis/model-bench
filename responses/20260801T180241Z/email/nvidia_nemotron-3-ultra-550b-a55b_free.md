Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am pleased to address each of your questions in detail below.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for administering organisations. The council must hold an active ABN, be registered for GST, and maintain appropriate public liability and workers’ compensation insurance covering the project activities. As you noted that the council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf, this arrangement satisfies our governance requirements. You will need to submit a signed Auspicing Agreement or Memorandum of Understanding between your group and the council that clearly outlines the council’s legal and financial responsibility for the grant funds and project delivery.

**Grant Amount Limits**
For this round, the Community Resilience Grant offers funding between **$5,000 and $50,000** (GST exclusive). Your proposed project scope appears to fall comfortably within this range. Please note that we cannot fund 100 per cent of project costs; a minimum co-contribution of 20 per cent (cash or in-kind) is required, which volunteer labour valued at standard industry rates can satisfy.

**Eligible Infrastructure Costs**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate impacts is a priority funding category. Rainwater harvesting systems (tanks, pumps, first-flush diverters, plumbing) and permanent shade structures (engineered shade sails, pergolas, or equivalent) are explicitly listed as eligible costs in the Program Guidelines. Installation labour, site preparation, and necessary permits are also claimable. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water rates, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. **Auspicing Agreement/MOU** signed by both parties.
2. **Council’s Certificate of Currency** for public liability ($20M minimum) and workers’ compensation.
3. **Project Budget** using the template provided on our website, showing GST-exclusive line items, co-contributions, and volunteer hour calculations.
4. **Site Plan & Photos** showing proposed tank and shade structure locations.
5. **Quotes** (minimum two per major expenditure item) from licensed contractors.
6. **Evidence of Land Tenure** (lease, licence, or letter of support from the landowner/council).
7. **Governance Documents** for your group (constitution, rules, or minutes confirming committee endorsement of the application).
8. **Annual Financial Statement** or simple income/expenditure summary verifying the ~$40,000 turnover.

**Additional Guidance**
Given your regional location, you may wish to highlight any community wellbeing, food security, or heat-refuge benefits in the Project Rationale section. The assessment panel places strong weight on demonstrated community need and volunteer capacity to sustain the asset post-completion.

The application portal opens on 1 June and closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I recommend drafting your responses in the offline Application Template first, then transferring them online.
