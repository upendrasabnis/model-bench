## 8. Common Pitfalls & How to Avoid Them  

| Pitfall | Symptom | Prevention |
|---------|---------|------------|
| **Over‑customization** | Complex UI, long training | Keep customizations minimal; prioritize out‑of‑the‑box features. |
| **Data Migration Errors** | Inconsistent records in the new system | Perform data validation, use automated migration tools. |
| **Ignoring Compliance** | Late disbursement, audit findings | Embed regulatory rules into the workflow; schedule regular checks. |
| **Insufficient User Support** | Low adoption rates | Invest in help‑desk, knowledge base, and super‑users. |
| **Lack of Integration** | Manual duplicate entry | Map correctness to existing accounting, HR, and CRM systems. |
