Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the clear description of your project. I’m pleased to provide the following information to help you determine your eligibility and to outline the documentation you may wish to prepare before the upcoming deadline.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
Our grant programme is open to both incorporated not‑for‑profit entities and unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by a public or private institution. In your case, the fact that your garden operates under the auspices of the local council satisfies the “auspice” requirement, provided that the council can confirm it will hold the necessary insurances and be listed as the primary applicant on any grant agreement. If the council is willing to act as the fiscal host, unincorporated groups are eligible to apply.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, we allocate funds based on the scope of the proposed work, the demonstrated community impact, and the overall budget request. Typical awards for infrastructure projects in the $30 k–$80 k range have been successful, but we encourage you to submit a realistic budget that reflects the true cost of the rainwater tanks and shade structure. If you request an amount significantly above the usual range, be prepared to provide additional justification and cost‑benefit analysis.

**Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Both rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible costs under the “Climate‑Adaptation and Infrastructure” category of this round. The key is that the items must be directly related to enhancing the garden’s resilience to extreme weather, must be installed on the council‑owned land (or with the council’s formal permission), and must have a useful life of at least five years. Any costs for design, installation, and ancillary works (e.g., plumbing, foundations, safety fencing) are also permissible, provided they are clearly itemized in your budget.

**Suggested supporting documents**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend compiling the following materials:

1. **Letter of support/auspice confirmation** from the council, confirming that it will act as the fiscal host, provide insurance coverage, and remain responsible for any project‑related liabilities.  
2. **Proof of insurance** (or a copy of the council’s policy) that demonstrates coverage for public liability and equipment.  
3. **Financial statements** for the most recent two years (or a simple cash‑flow summary if you lack formal audited reports). This should include revenue sources (produce stall, membership fees) and any existing assets.  
4. **Project plan** – a clear description of the rainwater tank capacity, placement, and integration with existing irrigation, as well as the design concept, dimensions, and materials for the shade structure.  
5. **Budget breakdown** – itemized costs for equipment, installation, labour (if applicable), and any contingency.  
6. **Community impact statement** – explaining how the project will benefit local residents, especially during hot summer periods, and how you will measure success (e.g., water savings, increased usage hours).  
7. **Photos or site plans** of the garden showing the proposed locations for the tanks and shade structure.

If you are able to provide these documents, you will be well positioned to submit a competitive application. Should you have any further questions about the grant guidelines or need assistance with formatting your proposal, please do not hesitate to contact me directly.
