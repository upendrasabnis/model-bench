**Auspiced groups**  
The grant program expressly allows unincorporated organisations to apply when they are operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—in this case, your local council. As long as the council provides a signed auspice agreement that confirms it will assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds, your group meets the eligibility criterion. Please ensure the auspice document outlines the council’s role in managing the grant, acquitting expenses, and maintaining any required insurances.

**Grant size**  
For this funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Projects that clearly demonstrate how the requested amount will directly contribute to resilience outcomes are favoured, so we recommend aligning your budget request with the specific costs of the rainwater tanks and shade structure you described.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that enhances community resilience to climate‑related stresses—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade installations—is considered an eligible expense, provided the items are new, essential to the project, and not already funded through other sources. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., stall utilities, membership administration) are not eligible, but you may include a modest contingency (up to 10 % of the total infrastructure cost) for unexpected expenses related to installation.
