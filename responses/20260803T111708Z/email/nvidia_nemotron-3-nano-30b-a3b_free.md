**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines allow applications from groups that are *not* formally incorporated, provided they are operating under the auspices of a local government authority that holds the requisite insurance and has a formal agreement (e.g., a Service Level Agreement or Memorandum of Understanding) with the group. Because your garden is administered by the regional council and the council holds the necessary public liability coverage on your behalf, you meet this condition. However, we will need a copy of the council’s written endorsement confirming that it has accepted responsibility for insurance and compliance on your behalf.  

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The CRG offers funding between **$5,000 and $25,000**. Projects that demonstrate a clear resilience benefit and a realistic budget are prioritized. Your proposed allocation for rain‑water tanks and a shade structure comfortably fits within this range, and we anticipate that a request for the full amount of your anticipated project cost would be well within the permissible ceiling.  

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances the capacity of the organisation to continue delivering community services—such as rain‑water harvesting systems, shade structures, or other built assets—is considered an eligible expense, provided it is:  

* Essential to the project’s resilience objectives;  
* Cost‑effective and proportionate to the overall budget; and  
* Incorporated into a detailed implementation plan with clear timelines and maintenance responsibilities.  

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To move forward, please prepare the following items:  

* A signed letter of endorsement from the council confirming insurance coverage and auspicing status;  
* A concise project proposal (max 2 pages) outlining objectives, expected outcomes, timeline, and budget breakdown;  
* Quotes or tenders for the rain‑water tanks and shade structure;  
* Your most recent financial statements or a budget summary showing the $40,000 annual turnover; and  
* Evidence of community support, such as letters of endorsement from at least two local stakeholders or beneficiaries.
