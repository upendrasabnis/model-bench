Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarification on each of your points.

First, regarding organisational structure: **unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are eligible to apply**, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that it is an Australian local government entity with an ABN and appropriate public liability insurance. Since you have confirmed the council holds the relevant insurances on your behalf, this arrangement satisfies our governance and risk-management requirements. You will need to submit a formal letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their acceptance of legal and financial responsibility for the grant, and evidence of their current public liability insurance certificate.

**Grant amounts:** For this round, the minimum request is $10,000 and the maximum is $150,000. Your proposed project scope appears to fall comfortably within this range.

**Eligible costs:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate impacts—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible expenditure category in the program guidelines. Installation costs, materials, and necessary site preparation are all allowable. Please note, however, that ongoing operational costs (e.g., water rates, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting documents to prepare:**
1. Council auspicing letter (as described above).
2. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 annual turnover.
3. Quotes from at least two suppliers for the tanks and shade structure.
4. A simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and evidence of community consultation (e.g., meeting minutes or survey results).
5. Proof of land tenure or council permission for the infrastructure installation.

I encourage you to review the full Program Guidelines and FAQ on our website, which contain the assessment criteria and a sample budget template.
