**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Many of our grant programmes accept unincorporated groups provided they operate under the fiscal and legal umbrella of a recognised entity—such as a local council—that can assume responsibility for the grant funds, insurance, and reporting. In practice, the council’s involvement often satisfies the “legal entity” requirement, so your group should be eligible. However, we do require a formal letter of support (or auspice agreement) from the council confirming that they will act as the primary point of contact for the grant and will retain the necessary insurances on your behalf.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
Our Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a strict floor or ceiling; awards are typically sized to reflect the scale and impact of the proposed project. Based on recent funding rounds, grants have ranged from roughly $5,000 to $75,000, with many awards clustered around $20,000–$40,000 for projects of the type you describe. The final amount will be determined by the overall score of your application, the competitiveness of the round, and the availability of funds.  

**Eligibility of rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and climate‑responsive shade structures is expressly permitted under the “Climate‑Adaptation and Resilience” component of this grant. These capital assets directly support the continuity of food‑production activities during extreme heat events, aligning perfectly with the programme’s objectives. We do expect a clear cost‑benefit analysis or justification demonstrating how each item will reduce water consumption or heat stress for your garden.  

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To assemble a strong application, we recommend the following documents:  

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – itemised costs for equipment, installation, and any contingency funds, referencing quotes where possible.  
3. **Letter of Support/Auspice Agreement** – from your local council confirming their role as fiscal sponsor and confirming that insurance coverage is in place.  
4. ** organisational Profile** – a one‑page overview of your volunteer team, governance structure, and any recent activities or achievements.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or a set of financial highlights if you are not required to have an audit) to demonstrate your $40,000 turnover and responsible stewardship of funds.  
6. **Insurance Certificate** – if the council holds the policy, a copy of the certificate confirming that your garden is named as an additional insured party.  
7. **Letters of Support** – from local businesses, community groups, or other stakeholders who can attest to the community benefit of your project.
