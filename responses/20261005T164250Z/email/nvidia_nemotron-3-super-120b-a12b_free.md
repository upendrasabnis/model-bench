I have addressed each of your questions below, drawing directly from the current grant guidelines and the assessment criteria used by our review panel.

**1. Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The grant is open to organisations that are either incorporated associations, registered charities, or unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity. A local council qualifies as an eligible auspicing body, provided it can demonstrate that it holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance, and that it will assume financial and administrative responsibility for the grant funds. Therefore, your garden group, operating under the auspices of your regional council, is eligible to apply, provided the council supplies a formal letter of auspice confirming its support and acceptance of liability.

**2. Grant amount range**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding from a minimum of **AU $5,000** to a maximum of **AU $50,000** per successful applicant. The amount awarded is proportionate to the scope, expected impact, and demonstrated need of the project. Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance community resilience—such as the installation of rainwater harvesting tanks and shade structures—are expressly listed as eligible expenses under the “Infrastructure Adaptation” category. Costs may include the purchase of equipment, installation labour, and associated engineering or design fees, provided they are itemised and supported by quotes or invoices. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., stall utilities, membership administration) are not covered, but they may be referenced in the project budget to show overall sustainability.

**4. Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please gather the following items:  

- **Auspice letter** from the council on official letterhead, stating that the council will act as the legal and financial sponsor, confirming insurance coverage, and endorsing the project.  
- **Proof of council endorsement** (e.g., a resolution or email thread) showing that the council has approved the garden’s use of its auspice status.  
- **Financial statements** for the past 12 months (even if informal) that demonstrate the $40,000 annual turnover, including a breakdown of stall revenue and membership fees.  
- **Project plan** outlining objectives, timeline, milestones, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will mitigate heat‑related risks and extend the garden’s operating season.  
- **Itemised quotes** from at least two suppliers for the tanks and shade structure, detailing unit costs, delivery, and installation.  
- **Risk management plan** addressing volunteer safety, insurance coverage, and maintenance responsibilities post‑installation.  
- **Letters of support** (optional but beneficial) from community members, local schools, or other stakeholders who will benefit from the enhanced resilience of the garden.

Once these documents are assembled, you can complete the online application form, ensuring that each section references the corresponding evidence (e.g., upload the auspice letter under “Legal Status” and the quotes under “Budget”).
