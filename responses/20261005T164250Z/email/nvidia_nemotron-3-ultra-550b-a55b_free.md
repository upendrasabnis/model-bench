Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for administering organisations. The council must hold an ABN, be registered for GST, and hold the necessary public liability and workers’ compensation insurances covering the project activities. You will need to provide a signed Auspicing Agreement or a letter from the council confirming their willingness to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant, including acquittal obligations.

Second, the Community Resilience Grant offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 per project. There is no minimum turnover threshold for applicants, so your annual turnover of approximately $40,000 does not preclude you. However, you must demonstrate the capacity to manage the funds and deliver the project within the funding agreement period, typically 12 to 18 months.

Third, the infrastructure you describe—rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure—falls squarely within eligible capital expenditure. The program guidelines explicitly list “water security infrastructure” and “climate adaptation assets” as priority investment areas. Costs for materials, contractor labour, permits, and reasonable project management fees are all claimable. Please note that ongoing operational costs (e.g., water rates, maintenance) are not eligible.

Finally, to streamline your application, I recommend preparing the following supporting documents now:
1. The council’s Auspicing Agreement or letter of support on official letterhead.
2. Your group’s current constitution or rules of operation.
3. A simple project plan with timeline, risk register, and itemised budget (quotes for tanks and shade structure are preferred).
4. Evidence of land tenure or a licence to occupy the garden site for at least the grant period plus five years.
5. Your most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover.
6. Letters of community support or partnership (e.g., from local schools, Landcare groups, or the council’s sustainability officer).

The application portal opens on 1 May and closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I strongly encourage you to draft your responses in the offline Application Template (available on our website) before entering them online.
