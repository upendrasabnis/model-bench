Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am pleased to provide clarification on each of your questions.

**Auspicing Arrangements & Eligibility:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are absolutely eligible to apply. The council must be the legal applicant (the "Administering Organisation") and will hold the funding agreement, insurance, and financial accountability. Your application should clearly outline the governance structure, including a signed Memorandum of Understanding or Auspicing Agreement between the garden group and the council that specifies roles, responsibilities, and financial management processes.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your project scope appears to sit comfortably within this range. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some level of co-contribution (cash or in-kind), though this is not a strict eligibility requirement for groups with an annual turnover under $50,000.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience is a priority for this program. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts and ensure ongoing operation during extreme weather events are considered highly eligible costs. Eligible expenses include purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation (e.g., plumbing, footings). Ongoing maintenance costs are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**
1.  **Auspicing Agreement** (signed by both parties).
2.  **Council’s Certificate of Currency** for Public Liability and Professional Indemnity insurance.
3.  **Project Budget** (itemised, with at least two quotes for major items over $5,000).
4.  **Letters of Support** (from council, community members, or partner organisations).
5.  **Evidence of Land Tenure** (lease, licence, or letter of consent from the landowner).
6.  **Simple Project Plan** with timeline and risk management strategy.
7.  **Most recent financial statement** for the garden group (income/expenditure summary).

Applications close at 5:00 PM AEST on the advertised date. I strongly recommend starting the draft in our online portal early, as the auspicing section requires council authorisation. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require further guidance as you develop your application.
