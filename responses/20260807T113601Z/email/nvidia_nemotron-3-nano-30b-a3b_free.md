Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful description of your community garden project. I’m pleased to provide clarification on the points you raised regarding the Community Resilience Grant.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our funding guidelines expressly allow organisations that operate under the auspices of a local council (or another approved governmental entity) to apply, provided the council holds the necessary insurance coverage on your behalf and can verify your legal standing. While the group itself need not be formally incorporated, you must submit a copy of the council’s letter of support confirming its role as your supervising authority, together with the council’s insurance documentation.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The current round offers grants ranging from a minimum of **$5,000** to a maximum of **$25,000**. Projects that demonstrate a clear link to climate‑resilience outcomes — such as water security or heat‑mitigation infrastructure — are prioritised for funding at the higher end of the scale. Your projected use of rain‑water tanks and a shade structure aligns well with these priorities, and we anticipate that a grant in the **$12,000–$20,000** range would be competitive, assuming your budget justification reflects realistic costings.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the long‑term sustainability of community facilities is eligible, provided it meets the following criteria: (1) it is a capital‑cost item, (2) it directly supports resilience to environmental stressors, and (3) it is not a routine maintenance expense. Rain‑water harvesting systems and shade structures qualify as eligible costs, as they contribute to water security and heat mitigation. You will need to supply detailed quotes, a project timeline, and a maintenance plan to demonstrate that the infrastructure will be properly installed and sustained.

**Supporting documentation**  
To accompany your application, please include:  

1. A signed letter from the council confirming its auspices and insurance coverage.  
2. The council’s insurance policy excerpt or certificate of coverage.  
3. Itemised quotations for the rain‑water tanks, shade structure, and any associated works.  
4. A project budget that outlines total costs, the requested grant amount, and any co‑funding sources.  
5. A risk‑mitigation and maintenance plan that describes how the infrastructure will be monitored and serviced after installation.  
6. Evidence of your annual turnover (e.g., audited financial statements or bank statements) to demonstrate that the grant will not exceed 50 % of your operating budget.

We hope this information assists you in preparing a strong and compliant application. Should you need further clarification or wish to discuss your proposal in more detail, please feel free to contact me directly.
