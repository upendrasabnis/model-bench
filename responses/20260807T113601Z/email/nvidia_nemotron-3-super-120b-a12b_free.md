Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with the community garden. I appreciate the opportunity to clarify the eligibility requirements for the Community Resilience Grant and to help you prepare a strong application.

**Auspicing arrangements**  
The grant program expressly permits unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. As long as the council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds, your garden can apply under this arrangement. Please ensure the auspice letter includes the council’s ABN, contact details, and a statement that they will manage the grant acquittal on your behalf.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per project. Applications are assessed on a competitive basis, and the amount awarded will reflect the scope, budget, and demonstrated need of the proposed activities.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related site works—is an eligible cost category. The grant can cover the purchase and installation of the tanks and shade structure, as well as any necessary engineering or design fees. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., stall fees, membership administration) are not eligible unless they are integral to the delivery of the funded infrastructure project.

**Supporting documentation**  
To substantiate your application, please prepare the following:

1. **Auspice agreement** – a signed letter from the local council confirming auspice arrangements and willingness to administer the grant.  
2. **Project plan** – a concise description of the rainwater tank and shade structure works, including timelines, milestones, and how the project will improve resilience to extreme heat.  
3. **Detailed budget** – itemised costs for tanks, foundations, plumbing, shade structure materials, labour, permits, and any professional services; clearly indicate which items are grant‑funded and which (if any) are contributed in‑kind or from other sources.  
4. **Quotations** – at least two written quotes for each major component (tanks and shade structure) to demonstrate cost‑effectiveness.  
5. **Evidence of need** – brief data or anecdotal information showing recent heat impacts on garden operations and community usage (e.g., temperature logs, volunteer feedback, stall attendance trends).  
6. **Organisational details** – a short profile of the garden, including its purpose, volunteer structure, and how it is linked to the council’s community services.  
7. **Insurance confirmation** – a copy of the council’s public liability insurance certificate that covers the garden’s activities.
