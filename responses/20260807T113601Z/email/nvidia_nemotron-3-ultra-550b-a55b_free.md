First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf, assume full financial and legal responsibility for the grant, and hold the required insurances. Your council’s existing insurance coverage for your operations satisfies a key requirement, though we will require a signed letter from the council’s Chief Executive Officer or authorised delegate confirming the auspicing arrangement and their acceptance of these obligations.

Second, your annual turnover of approximately $40,000 falls well within the program’s eligibility thresholds. There is no minimum grant amount for this round; however, the maximum request is capped at $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure to mitigate heat impacts—aligns directly with the program’s objective of building community capacity to withstand climate-related stressors. Capital infrastructure of this nature is considered an eligible cost, provided it is permanently affixed, remains the property of the auspicing body or the community group, and is supported by at least two written quotes from licensed contractors.

In terms of supporting documentation, you should prepare: (1) the aforementioned council auspicing letter; (2) your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report; (3) a simple project plan outlining timelines, risk management, and volunteer involvement; (4) the two independent quotes for each infrastructure component; (5) evidence of land tenure or council permission for the installation; and (6) letters of support from community stakeholders demonstrating the garden’s broader social and environmental value.
