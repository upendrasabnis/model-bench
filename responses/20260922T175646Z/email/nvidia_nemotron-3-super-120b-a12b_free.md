Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I am happy to provide clarification on the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant and to outline the documentation you will need to prepare should you decide to submit an application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant programme expressly permits applications from not‑for‑profit organisations, incorporated associations, and unincorporated groups that are operating under a formal auspice arrangement with a local government entity. Because your garden is supported by the regional council—which provides the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance—you meet the auspice requirement. You will need to submit a signed letter of auspice from the council confirming that they are acting as your legal and financial sponsor for the purposes of this grant.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant has a minimum award of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Funding is allocated on a competitive basis, with preference given to proposals that demonstrate clear community benefit, sustainability, and alignment with the programme’s resilience objectives.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience of community‑facilitated spaces is an eligible expense. Rainwater harvesting tanks and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat events fall within the scope of allowable costs. Please ensure that the budget items are itemised, that quotes are obtained from reputable suppliers, and that any ongoing maintenance or operational expenses are clearly distinguished from the capital expenditure requested.

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following:

1. **Auspice letter** – signed by an authorised officer of the local council, detailing the council’s role, insurance coverage, and commitment to overseeing the grant funds.  
2. **Proof of governance** – minutes or a resolution from your volunteer committee showing endorsement of the project and the auspice arrangement.  
3. **Financial statements** – a simple profit‑and‑loss statement or cash‑flow summary for the most recent fiscal year (showing the ~$40,000 turnover) and a projected budget for the grant period.  
4. **Supplier quotes** – at least two written quotes for the rainwater tanks and shade structure, including installation costs.  
5. **Project plan** – a brief (one‑ to two‑page) description outlining the need, objectives, implementation timeline, expected community impact, and how the project contributes to local climate resilience.  
6. **Letters of support** (optional but recommended) – from nearby residents, schools, or other community groups that will benefit from the garden’s continued operation.
