Thank you for reaching out and for the clear description of your project. I’m pleased to provide the following information to help you determine your eligibility and prepare a strong application for the upcoming Community Resilience Grant round.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Many grant programs, including ours, do accept unincorporated community groups provided they are formally auspiced by a legal entity that can assume fiscal and legal responsibility. In your case, the fact that your garden operates under the auspices of the local council—and that the council holds the required insurances—generally satisfies the sponsorship requirement. We typically ask for a written auspice agreement (or a letter of support) from the council confirming that it will act as the fiscal sponsor, that it will maintain liability and public liability insurance for the activities you propose, and that it consents to the use of its name on project materials. Please ensure this document is dated and signed by an authorised council officer.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
Our Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, we allocate funding based on the overall merit of the proposal, the scope of the work, and the available budget for the round. Past successful projects have ranged from roughly $5,000 to $50,000, but we welcome applications of any scale as long as they are proportionate to the demonstrated community need and the budget you propose.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Infrastructure that directly supports the long‑term sustainability and resilience of community initiatives is generally eligible. Rainwater tanks and shade structures clearly align with the grant’s objective of enhancing capacity to operate through extreme weather events. We consider these items eligible provided they are:  
1. Directly required for the project’s core activities (e.g., water for irrigation, shelter for users and produce);  
2. Accompanied by a detailed procurement plan, including quotes or estimates; and  
3. Demonstrated to deliver measurable benefits (e.g., water‑usage reduction, increased production during heat events).

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following:  

1. **Auspice agreement / council letter of support** (as noted above).  
2. **Proof of insurance** held by the council (certificate of liability and any property coverage).  
3. **Financial statements** for the most recent fiscal year (or a simple income‑and‑expense summary) to illustrate your $40,000 turnover and the sources of funding.  
4. **Project plan** – a clear timeline, deliverables, and milestones for the rainwater tanks and shade structure.  
5. **Budget breakdown** – itemized costs for equipment, installation, labour, and any contingency funds, with quotations where possible.  
6. **Community impact statement** – evidence of need (e.g., local climate data, heat‑wave frequency) and how the infrastructure will benefit garden users, nearby residents, and the wider community.  
7. **Letters of support** from local businesses, partner organisations, or community members.  

If you can provide these documents in a single PDF (or a shared folder), it will make the review process much smoother. Should you have any specific questions about formatting or additional requirements, please do not hesitate to contact me directly.
