Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in an application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants. The council must be willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf, assume full financial and legal accountability for the grant, and provide evidence of appropriate insurance coverage—which you have indicated they already hold. You will need to submit a signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming these arrangements as part of your application.

Second, the Community Resilience Grant offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 per project. There is no requirement to request the maximum amount; applicants should apply for the realistic cost of the proposed activities. Your project budget of rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears likely to fall within this range.

Third, infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate impacts—such as water security installations and heat-mitigation structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible capital cost in the program guidelines. Both rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure would qualify, provided they are fixed assets that remain with the garden site. Please note that ongoing maintenance costs, consumables, and volunteer labor (while valuable) are not eligible for grant funding, though volunteer hours can be documented as in-kind contributions to strengthen your application.

Finally, key supporting documents to prepare include: (1) the council auspicing agreement; (2) a current certificate of currency for public liability insurance held by the council; (3) a detailed project budget with at least two quotes for each major infrastructure item; (4) a simple project plan with timelines and risk management; (5) evidence of community need and benefit, such as letters of support from garden members or local stakeholders; and (6) your most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 annual turnover.

I encourage you to review the full program guidelines and application form on our website, which contain the assessment criteria and detailed instructions.
