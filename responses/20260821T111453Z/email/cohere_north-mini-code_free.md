**Eligibility of unincorporated groups with council auspice**  
Our grant program is designed to support a broad spectrum of community‑based initiatives, and unincorporated groups that operate under the formal auspice of a local council are indeed eligible to apply. The council’s involvement is viewed favourably because it demonstrates that the organization benefits from established governance structures, liability coverage, and access to public resources. The only prerequisite is that the council formally endorses the application – typically via a letter of support or a resolution confirming that it will act as the fiscal host for any awarded funds.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
We do not set a rigid floor or ceiling on individual awards. Instead, the grant size is determined by the scope, cost‑effectiveness, and strategic alignment of the proposed project with our resilience objectives. Past awards have ranged from roughly $5,000 to $50,000, but applications that present compelling community impact and clear budgeting have received substantially larger amounts. The key is to demonstrate that the requested funds are both necessary and proportionate to the outcomes you intend to achieve.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Infrastructure that directly contributes to climate‑adaptation and operational continuity is squarely within the scope of this grant. Rainwater harvesting systems and durable shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible capital costs because they reduce water consumption, mitigate heat‑related risks, and enable the garden to remain productive during extreme summer conditions. We recommend that you itemise each component (e.g., tank capacity, construction materials, installation labour) and attach unit costs or quotations, as this will strengthen the credibility of your budget.  

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To build a competitive application, we suggest assembling the following documents (all of which can be submitted electronically):  

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – line‑item breakdown of all costs, including contingency, with supporting quotations for major items such as tanks and structures.  
3. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your garden’s mission, volunteer base, and the council’s role as fiscal host (including a letter of support).  
4. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss summary and cash‑flow statement) demonstrating your $40,000 turnover.  
5. **Insurance and Risk Management Documentation** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming that the garden is covered under its policy.  
6. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, schools, or community groups that illustrate broader community backing.  
7. **Implementation Plan** – a Gantt‑style timeline or work‑plan outlining milestones, key deliverables, and responsible parties.  

Because the application window closes next month, I recommend beginning to collate these materials as soon as possible. Should you need a formal confirmation of eligibility prior to submission, please let us know, and we can provide a brief pre‑assessment of your proposal against the program guidelines.
