## 8. Implementation Roadmap: Deploying a GMS

### 8.1 Phase 1 – Discovery & Planning  
- **Stakeholder Interviews**: Understand pain points.  
- **Requirements Matrix**: List must‑have vs nice‑to‑have features.  
- **Vendor Shortlist**: Based on feature comparison.  

### 8.2 Phase 2 – Pilot & Customization  
- **Select a Pilot Grant Cycle**: Test the platform with a small subset of awards.  
- **Configure Workflows**: Map intake, assessment, disbursement, reporting, and impact modules.  
- **Data Migration**: Import existing applicant data, budgets, and previous reports.  

### 8.3 Phase 3 – Training & Change Management  
- 

- **User Guides**: Create role‑specific documentation.  
- **Webinars & Workshops**: Hands‑on sessions for funders, reviewers, and grantees.  
- **Change Champions**: Identify internal advocates to drive adoption.  

### 8.4 Phase 4 – Go‑Live & Continuous Improvement  
- **Soft Launch**: Run parallel with legacy processes for a short period.  
- **Collect Feedback**: Use surveys, usage analytics, and focus groups.  
- **Iterate**: Refine workflows, add dashboards, or adjust scoring weights.
