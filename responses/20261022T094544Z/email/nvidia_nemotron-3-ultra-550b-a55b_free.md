Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of auspiced groups**  
Unincorporated, volunteer‑run groups that operate under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in your case, the council) holds current public liability insurance and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to supply a signed auspicing agreement or letter from the council confirming this arrangement, along with a copy of the council’s certificate of currency for public liability insurance.

**Grant amount parameters**  
For this round, the minimum request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your projected project budget should fall within this range. If the total cost of the rainwater tanks and shade structure exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum amount and indicate co‑contributions (cash or in‑kind) to cover the balance.

**Eligible infrastructure costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat—is considered an eligible expense. Eligible costs include purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation. Ongoing maintenance, operational salaries, and recurrent utility costs are not fundable under this grant.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
In addition to the auspicing agreement and insurance certificate mentioned above, please have ready:  
1. A concise project plan (maximum two pages) outlining objectives, activities, timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. A detailed budget using the template provided on our website, showing all income (including the weekly stall and membership revenue) and expenditure.  
3. Evidence of community support (e.g., letters from local stakeholders, membership list, or minutes from a recent meeting endorsing the project).  
4. Your group’s most recent financial statement or a simple income‑and‑expenditure summary for the last financial year.  
5. Quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure item.

The application portal opens on 1 May and closes at 5 pm AEST on 30 June. I recommend drafting your responses in a Word document first, then copying them into the online form. If you would like feedback on a draft budget or project plan before submission, please feel free to send them to me at least two weeks prior to the deadline.
