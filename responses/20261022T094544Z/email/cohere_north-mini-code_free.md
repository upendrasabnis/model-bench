**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
The grant programme is intentionally inclusive and does accept applications from unincorporated community groups, provided they operate under the formal auspice of a recognised local government body. In your case, being administered by your council – which holds the necessary insurance, holds any required public liability coverage, and can act as the signatory for any funding agreement – satisfies the eligibility requirement for group structure. The council’s involvement also serves as a useful safeguard for compliance with any statutory obligations that may apply to public works or infrastructure installations.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
While the programme does not prescribe a strict minimum award, most successful applicants request between $10,000 and $50,000 for infrastructure‑focused projects. The maximum available for a single application is $75,000, and the programme typically funds up to 50 % of the total project cost, with the expectation that the remainder will be covered through in‑kind contributions, volunteer labour, or other funding sources. Your projected cost for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to fall comfortably within this range.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes – capital assets such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible expenditures under the “Community Infrastructure” category of the grant. These items must directly support the resilience or sustainability of the community activity (in your case, the garden’s ability to operate during extreme heat). It is important to note that the items must be new, purpose‑built for the project, and have a useful life of at least five years to qualify.

**Suggested supporting documentation**  
When you prepare the full application, we recommend assembling the following documents:

1. **Project Proposal** – a concise narrative (max 2 pages) outlining the problem you are addressing, the proposed solution, timelines, and anticipated outcomes.  
2. **Budget Table** – a detailed breakdown of all costs, including unit prices, quantities, and any in‑kind contributions (e.g., volunteer labour, council‑provided insurance).  
3. **Letters of Support** – brief endorsements from the council (confirming the auspice relationship and insurance coverage) and, if applicable, local businesses or community members who will benefit from the project.  
4. **Insurance Certificate** – the council’s public liability policy confirming coverage for the project period.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent two years of income and expenditure statements, along with a cash‑flow summary, to demonstrate financial viability.  
6. **Site Plans or Sketches** – a simple diagram showing the location of the rainwater tanks and shade structure within the garden.  
7. **Risk Assessment** – a basic assessment of health, safety, and environmental risks associated with the installation and use of the new infrastructure.  

We also advise that you review the full grant guidelines (available on our website) for any additional requirements, such as environmental impact assessments or compliance with local planning regulations.
