First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local council are indeed eligible to apply, provided the council is willing to act as the legal entity and sign the funding agreement on your behalf. The council must hold current public liability insurance and workers’ compensation coverage (where applicable), and you will need to supply a signed letter from an authorised council officer confirming the auspicing arrangement, the council’s ABN, and its agreement to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant.

Second, the current round offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000 (GST exclusive). There is no minimum project size, but the requested amount must fall within this band. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to sit comfortably within the range, though you should ensure the total project cost—including any in-kind contributions—is clearly itemised.

Third, infrastructure that directly supports climate adaptation and ongoing community use is an eligible cost category. Rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed in the program guidelines as eligible capital expenditure, provided they are fixed assets that remain with the garden site. Portable or temporary items (e.g., pop-up gazebos) would not qualify.

Finally, the key supporting documents you should prepare include: the council auspicing letter mentioned above; your group’s most recent financial statement or a simple income-and-expenditure summary showing the $40,000 turnover; quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure item; a site plan showing proposed tank and shade-structure locations; evidence of land tenure or a licence to occupy the garden site; and a brief project plan outlining timelines, risk management, and how the infrastructure will extend operating hours during extreme heat. If your group has a constitution or terms of reference, please include that as well.
