Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in detail.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that they are a legally constituted entity with an ABN, hold appropriate public liability insurance, and are willing to enter into a funding agreement on your group’s behalf. You will need to provide a signed letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their acceptance of financial and legal responsibility for the grant, and evidence of their current insurance certificates.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Given your project scope and annual turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within the $15,000–$30,000 range would be well aligned with the program’s objectives and the assessment criterion regarding “scale relative to organisational capacity.”

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate impacts is an eligible cost category. Rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible in the program guidelines (Section 4.2), provided they are fixed assets, comply with relevant Australian Standards and local planning requirements, and are located on land where the applicant (or auspice body) has secure tenure for at least five years. Quotes from licensed contractors must accompany the application.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
In addition to the council auspicing letter and insurance certificates, please prepare:
- A project plan with timeline, risk register, and maintenance schedule
- Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item
- Evidence of land tenure or a lease/licence agreement for the garden site
- Your group’s most recent financial statement (income/expenditure) and a simple budget for the project
- Letters of support from at least two community stakeholders (e.g., local school, neighbourhood house, or CFA brigade)
- A brief governance statement outlining how decisions are made within your volunteer group

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 28 June. The SmartyGrants portal will open for submissions on 1 May, and I strongly recommend starting a draft at least three weeks prior to the deadline to allow time for council sign-off. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require clarification on any of the above, or if you would like a brief pre-submission review of your draft budget.
