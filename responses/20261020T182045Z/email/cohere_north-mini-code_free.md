**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines expressly state that unincorporated community groups are eligible applicants, provided they operate under the formal auspices of a local government entity that can demonstrate responsibility for liability, insurance, and fiscal oversight. In your case, the fact that your council holds the relevant insurances on your behalf satisfies this requirement. You will, however, need to include a formal letter of support (or auspice agreement) from the council confirming that they will act as the fiscal sponsor for any funded activities. This letter should reference the specific project components you intend to implement (rainwater tanks and shade structures) and outline the council’s responsibilities regarding procurement, installation, and ongoing maintenance.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum award. Instead, funding levels are determined on a case‑by‑case basis, reflecting the scope, impact, and cost of the proposed activities. Historically, grants in this round have ranged from approximately $5,000 to $30,000, but we have approved projects outside this band when justified by exceptional need or scale. For your project, a realistic request would be in the $12,000‑$18,000 range, assuming you are seeking coverage for the full cost of two rainwater tanks (including delivery, installation, and water‑proofing) and a modular shade structure. Please note that the grant typically covers up to 75 % of eligible project costs; the remaining 25 % must be secured through other sources (e.g., council allocations, in‑kind contributions, or volunteer labor).

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Both rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are considered eligible expenditures under the “Climate‑Resilient Infrastructure” category of our guidelines. Eligible costs include:

1. **Purchase and delivery** of tanks that meet Australian Standards for potable water storage.  
2. **Installation services**, including foundation work, plumbing connections, and any required permits.  
3. **Materials and labor** for a durable, weather‑resistant shade structure (e.g., steel frame with UV‑protected fabric).  
4. **Project‑specific engineering or design fees** if you engage a qualified professional to ensure compliance with local building codes.

Please be aware that routine operational expenses (such as ongoing water‑usage fees or maintenance) are not covered by the grant.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following materials:

- **Project Narrative** – a clear description of the problem (heat stress on garden operations), objectives, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
- **Budget Table** – itemised breakdown of all costs, indicating which items are grant‑eligible and which will be funded through other sources.  
- **Letter of Support/Auspice Agreement** from the council confirming fiscal sponsorship and insurance coverage.  
- **Proof of Insurance** (if your group holds any additional policies beyond the council’s).  
- **Financial Statements** for the most recent two years (or a simplified income‑and‑expenditure statement, given your modest turnover).  
- **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your volunteer team, governance structure, and any past community projects.  
- **Site Plans or Sketches** illustrating where the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be located.  
- **Quotes or tenders** for the equipment and installation services, clearly labelled with vendor details and cost breakdowns.  
- **Community Engagement Evidence** – minutes or summaries from local stakeholder meetings demonstrating support for the initiative.
