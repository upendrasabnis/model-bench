**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to incorporated and unincorporated organisations that can demonstrate a clear governance structure and the capacity to manage grant funds. An unincorporated group that operates under the auspices of a local council is eligible, provided the council signs a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will act as the legal and financial accountable body for the grant. The letter should outline the council’s responsibility for receiving the funds, administering any reporting requirements, and ensuring compliance with the grant’s terms and conditions.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your project’s scope, a request in the $15,000‑$30,000 band would be typical, but you are free to propose any amount within the limits that reflects the true cost of the works.

**Eligible costs – infrastructure**  
Capital works such as the installation of rainwater tanks and shade structures are expressly listed as eligible expenses under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” category. This includes the purchase of materials, labour costs incurred by qualified contractors, and any necessary site preparation (e.g., foundations, plumbing connections). Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water usage fees, routine maintenance) are not covered, but they may be referenced in your project sustainability plan.

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice letter** from the local council on official letterhead, signed by an authorised officer, confirming the council’s role as the legal and financial accountable body.  
2. **Project plan** (maximum two pages) describing the current garden activities, the specific need for rainwater harvesting and shade, a timeline for implementation, and how the project will enhance resilience to hotter summers.  
3. **Detailed budget** broken down by line item (tanks, pumps, fittings, shade structure materials, labour, permits, contingency). Include quotes or indicative prices from at least two suppliers where possible.  
4. **Evidence of community support** – e.g., letters of endorsement from regular stall volunteers, members, or nearby residents, and a brief summary of membership fees or stall revenue demonstrating the $40,000 annual turnover.  
5. **Governance documentation** – although you are not incorporated, provide any existing minutes, volunteer agreements, or policies that show how decisions are made and how funds will be overseen.  
6. **Insurance confirmation** – a copy of the council’s public liability and volunteer insurance policy that covers the garden’s activities.
