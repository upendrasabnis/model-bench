Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I am happy to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant and to outline the information you will need to prepare a strong application.

**Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The grant programme expressly allows applications from groups that are not incorporated or registered as charities, provided they operate under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible sponsor—such as a local council, incorporated association, or other recognised entity. Your arrangement, in which the council holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurances on behalf of the garden, satisfies this requirement. When you submit the application, you will need to include a signed auspice agreement or letter of support from the council confirming that they will act as the legal and financial sponsor for the grant funds.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant has a minimum award of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Applications are assessed on the merit and scalability of the proposed resilience‑building activities, so there is no penalty for requesting an amount toward the lower or higher end of the range, provided the budget is realistic and well‑justified.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances a community’s ability to withstand climate‑related stresses—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, drought‑tolerant planting, and related installation labour—is considered an eligible cost. Both the purchase of the tanks and shade‑cloth/materials and any associated professional installation or engineering fees can be included in the budget. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., stall supplies, membership administration) are not covered unless they are demonstrably tied to the implementation of the funded infrastructure.

**Supporting documentation**  
To facilitate a smooth review, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice letter** – signed by an authorised council representative, outlining the council’s role as sponsor and confirming insurance coverage.  
2. **Project plan** – a concise description (max 2 pages) of the proposed rainwater tanks and shade structure, including site maps, anticipated water capture volume, and how the shade will extend the growing season.  
3. **Detailed budget** – itemised costs (equipment, delivery, installation, any contingency) with quotes or vendor estimates where possible; indicate the total amount requested and any co‑funding or in‑kind contributions.  
4. **Organisational information** – a brief profile of the garden (mission, volunteer structure, recent activities) and a summary of your $40,000 annual turnover, highlighting the produce stall and membership fees as revenue sources.  
5. **Risk management statement** – note any safety or environmental considerations (e.g., tank placement, overflow management) and confirm that the council’s insurance covers volunteers during installation and ongoing use.  
6. **Letters of support (optional but encouraged)** – from nearby residents, local schools, or other community groups that would benefit from the enhanced resilience of the garden.
