Thank you for reaching out and for the clear description of your project. I’m delighted to provide the information you requested and to confirm that your idea aligns well with the objectives of our Community Resilience Grant round.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
Our grant program does accept applications from unincorporated community groups, provided they are formally auspiced by a local government or another legally constituted entity that can act as the contracting party. In your case, operating under the auspices of your local council satisfies this requirement, and the council’s existing insurance coverage will be considered sufficient for the purposes of risk management. Please note that the auspicing organisation will need to sign the grant agreement and be responsible for the disbursement of funds.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant is designed to support a broad range of project scales. Applications may request any amount between $5,000 and $150,000, subject to the overall budget available for the round and the proportion of total eligible costs the project seeks to fund. Most successful applicants receive between $20,000 and $80,000, but we evaluate each proposal on its own merits rather than applying a strict formula.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes—capital assets such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are considered eligible expenditures. These items directly contribute to the resilience of your garden by reducing water dependency and protecting participants from extreme heat, both core themes of the grant. We also cover associated installation, permits, and any necessary site works, provided they are documented and justified in your budget.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following items:

1. **Project Description** – a concise narrative (maximum 2 pages) outlining the problem you are addressing, the proposed solution, and the expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Narrative** – a detailed line‑item budget that clearly separates capital costs (tanks, shade structure) from operational expenses, with explanations for each line.  
3. **Organizational Profile** – a one‑page overview of your group, its volunteer composition, and the council’s auspice agreement (if available).  
4. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and cash flow summary for the last fiscal year).  
5. **Risk Management Plan** – a brief document outlining safety measures, insurance coverage (leveraging the council’s policy), and contingency plans.  
6. **Community Engagement Evidence** – letters of support, membership lists, or attendance records demonstrating the garden’s impact and stakeholder buy‑in.  
7. **Timeline and Milestones** – a Gantt‑style chart or bullet‑point schedule indicating key activities, deliverables, and target dates.  
8. **Appendices** – any site plans, contractor quotes, or product specifications for the rainwater tanks and shade structure.

We strongly advise that you begin drafting these documents as soon as possible, given that the application deadline is next month.
