Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application, and I am pleased to provide the following guidance based on the details you have shared.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:**  
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local government authority—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., is an incorporated not-for-profit, local government body, or other approved entity). The auspicing organisation must sign the funding agreement, hold the required insurances (which you have confirmed your council already does), and accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a signed letter from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and evidence of their current public liability insurance.

**Grant Amounts:**  
This round offers grants between **$5,000 and $50,000**. There is no requirement to request the maximum; you should apply for the amount that accurately reflects your project’s scope and budget. Given your annual turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within this band would be considered proportionate.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:**  
Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure that enable continued operation during extreme heat events fall squarely within this definition. Eligible expenses include the purchase and installation of the tanks, structural materials, engineering certification (if required), and associated labour. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not fundable.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**  
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Auspicing letter from the council (on letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).
2. Council’s current public liability insurance certificate of currency.
3. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
4. Two quotes for each major infrastructure component (tanks, shade structure).
5. A simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and evidence of community need (e.g., letters of support, usage data from the produce stall).
6. Evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site.

Please note that applications close at **5:00 pm AEST on 28 June 2024**.
