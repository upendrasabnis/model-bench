Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) holds the requisite public liability insurance and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to submit a signed letter from an authorised council officer confirming the auspicing arrangement, the council’s ABN, and evidence of current insurance coverage.

Second, the grant guidelines specify a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Given your described budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, your project would fall comfortably within this range. Please note that the program is competitive and partial funding is common; you should therefore include a realistic contingency plan in your budget should the full amount not be awarded.

Third, capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as water security measures and heat-mitigation structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category. However, routine maintenance, operational expenses, and retrospective costs incurred prior to a funding agreement are not eligible. Ensure your budget separates capital expenditure from any in-kind volunteer labour, which can be valued and included as a contribution but not claimed as grant expenditure.

Finally, the key supporting documents you should prepare now include: (1) the council auspicing letter described above; (2) your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover; (3) a simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and maintenance schedule; (4) two written quotes for each major infrastructure item; (5) evidence of land tenure or council permission to install the tanks and shade structure; and (6) letters of support from at least two community stakeholders demonstrating broader benefit.

I encourage you to review the full Program Guidelines and Frequently Asked Questions on our website, as they contain detailed assessment criteria and the online application portal link.
