**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant programme does accept applications from unincorporated community groups that are formally auspiced by an eligible local government authority. In your case, the local council’s sponsorship—including the provision of public liability insurance and any necessary governance oversight—satisfies the auspice requirement. You will need to submit a signed auspice agreement from the council that confirms their willingness to act as the legal and financial holder of the grant funds should your application be successful.

**Grant amount range**  
The Community Resilience Grant has a minimum award of $5,000 and a maximum award of $50,000 per project. Funding is allocated on a competitive basis, and the assessment panel considers the scale of the proposed activities, the demonstrated need, and the organisation’s capacity to manage the funds. Given your estimated turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within the mid‑range of the programme (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be well‑aligned with typical award sizes for infrastructure projects of this nature.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works such as the installation of rainwater tanks and a shade structure are expressly listed as eligible expenses under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” category of the grant guidelines. Costs that can be covered include the purchase of materials, contractor labour, engineering design (if required), and associated permits. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., water utility bills, routine maintenance) are not eligible, but you may include a modest contingency (up to 10 % of the total budget) for unforeseen site‑specific costs.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**
