Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria for our Community Resilience Grant round. I’m pleased to confirm that unincorporated community groups that operate under the auspices of a local council are indeed eligible to apply, provided the council can demonstrate its oversight and responsibility for the proposed activities. The council’s existing insurance coverage is generally acceptable as proof of risk management, and we have successfully funded similar arrangements in previous cycles.

Regarding financial parameters, the grant programme does not prescribe a minimum or maximum award amount. Applications are assessed on the basis of merit, relevance to the programme objectives, and the proportion of eligible costs that the grant would cover. Typical awards have ranged from $5,000 to $50,000, but we encourage applicants to propose budgets that reflect the true scope of their project while remaining realistic and justifiable.

Infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures is expressly listed as an eligible cost under the “Climate‑Resilient Community Assets” category. These items must be directly linked to the project’s resilience goals, demonstrate long‑term durability, and include provision for installation, maintenance, and any necessary permits. We recommend that the budget line items be itemised with quotations or provisional estimates to facilitate the review process.

When preparing your application, we suggest the following supporting documents:

1. **Project Description** – a concise narrative (maximum 2 pages) outlining the problem, proposed solution, expected outcomes, and timeline.  
2. **Budget Table** – a detailed breakdown of all costs, including unit prices, quantities, and any contingency allowances.  
3. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from the council (confirming auspice status and insurance coverage) and, where appropriate, from local businesses or community members.  
4. **Insurance Certificate** – confirmation that the council maintains public liability and any required professional indemnity coverage.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts, plus a cash‑flow statement demonstrating the $40,000 turnover and the sources of revenue.  
6. **Site Plans or Sketches** – visual representations of the proposed tank and shade structure locations.  
7. **Risk Assessment** – a brief outline of potential safety or environmental risks and mitigation strategies.  

If you can provide these documents in a single PDF, it will streamline our internal review.
