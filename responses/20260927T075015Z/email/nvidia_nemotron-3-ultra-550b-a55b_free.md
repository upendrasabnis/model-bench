Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to clarify eligibility before investing time in an application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for administering organisations—specifically, that it is a legal entity with an ABN, holds appropriate public liability insurance, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to supply a signed auspicing agreement or letter from the council confirming this arrangement, including their ABN and insurance details.

**Grant Amounts:** This round offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000 (GST exclusive). There is no minimum project size, but the total grant request must fall within this band. Your proposed budget should reflect the full cost of the rainwater tanks and shade structure, including installation, engineering certification if required, and any necessary site preparation.

**Eligible Costs:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures to mitigate heat impacts—is explicitly listed as an eligible expense. Operational costs (e.g., ongoing maintenance, utilities, or staff wages) are not funded. Please ensure your budget separates capital items clearly.

**Supporting Documents:** In addition to the auspicing agreement, please prepare:
- A project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and maintenance schedule
- Two quotes for each major capital item
- Council development approval or confirmation that no approval is required
- Your group’s most recent financial statement (showing the ~$40,000 turnover)
- Evidence of community support (letters, petition, or meeting minutes)
- A brief governance statement outlining your committee structure and decision-making processes
