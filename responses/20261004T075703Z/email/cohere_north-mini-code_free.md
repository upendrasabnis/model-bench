**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines expressly permit unincorporated groups or informal associations, provided they operate under the fiscal and legal auspices of a recognised local authority. In your case, the council’s involvement—including holding the necessary insurances—satisfies this requirement. You will, however, need to submit a Letter of Support from the council confirming that it will act as your fiscal sponsor and that it will be responsible for the management of any grant funds awarded. This letter should also detail the council’s insurance coverage and any reporting obligations that may apply to the grant recipient.

**Minimum or maximum grant amounts**  
There is no fixed minimum or maximum grant size for this round; awards are determined on the basis of project merit, community impact, and the proportion of eligible costs that the project seeks to fund. Typical awards have ranged from roughly $5,000 to $75,000, but applications that request amounts outside this band are also considered if they are fully justified and supported by a robust budget.

**Eligibility of rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes, capital infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is an eligible cost, provided that the items are directly linked to the project’s resilience objectives (e.g., reducing water consumption and mitigating heat stress). We recommend that you itemise these costs in your budget and accompany them with specifications, quotations, and any necessary planning approvals.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application we suggest the following documentation:  

1. **Project Plan** – a clear description of the activities, timelines, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget Spreadsheet** – detailed line‑item costs for all eligible expenses, including contingency.  
3. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and cash‑flow summary).  
4. **Proof of Turnover** – tax returns or bank statements demonstrating the $40,000 annual revenue figure.  
5. **Letter of Support** – from your local council confirming its auspice role and insurance coverage.  
6. **Insurance Certificate** – confirming that the council holds appropriate public liability and property insurance.  
7. **Membership / Community Engagement Evidence** – membership rolls, volunteer sign‑ups, or local survey data illustrating community need.
