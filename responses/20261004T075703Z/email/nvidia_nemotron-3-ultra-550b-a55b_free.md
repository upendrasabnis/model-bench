Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarification on each of your questions.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspice arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., is an incorporated not-for-profit, local government body, or other approved entity). The council must sign the grant agreement, accept legal and financial responsibility for the project, and hold the required insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation, etc.) on your behalf. Please ensure you have a written auspice agreement in place before submitting your application.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. There is no requirement for matched funding, although evidence of in-kind contributions or community support will strengthen your application.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience—such as rainwater tanks, shade structures, irrigation systems, and associated installation costs—is considered an eligible expense. Costs must be essential to the project, represent value for money, and be supported by at least two quotes (or one quote with a justification for sole sourcing). Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water rates, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To avoid delays, please have the following ready:
1. Signed auspice agreement on council letterhead.
2. Council’s current Certificate of Currency for public liability and workers’ compensation insurance.
3. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report (showing the ~$40,000 turnover).
4. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item.
5. Site plan or photos showing proposed tank and shade structure locations.
6. Letters of support from community partners or council.
7. Brief project plan with timeline, risk management, and sustainability strategy.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June 2024. I encourage you to start the online application early so you can save drafts and return as you gather documents. If you would like feedback on a draft budget or project description before submission, please feel free to send it through.
