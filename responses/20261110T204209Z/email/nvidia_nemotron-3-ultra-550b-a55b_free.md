Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarification on each of the points you have raised.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., is an incorporated not-for-profit, local government body, or other eligible entity) and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to submit a signed auspicing agreement or letter from the council confirming their willingness to act as the legal applicant, their ABN, and evidence of their current public liability insurance.

**Grant Amounts**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. Please note that we cannot fund 100 per cent of project costs; a minimum co-contribution of 20 per cent (cash or in-kind) is required, which can include volunteer labour valued at the current Australian Bureau of Statistics volunteer rate.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is an eligible cost category. Items must be fixed assets that remain with the project site. Quotes must be obtained from at least two independent suppliers for any single item exceeding $5,000, and all infrastructure must comply with relevant Australian Standards and local planning requirements.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
In addition to the auspicing agreement, please prepare:
1. A project plan with timeline, risk management, and maintenance strategy.
2. A detailed budget (income and expenditure) showing the grant request, co-contributions, and GST status.
3. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item.
4. Evidence of land tenure or a letter of support from the landowner (council) confirming permission to install the tanks and shade structure.
5. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report (even if unaudited) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover.
6. A brief governance statement outlining your committee structure and decision-making processes.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June 2024. I recommend starting the online application early so you can save drafts and attach documents progressively. If you would like feedback on a draft budget or project plan before submission, please feel free to email them to me at least ten business days before the deadline.
