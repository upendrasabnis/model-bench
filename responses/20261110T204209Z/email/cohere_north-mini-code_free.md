**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our Community Resilience Grant program welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. While most successful applicants are registered charities or incorporated associations, unincorporated groups that operate under the formal auspices of a local government authority are also eligible, provided the council can act as the fiscal sponsor and guarantor for the grant. In practice this means the council must: (1) confirm that it holds the insurance and risk‑management arrangements required; (2) demonstrate that it will administer any funded project on behalf of the group; and (3) provide a written endorsement that the garden will be managed by the volunteer team you described. If your council can supply such a letter of support, we can consider your group eligible despite its unincorporated status.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant program does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum; rather, awards are calibrated to the scale and impact of the proposed project. Typical grants for community‑infrastructure initiatives range from approximately $5,000 to $75,000, though larger projects that demonstrate substantial community benefit and strong matching funds may receive up to $150,000. Your proposed rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure installation falls within the mid‑range of this spectrum, and we would expect a grant in the $20,000‑$40,000 range, assuming the budget aligns with the overall project cost.

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes—capital‑type infrastructure is an eligible cost under the “Resilience & Climate Adaptation” category of the grant. Both rainwater harvesting equipment and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as allowable expenditures because they directly enhance the garden’s ability to withstand hotter summer conditions, reduce water consumption, and protect volunteers and produce. We recommend that you itemise each component (tank capacity, vendor quotes, structural engineering plans) in your budget breakdown to demonstrate how each aligns with the program’s objectives.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest assembling the following documents (where applicable):  

1. **Council Endorsement Letter** – formal confirmation that the council will act as fiscal sponsor and that it holds insurance coverage for the project.  
2. **Project Narrative & Work Plan –** a clear description of the installation timeline, deliverables, and how the project will achieve resilience outcomes.  
3._Unified Budget Spreadsheet_ – detailed cost breakdown (materials, labour, installation, contingency) with supporting quotes for tanks, shade structures, and any ancillary equipment.  
4. **Financial Documentation** – recent audited accounts or cash‑flow statements showing your $40,000 annual turnover, plus evidence of revenue sources (stall sales, membership fees).  
5. **Proof of Insurance** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming coverage for the garden’s activities and assets.  
6. **Organizational Profile** – a short constitution or set of operating guidelines outlining the volunteer governance structure.  
7. **Community Impact Statement** – letters of support from local residents, schools, or partner organisations, and metrics (e.g., number of households served, expected water savings).
