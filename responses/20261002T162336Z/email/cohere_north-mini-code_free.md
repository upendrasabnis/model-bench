**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
Our Community Resilience Grant programme welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. While many successful applicants are registered charities, we also accept unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of a local government body—provided that the council can confirm it holds the necessary insurance, risk‑management and legal authority for the proposed activities. In your case, because the council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf and provides formal auspice, you should meet the eligibility criteria for an unincorporated group. The only additional step will be to include a letter of support (or an official council endorsement) that explicitly states the council’s auspice and its willingness to be responsible for any liabilities arising from the project.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
The grant round does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, we allocate funding based on the scope of the proposed work, the demonstrated community impact, and the budget justification you provide. Past applications for infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures have ranged from $15,000 to $75,000, but we are open to proposals that fall outside this band if the rationale and cost‑effectiveness are clearly articulated. Please bear in mind that the total budget you propose will need to be realistic and fully justified, as we assess the proportion of the project that can be supported by grant funds versus other sources.

**Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Yes—rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible costs under the “Environmental Sustainability and Climate Adaptation” component of the grant. These items are considered high‑impact investments that directly contribute to the garden’s ability to operate during hotter summer periods, aligning perfectly with the programme’s goal of building community resilience to climate‑related challenges. We encourage you to provide detailed specifications (e.g., tank capacity, material, durability ratings) and to reference any local water‑conservation policies that support the project’s necessity.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documentation:

1. **Project Narrative** – a clear description of the problem (increasing summer heat), objectives, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will address them.  
2. **Budget Table** – itemised costs for equipment, installation, any professional services, and a breakdown of how much you plan to contribute or raise from other sources.  
3. **Letter of Support from the Council** – confirming your auspice, insurance coverage, and any council‑provided resources that will be leveraged.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a short overview of your garden’s history, volunteer base, and community reach (membership numbers, weekly produce stall turnover, etc.).  
5. **Risk Assessment and Insurance Proof** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate (or a letter confirming coverage) and a brief risk‑management plan for the installation work.  
6. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or a set of basic accounts if you are unincorporated) showing your $40,000 turnover and how it is derived.  
7. **Community Engagement Evidence** – minutes from council meetings or local stakeholder consultations, flyers, or sign‑up sheets demonstrating local support.  
8. **Sustainability Plan** – an outline of how you will maintain the tanks and shade structure, including any training for volunteers and a maintenance schedule.
