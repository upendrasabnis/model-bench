First, regarding organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that it is a legally constituted entity with an ABN, holds appropriate public liability insurance, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. Your council’s existing insurance coverage for the garden satisfies the insurance requirement, though we will require a formal letter of support from the council confirming their willingness to act as auspice and administer the funds on your behalf.

Second, the current grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure to maintain operations during extreme heat—falls squarely within the program’s “climate adaptation infrastructure” priority and is considered an eligible capital cost. Please note that site preparation, delivery, and professional installation fees may be included, but ongoing maintenance costs are not.

Third, with an annual turnover of approximately $40,000, you are well within the program’s financial thresholds for small community organisations. You will need to provide the following supporting documents with your application: (1) the council’s signed auspice agreement letter; (2) a simple project budget with two quotes for each major cost item; (3) a brief project plan outlining timelines, risk management, and community benefit; (4) your most recent annual financial statement or treasurer’s report; and (5) evidence of land tenure or a council licence to occupy the garden site.

I encourage you to review the full program guidelines on our website for detailed assessment criteria and the online application portal link.
