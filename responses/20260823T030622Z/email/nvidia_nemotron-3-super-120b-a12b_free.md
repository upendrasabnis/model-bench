Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you and your volunteers are doing to strengthen community resilience through the garden. I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant and to outline the documentation you will need to prepare.

**Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The grant programme expressly permits applications from unincorporated community groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. As long as your council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will administer the grant funds, hold the necessary insurances, and assume financial and reporting responsibility on your behalf, your garden meets the auspice requirement. Please ensure the auspice letter is on council letterhead, signed by an authorised officer, and includes the council’s Australian Business Number (ABN) and contact details.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum award of **$5,000** and a maximum of **$50,000** per successful applicant. The amount requested should be proportionate to the scope of the proposed activities and must be justified in the budget section of the application. There is no requirement to request the full maximum; many projects receive funding in the $10,000–$20,000 range, which aligns well with the scale of rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure installations.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances the group’s capacity to withstand climate‑related stresses—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, solar‑powered irrigation, and related site‑works—is expressly listed as an eligible cost. Both the purchase of materials and reasonable labour costs (including contracted installation) can be included. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., weekly stall costs) are not eligible unless they are demonstrably tied to the implementation or maintenance of the funded infrastructure.

**Supporting documentation**  
To substantiate your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice letter** from the local council (as described above).  
2. **Proof of council insurance** covering the garden’s activities (certificate of currency).  
3. **Project plan** detailing the proposed rainwater‑tank capacity, shade‑structure specifications, timeline, and how the works will increase resilience to extreme heat.  
4. **Detailed budget** with itemised quotes (or supplier estimates) for tanks, pumps, fittings, shade‑structure materials, and any installation labour.  
5. **Evidence of community support** (e.g., letters of endorsement from neighbours, council officers, or local schools) and a brief description of how the garden serves the broader community (membership numbers, volunteer hours, produce stall turnover).  
6. **Financial statement** showing the garden’s annual turnover of approximately $40,000 (a simple profit‑and‑loss summary or bank‑statement excerpt is sufficient).  
7. **Governance outline** (even if informal) that explains decision‑making processes, volunteer roles, and how the council’s auspice arrangement will be managed.

Once these documents are assembled, you can complete the online application form, which will guide you through uploading each file and entering the narrative responses.
