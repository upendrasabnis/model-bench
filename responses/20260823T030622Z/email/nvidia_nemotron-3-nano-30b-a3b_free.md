Thank you for reaching out with your questions about the Community Resilience Grant. I appreciate the important work your volunteer‑run garden does for the community, and I’m happy to provide clarification on the points you raised.  

**Eligibility of groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by a local government authority, provided the sponsoring council can supply the necessary documentation confirming the group’s status and that the council holds the required public liability and professional indemnity insurances on the group’s behalf. In such cases, the council must be listed as the primary applicant or as a co‑signatory on the submission, and the application should include a letter of support from the council confirming the auspice arrangement.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The current funding round offers grants ranging from **$5,000 to $25,000**. Applications that request less than $5,000 are still eligible, but they may be considered for a “small‑scale” stream with a reduced evaluation threshold. Conversely, proposals seeking more than $25,000 will not be accepted under this round.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience of community facilities — such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, solar panels, or energy‑efficient lighting — is considered an eligible cost, provided the expenditure is:  
1. Essential to maintaining or improving service delivery during climate‑related stressors;  
2. Properly cost‑quoted and aligned with the project’s objectives; and  
3. Documented as a capital‑cost item (i.e., not an operational expense).  

**Supporting documentation**  
To accompany your application, we recommend preparing the following items:  
- A signed letter of support from the sponsoring council confirming the auspice relationship and insurance coverage.  
- A detailed project budget that breaks down capital costs (e.g., rainwater tanks, shade structure) and any matching contributions.  
- Quotes or tenders from approved suppliers for the proposed infrastructure.  
- Evidence of your annual turnover and sources of funding (e.g., produce‑stall invoices, membership fee records).  
- A brief project plan outlining timelines, expected outcomes, and how the grant will be administered.  

If you would like a template for any of these documents or further clarification on any of the above points, please let me know — I would be glad to assist.
