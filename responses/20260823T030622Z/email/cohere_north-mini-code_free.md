Thank you for reaching out and for the enthusiasm you and your garden team have for enhancing resilience during the hotter summer periods. I’m pleased to provide the following clarification on the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the upcoming Community Resilience Grant round.

**Eligibility of unincorporated, council‑auspiced groups**  
Our grant guidelines state that eligible applicants must be either a registered charitable organization or an incorporated association. However, we do permit unincorporated groups that operate under a formal auspice agreement with a local government entity, provided the council assumes fiscal responsibility, holds the required insurance, and can act as the primary point of contact for the grant. If your council can confirm in writing that it will serve as the fiscal sponsor and will be liable for any grant‑related obligations, your group will meet the eligibility requirement. Please have the council issue a brief letter of auspice that includes its name, address, contact details, and a statement that it will manage the grant funds and ensure compliance with all reporting obligations.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
There is no fixed minimum or maximum grant size; awards are determined on the merit of the proposal, the scope of the activities, and the available funding for the round. Typical awards for community infrastructure projects in your region range from $15,000 to $75,000, but applications that demonstrate strong community impact and sustainable operations may be considered for amounts outside this band. We encourage you to align your budget with the actual costs of the rainwater tanks and shade structure, while also highlighting any matching contributions or in‑kind support you can provide.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes, the installation of rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is expressly listed as an eligible expense under the “Climate‑Resilient Infrastructure” component of the grant. Both items must directly support the ongoing operation of the garden during periods of extreme heat and must be justified with clear specifications, cost estimates, and installation timelines.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
When you move forward with a full application, we recommend assembling the following documentation:  

1. **Letter of auspice** from the council confirming its sponsorship and insurance coverage.  
2. **Organizational profile** – a brief description of the garden’s mission, volunteer base, and community impact.  
3. **Financial statements** for the most recent two years (or a simplified income‑expense summary if full audited statements are not available).  
4. **Project plan** outlining objectives, timeline, and implementation steps for the rainwater tanks and shade structure.  
5. **Detailed budget** with line‑item costs, including equipment, installation, and any contingency funds.  
6. **Letters of support** from neighboring community groups, local businesses, or other stakeholders.  
7. **Risk assessment and insurance verification** (the council’s certificate will satisfy this requirement).  
8. **Monitoring and evaluation framework** that describes how you will measure the project’s success (e.g., water usage reductions, increased garden usage during heat events).
