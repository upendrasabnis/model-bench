Subject: Re: Eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant  

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant and for taking the time to outline your garden’s plans. I am happy to provide the clarification you requested so you can decide whether to proceed with a full application.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from unincorporated community groups that are formally auspiced by an eligible local government entity. In your case, the local council’s sponsorship—including the provision of public liability insurance and any required governance oversight—satisfies the auspicing requirement. You will need to submit a signed letter of support from the council confirming that they are acting as your auspicing body and that they accept responsibility for the grant funds should your application be successful.

**2. Grant amount range**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Projects that demonstrate clear community benefit and a realistic budget within this band are competitive. Given your estimated turnover of approximately $40,000, a request in the $15,000–$30,000 range would be appropriate, provided the budget aligns with the scope of works you describe.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that enhance the resilience of community‑owned spaces—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related site‑preparation works—are explicitly listed as eligible expenditures. Both the purchase and installation of the tanks and the shade structure would therefore qualify, as long as they are directly tied to the project’s objective of sustaining garden operations during hotter periods. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., staff salaries, routine maintenance) are not covered, but you may include a modest contingency (typically up to 10 % of the total project cost) for unforeseen installation expenses.

**4. Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please gather the following items:  

- A signed auspice agreement or letter of support from the local council, detailing their role and confirming insurance coverage.  
- A clear project plan (including timelines, milestones, and a brief risk assessment).  
- Itemised budget quotations for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, foundations, plumbing, and any required council permits or approvals.  
- Evidence of community need and benefit (e.g., letters from regular stallholders, membership statistics, photographs of current site conditions, and any climate‑impact data relevant to your region).  
- Your group’s most recent financial statement or a simple income‑expenditure summary showing the $40,000 annual turnover and how grant funds would supplement existing revenue.  
- If applicable, any relevant permits or development approvals already obtained, or a statement confirming that you will secure them prior to project commencement.

Once these documents are assembled, you can complete the online application form, upload the PDFs, and submit before the closing date (please verify the exact deadline on the grant portal). Should you encounter any difficulties or have further questions during the preparation stage, feel free to reach out to me directly or to the grants administration team at grants@[agency].gov.au.

We look forward to the possibility of supporting your garden’s efforts to build climate resilience.
