**Eligibility of Unincorporated Groups Auspiced by a Council**  
The grant guidelines state that eligible applicants include not only incorporated non‑profit entities but also unincorporated community groups that are formally auspiced—or “hosted”—by a local government body. In such arrangements the council typically provides the necessary administrative infrastructure, including insurance coverage, financial oversight, and compliance monitoring. Provided that the council can confirm its role as the auspicing body on official letterhead and that the grant funds will be managed through the council’s accounts, your group meets the eligibility criteria.

**Minimum and Maximum Grant Amounts**  
There is no fixed minimum award; applications are assessed on the merit of the proposed outcomes, relevance to the grant’s objectives, and the proportion of the total project cost that can be realistically funded. The maximum grant available for any single application in this round is $75,000, which is intended to cover the majority of a project’s eligible expenses while encouraging complementary funding from other sources. Your projected budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears well within this ceiling.

**Eligibility of Infrastructure Costs**  
The Community Resilience Grant explicitly supports capital‑intensive infrastructure that enhances community resilience to climate‑related challenges. Rainwater harvesting systems and climate‑adapted shading structures are listed as eligible project components because they reduce water consumption, lower heat‑island effects, and increase the year‑round viability of community food‑production sites. Both items must be directly tied to the project’s resilience objectives and must be procured from reputable suppliers with appropriate warranties.

**Supporting Documentation to Prepare**  
When you move forward with a full application, we recommend assembling the following documents:

1. **Auspice Confirmation Letter** – a formal letter from your local council confirming that it will act as the administering body for the grant, including contact details and insurance coverage.  
2. **Organizational Profile** – a brief description of your garden’s mission, volunteer base, and operating model.  
3. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for an unincorporated group, a set of cash‑flow statements and a summary of revenue sources). Your $40,000 annual turnover should be reflected here.  
4. **Project Plan** – a detailed timeline, scope of work, and breakdown of costs for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, and any ancillary expenses (e.g., installation, permits).  
5. **Community Impact Statement** – evidence of local support, such as letters from neighboring residents, schools, or community organizations, and metrics that illustrate the expected benefits (e.g., water savings, reduced cooling costs).  
6. **Risk Assessment and Mitigation** – a clear outline of potential project risks and how they will be managed, including maintenance plans for the installed infrastructure.  
7. **Matching Contributions** – documentation of any in‑kind or cash contributions you will provide or have already secured.  

Finally, please note that the grant portal requires an electronic copy of the council’s auspice letter before the application can be submitted. We recommend uploading this document early in the process to avoid any last‑minute delays.
