First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under the auspices of a local government authority are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants. The council must be the legal entity that enters into the funding agreement, holds the relevant insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation, etc.), and assumes financial and reporting responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a formal letter of support from the council confirming their willingness to act as auspice, along with their ABN and proof of incorporation.

Second, the current grant round offers funding between a minimum of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Given your described project scope and annual turnover of approximately $40,000, your request would likely fall comfortably within this range. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some capacity for co-contribution (financial or in-kind), though this is assessed proportionally to the organisation’s size and resources.

Third, the infrastructure you describe—rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure—is considered an eligible capital expenditure under the “Community Infrastructure” stream, provided the assets are fixed, improve the long-term resilience of the site, and are not considered routine maintenance. Portable or temporary structures would not qualify, so please ensure your plans specify permanent installation.

Finally, for supporting documentation, you should prepare: (1) the council auspice agreement/letter; (2) a detailed project plan with timeline and risk management; (3) at least two quotes for each major capital item; (4) your most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report; (5) evidence of land tenure or council permission for the works; and (6) letters of community support demonstrating the garden’s reach and volunteer base.

I encourage you to review the full Program Guidelines on our website for the assessment criteria and the online application portal user guide. The closing date is firm, so I recommend starting the application at least two weeks prior to allow time for the council’s internal sign-off.
