**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our Community Resilience Grant programme is designed to support projects that deliver measurable community benefit. While the primary applicant is normally required to be a registered charity or an incorporated not‑for‑profit entity, we do accept applications from unincorporated groups provided they are formally auspiced by an eligible organisation such as your local council. In such cases, the council must act as the fiscal sponsor, hold the requisite insurances, and be able to enter into a grant agreement on behalf of the group. The council’s involvement therefore satisfies our organisational‑structure requirement, and your group should be considered eligible.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant programme does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Awards typically range from approximately $5,000 to $150,000, depending on the scope, sustainability, and anticipated community impact of the proposed project. Your budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure falls comfortably within this range, and we would be happy to discuss the most appropriate funding level once a full application is submitted.  

**Infrastructure costs – rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes, infrastructure of this nature is explicitly listed as an eligible expense under the “Physical Assets and Infrastructure” category of our guidelines. Both rainwater harvesting equipment and permanent shade structures are considered capital assets that directly support the long‑term resilience of community operations, particularly in the face of climate‑related challenges such as extended hot summers.  

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following materials:  

1. **Project Narrative** – a clear description of the problem, objectives, implementation plan, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – itemised costs for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary expenses, with a note on how the council’s auspice will handle procurement.  
3. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from the council, local businesses, and community members.  
4. **Proof of Insurance** – the council’s certificate confirming coverage for the project site and activities.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts for the $40,000 turnover operation, plus a cash‑flow projection.  
6. **Risk Assessment and Safety Plan** – particularly important for the installation and use of water storage and shade structures.  
7. **Evaluation Framework** – how you will measure success (e.g., water usage reduction, increased garden operating days during heatwaves).
