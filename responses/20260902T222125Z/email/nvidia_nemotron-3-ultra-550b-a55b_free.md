Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that they are a legally constituted entity with current public liability insurance and an ABN. The council would need to sign the grant agreement on your behalf and assume legal and financial responsibility for the project. You will need to provide a letter from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and evidence of their current insurance policies.

Second, the current grant round offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 per project. There is no minimum total project cost, but the grant cannot exceed 80% of eligible project expenditure; the remaining 20% (or more) must be contributed by the applicant or auspicing body as cash or verified in-kind support (volunteer labour, donated materials, etc.). Given your estimated project scope, you would be well within these parameters.

Third, capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures to mitigate heat impacts—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category in the program guidelines. Eligible expenditure includes purchase and installation of tanks, pumps, plumbing, structural steel, shade cloth, engineering certification, and necessary permits. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not eligible.

Finally, to streamline your application, I recommend preparing the following supporting documents now: the council auspicing letter (referenced above); your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover; quotes from at least two suppliers for each major cost item; a simple project plan with timeline and risk register; evidence of land tenure or council permission for the infrastructure; and letters of support from community stakeholders demonstrating the garden’s broader resilience value.

The application portal opens on 1 June and closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I encourage you to draft your responses in the offline template available on our website before the portal opens. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require clarification on any of these points or would like a brief phone discussion to walk through the budget template.
