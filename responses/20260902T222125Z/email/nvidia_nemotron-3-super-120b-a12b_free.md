Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I appreciate you taking the time to clarify the eligibility criteria before preparing a full application for the Community Resilience Grant.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups under council auspices**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from not‑for‑profit organisations that are not incorporated as separate legal entities, provided they are auspiced by an eligible host organisation. A local government body such as your council qualifies as an eligible auspice, so your garden can apply under the council’s auspice arrangement. You will need to submit a signed letter of support from the council confirming that it will act as the legal and financial auspice for the grant, accept responsibility for reporting, and hold the necessary insurances on behalf of the project.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per successful application. Requests should be justified by a detailed budget that demonstrates how the funds will be used to achieve the stated resilience outcomes. There is no requirement to request the maximum amount; you may apply for any sum within this band that matches the scope of your project.

**Eligibility of proposed infrastructure**  
Capital works that enhance the capacity of a community organisation to withstand climate‑related stresses are considered eligible costs. Rainwater harvesting tanks and shade structures that directly support continued operation during extreme heat events fall within the scope of “infrastructure improvements” that the grant intends to fund. Please ensure that the budget clearly itemises each component (e.g., tank purchase, installation labour, shade‑cloth, footings, and any associated permits) and that you provide quotes or cost estimates from reputable suppliers.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please have the following documents ready for upload with your online submission:

1. **Auspice agreement** – a signed letter from the local council confirming its role as auspice holder, including details of insurance coverage and any financial management responsibilities.  
2. **Proof of not‑for‑profit status** – although you are not a registered charity, a copy of your garden’s constitution or terms of reference, together with a statement of your volunteer‑run nature, will satisfy the requirement.  
3. **Financial information** – a recent profit‑and‑loss statement or bank statement showing the approximate $40,000 annual turnover, and a breakdown of revenue sources (produce stall, membership fees).  
4. **Project plan** – a concise description (max 500 words) outlining the need for rainwater tanks and shade, the expected climate‑resilience benefits, a timeline for procurement and installation, and how the garden will continue to serve the community after completion.  
5. **Quotations** – at least two written quotes for each major item (tanks and shade structure) to demonstrate cost‑effectiveness.  
6. **Risk management summary** – a brief note on any health, safety, or environmental considerations associated with the installation (e.g., site preparation, water quality standards).
