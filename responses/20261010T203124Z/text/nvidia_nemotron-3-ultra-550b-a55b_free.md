## 1. Introduction: The High Stakes of Grant Stewardship

Grant funding—whether sourced from federal treasuries, state agencies, municipal budgets, private foundations, or corporate philanthropy—is not "free money." It is a **contractual obligation** wrapped in a fiduciary duty. The moment a grant agreement is executed, the grantee assumes the role of a steward for public or private trust assets.

**Acquittal reporting** is the formal mechanism by which that stewardship is proven. It is the evidentiary bridge between *intent* (the approved budget and project plan) and *reality* (actual expenditures, outputs, and outcomes).

Failure to acquit correctly triggers a cascade of consequences:
*   **Financial:** Clawbacks (repayment of funds), interest penalties, disallowed costs.
*   **Operational:** Funding suspension, contract termination, inability to draw down future tranches.
*   **Reputational:** Debarment from future government contracts (e.g., SAM.gov exclusions in the US, National Redress Scheme checks in Australia), loss of charity registration (ACNC in Australia, Charity Commission in UK, IRS Form 990 scrutiny in US), donor flight.
*   **Legal:** False Claims Act liability (US), fraud investigations, personal liability for directors/officers.

This guide moves beyond "checklist compliance" to establish a **Compliance Operating System (COS)**—an integrated framework of people, processes, and technology designed to make acquittal a non-event rather than a crisis.

## 2. Deconstructing the Acquittal: What It Is and Why It Matters

### 2.1 Definition: Beyond the Financial Statement
An **acquittal** (often used interchangeably with *Financial Acquittal Report*, *Final Financial Report (FFR)*, *Statement of Expenditure*, or *Grant Closeout Package*) is a certified declaration that:
1.  Funds were spent **solely** for the purposes approved in the Grant Agreement.
2.  Expenditures comply with the **Cost Principles** applicable to the funder (e.g., 2 CFR 200 / Uniform Guidance in US; CGRGs in Australia; Grant Standards in UK).
3.  The project delivered the **outputs/outcomes** promised (Performance Acquittal).
4.  Any **interest earned** or **program income** generated is accurately reported and treated per the agreement.
5.  All **unspent funds** are identified for refund or carry-over approval.

### 2.2 The Dual Nature: Financial vs. Performance Acquittal
Most grantees conflate the two. Funders separate them.

| Dimension | Financial Acquittal | Performance (Programmatic) Acquittal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Core Question** | "Did you spend the money exactly as budgeted?" | "Did you achieve what you promised?" |
| **Primary Evidence** | General Ledger (GL), bank statements, invoices, payroll registers, timesheets. | KPI dashboards, survey data, evaluation reports, beneficiary lists, photos, publications. |
| **Certification** | CFO / Finance Director / Authorized Signatory. | CEO / Program Director / Principal Investigator. |
| **Risk Focus** | Unallowable costs, math errors, commingling funds, procurement violations. | Scope creep, "output washing" (counting activities as outcomes), data integrity. |
| **Timing** | Usually 30–90 days post-period end. | Often due same time, but sometimes requires longitudinal tracking (e.g., 12-month post-project follow-up). |

### 2.3 The "Acquittal Equation"
> **Approved Budget + Approved Variations – Actual Allowable Expenditure = Residual Funds (Refundable / Carry-over)**

*If the equation doesn't balance to zero (or an approved carry-over), the acquittal fails.*

## 3. The Compliance Ecosystem: Government vs. Philanthropic Landscapes

While the *principles* of stewardship are universal, the *regulatory architecture* differs radically. A unified COS must map these differences.

### 3.1 Government Funders: The Regulatory Heavyweight
**United States (Federal Pass-Through & Direct):**
*   **Governing Law:** **2 CFR Part 200 (Uniform Guidance)**. This is the "Bible." Subpart E (Cost Principles), Subpart D (Post-Federal Award Requirements), Subpart F (Audit Requirements).
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   **Allowability:** Costs must be *Reasonable, Allocable, Consistent, & Adequately Documented* (RACAD).
    *   **Indirect Costs (F&A):** Negotiated Rate Agreement (NICRA) vs. De Minimis 10% MTDC. **Critical:** You cannot charge indirect costs on equipment >$5k, participant support costs, or capital expenditures unless specifically allowed.
    *   **Procurement Standards (§200.317–.327):** Micro-purchase (<$10k), Simplified Acquisition ($10k–$250k), Formal Procurement (>$250k). Sole source justification requirements are strict.
    *   **Subrecipient vs. Contractor Determination (§200.331):** Misclassification is a top audit finding. Subrecipients = programmatic decision making, compliance flow-down. Contractors = goods/services for grantee's own use.
*   **Reporting:** SF-425 (Federal Financial Report) standard format. Performance Progress Reports (PPR) via GrantSolutions/Research.gov.

**Australia (Commonwealth/State):**
*   **Governing Framework:** **Commonwealth Grants Rules and Guidelines (CGRGs)**, Financial Framework (Finance Act), PGPA Act.
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   **Grant Agreement (Schedule):** The specific terms override general guides.
    *   **GST Treatment:** Grants can be GST-free, GST-inclusive, or "Global Accounting." Getting this wrong creates massive BAS (Business Activity Statement) liabilities.
    *   **Acquittal Form:** Usually a specific Departmental template (e.g., Department of Industry, Science and Resources template) requiring a **Statutory Declaration** by the Accountable Authority.
    *   **Audit:** Often requires an **Independent Audit Report** (Agreed-Upon Procedures or Reasonable Assurance) by a Registered Company Auditor (RCA) for grants >$500k–$1M.

**United Kingdom (UKRI / NIHR / Lottery / Local Authority):**
*   **Governing Framework:** **Grant Terms & Conditions (T&Cs)**, *Managing Public Money* (HMG Treasury), Charity Commission guidance (for charitable grantees).
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   **Full Economic Costing (fEC):** Standard for research (UKRI). 80% funding rule (grantee absorbs 20%).
    *   **VAT:** Complex partial exemption calculations for universities/charities.
    *   **Transparency:** UKRI requires publication of grant details on "Gateway to Research."

**European Union (Horizon Europe / Erasmus+):**
*   **Governing Framework:** **Grant Agreement (GA)**, **Annotated Model Grant Agreement (AMGA)**.
*   **Key Concepts:**
    *   **Actual Costs vs. Unit Costs vs. Lump Sums.** Horizon Europe shift toward **Lump Sum Grants** changes acquittal from "proving every euro" to "proving work packages delivered."
    *   **Personnel Costs:** Strict timesheet requirements (monthly, signed by employee/supervisor) for Actual Cost grants.
    *   **CFSP (Certificate on the Financial Statements):** Mandatory for thresholds >€430k (Horizon) or specific calls.

### 3.2 Philanthropic & Corporate Funders: The Relationship Heavyweights
*   **Governing Document:** The **Grant Agreement / Award Letter**. No universal code (like 2 CFR 200) applies unless the foundation *adopts* it (many large US foundations reference OMB Circulars/Uniform Guidance).
*   **Flexibility:** Often allow "General Operating Support" (unrestricted) or "Capacity Building" – acquittal focuses on *organizational health* metrics, not line-item receipts.
*   **Restricted Grants:** Behave like government grants. Require budget-to-actuals.
*   **Reporting Cadence:** Narrative-heavy. "Storytelling" + Metrics.
*   **Variations:** Usually easier. A phone call/email to Program Officer often suffices for budget reallocations <10-20%, vs. formal amendment for government.
*   **Audit Rights:** Rarely exercised, but "Site Visits" are common substitutes.

### 3.3 Comparative Matrix: Compliance Intensity

| Feature | US Federal (2 CFR 200) | AU Commonwealth (CGRG) | UKRI / Horizon EU | Large US Foundation (e.g., Gates, Ford) | Community Foundation / Corporate |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost Principles** | Statutory (Subpart E) | CGRG + AASB Standards | fEC / AMGA | Often adopt 2 CFR 200 | Negotiated / General |
| **Indirect Cost Policy** | NICRA / 10% De Minimis | Usually capped % or excluded | fEC (80/20) / Overheads | Varies widely (often 10-15%) | Rarely allowed |
| **Procurement Rules** | Strict tiers (§200.320) | Value for Money / Probity | AMGA / National Law | "Prudent person" standard | Informal quotes |
| **Timesheet Requirement** | Mandatory (After-the-fact) | Mandatory (Project-based) | Mandatory (Monthly) | Sometimes / Project-based | Rare |
| **Audit Threshold** | $750k Federal Exp (Single Audit) | >$500k-$1M (Specific Audit) | >€430k (CFS) / Risk-based | Rarely required | Almost never |
| **Variation Process** | Formal Amendment (Prior Approval) | Deed of Variation / Schedule | Amendment (Prior Approval) | Email / Portal Request | Verbal / Email |
| **Unspent Funds** | Refund / Federal Claim | Refund / Consolidated Revenue | Refund / Next Payment Reduction | Negotiated (Carry-over common) | Flexible / Carry-over common |

## 4. Anatomy of a Compliant Acquittal Report

A compliant acquittal package is a **bound evidentiary dossier**, not just a spreadsheet. It typically comprises five distinct modules.

### Module 1: The Certification & Declaration (The "Cover Sheet")
*   **Entity Details:** Legal name, ABN/EIN/Charity Number, Address.
*   **Grant Details:** Agreement ID, Project Title, Period of Performance.
*   **Financial Summary Table:**
    *   Total Approved Budget (by Category).
    *   Total Actual Expenditure (by Category).
    *   Variance ($ and %).
    *   Funds Received to Date.
    *   Funds Expended.
    *   **Balance Due (Refund) / Carry-over Requested.**
*   **Signatures:**
    *   *Chief Financial Officer / Finance Director:* "I certify the financial data is true, complete, and compliant with [Cost Principles]."
    *   *Chief Executive / Authorized Officer:* "I certify the project activities occurred as described."
    *   *Statutory Declaration (AU/UK Govt):* Signed before Justice of Peace/Notary. **Penalty for false declaration: Prison.**

### Module 2: The Financial Reconciliation (The "Engine Room")
This maps the **General Ledger (GL)** to the **Grant Budget Categories**.

**Required Schedules:**
1.  **Budget vs. Actual (BvA) by Natural Account & Grant Category.**
    *   *Example:* Budget: Salaries $100k. GL: $98,500. Variance: -$1,500 (1.5%).
2.  **Reconciliation of Cash Drawdowns to Bank Statements.**
    *   Show every deposit from funder. Match to GL Revenue Code "Grant Income - [Project Code]".
    *   Reconcile Interest Earned on Grant Funds (separate bank account vs. pooled).
3.  **Indirect Cost / Overhead Calculation Schedule.**
    *   Show Base (MTDC), Rate, Calculation, Amount Charged.
    *   *Proof:* Copy of NICRA letter or Board-approved De Minimis election.
4.  **Program Income / Revenue Generated Schedule.**
    *   Workshop fees, publication sales, service fees generated *by* the grant.
    *   Show treatment: Deducted from expenditure? Added to budget? Refunded?
5.  **Subrecipient / Contractor Expenditure Register.**
    *   List every sub-award/contract >$25k (or threshold).
    *   Show: Name, Amount, Purpose, Procurement Method, Monitoring Performed.

### Module 3: The General Ledger Detail (The "Source of Truth")
*   **Full Transaction Listing (Detail Trial Balance)** for the Project Code/Cost Center for the Period.
*   **Filters:** Exclude $0 lines. Include: Date, Voucher/Journal ID, Vendor/Employee, Description, Account Code, Debit, Credit, Net.
*   **Cross-Reference Column:** Map each GL line to the **Acquittal Budget Category** (e.g., GL 6100 "Travel - Domestic" -> Acquittal Category "Travel").

### Module 4: Supporting Evidence Pack (The "Audit File")
*Do not send this unless asked. Keep it indexed and ready.*
*   **Payroll:** Timesheets (signed), Payroll Register extracts, Fringe Benefit Rate calculations.
*   **Procurement:** PO, 3 Quotes, Evaluation Scorecard, Contract, Invoices, Proof of Receipt (Delivery docket).
*   **Travel:** Itinerary, Business Justification, Boarding passes/Receipts, Per Diem policy reference.
*   **Equipment (>$5k/$10k):** Asset Register entry, Title/Invoice, Disposition plan (if project ends).
*   **Subrecipient:** Their approved budget, *their* acquittal reports, Monitoring letters (site visit notes, desk review checklists).

### Module 5: Performance Acquittal (The "Impact Evidence")
*   **KPI Scorecard:** Target vs. Actual (Quantitative).
*   **Narrative:** Context for variances (Qualitative). "Target 100 workshops; Delivered 85. Reason: Flood event Q3. Mitigation: Online pivot Q4."
*   **Data Quality Statement:** Source of data, methodology, limitations.
*   **Case Studies / Testimonials / Photos:** Humanizing the data.
*   **Evaluation Report:** (If mandated) Independent or Internal.

## 5. Record-Keeping Architecture: Building an Audit-Proof Evidence Trail

The #1 cause of audit findings is not fraud—it is **disorganization**. You cannot acquit what you cannot find.

### 5.1 The "Single Source of Truth" Principle
**Do not manage grants in spreadsheets.**
*   **ERP/Finance System:** NetSuite, Sage Intacct, Microsoft Dynamics, Xero, QuickBooks Enterprise, TechnologyOne, SAP.
*   **Configuration:** **Project/Dimension/Job Costing Module MUST be active.**
*   **Chart of Accounts (CoA) Mapping:** Create a **Grant Budget Mapping Table**.
    *   *GL Account 61200 (Airfare)* -> *Grant Category: Travel.*
    *   *GL Account 60100 (Salaries)* -> *Grant Category: Personnel.*
    *   *Automation:* Use "Project Codes" or "Dimensions" on *every* transaction line (AP Bill, Payroll Journal, Credit Card, Expense Claim).

### 5.2 The Digital Evidence Locker (Document Management)
**Standard:** ISO 15489 / ISO 27001 aligned.
*   **Structure:** `/Grants/[Funder_Code]-[Grant_ID]/[Fiscal_Year]/`
    *   `/01_Agreement/` (Signed contract, variations, approved budget).
    *   `/02_Financial/` (Bank statements, Drawdown requests, BvA workpapers).
    *   `/03_Payroll/` (Timesheets, Pay runs, Fringe calcs).
    *   `/04_Procurement/` (Subfolders per Vendor: PO, Quotes, Contract, Invoices).
    *   `/05_Travel/` (Subfolders per Trip).
    *   `/06_Assets/` (Register, Disposal).
    *   `/07_Subrecipients/` (Sub-agreements, Their Reports, Monitoring).
    *   `/08_Performance/` (KPI Data, Surveys, Evaluation, Photos).
    *   `/09_Correspondence/` (Emails re: approvals, variations – **save as PDF/A**).
    *   `/10_Acquittal_Submission/` (Final PDF package, Submission receipt, Funder acknowledgement).

### 5.3 Retention Policies: The Legal Minimums
| Jurisdiction / Funder | Minimum Retention Period | Trigger Start Date |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **US Federal (2 CFR 200.334)** | **3 Years** | Submission of *Final* FFR (SF-425) for *entire* award (not just year). |
| **US Federal (False Claims Act)** | **6-10 Years** (Statute of Limitations) | Date of violation / Discovery. **Best Practice: 7-10 Years.** |
| **Australia (CGRG / Tax Law)** | **5 Years** (Tax) / **7 Years** (Corps Act) | End of financial year / Lodgement. Grant deeds often specify **7 Years**. |
| **UK (HMRC / Charity Comm)** | **6 Years** | End of financial year. |
| **EU (Horizon Europe)** | **5 Years** | Payment of balance. **Lump Sum:** 5 years from last payment. |
| **General Best Practice** | **7-10 Years** | Covers most statutes of limitations globally. |

**Destruction Protocol:** Never "delete." Use "Legal Hold" flags. Shred physical / Cryptographic erase digital. Log destruction (Date, Authority, Method, Witness).

### 5.4 Timesheet & Effort Reporting: The Achilles Heel
*   **Standard:** After-the-fact (not budget-based), Signed by Employee + Supervisor, Monthly/Semi-monthly.
*   **System:** Electronic (Timesheet module in ERP, or dedicated: Harvest, Toggl, Replicon, ClickTime) with **Audit Trail** (IP address, Timestamp, Edit Log).
*   **Cost Share / In-Kind:** Must be tracked *identically* to grant-funded time. If you promise 20% PI effort as cost-share, you need timesheets proving 20% effort occurred.

### 5.5 Asset Management (Property Standards)
*   **Threshold:** Usually $5,000 (US) / $5,000-$10,000 (AU) / €5,000 (EU).
*   **Register Fields:** Tag #, Description, Serial #, Fund Source %, Acquisition Date/Cost, Location, Condition, Disposal Date/Method/Proceeds.
*   **Physical Inventory:** Mandatory **Biennial** (US) / **Annual** (AU/UK) physical sighting. Reconcile to Register. Investigate missing items immediately (Police report for theft = insurance/grant claim).

## 6. The Audit Gauntlet: Types, Triggers, and Survival Strategies

### 6.1 Taxonomy of Audits
| Audit Type | Who Performs | Scope | Trigger | Grantee Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Single Audit (US)** | Independent CPA Firm (hired by Grantee) | **Entity-wide:** All Federal Awards >$750k. Compliance + Internal Controls. | Annual Exp > $750k Federal. | $20k–$100k+ (Allowable as Indirect/Cost Share). |
| **Program-Specific Audit (US)** | CPA Firm | Single Program (if only one award / funder mandates). | Funder request / Specific CFDA requirement. | Lower than Single Audit. |
| **Grant-Specific Audit (AU/UK/EU)** | Registered Auditor (RCA/Statutory) | Specific Grant Agreement terms. | Grant Deed clause (usually >$500k). | Direct Grant Cost (Budget for it!). |
| **Agreed-Upon Procedures (AUP)** | Auditor | Specific procedures only (e.g., "Verify payroll matches timesheets"). | Funder request / Lower cost option. | Lower. No "Opinion," just "Findings." |
| **Funder Site Visit / Desk Review** | Funder Staff (Program/Finance) | High-risk areas: Procurement, Payroll, Subrecipients. | Risk Assessment / Rotation / Whistleblower. | Staff Time. |
| **OIG / NAO / ANAO Audit** | Inspector General / National Audit Office | Systemic / High Value / Fraud. | Random / Tip-off / High Risk Profile. | High Stress / Legal Counsel. |

### 6.2 The Audit Risk Model: How You Get Picked
Funders (and OIGs) use risk scoring.
**High Risk Indicators:**
1.  **New Grantee** (No track record).
2.  **High Dollar Value** (>$1M / >$5M).
3.  **Prior Findings** (Late reports, disallowed costs, turnover).
4.  **Complexity** (Multiple subrecipients, international work, R&D).
5.  **Personnel Turnover** (CFO/PI left mid-grant).
6.  **Late/Inaccurate Reporting History.**
7.  **Media/Whistleblower Complaints.**

### 6.3 Surviving the Audit: The "Audit Ready" Protocol
**Phase 1: Notification (Don't Panic)**
*   Acknowledge receipt in writing (24 hrs).
*   Assign **Audit Liaison Officer (ALO)** (Senior Finance/Grants Manager).
*   Request **Entrance Conference** Agenda: Scope, Period, Auditors, Document Request List (DRL/BCL - "Prepared by Client List").

**Phase 2: Fieldwork (Control the Flow)**
*   **Centralized Document Portal:** SharePoint/OneDrive/Box/Data Room with **Permissioned Access** (Read-only for auditors, Edit for ALO).
*   **Do not email spreadsheets.** Upload to portal. Version control is critical.
*   **Daily Stand-ups (15 mins):** ALO + Lead Auditor. "What do you need today? What did you finish? Blockers?"
*   **The "No Surprises" Rule:** If you find an error *before* they do, disclose it immediately with a Corrective Action Plan (CAP). Voluntary disclosure reduces penalties (US: Safe Harbor; AU: Voluntary Disclosure).

**Phase 3: Findings Management**
*   **Finding Structure:** Condition (What is wrong) -> Criteria (Rule) -> Cause (Why) -> Effect ($ Impact / Risk) -> Recommendation.
*   **Response Timeline:** Usually 30 days for Draft Report response.
*   **CAP Template:**
    *   Finding Reference.
    *   Root Cause Analysis (5 Whys / Fishbone).
    *   Corrective Action (Specific, Measurable, Owner, Deadline).
    *   Preventive Action (Systemic fix: Policy change, System config, Training).
    *   Evidence of Implementation (Screenshot, New Policy Doc, Training Roster).

**Phase 4: Resolution**
*   **Management Decision Letter (Funder):** "Finding Sustained / Partially Sustained / Not Sustained."
*   **Questioned Costs:** Dollar amount in dispute.
*   **Disallowed Costs:** Final determination -> **Refund Check / Offset.**
*   **Closeout:** Ensure formal closeout letter received. Update SAM.gov / GrantConnect / Charity Register status.

## 7. The "Dirty Dozen": Most Frequent Compliance Failures & Mitigation Tactics

Based on 20+ years of OIG/GAO/ANAO/Charity Commission reports and Single Audit "Top Findings" lists.

### Failure 1: Inadequate Time & Effort Reporting (Personnel Costs)
*   **The Violation:** Estimating % effort ("I think I spent 50% on Grant A"). Using budget % instead of actuals. Missing signatures. Timesheets completed quarterly/annually.
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Policy:** "After-the-fact, per pay period, dual signature."
    *   **System:** Enforce via Payroll/HRIS lockout (no pay without approved timesheet).
    *   **Cost Share:** Track *identically*.
    *   **Semi-Annual Certification:** For "Institutional Base Salary" (IBS) vs. Grant Salary (NIH/NSF specific).

### Failure 2: Procurement Violations (Sole Source Abuse / Lack of Competition)
*   **The Violation:** Buying from "preferred vendor" without quotes. Splitting purchases to stay under micro-purchase threshold ($10k US). No written justification for Sole Source (>$250k US / >$80k AU).
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Procurement Matrix:** Embedded in ERP. Auto-routes based on $ value.
    *   **Sole Source Justification Form:** Mandatory fields: Unique Capability, Urgency (with proof), Patent/IP, Continuity. Signed by CFO *before* PO issued.
    *   **Debarment/Suspension Check (SAM.gov / National Registers):** **Mandatory** for every vendor >$25k (US) / every vendor (AU Govt). Save screenshot PDF in vendor folder.

### Failure 3: Unallowable Costs Charged to Grant
*   **Top Offenders:**
    *   **Alcohol / Entertainment / Lobbying.** (Strictly unallowable 2 CFR 200.423/425/450).
    *   **Fines & Penalties** (Late tax payment, parking tickets).
    *   **First Class / Business Class Airfare** (Unless policy allows & medically required).
    *   **Membership Fees** (Country clubs, lobbying orgs, individual professional dues unless org membership).
    *   **Contingency Reserves / "Slush Funds"** in budget.
    *   **Costs outside Period of Performance** (Pre-award costs without approval; Post-award spending after end date).
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Unallowable Cost Account Codes** in GL (e.g., 69900 "Unallowable - Lobbying"). Map to "Excluded from Grant" in reporting.
    *   **AP Approval Workflow:** "Grant Coding" field mandatory. System flags unallowable account codes on Grant Projects.
    *   **Pre-Payment Audit:** Sample 10% of invoices monthly for allowability.

### Failure 4: Indirect Cost (F&A) Rate Errors
*   **The Violation:** Using expired NICRA. Applying rate to wrong base (Total Direct Costs vs. MTDC). Charging indirect on Equipment/Participant Support/Subawards >$25k. Charging indirect on Cost Share.
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Rate Master Table** in ERP: Effective Date, Expiry Date, Base Type (MTDC/TDC/Salaries).
    *   **Automated Calculation:** System calculates IDC monthly based on *actual* MTDC incurred.
    *   **Reconciliation:** Quarterly: GL IDC Charge vs. System Calc. Adjust via Journal.

### Failure 5: Subrecipient Monitoring Failures (The "Pass-Through" Blind Spot)
*   **The Violation:** No risk assessment. No pre-award financial review. No during-award monitoring (desk reviews/site visits). No collection of *their* Single Audit reports. No Management Decision on *their* findings.
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Subrecipient Risk Assessment Tool:** Score on: Audit History, Financial Health, Internal Controls, Complexity, % of Budget.
    *   **Monitoring Plan:** High Risk = Site Visit + Quarterly Desk Review. Low Risk = Annual Desk Review + Audit Report Collection.
    *   **Flow-Down Clauses:** Ensure Sub-agreement includes *all* prime flow-downs (Audit, Lobbying, Trafficking, FFATA).
    *   **FFATA/FSRS Reporting (US):** Report sub-awards >$30k in FSRS by end of month +1. **Late filing = Finding.**

### Failure 6: Program Income Mismanagement
*   **The Violation:** Generating revenue (fees, sales, rent) from grant-funded assets/activities and keeping it in operating account without reporting/offsetting.
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Identify at Proposal:** "Will this generate income?"
    *   **Tracking:** Separate GL Revenue Code: "Program Income - Grant X".
    *   **Treatment Logic:** Deductive (Default US) -> Reduces outlay. Additive -> Increases budget. Matching -> Cost Share. **Get approval in writing.**

### Failure 7: Period of Performance Violations (The "Spend-It-or-Lose-It" Rush)
*   **The Violation:** Invoices dated *after* project end date. Salary for effort *after* end date. "Obligating" funds (signing PO) after end date (US: Obligation = Legal liability).
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Hard Close Calendar:** 90 Days Out: No new POs. 60 Days: Travel freeze. 30 Days: Final Payroll. 15 Days: Final AP Run. Day 0: GL Lock.
    *   **Accrual Discipline:** Accrue *known* obligations (goods received/services rendered) *before* end date. Reverse if invoice doesn't arrive.

### Failure 8: Equipment & Asset Management Lapses
*   **The Violation:** No tags. No serial numbers. Assets "walking" (theft/personal use). Disposing without funder approval (US: >$5k residual value = Federal share refund). Using grant equipment for other projects without pro-rata charging.
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Asset Tagging at Receiving Dock:** Barcode/QR code. Photo. Enter Register immediately.
    *   **Annual Physical Inventory:** Blind count (Counter doesn't see list). Reconcile variances.
    *   **Disposal Request Form:** Required for *any* disposal. Calculates Federal Share = (Federal % * Current Fair Market Value).

### Failure 9: Conflict of Interest (COI) & Related Party Transactions
*   **The Violation:** PI's spouse owns vendor. Board member's company gets contract. No disclosure. No mitigation plan.
*   **The Fix:**
    *   **Annual COI Disclosure:** Mandatory for all "Key Personnel" & Procurement Staff.
    *   **Vendor Master File:** "Related Party?" Flag. Triggers mandatory 3 quotes + Board Approval + Funder Prior Approval.
    *   **Organizational COI (OCI):** For R&D/Advisory grants. Mitigation: Firewalls, Exclusion from competitor data.
