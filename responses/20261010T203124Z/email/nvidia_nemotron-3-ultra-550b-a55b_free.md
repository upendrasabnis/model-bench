First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under the auspices of a local government authority are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) is a legally constituted entity that holds appropriate public liability insurance and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to submit a formal letter of support from the council confirming their willingness to act as auspice, along with a copy of their current certificate of currency for public liability insurance.

Second, the grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Given your described budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, your project appears to fall comfortably within this range.

Third, capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as water security installations and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category in the program guidelines. Please note, however, that ongoing operational costs (e.g., utilities, maintenance, or staff wages) are not funded.

Finally, in addition to the auspice letter and insurance certificate mentioned above, you should prepare: a simple project plan with timelines and risk management; two written quotes for each major infrastructure item; your most recent annual financial statement (or a treasurer’s report if unaudited); evidence of land tenure or a licence to occupy the garden site; and letters of support from at least two community stakeholders demonstrating the garden’s broader social impact.
