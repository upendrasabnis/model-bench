Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed description of your community garden’s plans. I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Community Resilience Grant so you can decide whether to proceed with a full submission.

**Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The grant program expressly allows applications from groups that are not incorporated charities, provided they operate under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible sponsor—such as a local council, incorporated association, or registered charity. Your arrangement, in which the council holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurances on your behalf, satisfies this requirement. You will need to submit a signed auspice agreement (or a letter of support) from the council confirming that they will act as the legal and financial sponsor for the grant, including responsibility for acquittal reporting.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant round offers funding ranging from a minimum of **$5,000** to a maximum of **$50,000** per project. There is no prescribed “target” amount; the assessment panel evaluates the budget against the proposed activities and the demonstrated need. Given your estimated turnover of $40,000, a request in the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be competitive, but you may apply for any amount within the stated limits that reflects the true cost of your rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure installation.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, drought‑tolerant planting, and related site works—is explicitly listed as an eligible expense. Both the purchase and installation of the tanks and the shade structure (including any necessary foundations, plumbing, and labor) can be included in your budget. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., utilities, staff wages) are not eligible unless they are a direct, demonstrable component of the resilience outcome.

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice letter/agreement** from the local council confirming sponsorship, insurance coverage, and willingness to manage grant funds.  
2. **Project plan** detailing the scope, timeline (including milestones for tank delivery, installation, and shade‑structure erection), and how the interventions will mitigate heat‑stress and water‑scarcity risks.  
3. **Detailed budget** (itemised) showing quotes or estimates for the rainwater tanks, pumps, fittings, shade‑structure materials, labor, and any ancillary costs (e.g., site preparation, permits).  
4. **Evidence of community need** – photographs of the garden, records of recent heat‑related impacts, membership or stall‑sales data demonstrating the garden’s role, and any letters of support from volunteers, council officers, or local residents.  
5. **Governance outline** – a brief description of how decisions are made within the volunteer group, how the council will oversee financial accountability, and how you will monitor and report on project outcomes.  
6. **Insurance certificates** (public liability and volunteer coverage) held by the council, or a statement confirming that such coverage is in place.
