Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

Firstly, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that it is a legally constituted entity with an ABN, holds appropriate public liability insurance, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to upload a signed Auspicing Agreement or a letter from the council confirming the arrangement, including their ABN and insurance details, as part of the application.

Regarding funding limits, the current round offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000. There is no minimum project size, but the total grant request must fall within this band. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to sit comfortably within the range, provided the costs are substantiated by quotes.

Infrastructure that directly supports climate adaptation and ongoing community use—such as water security assets and shade structures—is listed as an eligible capital cost in the program guidelines. However, routine maintenance, consumables, or assets that revert to private ownership are excluded. As your garden operates on council land for public benefit, the infrastructure you describe qualifies.

For supporting documents, please prepare: (1) the auspicing agreement/letter; (2) your group’s most recent financial statement or a simple income-and-expenditure summary showing the $40,000 turnover; (3) two written quotes for each major capital item; (4) a brief project plan with timeline, risk register, and evidence of community consultation; and (5) a letter of support from the council confirming land tenure and ongoing access.

I encourage you to review the full Program Guidelines and FAQ on our website, and please do not hesitate to contact me if you need further clarification as you develop your application.
