Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria for our Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate your proactive approach in ensuring that your project aligns with the program’s requirements before you commit to a full application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines expressly permit unincorporated community groups to apply, provided they are formally auspiced by a local government entity that can supply the necessary legal and insurance coverage. In your case, the fact that your council holds the relevant insurances on your behalf satisfies this requirement. You will, however, need to submit an “Auspice Agreement” or a letter of support from the council confirming that it will act as the administering body for any awarded funds. This document should outline the council’s responsibilities regarding financial management, reporting, and compliance with grant conditions.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, we assess each proposal against the scope of the proposed work, the demonstrated community benefit, and the proportion of total project costs that the grant would cover. Historically, awards have ranged from $5,000 to $50,000, but we are prepared to consider requests outside this band if the justification is compelling and the budget is realistic.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes, capital infrastructure that directly supports the sustainability and operational continuity of community initiatives is an eligible expense. Rainwater harvesting systems and weather‑resilient shade structures clearly fall within the “environmental resilience” component of the grant, provided they are essential to the garden’s ongoing function and are justified with technical specifications and cost breakdowns.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
When you move to the application stage, we recommend compiling the following materials:  

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, objectives, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget Table** – itemized costs for equipment, installation, and any ancillary expenses, with a clear indication of the grant request versus other funding sources.  
3. **Auspice Agreement / Council Support Letter** – confirming that the council will manage the funds and provide necessary insurance.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your volunteer team, governance structure, and any recent activities (annual report, newsletter excerpts, or social‑media engagement metrics).  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and cash‑flow summary).  
6. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from neighbouring community groups, local businesses, or council representatives.  
7. **Site Plans / Drawings** – schematic layouts of the proposed rainwater tank and shade structure installation.  
8. **Risk Assessment and Safety Plans** – documentation that the installation will meet occupational health and safety standards, again leveraging the council’s existing risk management framework where appropriate.

We strongly encourage you to review the full grant guidelines on our website (specifically the “Eligibility and Assessment” section) and to contact me directly if any aspect of the above remains unclear. Should you decide to proceed, we are happy to provide a brief pre‑application checklist or even schedule a short consultation to help refine your proposal.
