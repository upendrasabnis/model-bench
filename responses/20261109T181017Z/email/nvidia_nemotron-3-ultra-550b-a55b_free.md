Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in an application, and I am pleased to provide the following guidance addressing each of your questions.

**Auspicing Arrangements:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with a local council are eligible to apply, provided the council holds an Australian Business Number (ABN), appropriate public liability insurance, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to submit a signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming these arrangements as part of your application.

**Grant Amounts:** This round offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000. There is no minimum project size, but the requested amount must fall within this range. Your proposed budget of approximately $40,000 in annual turnover suggests a project within the mid-range would be appropriate, though you should request only the amount required to deliver the specific infrastructure.

**Eligible Costs:** Capital infrastructure that builds long-term community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible expenditure category. Costs may include materials, contractor labour, permits, and reasonable project management fees. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., utilities, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents:** In addition to the auspicing agreement, please prepare: a project plan with timelines and risk management; two quotes for each major procurement item; your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report; evidence of land tenure or council permission for the infrastructure; and letters of community support demonstrating the garden’s broader benefit.

The application portal opens on 1 May and closes 30 June. I recommend drafting your responses in a Word document first, as the portal does not auto-save. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require further clarification.
