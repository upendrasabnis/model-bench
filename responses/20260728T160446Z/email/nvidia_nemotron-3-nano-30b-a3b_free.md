Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant  

Dear Jamie,  

Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed description of your community garden project. I’m pleased to confirm that your group falls within the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant, and I hope the following information will help you prepare a strong application.  

1. **Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
   The grant program explicitly includes organisations that operate under the auspices of a local council, provided the council holds the necessary insurance and that the group can demonstrate a formal agreement (e.g., a memorandum of understanding) with the council confirming its role as the primary administrator. Your arrangement with the council meets this requirement.  

2. **Grant amount limits**  
   The current funding round offers grants ranging from **$5,000 to $25,000**. While there is no fixed minimum, projects that request less than $5,000 are still considered, but they must clearly demonstrate how the requested sum will achieve measurable resilience outcomes.  

3. **Eligible project costs**  
   Infrastructure that directly enhances climate‑related resilience is eligible, including the installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, solar panels, and related site works. However, the cost must be **capital‑based** and not primarily for routine operational expenses. Your proposed rainwater tanks and shade structure are therefore eligible cost categories.  

4. **Supporting documentation to prepare**  
   - A signed **Letter of Support** from the council confirming its endorsement and outlining the scope of the auspicing arrangement.  
   - A detailed **Project Plan** (timeline, milestones, and expected outcomes).  
   - A **Budget Breakdown** that itemises each cost category, including quotes or quotations for major items.  
   - Evidence of **financial capacity** (e.g., recent financial statements or a summary of your $40,000 annual turnover).  
   - Any required **insurance certificates** held by the council on your behalf.  

Please let us know if you would like templates for any of the above documents or if you have further questions as you develop your proposal. We are happy to provide feedback on draft applications before the submission deadline.  

We look forward to seeing your project succeed and to hearing about how the new infrastructure will help your garden continue to serve the community during hotter summers.
