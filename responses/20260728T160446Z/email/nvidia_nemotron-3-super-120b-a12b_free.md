Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I appreciate the time you are taking to confirm eligibility before preparing a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant is expressly designed to support projects that strengthen local capacity, and the program recognises that many valuable initiatives are delivered by informal or volunteer‑run groups. An unincorporated organisation that operates under the formal auspices of a local government body (such as a council) is eligible to apply, provided that the council signs the auspice agreement and assumes responsibility for the grant’s financial and reporting obligations. In practice, this means the council would be the legal applicant, while your garden group would be identified as the project‑delivery partner.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications are assessed on a merit‑based scale, and the amount awarded reflects the scope, budget, and anticipated impact of the proposed work. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure would comfortably sit within this range, assuming the total project cost does not exceed the maximum limit.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance community resilience — such as water‑harvesting systems, shade structures, renewable‑energy installations, or flood‑mitigation measures — are explicitly listed as eligible expenses. Both the purchase and installation of rainwater tanks and the construction of a shade structure qualify, provided they are essential to the project’s objectives and are supported by detailed quotes or supplier estimates.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please have the following ready:

1. **Auspice letter** – a signed statement from the local council confirming they will act as the legal applicant and will manage the grant funds on behalf of the garden group.  
2. **Project plan** – a clear description of the rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure works, including timelines, milestones, and how the infrastructure will improve resilience to hot summers.  
3. **Detailed budget** – itemised costs (including GST where applicable), supported by at least two written quotes for each major component.  
4. **Organisational information** – a brief outline of the garden’s governance (e.g., meeting minutes showing volunteer coordination), proof of the council’s insurance coverage, and a summary of your annual turnover (the $40,000 figure you mentioned).  
5. **Letters of support** – optional but recommended; any endorsements from nearby residents, local schools, or the council’s community‑development officer can demonstrate broader community benefit.  
6. **Risk management plan** – a short note on health‑and‑safety considerations for installation and ongoing maintenance.
