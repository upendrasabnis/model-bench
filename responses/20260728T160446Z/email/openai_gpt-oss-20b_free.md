**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups under council auspices**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to any organisation that can demonstrate a clear line of accountability for the use of grant funds, whether that is a registered charity, a community association, or an unincorporated group operating under the auspices of a local council. In your case, the council’s insurance and legal responsibility on your behalf is a strong indicator that you meet the accountability requirement. To satisfy the Grant Review Panel you will need to provide:  
- A written statement from the council confirming that it will oversee the project and that insurance coverage applies to the grant‑funded activities.  
- A copy of any formal agreement (e.g. a memorandum of understanding) that details the council’s role and the group’s responsibilities.

Your proposed infrastructure (rainwater tanks and shade structure) would comfortably fall within that range, provided the total project cost does not exceed $25,000.
