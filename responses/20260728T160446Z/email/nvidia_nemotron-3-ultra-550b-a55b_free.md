Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you taking the time to clarify these eligibility details before committing resources to a full application. Below, I address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing Arrangements and Eligibility**  
Unincorporated groups that operate under a formal auspicing agreement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., it is a not‑for‑profit entity, holds appropriate insurances, and can enter into a funding agreement on your behalf). Your council’s willingness to hold insurances and act as auspice satisfies this requirement. Please ensure you obtain a written letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, its duration, and the council’s acceptance of financial and legal responsibility for the grant.

**Grant Amount Limits**  
For this round, the Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should fall within this range. If your total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount and indicate additional funding sources in your budget.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat—is considered an eligible cost. Eligible expenses include purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not funded through this grant.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**  
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. **Auspicing Agreement / Council Letter of Support** – signed by an authorised council officer.
2. **Certificate of Currency** – for public liability and any other insurances held by the council on your behalf.
3. **Project Plan** – including objectives, timeline, risk management, and community benefit statement.
4. **Detailed Budget** – itemised quotes for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary costs (e.g., plumbing, engineering certification).
5. **Governance Documentation** – your group’s constitution or rules, minutes showing member endorsement of the project, and a list of current office‑bearers.
6. **Financial Statements** – a simple income and expenditure statement for the most recent financial year (showing the ~$40,000 turnover).
7. **Letters of Support** – from community partners, local businesses, or residents demonstrating demand for the garden’s continued operation.

**Next Steps**  
The application portal opens on 1 May and closes at 5 pm AEST on 31 May. I recommend drafting your responses in a Word document first, then transferring them to the online form. If you would like a brief pre‑submission review, please email a draft of your project plan and budget to grants@communityresilience.gov.au at least five business days before the deadline.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you need further clarification on any of the above. We are keen to support initiatives like yours that strengthen local food security and climate adaptation.
