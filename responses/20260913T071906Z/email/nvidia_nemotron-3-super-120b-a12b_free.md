**Auspice arrangements**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from unincorporated groups that are auspiced by a recognised legal entity, such as a local council. In your case, a formal letter of support from the council confirming that it will act as the auspicing body, accept financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds, and maintain the required public liability and volunteer insurance on your behalf will satisfy the auspice requirement. The letter should be on council letterhead, signed by an authorised officer, and include the council’s ABN and contact details.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant operates within a range of **AU$5,000 to AU$50,000** per successful applicant. There is no mandatory minimum turnover for applicants; however, the assessment panel does look for evidence that the organisation can manage the proposed budget responsibly. Your reported annual turnover of approximately AU$40,000 comfortably falls within the scope of projects previously funded under this program.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience—such as rainwater harvesting tanks, shade structures, permeable paving, or water‑saving irrigation—is considered an eligible cost, provided the items are essential to the project’s objectives and are quoted at market rates. Operational expenses (e.g., staff wages, ongoing utilities) are not eligible unless they are demonstrably linked to the installation and commissioning of the infrastructure.
