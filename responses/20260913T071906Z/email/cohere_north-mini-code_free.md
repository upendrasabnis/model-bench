**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. Unincorporated groups are eligible provided that they have a clearly identified “responsible entity” that can enter into a grant agreement, manage funds, and assume accountability for reporting and compliance. In practice, many groups operate under the auspices of a local council, which can act as the fiscal sponsor and hold the required insurances on behalf of the group. If your council agrees to be the named recipient of the grant and to manage the funds in trust for your garden, your group will meet the eligibility criteria. It is helpful to attach a letter from the council confirming this arrangement, together with any governing documents that outline the council’s authority to administer the grant on your behalf.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, we assess each proposal against the program’s strategic objectives, the scale of the proposed work, and the availability of funds in the current round. Historically, awards have ranged from approximately $5,000 to $50,000, although larger projects that demonstrate strong community impact and robust feasibility may be considered for higher amounts. Your projected budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should be aligned with the overall scope of work, and we encourage you to propose a amount that reflects the full cost of delivering the infrastructure while remaining within the overall funding envelope for the round.

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Infrastructure that directly supports the resilience and sustainability of community initiatives is expressly eligible under the “Environmental Improvement” and “Climate Adaptation” categories of the grant. Rainwater tanks are viewed favourably because they reduce reliance on municipal water supplies, mitigate the impact of drought, and support the garden’s year‑round operation during hot summers. Similarly, a well‑designed shade structure that protects both produce and volunteers from extreme heat aligns with the program’s goal of enhancing community capacity to cope with climate‑related challenges. To strengthen your case, please quantify the expected water‑saving benefits, provide lifecycle cost estimates, and include drawings or mock‑ups that illustrate how the infrastructure will be integrated into the garden’s layout.

**Recommended supporting documents**  
When you prepare your full application, we suggest compiling the following materials:

1. ** organisational overview** – a one‑page description of the garden’s mission, history, and volunteer base, together with the council’s auspice letter.  
2. **Financial documentation** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and cash‑flow summary). Include a breakdown of the $40,000 turnover, highlighting revenue sources and any existing reserves.  
3. **Project plan and timeline** – a detailed description of the rainwater‑tank installation, shade‑structure design, implementation phases, and expected completion dates.  
4. **Budget and cost breakdown** – itemised estimates for equipment, installation, permits, and any ongoing maintenance costs, with quotations where possible.  
5. **Risk assessment and insurance** – confirmation that the council’s insurance covers the project activities, plus any additional coverage you may require.  
6. **Letters of support** – endorsements from local businesses, neighbouring community groups, or council representatives that attest to the broader community benefit.  
7. **Monitoring and evaluation plan** – clear metrics (e.g., water usage reduction, increased production during hot periods) that will be used to measure success.  
8. **Bank account details and tax identification** – to facilitate disbursement of funds, should your application be successful.

Finally, please note that the grant application portal will open two weeks before the closing date, and we strongly recommend submitting your application well in advance of the deadline to allow time for any additional clarification we may request.
