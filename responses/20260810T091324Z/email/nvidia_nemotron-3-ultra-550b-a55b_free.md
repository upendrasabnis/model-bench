Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, hold the relevant insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation, etc.), and sign the funding agreement on your behalf. You will need to provide a signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming their willingness to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. Please note that we rarely fund 100 per cent of project costs; a cash or in-kind contribution from your group or the council (volunteer labour, donated materials, etc.) is looked upon favourably and should be documented in your budget.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience—such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures—is an eligible expenditure category. Costs must be for purchase and installation only; ongoing maintenance, operational expenses, or movable equipment (e.g., garden tools) are not covered. Quotes must be from licensed contractors and itemised.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**
1. Council auspicing agreement/letter (on letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).
2. Current public liability insurance certificate (held by council).
3. Two itemised quotes for each infrastructure component.
4. Simple project plan with timeline, risk management, and volunteer safety protocols.
5. Most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover.
6. Evidence of land tenure or council permission to install infrastructure on the site.
7. Letters of community support (minimum two) demonstrating broader benefit.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November 2025. I strongly recommend drafting your responses in the online portal well before the deadline and contacting me if you require clarification on any budget line or eligibility detail.
