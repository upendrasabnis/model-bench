Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed overview of your community garden project. I’m pleased to provide clarification on each of the points you raised so that you can move forward with confidence.  

**1. Eligibility of groups auspiced by a council**  
Our funding guidelines expressly include “unincorporated associations or groups that are formally auspiced by a local government authority” as eligible applicants, provided that the auspicing body can supply the required proof of insurance, financial oversight, and a letter of support confirming the project’s alignment with council objectives. Since your garden operates under the umbrella of your local council and the council holds the necessary public liability insurance on your behalf, you meet the eligibility criteria. We will still need a copy of the council’s insurance certificate and a signed endorsement letter from the council’s community‑services officer.  

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
For the current round, the grant ceiling is **$25,000** per project, and the minimum award is **$2,000**. Projects that request less than $5,000 are still welcome, but they must demonstrate a clear leverage of additional resources (e.g., in‑kind contributions or volunteer labour). Your proposed works fit comfortably within the permissible range.  

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience of community facilities is an eligible expense. Rain‑water harvesting systems, shade structures, and associated plumbing or engineering works are covered, provided they are:  
- Integral to the operation of the garden (e.g., essential for irrigation or participant comfort),  
- Documented in a detailed project budget, and  
- Supported by quotes or tenders from approved suppliers/contractors.  

**4. Recommended supporting documentation**  
To ensure a smooth assessment, please prepare the following:  
- A signed **Letter of Support** from the council confirming the auspice arrangement and insurance coverage.  
- The council’s **Public Liability Insurance Certificate** (or a summary thereof).  
- A **Project Proposal** (max 2 pages) outlining the objectives, timeline, and expected community benefits.  
- **Itemised quotations** for the rain‑water tanks, shade structure, and any related installation works.  
- A **Budget Sheet** showing total project costs, the requested grant amount, and any matched funding or in‑kind contributions.  
- Evidence of **annual turnover** (e.g., audited financial statements or bank statements) to substantiate the $40,000 figure.  
- A brief **risk‑management plan** addressing safety, maintenance, and sustainability after the grant period.
