UNDERSTAND THE FUNDER LANDSCAPE
IDENTIFY FUNDER TYPES: GOVERNMENT, FOUNDATION, CORPORATE, INDIVIDUAL

- LIST TOP 20 POTENTIAL FUNDERS

SECTION 3: CRAFTING A COMPELLING NEEDS STATEMENT
DEFINE THE PROBLEM WITH DATA
USE QUANTITATIVE AND QUALITATIVE EVIDENCE
CONNECT LOCAL CONTEXT TO BROADER TRENDS
ARTICULATE THE GAP YOUR PROJECT FILLS
AVOID JARGON; SPEAK TO HUMAN IMPACT
EXAMPLE NEEDS STATEMENT
IN THE CITY OF RIVERBEND, 38% OF RESIDENTS LIVE IN FOOD DESERTS, ACCORDING TO THE 2023 USDA FOOD ACCESS RESEARCH ATLAS. CHILDREN IN THESE AREAS EXPERIENCE A 27% HIGHER RATE OF OBESITY COMPARED TO CITY‑WIDE AVERAGES (CDC, 2022). DESPITE EXISTING COMMUNITY GARDENS, ONLY 12% OF LOW‑INCOME HOUSEHOLDS HAVE RELIABLE ACCESS TO FRESH PRODUCE. OUR PROPOSED MOBILE MARKET WILL BRIDGE THIS GAP BY DELIVERING AFFORDABLE, CULTURALLY RELEVANT FRUITS AND VEGETABLES DIRECTLY TO NEIGHBORHOOD HUBS.
CHECKLIST: NEEDS STATEMENT
- CITE AT LEAST THREE REPUTABLE DATA SOURCES
- INCLUDE COMMUNITY VOICES OR TESTIMONIALS
- SHOW CLEAR CAUSAL LINK BETWEEN PROBLEM AND PROPOSED SOLUTION
- LIMIT TO ONE PAGE OR 300 WORDS
- REVIEW WITH PROGRAM STAFF FOR ACCURACY
- ENSURE ALIGNMENT WITH FUNDER’S PRIORITY LANGUAGE

SECTION 4: DEVELOPING GOALS, OBJECTIVES, AND OUTCOMES
WRITE SMART GOALS: SPECIFIC, MEASURABLE, ACHIEVABLE, RELEVANT, TIME‑BOUND
DISTINGUISH BETWEEN OUTPUTS (ACTIVITIES) AND OUTCOMES (CHANGES)
USE A LOGIC MODEL TO MAP THEORY OF CHANGE
EXAMPLE LOGIC MODEL COMPONENTS
INPUTS: STAFF, VOLUNTEERS, VEHICLE, PRODUCE SUPPLY CONTRACTS
ACTIVITIES: WEEKLY MOBILE MARKET STOPS, NUTRITION WORKSHOPS, COMMUNITY OUTREACH
OUTPUTS: 48 MARKET DAYS PER YEAR, 1,200 WORKSHOP ATTENDEES, 5,000 POUNDS OF PRODUCE DISTRIBUTED
SHORT‑TERM OUTCOMES: INCREASED KNOWLEDGE OF NUTRITION, IMPROVED ACCESS TO FRESH FOOD
INTERMEDIATE OUTCOMES: HIGHER FRUIT/VEGETABLE CONSUMPTION, REDUCED FOOD INSECURITY SCORES
LONG‑TERM IMPACT: LOWER CHILDHOOD OBESITY RATES, STRONGER COMMUNITY COHESION
CHECKLIST: GOALS & LOGIC MODEL
- LIST ALL INPUTS WITH QUANTITIES AND COSTS
- MAP EACH ACTIVITY TO AT LEAST ONE OUTPUT
- DEFINE INDICATORS FOR EVERY OUTCOME LEVEL
- ASSIGN RESPONSIBLE STAFF AND TIMELINES
- VALIDATE LOGIC MODEL WITH EXTERNAL EVALUATOR
- ENSURE EACH GOAL TIES BACK TO NEEDS STATEMENT

SECTION 5: DESIGNING THE PROJECT PLAN AND TIMELINE
BREAK PROJECT INTO PHASES: PLANNING, IMPLEMENTATION, MONITORING, CLOSEOUT
CREATE A GANTT CHART OR MILESTONE TABLE
BUILD IN CONTINGENCY BUFFERS (10‑15% TIME)
ALIGN MILESTONES WITH FUNDER REPORTING DATES
EXAMPLE MILESTONE TABLE
MONTH 1‑2: STAFF HIRING, VEHICLE PURCHASE, PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENTS
MONTH 3: LAUNCH FIRST MARKET STOP, BASELINE SURVEY DEPLOYMENT
MONTH 4‑6: WEEKLY MARKETS, MONTHLY WORKSHOPS, DATA COLLECTION
MONTH 7: MID‑TERM EVALUATION REPORT TO FUNDER
MONTH 8‑11: CONTINUE OPERATIONS, ADJUST BASED ON FEEDBACK
MONTH 12: FINAL DATA ANALYSIS, IMPACT REPORT, SUSTAINABILITY PLAN
CHECKLIST: PROJECT PLAN
- IDENTIFY CRITICAL PATH ACTIVITIES
- ASSIGN OWNER AND DEADLINE FOR EACH TASK
- INCLUDE PROCUREMENT LEAD TIMES
- SCHEDULE REGULAR TEAM CHECK‑INS
- DOCUMENT RISK MITIGATION STRATEGIES
- ALIGN WITH FUNDER’S REQUIRED DELIVERABLES

SECTION 6: BUDGET DEVELOPMENT BEST PRACTICES
USE FUNDER’S BUDGET TEMPLATE IF PROVIDED
SEPARATE DIRECT AND INDIRECT COSTS
JUSTIFY EVERY LINE ITEM WITH NARRATIVE
INCLUDE IN‑KIND CONTRIBUTIONS AND MATCHING FUNDS
APPLY REALISTIC FRINGE BENEFIT RATES
PLAN FOR CONTINGENCY (5‑10% OF TOTAL DIRECT COSTS)
EXAMPLE BUDGET SNAPSHOT (ANNUAL)

SUPPLIES: PRODUCE PURCHASE $45,000, MARKETING MATERIALS $3,200
CONTRACTUAL: NUTRITIONIST CONSULTANT $15,000
INDIRECT COSTS (10% MTDC): $19,454
TOTAL REQUESTED: $259,994
MATCHING FUNDS: CITY GRANT $50,000, IN‑KIND VOLUNTEER HOURS $12,000
CHECKLIST: BUDGET
- VERIFY ALL CALCULATIONS WITH SPREADSHEET FORMULAS
- ENSURE BUDGET NARRATIVE EXPLAINS EACH CATEGORY
- CONFIRM ALIGNMENT WITH FUNDER’S ALLOWABLE COSTS
- INCLUDE SUSTAINABILITY FUNDING PLAN POST‑GRANT
- REVIEW WITH FINANCE OFFICER FOR COMPLIANCE
- ATTACH SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS (LEASE, QUOTES)

SECTION 7: WRITING THE NARRATIVE: STRUCTURE AND STYLE
FOLLOW FUNDER’S REQUIRED SECTIONS EXACTLY
USE CLEAR HEADINGS THAT MIRROR RFP OUTLINE
WRITE IN ACTIVE VOICE, CONCISE SENTENCES
INTEGRATE DATA VISUALS: CHARTS, MAPS, INFOGRAPHICS
TELL A STORY: PROBLEM → SOLUTION → IMPACT
EXAMPLE OPENING PARAGRAPH
"CITY GREEN’S MOBILE MARKET INITIATIVE WILL TRANSFORM FOOD ACCESS IN RIVERBEND’S MOST UNDERSERVED NEIGHBORHOODS BY DELIVERING FRESH, AFFORDABLE PRODUCE DIRECTLY TO COMMUNITY HUBS, COUPLED WITH CULTURALLY TAILORED NUTRITION EDUCATION. BUILDING ON OUR 2022 PILOT THAT REACHED 1,200 RESIDENTS, THIS PROJECT SCALES TO 5,000 POUNDS OF PRODUCE ANNUALLY, TARGETING A 15% REDUCTION IN HOUSEHOLD FOOD INSECURITY WITHIN TWO YEARS."
CHECKLIST: NARRATIVE QUALITY
- MATCH EVERY RFP QUESTION WITH A DEDICATED SECTION
- LIMIT JARGON; DEFINE ACRONYMS ON FIRST USE
- USE BULLET POINTS FOR READABILITY WHERE APPROPRIATE
- INCLUDE AT LEAST ONE QUOTE FROM COMMUNITY MEMBER
- PROOFREAD FOR GRAMMAR, CONSISTENCY, AND WORD COUNT
- HAVE AT LEAST TWO INTERNAL REVIEWERS SIGN OFF

ESTABLISH BASELINE, MIDLINE, ENDLINE DATA POINTS
ASSIGN EVALUATION RESPONSIBILITY (INTERNAL OR EXTERNAL)

3. WHAT CHANGES IN CHILDHOOD OBESITY RATES ARE OBSERVED IN SERVICE AREAS AFTER TWO YEARS?
CHECKLIST: EVALUATION PLAN
- LIST ALL INDICATORS WITH DATA SOURCES AND FREQUENCY
- SPECIFY SAMPLING STRATEGY AND SAMPLE SIZE CALCULATIONS
- INCLUDE DATA MANAGEMENT AND PRIVACY PROTOCOLS
- BUDGET FOR EVALUATION ACTIVITIES (STAFF, TOOLS, ANALYSIS)
- PLAN FOR DISSEMINATION OF FINDINGS TO FUNDER AND COMMUNITY

SECTION 11: COMMON PITFALLS AND HOW TO AVOID THEM
MISALIGNMENT WITH FUNDER PRIORITIES → CONDUCT DEEP RESEARCH AND TAILOR LANGUAGE
OVERPROMISING OUTCOMES → SET REALISTIC, EVIDENCE‑BASED TARGETS
INCOMPLETE BUDGET JUSTIFICATION → PROVIDE DETAILED NARRATIVE FOR EVERY LINE
NEGLECTING EVALUATION → INTEGRATE EVALUATION FROM DAY ONE
MISSING DEADLINES → USE AUTOMATED CALENDAR REMINDERS AND BUFFER TIME
POOR STORYTELLING → INCORPORATE HUMAN‑CENTERED NARRATIVES AND VISUALS
IGNORING INDIRECT COST POLICIES → VERIFY FUNDER’S RATE AND APPLY CONSISTENTLY
LACK OF SUSTAINABILITY PLAN → OUTLINE FUTURE FUNDING SOURCES AND REVENUE MODELS
CHECKLIST: PITFALL PREVENTION
- RUN A “RED TEAM” REVIEW WITH EXTERNAL PEER
- COMPARE APPLICATION AGAINST FUNDER’S SCORING RUBRIC IF AVAILABLE
- VALIDATE ALL DATA POINTS WITH SOURCE DOCUMENTS
- ENSURE ALL REQUIRED SIGNATURES ARE COLLECTED EARLY
- CONDUCT A MOCK SUBMISSION TO TEST PORTAL FUNCTIONALITY
