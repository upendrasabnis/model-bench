Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. It is encouraging to hear about the valuable work your community garden is undertaking, and I am pleased to provide clarification on the eligibility criteria and application requirements you have raised.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are absolutely eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, hold the grant agreement, and assume full financial and legal responsibility for the project. Your council’s willingness to hold insurances and act as auspice is a strong foundation. Please ensure the application clearly outlines the governance structure, including a signed Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) or Auspicing Agreement between the garden group and the council that specifies roles, reporting lines, and financial management arrangements.

**Grant Amount Parameters**
For this round, the Community Resilience Grant offers funding between **$5,000 and $50,000** (GST exclusive). There is no requirement for matched funding, though evidence of in-kind contributions or community support is viewed favourably during assessment. Your project scope appears to sit comfortably within this range.

**Eligible Infrastructure Costs**
Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience to climate impacts—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures to mitigate heat—is explicitly listed as an eligible expenditure category. Eligible costs include purchase of tanks, pumps, plumbing, footings, shade sails or structural frames, and professional installation fees. Operational costs (e.g., ongoing water rates) and routine maintenance are not eligible.

**Supporting Documentation to Prepare**
To streamline your application, I recommend gathering the following now:
1.  **Auspicing Agreement/MOU** signed by both parties.
2.  **Council’s ABN, incorporation certificate, and current public liability insurance certificate of currency.**
3.  **Project budget** (GST exclusive) with at least two quotes for major items (tanks, structure).
4.  **Site plan/photos** showing proposed tank and shade structure locations.
5.  **Evidence of land tenure/permission** (e.g., lease, licence, or letter from landowner/council).
6.  **Governance documents** for the garden group (constitution, committee list, minutes authorising the application).
7.  **Financial records** (simple income/expenditure statement for the last financial year) to demonstrate financial viability.
8.  **Letters of support** from community stakeholders or partner organisations.

Applications close at 5:00 PM AEST on the published closing date next month via the SmartyGrants portal. I strongly encourage you to start a draft application early to allow time for the council’s finance and governance teams to review the submission before lodgement.
