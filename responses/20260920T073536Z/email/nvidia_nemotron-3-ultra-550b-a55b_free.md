Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility details before investing time in an application—this is exactly the kind of due diligence that strengthens the overall process. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local council are eligible to apply, provided the council holds appropriate public liability insurance and is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. You will need to supply a signed letter from an authorised council officer confirming the auspicing arrangement, the council’s ABN, and evidence of current insurance coverage that extends to your garden’s activities.

Second, the grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. With an annual turnover of approximately $40,000, your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure falls comfortably within this range. Please note that requests must represent no more than 80 % of total project costs; the remaining 20 % can be met through in-kind contributions, volunteer labour (valued at $30 per hour), or other cash sources.

Third, infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience—such as water-harvesting systems and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat—is explicitly listed as an eligible capital cost in the program guidelines. Installation labour, engineering certification, and necessary permits are also allowable, whereas ongoing maintenance or operational expenses are not.

Finally, to streamline your application, please prepare the following supporting documents: (1) the council auspicing letter described above; (2) your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report; (3) a simple project plan with timelines, risk management, and a detailed budget showing quotes for tanks and shade materials; (4) evidence of land tenure or council permission to install permanent infrastructure on the site; and (5) letters of support from at least two community stakeholders demonstrating broader benefit.

The application portal opens on 1 June and closes at 5 pm AEST on 30 June. I strongly recommend drafting your responses in a Word document first, as the portal times out after 60 minutes of inactivity. If you would like, I can arrange a brief 15-minute video call next week to walk through the budget template or answer any follow-up questions.

We value the work your garden does for community cohesion and food security, and I look forward to potentially supporting this important resilience project.
