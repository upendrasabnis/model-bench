Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant and for taking the time to clarify the eligibility requirements before preparing a full application. I have addressed each of your questions below.

**1. Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The grant programme does accept applications from unincorporated community groups that are auspiced by an eligible entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. To qualify under this arrangement you must provide a formal letter of auspice from the council confirming that:  
- the council agrees to act as the legal and financial sponsor for the project;  
- the council will hold any funds awarded and will be responsible for acquittal reporting; and  
- the council’s public liability and other relevant insurances extend to the activities undertaken by the garden group.  

If the council can supply such a letter, your group meets the eligibility criterion.

**2. Grant amount limits**  
For the current funding round the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exempt). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated turnover of approximately $40,000, a request for the full $50,000 would be permissible, provided the budget is justified and demonstrates sound financial management.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that enhance the resilience of community‑based facilities are expressly eligible. Rainwater‑harvesting tanks, associated plumbing, and shade structures that mitigate heat‑stress and enable continued operation during extreme weather events fall within the scope of allowable expenditures. Please ensure that any proposed infrastructure is:  
- directly linked to the stated resilience outcome (e.g., reduced water‑use vulnerability, improved participant safety during heatwaves);  
- supported by itemised quotes or vendor estimates; and  
- compliant with any relevant local building or planning approvals (if required).  

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:  

- **Auspice letter** from the local council (signed and dated).  
- **Proof of council insurance** covering the garden’s activities (certificate of currency).  
- **Financial statements** for the past 12 months (profit‑and‑loss, balance sheet, or a simple cash‑flow summary) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover.  
- **Project plan** outlining the objectives, timeline, milestones, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will increase resilience.  
- **Detailed budget** with line‑item costs, including quotes for tanks, pumps, fittings, shade‑structure materials, and installation labour.  
- **Risk assessment / safety plan** addressing any occupational health and safety considerations for the construction phase.  
- **Letters of support** (optional but helpful) from community members, local schools, or other stakeholders who benefit from the garden’s produce stall.  

All documents should be uploaded as PDFs via the online grant portal; the system accepts files up to 10 MB each.
