Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in detail.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and assume full financial and legal responsibility for the grant. As your local council already holds the relevant insurances, they would need to submit the application on your behalf and provide a letter of support confirming the auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and evidence of their public liability and workers’ compensation coverage.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your projected project scope appears to fall comfortably within this band. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some level of co-contribution (cash or in-kind) equivalent to at least 10% of the total project value, although this can be waived for groups in designated disadvantaged regional areas—your town may qualify, so please check the postcode eligibility list in the guidelines.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly builds climate resilience is a priority for this program. Rainwater harvesting systems (tanks, pumps, first-flush diverters, and associated plumbing) and permanent shade structures engineered to relevant Australian Standards are explicitly listed as eligible expenditure. Ineligible costs include routine maintenance, consumables, and any infrastructure not directly tied to the stated resilience outcomes.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:** To streamline the process, the council should gather the following ahead of the application portal opening:
1. A signed auspicing agreement or Memorandum of Understanding between the garden group and the council.
2. Council’s current Certificate of Currency for Public Liability ($20M minimum) and Workers’ Compensation.
3. A detailed project budget with at least two quotes for each major line item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
4. A simple project plan showing timelines, risk management, and how the infrastructure will extend operating hours during extreme heat.
5. Evidence of community need—letters of support from local health services, the shire’s heat‑health plan, or member surveys.
6. Your group’s most recent financial statement (even if unaudited) showing the $40,000 turnover, plus a brief narrative on financial sustainability.

Once the council has these documents, the online application form will guide you through the merit criteria: community benefit, climate resilience impact, value for money, and capacity to deliver.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you need clarification on any of the above or would like a brief pre‑application phone call. We look forward to receiving a strong proposal from your community.
