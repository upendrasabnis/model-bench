Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the vital work you and your garden team are doing in your regional community. I’m happy to clarify a few key points about the Community Resilience Grant (CRG) to help you decide whether a full application is the right next step.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups supported by a council**  
The CRG is open to any community organisation that can demonstrate a clear, community‑benefiting project. Unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of a local council are eligible **provided** the council can act as a statutory guarantor. In practice this means the council must:  
- Hold all statutory insurance (liability, public liability, etc.) on behalf of the garden; and  
- Be willing to sign a “Council Sponsorship Agreement” that confirms Exit/Continuity responsibilities for the project.  

If your council is prepared to sign this agreement, you can proceed as an eligible applicant. If you’re unsure whether the council is willing, it may be worth arranging a brief meeting with the council’s community services officer to confirm their support.

**2. Grant funding limits**  
The CRG offers award ranges from $5,000 to $25,000. The exact amount is determined by the complexity of the project and the total cost of eligible items. Because your project involves installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure, you can expect an award somewhere in the middle of that range—provided you supply a detailed cost breakdown. We will review the application against the available funding pool; if the total cost is $30,000, we would consider a proportional award up to the maximum of $25,000.

**3. Eligible infrastructure costs**  
Yes, infrastructure projects such as rainwater tanks, shade structures, fencing, and associated civil works are **fully eligible** under the CRG. They must be directly related to building resilience against extreme weather, improving the garden’s operational continuity, and enhancing community well‑being. Installation, maintenance and any necessary engineering consultation are also covered.

**4. Supporting documentation you should prepare**  
To streamline the application process, gather the following documents:

| Document | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Council Sponsorship Agreement** | Confirms council’s statutory responsibility and insurance coverage. |
| **Financial statements** (last 12‑month) | Demonstrates turnover, sources of income, and financial sustainability. |
| **Business plan / project proposal** | Outline of objectives, timeline, and impact assessment. |
| **Detailed cost estimate** | Itemised list of the rainwater tank(s), shade structure, labor, permits, and contingency. |
| **Insurance certificates** | Proof of public liability and any other required Manche. |
| **Community impact statement** | Evidence of community benefit, involvement, volunteer hours. |
| **Photographs of current garden** | Baseline visual context for the proposed upgrades. |
| **Council resolution or minutes** | Official endorsement or approval of the project. |
