1. **Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
   The Community Resilience Grant program permits applications from unincorporated entities that operate under the auspices of a local council, provided the council assumes the necessary insurance and governance responsibilities on their behalf. To meet this condition, you must supply a copy of the council’s endorsement letter and a recent council‑issued insurance certificate confirming coverage for the activities you intend to fund.  

2. **Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
   The current funding round allocates grants ranging from **$5,000 to $50,000**. Applications for amounts outside this band are not considered, although you may submit a request for any figure within the range that aligns with your project budget.  

3. **Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
   Yes, the installation of rainwater tanks, shade structures, and associated site works are classified as eligible infrastructure expenses, provided they directly enhance the garden’s capacity to withstand climate‑related stressors (e.g., heat, drought). The infrastructure must be new, permanent, and tied to the specific resilience outcomes outlined in the grant guidelines.  

4. **Supporting documentation to prepare**  
   - **Council endorsement letter** confirming the auspice arrangement and insurance coverage.  
   - **Proof of insurance** (policy number, insurer, and coverage limits).  
   - **Project budget breakdown** detailing the requested grant amount and how it will be allocated to eligible costs.  
   - **Site plan or schematic** illustrating the placement of the rainwater tanks and shade structure.  
   - **Evidence of annual turnover** (e.g., audited financial statements or bank statements) demonstrating that the garden’s revenue falls within the $40,000 ± 10 % range.  
   - **Letters of support** from community stakeholders or council representatives that attest to the project’s local impact.
