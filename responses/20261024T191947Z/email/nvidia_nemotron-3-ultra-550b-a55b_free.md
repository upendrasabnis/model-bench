Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local council are eligible to apply, provided the council holds the relevant insurances and enters into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. The council would act as the legal entity receiving the funds and assumes full financial and reporting accountability for the grant. You will need to supply a signed letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and evidence of their current public liability and workers’ compensation insurance policies.

Second, the grant round has a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to fall comfortably within this range. Please note that requests for the maximum amount are highly competitive and require a particularly strong demonstration of community impact and value for money.

Third, capital infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category in the program guidelines. Eligible expenditure includes purchase of materials, contractor labour, necessary permits, and reasonable project management fees (capped at 10 % of the total grant). Ongoing operational costs, such as water bills or garden maintenance, are not eligible.

Finally, to streamline your application preparation, I recommend gathering the following supporting documents now:
1. Council auspicing letter and insurance certificates (as above).
2. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 annual turnover.
3. Quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure item.
4. A simple project plan with timeline, risk register, and sustainability strategy.
5. Letters of support from at least two community partners or stakeholder groups.
6. Evidence of land tenure or a licence to occupy the garden site for a minimum of five years.

The guidelines, application form, and a budget template are available on our website. Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November 2024. Should you require further clarification as you develop your submission, please do not hesitate to contact me directly.
