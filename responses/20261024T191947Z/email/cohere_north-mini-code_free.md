**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
Our grant programme welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. Unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of a local council are indeed eligible, provided the council can confirm that it will act as the primary point of contact for the grant, hold any required insurance, and assume financial responsibility for the awarded funds. In practice, we require a formal letter of support (or an official council resolution) that outlines the council’s endorsement of the project and its willingness to act as the fiscal agent if the application is successful.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award. Instead, we assess proposals on their merit, community impact, and alignment with the programme’s objectives. Typical awards range from $5,000 to $50,000, but we have funded projects both below and above this band in previous rounds. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appear well within the usual scope of funding.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes, capital infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is fully eligible, as these assets directly enhance the garden’s resilience to climate‑related stresses. We do, however, require that all equipment and installations comply with relevant Australian standards and local building regulations. If any specialist contractors are involved, please ensure they provide quotes that break down labour, materials, and any permits.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
When you begin drafting the full application, we recommend assembling the following documentation:

1. **Project description and outcomes** – a clear narrative linking the infrastructure to resilience benefits.  
2. **Budget breakdown** – detailed quotes for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary costs (e.g., permits, contingency).  
3. **Financial statements** – the most recent two years of turnover and expense records (your $40,000 annual turnover will be useful for demonstrating community need).  
4. **Letter of support from the council** – confirming auspice status, insurance coverage, and fiscal responsibility.  
5. **Risk assessment and safety plan** – particularly important for water storage and structural installations.  
6. **Volunteer roster and governance outline** – even though you are unincorporated, demonstrating a structured volunteer base strengthens the application.  

We strongly advise that you submit a draft application to our grants team at least two weeks before the closing date. This allows us to review your proposal, ensure all eligibility criteria are met, and provide any final adjustments.
