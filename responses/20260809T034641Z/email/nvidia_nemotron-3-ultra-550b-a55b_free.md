Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am pleased to address each of your questions in detail below.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups**
Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local council are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., is a legal entity with an ABN, holds appropriate insurances, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant). Your council’s willingness to hold insurances and act as the legal contracting party satisfies this requirement. You will need to submit a signed Auspicing Agreement or a letter from the council confirming the arrangement, including their ABN and contact details for the authorised signatory.

**Grant Amount Limits**
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. If the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you demonstrate confirmed co-contributions (cash or in-kind) for the balance.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts and ensure continued operation during extreme weather events align squarely with the program’s objectives. Eligible expenses include purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation or plumbing/electrical work. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not fundable.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Council auspicing letter/Agreement (as above).
2. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
3. Quotes (minimum two per major item) for tanks, shade structure, and installation.
4. Project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and evidence of community need (e.g., temperature data, usage statistics).
5. Letters of support from partner organisations or community members.
6. Council confirmation of land tenure/permission for the infrastructure.
7. Insurance certificates of currency (public liability, volunteer/worker’s compensation) held by the council on your behalf.

Please ensure all documents are current (dated within the last six months) and clearly labelled. The application portal opens on 1 June and closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I encourage you to start drafting in the portal early; you can save and return as needed.
