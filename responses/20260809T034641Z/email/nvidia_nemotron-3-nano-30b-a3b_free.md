1. **Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
   The Community Resilience Grant explicitly welcomes applications from unincorporated community groups that are formally auspiced by a local government authority, provided the auspicing body holds the requisite insurance and can demonstrate financial oversight. In such cases the grant application should be submitted in the name of the auspicing council, with a clear statement that the council acts on behalf of the volunteer group. This arrangement satisfies the “legal entity” requirement while preserving the independence of your garden volunteers.

2. **Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
   For the current funding round the grant ceiling is **$25,000** per project, and the minimum award is **$2,000**. Projects that request amounts below the minimum are still eligible, but they may receive a lower priority score in the evaluation matrix. Your proposed investment in rain‑water tanks and a shade structure falls comfortably within the $2,000–$25,000 band, so you may submit a request up to the full $25,000 if your cost estimate supports it.

3. **Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
   Infrastructure that enhances the long‑term resilience of a community asset is an allowable expense. Both rain‑water harvesting systems and shade structures are classified as “critical infrastructure” under the grant’s eligibility criteria, provided they are directly tied to climate‑adaptation outcomes (e.g., water security and heat mitigation). Costs must be itemised, reasonable, and supported by at least one quote from a qualified supplier or contractor.
