Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. We welcome applications from community-led initiatives like yours, and I am pleased to address each of your questions in detail below.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets our organisational eligibility criteria—specifically, that it is a legally constituted entity with an ABN, holds appropriate public liability insurance, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to supply a signed auspicing agreement or letter from the council confirming this arrangement, along with the council’s ABN and insurance certificate of currency.

**Grant Amount Parameters:** For this round, the minimum request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project scope appears to fall comfortably within this band. Please note that we require a co-contribution (cash or in-kind) of at least 10 per cent of the total project value for grants over $20,000; volunteer labour may be valued at $30 per hour for in-kind purposes.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is an eligible expenditure category. Costs must be for purchase and installation only; ongoing maintenance, operational expenses, or movable equipment (e.g., garden tools) are not covered. Quotes must be from licensed contractors and itemised.

**Supporting Documentation Checklist:** In addition to the auspicing agreement and council insurance, please prepare:
1. A concise project plan (max two pages) outlining objectives, timeline, risk management, and community benefit.
2. A detailed budget using our template (attached), showing all income sources and expenditure line items.
3. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure component.
4. Evidence of land tenure or a letter of consent from the landowner (council) for the proposed works.
5. Your group’s most recent financial statement (simple income/expenditure spreadsheet is acceptable given your turnover).
6. Letters of support from at least two community stakeholders.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 28 June. I recommend submitting at least 48 hours early to allow time for any validation queries. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require further clarification or wish to discuss your project concept before finalising the application.
