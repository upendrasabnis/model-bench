For grant amounts, there is no minimum or maximum funding limit specified in this round. Applications are assessed based on the project’s scope, alignment with resilience goals, and demonstrated need. That said, infrastructure projects like rainwater tanks and shade structures are generally eligible expenses, as they directly contribute to sustainability and climate adaptation—key priorities for this grant. You’ll need to provide detailed costings and explain how these installations will enhance your garden’s ability to withstand extreme weather conditions.

1. A copy of your group’s constitution or governing rules (if available).  
2. A formal letter of support from your local council confirming their auspice role and insurance coverage.  
3. Financial statements for the past 12 months, including income and expenditure details.  
4. Project plans, quotes for materials/labor, and a timeline for implementation.  
5. Evidence of community engagement, such as photos, testimonials, or records of volunteer participation.
