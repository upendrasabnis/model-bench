Thank you for reaching out and for the enthusiasm you and your community garden team have shown toward the upcoming Community Resilience Grant round. I’m happy to provide clarification on the eligibility criteria and application requirements you’ve outlined.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program requires that applicants be either a registered charity or an incorporated not‑for‑profit entity. However, we do accept applications from unincorporated groups that are formally **auspiced** by a local government or other eligible fiscal sponsor, provided the sponsor assumes legal responsibility for the use of funds and can demonstrate that it holds appropriate insurance, tax‑exempt status, and the authority to enter into grant agreements. In your case, because your council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf and can act as the fiscal sponsor, your group should be considered eligible, **provided we receive a formal letter of auspice from the council** confirming this arrangement. If the council has not yet issued such a letter, we recommend requesting one before you proceed with the full application.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed award size; instead, we assess each proposal against its expected community impact, alignment with program objectives, and the total budget required. Typical awards have ranged from **$5,000 to $75,000**, but we have funded projects both below and above this range where the case for investment was particularly strong. For a project focused on rainwater tanks and a shade structure, you might anticipate an award in the **$15,000‑$40,000** bracket, though the final amount will depend on the detailed budget you submit and the overall competitiveness of the pool.

**Infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes—capital infrastructure that enhances the sustainability and resilience of community assets is an eligible cost. Both **rainwater harvesting systems** (tanks, gutters, pumps, and associated plumbing) and **shade structures** (e.g., pergolas, pop‑up canopies, or permanent shelters) qualify, provided they directly support the ongoing operation of the garden and are justified within the project’s resilience objectives (e.g., reducing water reliance during drought, mitigating heat stress, extending the growing season). We do require that all equipment be **new or refurbished** (with documented condition) and that the procurement follows fair‑market practices.

**Supporting documents we recommend preparing**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest compiling the following documents:

1. **Project Narrative** – a clear description of the problem, objectives, timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – line‑item costs for tanks, shade structures, installation, contingency, and any other expenses, with quotations where possible.  
3. **Letter of Auspice** – an official letter from your local council confirming its role as fiscal sponsor, its insurance coverage, and authority to manage grant funds.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief constitution or charter, membership list, and any existing governance documents.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for small groups, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover you referenced.  
6. **Proof of Insurance** – a copy of the council’s insurance policy or a certificate confirming coverage for public liability and property damage.  
7. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, schools, or community groups that illustrate broader community backing.  
8. **Risk Assessment and Management Plan** – outlining potential safety, environmental, or operational risks and how they will be mitigated.  
9. **Evaluation Plan** – an outline of how you will measure success (e.g., water savings, increased garden usage, temperature reduction) and report back to the funder.  

We also advise that you review the full grant guidelines on our website (specifically the “Eligibility” and “Eligible Activities” sections) to ensure no other requirements are missed.
