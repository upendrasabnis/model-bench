| Reporting | PDF uploads, manual consolidation | Centralized dashboards, auto‑extraction of metrics |

## 10. Common Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

| Pitfall | Why It Happens | Mitigation |
|---------|----------------|------------|
| **Over‑Customisation** | Trying to fit everything into one platform. | Adopt a modular approach; use plugins where possible. |
| **Data Silos** | Separate systems for budgeting, reporting, and impact. | Choose a vendor offering full‑stack integration. |
| **Under‑Training** | Users rely on memory, not the system. | Mandatory training, refresher courses, and a knowledge base. |
| **Ignoring Change Management** | Users resist new workflows. | Engage champions early, communicate benefits clearly. |
| **Skipping Security Review** | Data breaches, non‑compliance. | Perform a penetration test, audit logs, and GDPR/CCPA checks. |
