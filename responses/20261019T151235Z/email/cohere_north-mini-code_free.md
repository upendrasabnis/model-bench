Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful description of your project. I’m pleased to provide some clarification on the points you raised, and I hope this information helps you decide whether to proceed with a full application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated, council‑auspiced groups**  
Our Community Resilience Grant is designed to support projects that deliver clear public benefit, regardless of whether the applicant is a registered charity. Unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of a local council are generally eligible, provided that the council can demonstrate its support—typically through a formal auspice agreement, a letter of endorsement, or confirmation that it will hold the required insurances and be the signatory on any grant agreement. If your council does not have a pre‑existing partnership with us, a brief endorsement letter confirming that it will act as the fiscal host and maintain appropriate liability and public liability insurance will usually satisfy the eligibility requirements.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant program does not prescribe a fixed award size; instead, awards are determined on the basis of project merit, alignment with the program’s strategic objectives, and the proportion of total eligible costs that the project requires. Historically, awards have ranged from approximately **$5,000** for smaller infrastructure upgrades to **$150,000** for more extensive, multi‑component initiatives. Your projected budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure falls comfortably within this range, and we would expect a grant contribution of roughly **30‑40 %** of total eligible costs to be competitive.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes—capital assets such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible expenses under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” category of the guidelines. The key is that the items must be **directly attributable to the project’s resilience objectives**, be necessary for the ongoing operation of the garden, and have a useful life of at least three years. Both the tanks and the shade structure meet these criteria, provided they are procured from reputable suppliers and installed according to relevant building and plumbing regulations.

**Supporting documentation you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following materials (most can be compiled into a single PDF and uploaded via our grants portal):

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem you are addressing (e.g., water scarcity and heat stress), the proposed solution, expected outcomes, and how the project aligns with the grant’s resilience goals.  
2. **Budget and Budget Narrative** – a line‑item breakdown of all costs, highlighting the portion you seek from the grant and any co‑funding or in‑kind contributions.  
3. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your garden’s history, volunteer base, and community impact, together with a copy of the council’s auspice agreement or endorsement letter.  
4. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover you mentioned.  
5. **Insurance Certificate** – confirmation from the council that it holds public liability and professional indemnity insurance covering the project activities.  
6. **Letters of Support** – at least two endorsements from local stakeholders (e.g., neighboring community groups, businesses, or council representatives) attesting to the community benefit.  
7. **Project Timeline and Milestones** – a Gantt‑style chart or bullet‑point schedule outlining key activities, deliverables, and target dates (including the grant’s anticipated start date).  
8. **Environmental Impact Statement** – a brief note on how the rainwater tanks will reduce potable water use and how the shade structure will mitigate heat island effects, linking back to climate‑resilience outcomes.
