Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am pleased to provide clarification on each of your points.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and assume financial and reporting responsibility for the grant. Your council’s willingness to hold insurances and act as auspice is a strong foundation. Please ensure the application includes a signed letter from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and their agreement to the grant terms.

**Turnover and Organisational Status:** An annual turnover of approximately $40,000 falls well within the program’s parameters for small community organisations. Not being a registered charity is not a barrier, provided the auspicing body meets the eligibility criteria (e.g., incorporated association, local government entity, or company limited by guarantee).

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your project scope sounds appropriately scaled for this range.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience to climate impacts—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible expenditure category. Costs may include materials, contractor labour, necessary permits, and reasonable project management fees. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water bills, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**
1.  **Auspicing Agreement / Letter of Support** from the council (on letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).
2.  **Project Plan** with timeline, risk management, and volunteer coordination details.
3.  **Detailed Budget** (GST-exclusive) with at least two quotes for major items (tanks, shade structure).
4.  **Evidence of Land Tenure / Permission** to install infrastructure on the site (council lease, licence, or letter of consent).
5.  **Current Public Liability Insurance** certificate (held by council).
6.  **Most Recent Financial Statement** or treasurer’s report showing the $40k turnover.
7.  **Letters of Community Support** (2–3) demonstrating broader benefit.
