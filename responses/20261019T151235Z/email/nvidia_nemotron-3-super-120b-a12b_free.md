Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the valuable work you and your team do to sustain the community garden. I am pleased to provide clarification on the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Community Resilience Grant (CRG) round that closes on [insert exact date].

**1. Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The CRG programme expressly permits applications from organisations that are not incorporated or registered as charities, provided they operate under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council, incorporated association, or registered charity. Your garden’s arrangement, whereby the local council holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurances on your behalf, satisfies this requirement. When you submit the application, you will need to upload a signed auspice agreement or a letter from the council confirming that they are acting as the legal and financial sponsor for the project, including confirmation of insurance coverage.

**2. Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated project costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, you should prepare a detailed budget that falls within these limits; if the total exceeds $50,000, consider phasing the works or seeking co‑funding from other sources to bring the requested amount into the allowable band.

**3. Eligible infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that enhance the resilience of community‑based assets are explicitly listed as eligible expenditures. This includes, but is not limited to, water‑harvesting systems (rainwater tanks, pumps, filtration), shade structures, pergolas, and associated site‑works (foundations, anchoring, and minor civil works). Operational costs such as staff salaries, ongoing utilities, or routine maintenance are not covered, so your budget should clearly separate the capital components (eligible) from any recurring expenses (ineligible).

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To facilitate a smooth assessment, please prepare the following items:

- **Auspice agreement or letter of support** from the local council, detailing the sponsorship arrangement, insurance coverage, and any financial oversight they will provide.  
- **Proof of organisational status** (e.g., minutes of a garden committee meeting showing the decision to apply, or a simple constitution if one exists).  
- **Project plan** outlining the objectives, timeline (including procurement and installation milestones), and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will improve resilience to extreme heat.  
- **Detailed budget** (itemised, with quotes or indicative costs) showing the total requested amount and any co‑contributions.  
- **Evidence of community need** (e.g., photographs of current conditions, records of produce stall attendance, or letters from members/visitors describing heat‑related challenges).  
- **Insurance certificates** held by the council that cover volunteer activities and public liability for the garden site.  
- **Financial statements** (if available) or a simple income‑expenditure summary demonstrating the $40,000 annual turnover and the proportion derived from the stall and membership fees.

All documents should be uploaded as PDF files through the online grant portal; the system accepts files up to 10 MB each. If you encounter technical difficulties, the grants helpdesk can be reached at grants@[agency].gov.au or 1800 555 123.
