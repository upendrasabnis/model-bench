Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I am happy to provide clarification on the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Community Resilience Grant so you can decide whether to proceed with a full submission.

**Auspiced groups**  
The grant program expressly allows unincorporated organisations to apply when they are operating under an auspicing arrangement with a recognised legal entity, such as a local council, incorporated association, or registered charity. In your case, because the garden’s activities are covered by the council’s insurance and the council agrees to act as the auspicing body, your group meets this requirement. You will need to include a signed letter of support from the council confirming the auspice arrangement, the council’s willingness to administer any awarded funds on your behalf, and details of the insurance coverage that extends to the garden’s operations.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per successful applicant. Projects that demonstrate clear community benefit, scalability, and alignment with the program’s resilience objectives are typically favoured for the higher end of this range, but there is no penalty for requesting a smaller amount if that better matches your budget.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances the organisation’s ability to withstand climate‑related stresses is an eligible expense. Rainwater harvesting tanks and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat fall squarely within this category. Please ensure that the budget clearly itemises each component (e.g., tank purchase, installation labour, shading material, any required permits) and that quotes or cost estimates are attached.

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend preparing the following items:

1. **Auspice agreement** – a signed letter from the local council outlining the auspice relationship, insurance coverage, and any financial administration responsibilities.  
2. **Project plan** – a concise description (no more than two pages) of the proposed rainwater tanks and shade structure, including site maps, timelines, anticipated installation dates, and how the improvements will increase resilience to hot summers.  
3. **Budget spreadsheet** – detailed, itemised costs with attached quotes or vendor estimates; indicate any in‑kind contributions or volunteer labour.  
4. **Organisational information** – a brief profile of the garden (mission, membership numbers, recent activities) and evidence of community support (e.g., letters from members, photos of the produce stall, or testimonials).  
5. **Financial statements** – although you are not a registered charity, providing the council’s financial statement or a simple income‑expenditure summary for the garden (showing the $40,000 turnover) helps demonstrate fiscal responsibility.  
6. **Risk management** – a short note outlining any safety or environmental considerations related to the installation (e.g., grounding of tanks, compliance with local building codes).
