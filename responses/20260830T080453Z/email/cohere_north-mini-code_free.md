Thank you for reaching out and for the clear description of your wonderful project. I’m pleased to provide the following information to help you determine your eligibility and prepare a strong application for the upcoming Community Resilience Grant round.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. While many applicants are registered charities or incorporated associations, we also accept unincorporated groups provided they operate under a formal auspice arrangement with a legal entity that can assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant. In your case, the fact that your garden operates under the auspices of your local council—and that the council holds the necessary insurances—generally satisfies this requirement. However, we do require a written agreement (or a letter of support) from the council confirming that it will act as the fiscal sponsor, oversee the use of funds, and be accountable for any reporting obligations. If such a document is not already in place, we recommend drafting one that outlines the council’s responsibilities and the scope of your partnership.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
There is no fixed minimum or maximum grant award for this round; awards are determined on the merit of the proposal, the scope of the activities, and the available budget. Typical awards have ranged from approximately $5,000 to $75,000, but projects of any scale that demonstrate clear community impact and sustainable outcomes are encouraged to apply. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appear well within the usual parameters.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes, capital infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is an eligible expense under the “Resilience and Climate Adaptation” component of the grant. We consider these items to be high‑value, long‑term assets that directly support the garden’s ability to operate during extreme heat events. Please ensure that any equipment or materials listed are clearly justified in relation to the project objectives and include specifications where possible.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To streamline the review process, we recommend assembling the following documents:  

1. **Project narrative** – a concise description of the problem you are addressing, the proposed solution, expected outcomes, and a timeline.  
2. **Detailed budget** – breaking down all costs (equipment, installation, labour, contingency) and indicating how each line item aligns with eligible activities.  
3. **Organizational profile** – a brief overview of your group’s mission, activities, and volunteer base, together with the council’s auspice agreement or letter of support.  
4. **Proof of insurance** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming coverage for the project activities.  
5. **Financial statements** – most recent audited or management‑prepared statements (or a cash‑flow summary) reflecting your $40,000 turnover.  
6. **Letters of support** – endorsements from local businesses, community members, or partner organisations.  
7. **Site plans or drawings** – conceptual sketches showing where the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be located.  

If you follow these guidelines and submit a well‑structured application package, you will be in a strong position to demonstrate the value and feasibility of your project.
