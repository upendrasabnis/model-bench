Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:**  
Yes, unincorporated groups that operate under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be an incorporated body (e.g., a local government authority) that holds appropriate public liability insurance and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a signed auspice agreement that clearly outlines the roles, responsibilities, and reporting obligations of both parties.

**Grant Amount Limits:**  
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. If your total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you can demonstrate confirmed co‑funding or in‑kind contributions for the balance.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:**  
Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure designed to mitigate heat impacts and ensure continued operation during extreme weather events are considered eligible, provided they are fixed assets that remain with the garden site. Items such as portable equipment, ongoing operational expenses, or retrospective costs are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**  
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Signed auspice agreement with the council (including their ABN and insurance certificates of currency).
2. Your group’s most recent financial statement or a simple income/expenditure summary showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
3. A concise project plan outlining objectives, activities, timeline, and risk management.
4. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item (tanks, shade structure) from licensed suppliers.
5. Evidence of land tenure or a letter of support from the landowner/council confirming long‑term access to the site.
6. A brief demonstration of community need (e.g., letters of support, usage statistics, climate data for your region).
