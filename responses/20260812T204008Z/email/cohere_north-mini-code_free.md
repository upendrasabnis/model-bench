Thank you for reaching out and for the clear description of your garden’s objectives. I’m pleased to provide the following guidance to help you determine eligibility and prepare a strong application for the upcoming Community Resilience Grant round.

**Eligibility – unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program accepts applications from a broad range of applicants, including incorporated not‑for‑profit organisations, registered charities, and, in certain cases, unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of an eligible local government entity. If your council holds the required insurances and can act as the primary applicant or co‑applicant, your garden can generally proceed as a “council‑auspiced” project. However, we do require a formal letter of support from the council confirming that it will assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds, and that it will manage any reporting obligations on your group’s behalf. If the council is not itself an eligible grantee (e.g., it does not meet our “public‑sector” criteria), you may need to incorporate your group or obtain charitable status before the submission deadline.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a minimum award. The maximum award is $150,000 per fiscal year, and most successful projects receive between $30,000 and $80,000. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should be detailed in the budget section, and we will assess the alignment of the requested amount with the overall project scope and anticipated community impact.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes, capital infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is expressly permitted under the “Climate‑Resilience” component of this grant. These items must be essential to the project’s ability to mitigate heat‑related risks and must demonstrate clear long‑term benefits for community health, food security, and environmental sustainability.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application we recommend assembling the following materials:

1. **Project narrative** – a concise description of the problem, objectives, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed budget** – itemised costs for tanks, shade structure, installation, contingency, and any staffing or training expenses.  
3. **Financial statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for unincorporated groups, a set of cash‑flow statements and a brief explanation of revenue sources).  
4. **Organizational profile** – including a brief history, volunteer base, and any governing documents (e.g., a memorandum of association if you have one).  
5. **Council support letter** – confirming auspice status, insurance coverage, and willingness to oversee the grant.  
6. **Letters of support** – from local businesses, community organisations, or stakeholder groups that will benefit from the project.  
7. **Risk assessment and insurance proof** – demonstrating that the project will be managed safely and that coverage is in place.  

We advise you to review the full grant guidelines on our website (specifically the “Eligibility” and “Application Requirements” sections) to ensure that all elements are addressed.
