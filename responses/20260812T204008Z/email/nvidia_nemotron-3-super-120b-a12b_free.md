I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Community Resilience Grant so you can decide whether to proceed with a full submission.

**Auspicing arrangements**  
The grant program expressly allows unincorporated groups to apply when they are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, incorporated association, or registered charity. In your case, the council’s formal auspice—evidenced by a signed auspice agreement or letter of support—satisfies the requirement that the applicant have a governing body capable of accepting and managing grant funds. Therefore, your garden is eligible to apply under the council’s auspice.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant operates within a funding band of **$5,000 to $50,000** per project. There is no prescribed minimum turnover for applicants; the assessment focuses on the project’s merit, community impact, and the applicant’s capacity to deliver the proposed activities. Your reported annual turnover of approximately $40,000 well within the range of organisations that have previously been successful.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances resilience—such as rainwater harvesting tanks, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, or similar adaptations to climate stressors—is considered an eligible expense, provided the items are essential to the project’s objectives and are supported by competitive quotes or supplier estimates. Operational costs (e.g., stall‑running expenses, membership administration) are not covered unless they are integrally linked to the delivery of the infrastructure component.

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice agreement or letter of support** from the local council confirming they will act as the legal auspice holder and will manage the grant funds on your behalf.  
2. **Project plan** (maximum two pages) outlining the need, objectives, activities, timeline, and expected resilience outcomes.  
3. **Detailed budget** with itemised costs, including at least two written quotes for the rainwater tanks and shade structure.  
4. **Financial statement** (most recent 12‑month period) showing the $40,000 turnover and confirming that the council’s auspice arrangement does not create a conflict of interest.  
5. **Evidence of community support** (e.g., letters from garden members, local residents, or partner organisations) demonstrating demand for the proposed upgrades.  
6. **Insurance certificate** held by the council that covers the garden’s activities and the proposed works.
