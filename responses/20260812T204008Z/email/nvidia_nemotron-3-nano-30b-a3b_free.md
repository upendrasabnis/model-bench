Thank you for reaching out and for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant. I’m happy to clarify the points you raised so you can prepare a strong and compliant application.  

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our funding guidelines allow organisations that are “auspiced” by a local government authority to be considered eligible, provided the auspicing body holds the appropriate legal status and insurance coverage on behalf of the project. Because your garden operates under the council’s auspices and the council maintains the required public liability and assets insurance, you meet this criterion. However, the application must be submitted in the name of the council as the lead entity, with a clear letter of support from the council confirming its role as the responsible party for the project.  

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The current funding round offers grants ranging from **$5,000 to $50,000**. Projects that demonstrate a clear link to climate‑resilience outcomes and a realistic budget are encouraged to apply for the full amount, but any request within the above range is acceptable.  

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the capacity of a community facility to withstand climate‑related stresses is eligible, provided it is directly tied to the project’s resilience objectives. Rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related site works fall under “eligible capital expenditure” when they are integral to the garden’s operational sustainability.  

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To accompany your application, please include:  

- A signed **letter of support** from the council confirming its auspicing role and outlining any in‑kind contributions.  
- A detailed **project budget** that breaks down the requested grant amount, co‑funding (if any), and a line‑item justification for the rainwater tanks and shade structure.  
- Evidence of **insurance coverage** held by the council on your behalf (typically a copy of the policy or a summary).  
- A concise **project plan** (timeline, milestones, and expected outcomes).  
- If available, **financial statements** or audited accounts showing your $40,000 turnover, to demonstrate fiscal transparency.
