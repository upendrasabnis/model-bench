Thank you for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant (CRG) and for reaching out with your questions. I’m pleased to confirm that your community garden is a strong fit for this funding round, and I’ll outline the key points you mentioned below.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**

The CRG welcomes applications from unincorporated community groups, including those that operate under the auspices of a local council. The critical criteria are:

- The applicant must be a bona‑fide community‑based organisation that can demonstrate a clear governance structure (e.g., a board, committee, or steering group).  
- The council’s insurance and legal umbrella is acceptable; the council must provide a written statement confirming that it holds the necessary insurance and that the garden’s activities fall within its statutory responsibilities.  
- You will need to supply a copy of that council statement, as well as evidence of your group’s existence (e.g., minutes from a founding meeting, a simple constitution, or a letter of confirmation from a council officer).

**2. Grant amount limits**

The CRG offers funding in the range of **$5,000 – $25,000** per project. The exact award will depend on the scope of the proposal, projected outcomes, and the overall balance of the funding pool. Because your project involves infrastructure (rainwater tanks and a shade structure) that is directly related to resilience and sustainability, we anticipate a favorable review.  

**3. Eligible costs – infrastructure**

Capital infrastructure is a core component of the CRG. In your case, the cost of:

- Rainwater tanks (including supply, delivery, installation, and associated plumbing)  
- Shade structures (materials, delivery, installation)  

will be treated as eligible capital expenditure. We do, however, require a detailed quotation that itemises each component, the supplier’s GST registration number, and any warranties.  

**4. Supporting documentation**

To strengthen your application, please prepare the following:

1. **Project brief** – a concise description of the garden, its mission, and how the infrastructure will enhance resilience.  
2. **Governance documents** – council statement, minutes of a recent meeting, and any written governance structure.  
3. **Financials** – an annual statement (or a simple balance sheet) showing turnover and expenses, plus a projected budget for the next 12 months.  
4. **Cost breakdown** – itemised quotations for the tanks and shade structure, including delivery and installation.  
5. **Impact and outcome indicators** – measurable goals (e.g., % increase in produce yield, reduction in water usage, hours of shade available).  
6. **Insurance evidence** – copy of the council’s insurance policy or a letter confirming coverage for the garden’s activities.
