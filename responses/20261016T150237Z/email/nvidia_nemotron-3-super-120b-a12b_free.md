**Auspicing Arrangements**  
The grant program expressly permits unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. As long as your council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will act as the legal and financial sponsor for the project—and that it holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance—you meet the auspicing requirement. Please ensure the letter includes the council’s ABN, contact details, and a statement accepting responsibility for the grant funds and reporting obligations.

**Grant Size**  
For this funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications are assessed on a merit basis, and the requested amount should reflect the scale of the proposed works and the demonstrated need. Your estimated project cost for rainwater tanks and a shade structure would comfortably fall within this range.

**Eligible Costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances resilience to climate‑related stressors is an eligible expense. Rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and similar improvements that enable continued operation during extreme heat or drought are explicitly listed as allowable costs. Operational expenses (e.g., staff wages, ongoing utilities) are not covered, but the purchase and installation of the tanks and shade structure qualify.

**Supporting Documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice Letter** – signed by an authorised officer of the local council, confirming sponsorship, insurance coverage, and acceptance of financial responsibility.  
2. **Project Plan** – a brief (one‑ to two‑page) description of the proposed works, including site maps, specifications for the tanks and shade structure, a timeline, and how the improvements will increase resilience to hot summers.  
3. **Budget Breakdown** – itemised costs (quotes or supplier estimates) for the tanks, shade structure, any associated plumbing or foundations, and a clear total that matches the requested grant amount.  
4. **Organisational Information** – a short profile of the garden (mission, volunteer base, annual turnover, and any existing partnerships).  
5. **Evidence of Need** – any local climate data, council heat‑wave reports, or community feedback that demonstrates the increasing summer temperatures affecting your operations.  
6. **Financial Statements** – although you are not a registered charity, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement or bank statement showing the $40,000 annual turnover helps verify financial capacity.
