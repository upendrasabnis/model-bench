Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I’m pleased to provide the information you requested regarding the Community Resilience Grant (CRG) round that closes next month.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The CRG expressly allows applications from unincorporated community groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local government authority. In order to be considered eligible, you must submit a formal letter of auspice from the council confirming that:

1. The council accepts legal and financial responsibility for the grant funds,  
2. The council holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance covering your activities, and  
3. The council will act as the signatory to the funding agreement and will be the recipient of any acquittal reports.

If these conditions are met, your garden can proceed with an application despite not being a registered charity or incorporated association.

**Grant size**  
For this funding round, the minimum grant amount is **$5,000** and the maximum is **$50,000** (GST exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your projected annual turnover of approximately $40,000, a request in the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be well‑aligned with the program’s intent to support scalable, resilience‑building projects.

**Eligible costs – rainwater tanks and shade structure**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience is an explicit priority of the CRG. Both rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are considered eligible costs, provided they:

* Are essential to the delivery of the project’s resilience outcomes,  
* Are sourced from reputable suppliers with itemised quotes, and  
* Include any necessary installation, permits, and ancillary works (e.g., footings, plumbing connections).

Operational expenses such as ongoing maintenance, staff wages, or general garden supplies are not eligible unless they are demonstrably tied to the installation and commissioning phase of the infrastructure.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please collate the following items:

1. **Letter of auspice** from the local council (on official letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).  
2. **Council insurance certificate** showing coverage for volunteer activities and public liability.  
3. **Project plan** (max two pages) outlining the problem statement, objectives, implementation timeline, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will increase resilience to heat and water scarcity.  
4. **Detailed budget** (Excel or PDF) with itemised costs, including at least two written quotes for each major item (tanks, pumps, fittings, shade structure, installation).  
5. **Evidence of community support** (e.g., letters from members, a petition, or photos of current garden use) to demonstrate demand and local endorsement.  
6. **Financial summary** of the garden’s recent turnover (bank statements or a simple profit‑and‑loss sheet) to confirm the $40,000 annual figure you mentioned.  
7. **Risk management plan** (brief) identifying any safety, environmental, or regulatory risks associated with the works and mitigation measures.

Once you have assembled these documents, you can submit your application through the online grants portal before the closing date.
