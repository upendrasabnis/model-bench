Thank you for reaching out and for the clear description of your garden’s objectives. I’m pleased to provide the following information to help you determine your eligibility and prepare a strong application for the upcoming Community Resilience Grant round.  

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our program does accept applications from unincorporated community groups provided they operate under the formal auspices of a local government entity that can act as the primary applicant and assume legal responsibility for the grant. In your case, the council’s involvement—holding insurance, managing contracts, and providing administrative support—satisfies this requirement. While registration as a charity is not a prerequisite, we do require a signed agreement from the council confirming that it will oversee the project, manage any funds awarded, and be accountable for compliance with grant conditions.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant is designed to fund projects ranging from $5,000 to $150,000. The actual award will depend on the scope, budget, and demonstrated community impact. Your proposed infrastructure (rainwater tanks and a shade structure) falls well within the typical cost parameters of previous successful applications, which have averaged between $30,000 and $70,000 for similar upgrades.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes—capital infrastructure that enhances environmental sustainability and community resilience is explicitly eligible. This includes:  

1. **Rainwater harvesting systems** (tanks, gutters, filtration, and distribution) that reduce reliance on municipal water and mitigate the effects of drought.  
2. **Shade structures** (e.g., pergolas, canopies, or modular shade tents) that provide heat relief, extend the usable season of the garden, and improve participant safety.  

Both items must be directly tied to the project’s resilience objectives, clearly described in the narrative, and justified with quantitative or qualitative impact measures.  

**Supporting documentation you should prepare**  

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise (2–3 pages) description of the problem, proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – line‑item breakdown of all costs, including equipment, installation, labour (if applicable), and a contingency of 10 % for unforeseen expenses.  
3. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from the council, local businesses, or community stakeholders confirming endorsement and willingness to collaborate.  
4. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a set of basic cash‑flow statements and the $40,000 turnover breakdown).  
5. **Insurance Confirmation** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming that coverage extends to the proposed activities and any third‑party liabilities.  
6. **Site Plans or Drawings** – schematic layouts showing where the tanks and shade structure will be located, plus any required permits or council approvals.  
7. **Risk Assessment** – a brief assessment of health, safety, and environmental risks associated with the installation and use of the new infrastructure.  

We recommend that you first review the full grant guidelines on our website (specifically the “Eligibility and Requirements” section) to ensure no other criteria are missed.
