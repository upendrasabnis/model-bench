Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarification on each of your questions.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under the auspices of a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for legal entities. The council must be willing to enter into a funding agreement on your behalf, accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant, and hold the required insurances, which you have confirmed they already do. You will need to provide a formal letter of support from the council confirming their auspicing arrangement and their agreement to these obligations.

Second, the grant round has a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to fall comfortably within this range. Please note that the program requires a co-contribution—either cash or in-kind—of at least 20% of the total project value, which can include volunteer labour valued at the standard rate published in the guidelines.

Third, infrastructure that builds long-term climate resilience is a core priority of this grant. Permanent installations such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible capital expenditure, provided they are fixed assets that remain the property of the auspicing body or the community group. Costs for site preparation, professional installation, and necessary permits are also claimable.

Finally, to streamline your application, I recommend preparing the following supporting documents now: the council’s auspicing letter; your group’s most recent financial statement (a simple income and expenditure summary is sufficient given your turnover); quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure item; a project plan with timelines; evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site; and your group’s constitution or rules of operation, even if informal.

The guidelines and application form are available on our website, and I encourage you to review the assessment criteria, particularly the sections on community benefit and climate adaptation outcomes. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require further clarification as you develop your application.
