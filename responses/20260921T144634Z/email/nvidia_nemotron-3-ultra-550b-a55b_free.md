Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are indeed eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) holds appropriate public liability insurance and is willing to enter into a formal funding agreement on your behalf. The council would act as the legal entity receiving the funds and would assume contractual responsibility for the grant's acquittal and compliance requirements. This is a common and accepted arrangement for community gardens and similar volunteer-run initiatives.

Second, the current grant round offers a minimum award of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to fall well within this range. Infrastructure of this nature—specifically water security and heat mitigation assets that enable continued operation during extreme weather events—is explicitly listed as an eligible capital cost in the program guidelines. Both items align directly with the grant's objective of building community resilience to climate impacts.

In terms of supporting documentation, you should prepare the following: a letter of support and formal auspice agreement from your local council confirming their willingness to administer the grant; evidence of the council's current public liability insurance; your group's most recent financial statements (a simple income and expenditure statement is sufficient given your $40,000 turnover); a detailed project budget with at least two quotes for the tanks and shade structure; a project plan outlining timelines, volunteer roles, and risk management; and evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site.

Your annual turnover of approximately $40,000 derived from produce sales and membership fees does not affect eligibility; the program is designed to support grassroots organisations of varying financial scales.

I recommend reviewing the full Program Guidelines and Frequently Asked Questions on our website prior to submission, as they contain detailed assessment criteria and mandatory requirements. The application portal opens two weeks before the closing date, and I encourage you to start a draft early to allow time for council sign-off.
