Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

First, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local council are eligible to apply, provided the council holds the legal and financial accountability for the grant. The auspicing body must be an incorporated entity (such as your local council) with current public liability insurance and the capacity to enter into a funding agreement on your behalf. You will need to supply a signed letter from the council confirming their willingness to auspice the project, along with their ABN, insurance certificates of currency, and a brief outline of the governance arrangements between your group and the council.

Second, the current round offers grants between a minimum of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000. Your proposed budget should fall within this range; if the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you must demonstrate confirmed co-contributions or in-kind support for the balance.

Third, infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures is considered an eligible capital expenditure, provided the assets are permanently affixed to the site, directly support the project’s resilience objectives, and comply with relevant Australian standards and local planning requirements. Quotes from licensed contractors and, where applicable, development approval documentation should be included.

Finally, supporting documents you should prepare include: the council auspicing letter; your group’s most recent financial statement (or a simple income-and-expenditure summary given your $40,000 turnover); a detailed project plan with timelines; at least two written quotes for each major infrastructure item; evidence of land tenure or a council letter confirming ongoing access to the site; and a risk management plan addressing extreme heat and water security. Letters of community support are encouraged but not mandatory.

Please ensure all documents are compiled into a single PDF before submission via the online portal by the closing date. If you require further clarification or would like a brief pre-application discussion, do not hesitate to contact me.
