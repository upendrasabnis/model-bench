Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I appreciate the opportunity to clarify the eligibility requirements for the Community Resilience Grant before you invest further time in preparing an application.

**Auspice arrangements**  
Groups that are not incorporated charities may apply provided they are operating under a formal auspice agreement with an eligible sponsoring organisation—in this case, your local council. The council must be able to demonstrate that it holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance for the garden’s activities and that it will assume financial and reporting responsibility for any grant funds awarded. A signed letter of auspice from the council, outlining the scope of its support and confirming insurance coverage, is therefore a required supporting document.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant programme has a minimum award of AU$5,000 and a maximum of AU$50,000 per project. Applications are assessed on the scale and impact of the proposed resilience‑building activities, so your requested budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should fall within this range to be considered.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances the organisation’s ability to withstand climate‑related stresses—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, drought‑tolerant landscaping, or water‑saving irrigation—is explicitly listed as an eligible expense. Both the purchase and installation costs (including contractor quotes) can be included in the budget, provided they are reasonable, necessary, and clearly linked to the project’s resilience objectives.

**Supporting documentation checklist**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice letter** from the local council (signed, on council letterhead) confirming the sponsorship arrangement and insurance coverage.  
2. **Project plan** (max 2 pages) describing the current garden operations, the specific climate‑risk challenges you face, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will mitigate those risks.  
3. **Detailed budget** (itemised) showing anticipated costs for tanks, shade structure, any site works, and a brief justification for each line item. Include at least two written quotes for major items.  
4. **Evidence of community support** (e.g., letters of endorsement from regular volunteers, members, or local residents).  
5. **Financial statements** (or a simple income‑expenditure summary) demonstrating the AU$40,000 annual turnover and showing that the garden is financially viable.  
6. **Insurance certificate** (or council‑provided confirmation) covering public liability and volunteer injury for the garden’s activities.

Once these documents are assembled, you can complete the online application form, which will guide you through uploading each file and entering the budget details. The assessment panel will review submissions against the criteria of climate resilience, community benefit, feasibility, and value for money.
