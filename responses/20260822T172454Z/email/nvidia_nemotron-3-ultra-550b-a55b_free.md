Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that it is a legally constituted entity with an ABN, holds appropriate public liability insurance, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. Your council’s existing insurance coverage for the garden satisfies the insurance requirement, though we will require a formal letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement and its willingness to administer the funds on your behalf.

Second, the current grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure falls well within this range. Capital infrastructure of this nature—specifically water security assets and climate-adaptation structures that enable continued community use during extreme heat—is considered an eligible cost under the “Community Infrastructure” stream, provided the assets are fixed, non-movable, and remain the property of the auspicing council for the benefit of the broader community.

Third, your annual turnover of approximately $40,000 does not disqualify you; there is no turnover cap for this round, though we do ask applicants to demonstrate financial viability. A simple income-and-expenditure statement for the most recent financial year, signed by your treasurer, will suffice.

In terms of supporting documents, please prepare the following for submission with your application:
1. Council auspicing letter (on letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).
2. Current certificate of currency for public liability insurance (council’s policy).
3. Project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and maintenance schedule for the tanks and shade structure.
4. Two quotes for each major infrastructure item (rainwater tanks, shade structure).
5. Evidence of land tenure or a licence agreement confirming the garden’s right to occupy the site for at least five years post-completion.
6. Letters of support from at least two community stakeholders (e.g., local school, neighbourhood house, or health service).
7. Your group’s most recent annual financial statement.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November. I strongly recommend drafting your responses in the online portal well before the deadline to avoid any technical issues.
