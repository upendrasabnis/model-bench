**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines explicitly state that unincorporated community groups are eligible applicants, provided they operate under the formal auspices of a local government body that can demonstrate fiscal responsibility and insurance coverage. In your case, the council’s assumption of insurance and its role in overseeing your activities satisfy this criterion. However, you will need to submit a “host‑organisation agreement” that outlines the council’s support, including any indemnity provisions and a letter confirming that the council will act as the primary point of contact for grant disbursements.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award. Instead, funding is allocated based on the overall budget of the proposed project, the demonstrated community benefit, and the proportion of total eligible costs that the grant would cover. Past awards have ranged from $5,000 to $150,000, but we encourage you to submit a realistic budget that reflects the scope of your rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure installation. If your total eligible costs are substantially lower than the average, we can still consider a proportional award.

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes, capital infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience and sustainability of community operations is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks, shade structures, and associated site‑works (e.g., foundations, plumbing, and minor earthworks) are expressly permitted, provided they are essential to the project’s objectives and comply with local building and environmental regulations. Please ensure that each item is justified in terms of its contribution to the garden’s ability to operate during hot summers.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following materials:

1. **Project narrative** – a clear description of the problem, objectives, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget breakdown** – itemized costs for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary expenses.  
3. **Host‑organisation agreement** – a formal letter from the council confirming its auspice status, insurance coverage, and willingness to act as fiscal agent.  
4. **Organizational profile** – a brief overview of your volunteer team, governance structure (even if informal), and any past community‑impact reports.  
5. **Site plans and technical specifications** – drawings or sketches showing where the tanks and shade will be located, plus product specifications.  
6. **Environmental and regulatory compliance** – any permits required from local authorities or water authorities.  
7. **Financial statements** – most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or a statement of cash flows) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover and sources of revenue.  
8. **Letters of support** – endorsements from neighbouring organisations, local businesses, or community members.

We would be happy to provide a more detailed checklist or answer any follow‑up questions you may have as you develop your application. Please note that the submission deadline is still just over a month away, so we recommend beginning the drafting process soon to allow adequate time for gathering the necessary documentation.
