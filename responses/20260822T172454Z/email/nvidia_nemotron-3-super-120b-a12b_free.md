Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you and your volunteers are doing to strengthen your community garden. I appreciate you taking the time to clarify eligibility before preparing a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**1. Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The Community Resilience Grant expressly permits applications from organisations that are not incorporated or registered as charities, provided they are operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible sponsor—in your case, the local council. The council must be able to demonstrate that it holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance, and that it will assume financial and administrative responsibility for the grant funds should your application be successful. You will need to include a signed auspice agreement (or a letter of support) from the council that outlines these responsibilities.

**2. Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your projected turnover of approximately $40,000, a request in the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be well‑aligned with the program’s intent to support scalable, community‑driven resilience projects.

**3. Eligible infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance the organisation’s ability to withstand climate‑related stresses are eligible. This includes the purchase and installation of rainwater harvesting tanks, associated plumbing, and shade structures (such as pergolas or shade sails) that protect both volunteers and produce from extreme heat. Costs for design, engineering, and necessary permits are also covered, provided they are itemised and reasonable. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., seeds, routine maintenance) are not eligible under this grant.

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following:
- A copy of the auspice agreement or letter of support from the local council, confirming insurance coverage and financial oversight.
- A detailed project budget that itemises each cost component (tanks, fittings, labour, shade structure, permits, contingency) and shows any co‑funding or in‑kind contributions.
- Quotations or vendor estimates for the major items (tanks and shade structure) to demonstrate cost‑effectiveness.
- A brief project plan (no more than two pages) outlining the timeline, milestones, and how the infrastructure will improve resilience during hot summers.
- Evidence of community support, such as letters from regular stall customers, membership records, or a summary of volunteer hours contributed over the past year.
- Your most recent financial statement (even a simple income‑expenditure summary) to illustrate the $40,000 annual turnover.

I encourage you to submit a draft of your budget for a quick pre‑review if you would like feedback before the final submission deadline.
