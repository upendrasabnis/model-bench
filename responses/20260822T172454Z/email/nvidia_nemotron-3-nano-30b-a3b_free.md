Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant. I appreciate the thoughtful questions you have raised and have provided detailed responses below.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant program explicitly welcomes applications from unincorporated community groups that operate under the auspices of a local government authority, provided the council holds the necessary insurance and legal responsibilities on the group’s behalf. In such cases, the council can act as the “responsible entity” for the purpose of grant administration. To proceed, you will need to submit a formal letter of support from the council confirming this relationship, together with a copy of the council’s insurance certificates.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The current funding round offers grants ranging from **$5,000 to $25,000**. Projects that demonstrate a clear community benefit and a realistic budget within this range are prioritized. While there is no strict ceiling on the amount you may request, the maximum award is capped at $25,000 per project. Applications that request less than $5,000 are considered but may receive lower priority.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the resilience of a community facility — such as rain‑water harvesting systems and shade structures — is fully eligible as long as it directly supports the project’s resilience objectives and is essential to maintaining operations under adverse conditions. Detailed cost breakdowns, quotations, and a justification linking each item to the resilience outcome are required.

**4. Supporting documentation**  
Please include the following with your application:  

- A letter of endorsement from the council confirming its role as the responsible entity.  
- The council’s current insurance certificates (public liability and asset coverage).  
- A detailed project budget that outlines eligible costs (e.g., materials, labour, professional fees).  
- Quotes or pro‑forma invoices for major items such as the rain‑water tanks and shade structure.  
- A concise project plan (timeline, objectives, expected outcomes) and a risk‑mitigation strategy.
