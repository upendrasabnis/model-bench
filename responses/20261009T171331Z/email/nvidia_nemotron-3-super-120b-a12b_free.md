Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with the community garden. I appreciate you taking the time to verify eligibility before preparing a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in detail.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant is expressly designed to support initiatives that strengthen local capacity to adapt to climate‑related stresses, and it recognises that many valuable projects are delivered by informal or volunteer‑led organisations. Under the grant’s eligibility criteria, an applicant may be either a legally incorporated entity **or** an unincorporated group that is formally auspiced by an eligible host organisation—such as a local government council, a registered charity, or a incorporated association. In your case, because the local council holds the relevant public liability and volunteer insurances on your behalf and has agreed to act as the auspicing body, your garden would meet the auspice requirement. You will need to provide a signed letter of auspice from the council confirming their support, the scope of their responsibility (insurance, financial oversight, and reporting), and the duration of the auspice arrangement for the grant period.

**2. Grant amount range**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is **$5,000** and the maximum award is **$50,000** per successful project. The assessment panel considers the scale and impact of the proposed activities relative to the requested budget, so a request that aligns closely with the documented costs of your rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure installation will be viewed favourably. There is no prescribed “ideal” amount; rather, the panel looks for a clear, justified budget that demonstrates value for money and sustainability beyond the grant period.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance the resilience of a community asset are eligible expenditures, provided they are essential to achieving the project’s stated outcomes and are not purely aesthetic or recreational in nature. Installing rainwater tanks to secure water supply during drought periods and erecting a shade structure to mitigate heat stress on both volunteers and plants are precisely the types of infrastructure the grant intends to support. Accordingly, both the purchase and installation of the tanks (including any necessary plumbing, foundations, and filtration components) and the design, materials, and labour for the shade structure can be included in your budget. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are generally not covered unless they are clearly tied to a defined period of post‑implementation monitoring (e.g., the first 12 months after installation).

**4. Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please compile the following items:

- **Auspice Letter** – A signed letter from the local council confirming they will act as the auspicing body, outlining insurance coverage, financial oversight responsibilities, and the duration of the auspice.
- **Project Plan** – A concise (1‑2 page) description of the garden’s current operations, the climate challenges you face, the specific objectives of the rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure installation, and how these will improve resilience and continuity of service.
- **Budget Spreadsheet** – Itemised costs for each component (tank purchase, fittings, labour, shade‑structure materials, engineering/design fees, permits, and any contingency). Include quotes or estimates from suppliers where possible, and clearly indicate which costs are being requested from the grant versus any co‑funding or in‑kind contributions.
- **Evidence of Need** – Any local climate data, council heat‑wave reports, or community feedback that demonstrates increasing summer temperatures and water scarcity affecting the garden.
- **Governance Documents** – Although you are not a registered charity, provide the garden’s operating procedures, volunteer roster, and a brief statement of how decisions are made (e.g., monthly meetings, consensus process).
- **Insurance Certificates** – Copies of the public liability and volunteer insurance policies held by the council, showing coverage for the proposed works.
- **Letters of Support** (optional but helpful) – Endorsements from nearby residents, local schools, or other community groups that benefit from the garden’s produce stall or educational activities.

When you have assembled these documents, you can submit them through the online grant portal by the closing date (please verify the exact date on the grant website, as extensions are rarely granted). If you encounter any technical difficulties or need clarification on specific budget lines, feel free to contact me directly at grants@agency.gov.au or call (02) 5550 1234.
