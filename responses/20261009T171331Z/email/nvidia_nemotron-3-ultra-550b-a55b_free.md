Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. We appreciate the valuable work your community garden undertakes, and I am pleased to address each of your questions in detail to assist with your planning.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:**  
Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must hold current public liability insurance (minimum $20 million) and agree to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a signed auspicing agreement or letter from the council confirming this arrangement, including their ABN and insurance details.

**Grant Amount Limits:**  
For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. If the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you demonstrate confirmed co‑funding or in‑kind contributions for the balance.

**Eligible Infrastructure Costs:**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible expenditure category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure that mitigate climate impacts (heat, water scarcity) and enable continued operation during extreme weather are considered eligible, provided they are fixed assets, comply with relevant Australian Standards and local planning regulations, and are not classified as routine maintenance or movable equipment. Quotes must be from licensed contractors and include itemised costs.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**  
1. Completed application form (available on our portal).  
2. Auspicing agreement/letter from the council.  
3. Certificate of Currency for the council’s public liability insurance.  
4. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item.  
5. Project plan with timeline, risk management, and sustainability strategy.  
6. Evidence of community support (letters, survey results, membership list).  
7. Most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover.  
8. Council development approval or evidence that no approval is required.
