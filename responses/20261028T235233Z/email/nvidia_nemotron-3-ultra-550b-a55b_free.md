Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to clarify eligibility before investing time in an application—this is exactly the kind of due diligence that strengthens the overall process. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing Arrangements & Eligibility**  
Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., incorporated not-for-profit, local government authority, or other incorporated entity). The council would need to sign the grant agreement on your behalf, assume legal and financial responsibility for the grant, and hold the required insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation, etc.), which you have confirmed they already do. Please ensure the auspicing arrangement is documented in a signed Memorandum of Understanding or Auspicing Agreement that clearly outlines roles, responsibilities, and financial management processes; this document will be required at application stage.

**Grant Amounts**  
For this round, the Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your projected project scope—rainwater tanks and a shade structure—falls comfortably within this range. If your total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you demonstrate confirmed co-contributions (cash or in-kind) for the balance.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible expenditure category. Rainwater harvesting systems (tanks, pumps, plumbing, first-flush diverters) and permanent shade structures (engineered, certified, and compliant with relevant Australian Standards) are both considered eligible. Costs for site preparation, professional design/certification, and installation labour are also allowable. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water rates, maintenance) and movable equipment (e.g., garden tools) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**  
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Signed Auspicing Agreement/MOU with the council.
2. Council’s Certificate of Currency for Public Liability ($20M minimum) and Workers’ Compensation.
3. Your group’s most recent annual financial statement (simple income/expenditure statement is sufficient given your $40k turnover).
4. Quotes (minimum two) for each major cost item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
5. Site plan showing proposed locations, plus evidence of land tenure/permission (council letter of support if on council land).
6. Project plan with timeline, risk management, and volunteer capacity statement.
7. Letters of community support (2–3) demonstrating broader benefit.

**Next Steps**  
The guidelines and application form are available on our website. I strongly recommend attending the optional Applicant Information Webinar on 14 May (registration link in the guidelines) as it covers assessment criteria in detail. Applications close 5:00 pm AEST, 14 June 2025. Late submissions cannot be accepted.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you need further clarification on any point. We are keen to support grassroots initiatives like yours that build tangible climate resilience in regional communities.
