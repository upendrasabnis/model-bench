Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to clarify eligibility before investing time in an application, and I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Eligibility of auspiced groups:** Unincorporated community groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation (in this case, your local council) holds the required public liability insurance and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to supply a signed letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and evidence of current insurance coverage.

**Grant amount limits:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. If the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you demonstrate confirmed co‑funding or in‑kind contributions for the balance.

**Eligible infrastructure costs:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures to mitigate heat impacts—is explicitly listed as an eligible expenditure category. Costs for materials, professional installation, and necessary permits are all allowable. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water bills, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting documents to prepare:**
1. Auspicing agreement letter from the council (as above).
2. A simple project plan outlining objectives, timeline, risk management, and how the infrastructure will improve resilience.
3. Two written quotes for each major cost item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
4. Your group’s most recent annual financial statement or a treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
5. Evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the garden site.
6. Letters of community support (minimum two) demonstrating broader benefit.

Please ensure all documents are uploaded as PDFs via the online portal before the closing date. If you would like feedback on a draft budget or project plan prior to submission, I am happy to review them informally.
