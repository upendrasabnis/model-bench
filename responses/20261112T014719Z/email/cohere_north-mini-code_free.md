Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant  

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for sharing an overview of your community‑garden project. I am pleased to confirm that the Community Resilience Grant is designed to support a broad spectrum of applicants, and many of the characteristics you described are fully compatible with our programme’s eligibility criteria.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups** – Yes, unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of a local council are eligible to apply. The council’s role as an auspice (or “host” organization) can satisfy the governance and accountability requirements we normally expect from a legal entity. If your council holds public liability insurance, that can also serve as evidence of risk coverage. However, we will need a formal letter of support or a memorandum of understanding from the council confirming that it will act as the lead organisation for the grant’s activities. This letter should outline the scope of the project, the responsibilities of each party, and any conditions the council may impose.

**Grant size – minimum and maximum amounts** – Our grant range for this round is $5,000 to $75,000. There is no strict minimum application amount; projects that request less than $5,000 are still welcome, but they will need to clearly justify the requested sum and demonstrate that it will achieve a meaningful outcome. The maximum reflects the typical scale of infrastructure and capacity‑building initiatives we fund.

**Infrastructure costs – rainwater tanks and shade structures** – Both items are considered eligible costs, provided they directly support the project’s resilience objectives (e.g., reducing water consumption, extending garden usability during extreme heat). We typically fund the purchase, delivery, and installation of the equipment, as well as any necessary civil works (e.g., excavation, foundations, and connection to existing utilities). Please ensure any quotations include installation, permits, and maintenance considerations.

**Supporting documentation** – To strengthen your application, we recommend preparing the following:

1. **Project Proposal** – a concise narrative outlining the problem, solution, expected outcomes, and timeline.  
2. **Budget and Narrative** – a detailed line‑item budget with quotations for the tanks and shade structure, plus a brief explanation of each cost.  
3. **Letters of Support** – one from your local council confirming its auspice role and insurance coverage, and additional letters from community partners or stakeholders.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief description of your group’s volunteer base, governance (even if informal), and any past projects.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple income‑statement and balance sheet) demonstrating your $40,000 turnover.  
6. **Risk Assessment** – a basic risk register addressing safety, environmental, and operational concerns.  
7. **Implementation Plan** – a Gantt‑style timeline with key milestones and responsible parties.

If you decide to proceed with a full application, please upload these documents through the online portal no later than the closing date (next month). Should you have any questions about formatting or require clarification on any of the above, please do not hesitate to contact me directly.
