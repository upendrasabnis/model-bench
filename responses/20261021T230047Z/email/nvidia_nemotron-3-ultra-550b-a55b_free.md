Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are indeed eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program’s eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that they are a legal entity with an ABN, hold appropriate public liability insurance, and are willing to enter into a funding agreement on your behalf. The council would be the contracted party and would assume legal and financial responsibility for the grant, so I strongly recommend confirming their willingness to act as auspice early in the process.

Your annual turnover of approximately $40,000 falls well within the program’s thresholds; there is no minimum turnover requirement, and the maximum annual turnover for this stream is $500,000. The grant amount available in this round ranges from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per project. Your proposed infrastructure—rainwater tanks and a fixed shade structure—is classified as “capital works / minor infrastructure” and is an eligible cost category, provided the items are permanently affixed, essential to the project’s resilience outcomes, and supported by at least two written quotes from licensed suppliers.

In terms of supporting documentation, you should prepare:
1. A letter of support and formal auspice confirmation from the council (on letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).
2. The council’s current Certificate of Currency for public liability insurance ($20 million minimum).
3. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover.
4. Two itemised quotes for each infrastructure component (tanks and shade structure).
5. A simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and maintenance schedule.
6. Evidence of land tenure or a council letter confirming permission to install infrastructure on the site.

Please note that applications must be submitted via the online portal by 5:00 pm AEST on the closing date. I encourage you to draft your responses in a Word document first, as the portal does not auto-save.
