**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. Unincorporated groups are indeed eligible provided that they operate under the formal auspice of a local government entity that can act as the fiscal sponsor and assume responsibility for any insurance, liability, or contractual obligations. In your case, the fact that the council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf satisfies this requirement. However, we will need a written endorsement from the council confirming that they will be the named applicant and will manage any grant‑related funds and reporting on your group’s behalf. This endorsement should include the council’s official name, the contact details of the responsible officer, and a statement that the council agrees to assume fiscal accountability for the project.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, the funding level is determined by the overall budget of the proposed project, the demonstrated community impact, and the availability of funds in the current round. Typical grants for infrastructure‑focused initiatives range from $5,000 to $50,000, but applications that fall outside this band are still considered on their merits. When you prepare your budget, please be sure to break down costs clearly and justify each line item in terms of how it contributes to the project’s resilience objectives.

**Eligibility of rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances a community’s resilience to climate‑related stresses is fully eligible under the grant’s “Sustainable Infrastructure” category. Rainwater tanks and shade structures meet these criteria because they reduce water consumption, mitigate heat stress, and support year‑round operation of essential food‑production activities. We encourage you to include detailed specifications (e.g., tank capacity, material, durability ratings) and any relevant certifications in your budget documentation.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following materials:

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, the proposed solution, expected outcomes, and how the project aligns with the grant’s resilience goals.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – a line‑item spreadsheet with quotations or estimates for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary costs (e.g., permits, contingency).  
3. **Council Endorsement Letter** – as noted above, a formal letter confirming the council’s auspice and fiscal responsibility.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your group’s composition, volunteer base, and any previous community projects.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover you mentioned.  
6. **Insurance Certificate** – a copy of the council’s insurance policy that specifically covers the activities to be undertaken under the grant.  
7. **Supporting Letters** – endorsements from local businesses, community groups, or other stakeholders that highlight the broader impact of the project.  
8. **Risk Assessment and Management Plan** – outlining potential construction, safety, and environmental risks and how they will be mitigated.
