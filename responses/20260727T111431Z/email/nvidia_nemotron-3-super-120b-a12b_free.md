**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant program expressly allows applications from organisations that are not incorporated or registered as charities, provided they are operating under a formal auspice arrangement with an eligible legal entity—in your case, the local council. The council must be willing to act as the legal and financial sponsor, sign the grant agreement, and accept responsibility for acquittal reporting. If the council can confirm its willingness to undertake these responsibilities, your group will meet the eligibility criterion.

**Grant amount range**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications are assessed on the scale and impact of the proposed project relative to the requested amount, so we encourage you to align your budget with this range.

**Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Capital works that enhance community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures—are explicitly listed as eligible costs under the “infrastructure improvement” category. Both items qualify, provided they are directly linked to increasing the garden’s capacity to operate during extreme heat and to secure water supply. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., staff wages, routine maintenance) are not covered, but the purchase and installation of the tanks and shade structure are permissible.

**Supporting documentation**  
To substantiate your application, please prepare the following:

1. **Auspice agreement** – a signed letter or memorandum of understanding from the local council confirming its role as sponsor, including acceptance of financial and reporting obligations.

3. **Project plan and budget** – a detailed description of the rainwater tank and shade structure specifications, quotes from at least two suppliers, and a line‑item budget showing total costs and the amount requested from the grant.  
4. **Evidence of community need and impact** – photographs of the site, a brief statement of how increasing heat and water scarcity affect garden participation, and any letters of support from council members, local residents, or partner organisations.
