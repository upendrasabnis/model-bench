Thank you for reaching out and for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant. I appreciate the important work your volunteer‑run garden does for the town, and I am happy to clarify the points you raised.  

**Eligibility of groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant program expressly welcomes applications from unincorporated community groups that are formally auspiced by a local council, provided the council’s insurance and financial oversight arrangements are documented in the application. Your arrangement satisfies this requirement, so you remain eligible to apply.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The current funding round allocates grants ranging from **$5,000 to $25,000**. Applications that request an amount below $5,000 are not considered, while projects seeking up to $25,000 may be funded, subject to the usual merit‑based assessment.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the capacity of a community facility to withstand climate‑related stressors is an eligible cost category. Rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and associated civil works (e.g., site preparation, foundations, and connections to existing services) qualify as eligible expenditures, provided they are directly tied to the project’s resilience objectives and are appropriately cost‑rated in the budget.  

**Suggested supporting documentation**  
To accompany your application, we recommend the following documents:  

1. **Letter of endorsement** from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement and outlining the insurance coverage in place.  
2. **Detailed project budget** that breaks down costs for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, and any ancillary works, with a clear justification of each line item.  
3. **Project timeline** (Gantt chart or similar) showing key milestones and completion dates.  
4. **Proof of incorporation or registration status** (if any) and the council’s registration of your group as a community entity.  
5. **Evidence of annual turnover** (e.g., audited financial statements or a summary of income sources) to demonstrate compliance with the $40,000 threshold.  
6. **Community benefit statement** describing how the proposed work will improve resilience for residents and users of the garden.  

Please let us know if you would like templates for any of these items or if you have further questions as you develop your proposal. We look forward to receiving your application before the upcoming deadline.
