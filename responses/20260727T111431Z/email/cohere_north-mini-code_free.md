**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our Community Resilience Grant is designed to support projects that deliver clear public benefit and demonstrate strong community involvement. Unincorporated groups are indeed eligible, provided that an auspicing organisation—such as your local council—offers the necessary governance structures, insurance coverage, and financial oversight. In practice, we require a formal letter of support from the council confirming that it will act as the fiscal host for any grant funds received, and that it will maintain the required public liability and professional indemnity insurance for the duration of the project. This arrangement ensures that the grant is administered responsibly while still allowing the grassroots group to retain operational control.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant program does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award; rather, the funding level is determined by the scope, impact, and cost‑effectiveness of the proposed project. Typical awards for infrastructure‑focused initiatives range from $5,000 to $30,000, but applications that demonstrate exceptional community benefit or innovative design may be considered for higher amounts. We encourage you to propose a budget that aligns closely with the actual costs of the rainwater tanks and shade structure, while also highlighting any cost‑sharing or in‑kind contributions you can bring to the table.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Both rainwater tanks and shade structures are considered eligible expenses under the “Climate‑Resilient Infrastructure” category of our grant. We define eligible infrastructure as any permanent or semi‑permanent installation that enhances the sustainability, accessibility, or resilience of community spaces. In addition to the purchase and installation of the tanks and shade, you may also include site preparation, plumbing, foundation work, and any necessary permits or approvals. Please ensure that each line item is clearly described and justified in terms of how it contributes to the project’s resilience objectives.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, objectives, implementation plan, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Narrative Budget Table** – itemising all costs, including equipment, labour, permits, and any contingency.  
3. **Letter of Support from the Council** – confirming auspicing, insurance coverage, and willingness to act as fiscal host.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your volunteer team, governance (even if informal), and any prior projects.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed statements (or, for very small groups, a simple income‑statement and balance sheet) demonstrating your $40,000 turnover.  
6. **Risk Assessment and Safety Plan** – outlining how you will manage installation safety and ongoing maintenance.  
7. **Community Engagement Evidence** – membership lists, volunteer hours, or letters from local residents illustrating demand and support.  

If you are able to assemble these documents, we will be able to assess your application more efficiently and provide a clearer indication of the likely funding range.
