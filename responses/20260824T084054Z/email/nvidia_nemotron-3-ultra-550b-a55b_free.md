I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing Arrangements and Eligibility:**  
Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local government authority—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and assume full financial and reporting responsibility for the grant. Your council’s willingness to hold insurances and act as auspice is a positive indicator. Please ensure the application includes a signed letter from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, its duration, and the council’s acceptance of all grant obligations.

**Grant Amount Parameters:**  
For this round, the minimum request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST-exclusive). Given your projected project scope, you should aim to submit a budget within this range. If the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you demonstrate confirmed co-contributions (cash or in-kind) for the balance.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures to mitigate heat impacts—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category in the program guidelines. Eligible expenses include purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation or permitting fees. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not fundable.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**  
To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. Council auspicing letter (as described above).
2. Current public liability and volunteer insurance certificates held by the council.
3. A simple income-and-expenditure statement for the most recent financial year (showing the ~$40,000 turnover).
4. Quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure item.
5. A brief project plan with timeline, risk register, and evidence of community consultation (e.g., meeting minutes or survey results).
6. Letters of support from at least two local stakeholders (e.g., neighbourhood house, school, or health service).

The guidelines, application form, and a budget template are available on our website. Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I encourage you to submit a draft at least five business days before the deadline so we can provide a completeness check.
