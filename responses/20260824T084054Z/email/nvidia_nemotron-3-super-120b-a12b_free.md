Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the valuable work your volunteer‑run community garden does in strengthening local resilience. I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant and to outline the documentation you will need to prepare.

**Auspice arrangements**  
Unincorporated groups that operate under the formal auspice of a local council are eligible to apply, provided the council supplies a written letter of auspice that confirms it will act as the legal and financial sponsor for the grant, holds the necessary public liability insurance, and will be responsible for acquitting any funds awarded. Please attach this letter (on council letterhead, signed by an authorised officer) with your application.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant program has a minimum award of **AUD 5,000** and a maximum of **AUD 50,000** per project. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should fall within this range; if the total cost exceeds the maximum, you may consider a phased approach or seek additional co‑funding sources.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting tanks and shade structures is an eligible expense when it is directly linked to the project’s resilience outcomes (e.g., ensuring continued garden operation during extreme heat). Costs must be reasonable, supported by itemised quotes, and clearly justified in the project budget. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., stall fees, membership administration) are not covered unless they are demonstrably tied to the delivery of the infrastructure component.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
1. **Project plan** (objectives, activities, timeline, and expected resilience benefits).  
2. **Detailed budget** with quotes for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary works.  
3. **Council auspice letter** (as described above).  
4. **Proof of public liability insurance** held by the council (certificate of currency).  
5. **Governance evidence** (e.g., minutes of a garden committee meeting showing endorsement of the project).  
6. **Financial summary** demonstrating the approximate AUD 40,000 annual turnover (bank statements or a simple profit‑and‑loss summary).  
7. **Letters of community support** (optional but Strengthening the application).  
8. **Risk management plan** addressing safety, maintenance, and long‑term sustainability of the infrastructure.
