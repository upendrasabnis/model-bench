Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I am happy to provide clarification on the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Community Resilience Grant round that closes next month.

**Auspicing Arrangement**  
The grant program does allow unincorporated groups to apply provided they are auspiced by an eligible legal entity—in this case, your local council. The council must be able to demonstrate that it holds the necessary public liability and workers’ compensation insurances for the project activities, and it will need to sign a formal auspice agreement confirming its responsibility for the grant funds and reporting obligations. If the council is willing to act as the auspicing body, your group meets this eligibility criterion.

**Grant Size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per successful applicant. Requests should be scoped to reflect the scale of the proposed works and the demonstrated need within the community. Given your estimated annual turnover of approximately $40,000, a request in the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be competitive, provided the budget aligns with the project scope.

**Eligible Costs**  
Infrastructure investments that enhance climate resilience—such as rainwater harvesting tanks, shade structures, and related site‑works—are explicitly listed as eligible expenses. Costs may include the purchase and installation of the tanks, structural engineering or design fees, foundations, and any necessary permits. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., stall utilities, membership administration) are not covered, but you may include a modest contingency (up to 10 % of the total budget) for unforeseen installation expenses.

**Supporting Documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice Letter** – a signed statement from the local council confirming its auspice role, insurance coverage, and acceptance of financial and reporting responsibilities.  
2. **Project Plan** – a brief description (max 500 words) outlining the current garden activities, the specific climate‑resilience challenges you face, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will mitigate those challenges.  
3. **Detailed Budget** – an itemised spreadsheet showing quantities, unit costs, and total costs for each component (tanks, shade structure, labour, permits, contingency).  
4. **Quotes or Estimates** – at least two written quotes for major items (tanks and shade structure) to demonstrate cost‑effectiveness.  
5. **Evidence of Community Support** – letters of support from council representatives, local residents, or partner organisations, and any relevant photographs of the garden site.  
6. **Financial Overview** – a summary of your group’s recent income and expenditure (the $40,000 turnover figure) to illustrate financial viability and the need for external funding.

If you have any further questions—particularly regarding the auspice agreement format or the permissible percentage of contingency—please do not hesitate to contact me directly at grants@agency.gov.au or on 02 1234 5678. I encourage you to submit a draft budget for informal review before the final deadline; this often helps identify any missing elements early in the process.
