**Eligibility of Unincorporated Groups under Council Auspice**  
The grant program welcomes applications from both incorporated not‑for‑profit entities and unincorporated groups, provided that the group operates under a formal auspice arrangement with a legal entity that can assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant. An established council auspice typically satisfies this requirement, especially when the council holds the necessary insurances, holds any required permits, and can act as the primary point of contact for reporting and audit purposes. In practice, we have approved projects led by volunteer‑run gardens that are formally sponsored by their local councils, so your structure should be eligible. The key is to submit a clear auspice agreement that outlines the council’s responsibilities, the governance role it will play, and any indemnification provisions.

**Minimum and Maximum Grant Amounts**  
There is no fixed minimum or maximum grant size for this round; awards are determined on the merit of the proposal, the extent of community benefit, and the proportion of eligible costs that the applicant can realistically fund. Past awards have ranged from roughly $5,000 to $150,000, but applications that present well‑scoped projects with strong cost‑effectiveness often receive funding that covers 40‑70 % of total eligible expenses. If your budget aligns with the scope of the proposed rainwater tanks and shade structure, you should be able to demonstrate how the requested amount fits within these parameters.

**Eligibility of Infrastructure Costs**  
Infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures is expressly permitted under the “Resilience and Climate Adaptation” component of the grant. Both items are considered eligible because they directly enhance the garden’s capacity to operate during extreme heat events, thereby increasing community resilience. To maximize your chances, please ensure that the proposed equipment meets any relevant Australian standards (e.g., AS/NZS 3500 for rainwater tanks) and that the installation plan includes maintenance considerations.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare**  
We recommend assembling the following documentation to accompany your application:

1. **Auspice Agreement** – a signed memorandum of understanding with the council confirming insurance coverage, financial oversight, and reporting responsibilities.  
2. **Organizational Profile** – a brief description of the garden’s mission, volunteer composition, and community impact.  
3. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) showing the $40,000 turnover and sources of income.  
4. **Budget and Cash Flow** – a detailed budget for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ongoing maintenance costs, broken down line‑item style.  
5. **Project Plan** – a timeline with milestones, a description of the implementation methodology, and a risk‑mitigation strategy.  
6. **Proof of Insurance** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming that the project will be covered under the council’s policy.  
7. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, community groups, or other stakeholders that underscore the broader benefits of the project.  
8. **Site Plans or Drawings** – schematic layouts indicating where the tanks and shade structure will be placed.  

Please note that all documents should be clearly labeled, formatted consistently, and submitted in PDF format. If any of the required items are not yet available (for example, a formal auspice agreement), it is acceptable to indicate in your application that these will be finalized prior to submission, provided you outline the steps you will take to secure them.
