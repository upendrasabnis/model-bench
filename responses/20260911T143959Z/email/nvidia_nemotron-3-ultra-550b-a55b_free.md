Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarification on each of your questions.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local council are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the council is willing to act as the legal entity entering into the funding agreement. The council would be required to sign the grant deed, accept financial accountability, and ensure appropriate insurance coverage (public liability, workers’ compensation, and volunteer insurance) is in place for the project’s duration. Please ensure your application includes a signed letter from the council’s CEO or authorised delegate confirming their willingness to auspice the project and outlining the governance arrangements between the garden group and the council.

Second, the current grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure—falls squarely within the “capital infrastructure” category, which is an eligible cost provided the assets are permanently affixed, remain the property of the auspicing council (or are transferred via a formal asset agreement), and directly support the grant’s objectives of enhancing community resilience to climate impacts. Quotes from at least two licensed suppliers must accompany the application for any individual item exceeding $10,000.

Third, in terms of supporting documentation, please prepare the following: the council auspicing letter referenced above; your group’s most recent financial statement (a simple income and expenditure report is sufficient given your $40,000 turnover); evidence of land tenure or a licence to occupy the garden site for at least five years beyond project completion; two written quotes for each major infrastructure component; a brief project plan with milestones and a risk management matrix; and letters of support from at least two community stakeholders (e.g., a local school, neighbourhood house, or health service) demonstrating broader community benefit.

Finally, I strongly encourage you to attend the optional applicant webinar scheduled for 10:00 am AEST on 14 May; a calendar invitation and Zoom link are available on the grant webpage. If you have further questions after reviewing the guidelines, please do not hesitate to contact me directly.
