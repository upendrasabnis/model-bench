Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. We welcome applications from community-led initiatives like yours, and I am pleased to provide clarification on each of the points you have raised.

**Eligibility of auspiced groups:** Unincorporated groups operating under the auspices of a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) holds an Australian Business Number (ABN), maintains appropriate public liability insurance, and is willing to enter into a legally binding funding agreement on your behalf. The council must also provide a letter of support confirming their willingness to auspice the project and manage the financial acquittal responsibilities.

**Grant amount parameters:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. If the total project cost exceeds $50,000, you may still apply for the maximum grant amount provided you can demonstrate confirmed co-contributions (cash or in-kind) for the balance.

**Eligible costs – infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible expenditure category. Rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible items in the program guidelines, provided they are fixed assets that remain the property of the auspicing organisation or the community group. Costs for site preparation, installation, and necessary plumbing or engineering certification are also allowable. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not funded.

**Supporting documentation to prepare:** To streamline your application, please gather the following:
1. A letter of auspice from the council CEO or authorised delegate, including their ABN and insurance details.
2. Your group’s current constitution or rules of operation (even if informal).
3. A simple project plan with timeline, risk management strategy, and evidence of community need (e.g., temperature data, council heat-mapping reports).
4. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item (tanks, shade sails/structure, installation).
5. A detailed project budget showing GST-exclusive amounts, the grant amount sought, and any co-contributions.
6. Evidence of land tenure or a licence/lease agreement for the garden site (minimum five years remaining or a council commitment to ongoing tenure).
7. Your most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 annual turnover.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 28 February 2025. I strongly recommend submitting a draft at least one week prior so our team can conduct a preliminary eligibility check and provide feedback.
