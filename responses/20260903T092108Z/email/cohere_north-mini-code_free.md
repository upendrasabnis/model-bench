**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant programme welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. While most successful applicants are registered charities or incorporated associations, we also consider unincorporated groups provided they operate under a formal auspice arrangement with a municipal or governmental body. In your case, the fact that your garden is hosted by the local council and that the council holds the necessary insurances on your behalf satisfies the primary eligibility requirement for “organisational capacity.” To further strengthen your application, please include a letter from the council confirming the auspice relationship, outlining any responsibilities the council retains (e.g., liability, procurement) and confirming that you have authority to implement the project on its behalf.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
We do not stipulate a fixed minimum or maximum grant size. Instead, awards are determined by the scope, impact, and budget of each proposal relative to the overall funding pool for the round. Typical grants for infrastructure projects of the type you describe range from $15,000 to $75,000, but applications that demonstrate clear cost‑effectiveness and strong community benefit may receive funding outside this band. Your projected budget for the rainwater tanks and shade structure should be realistic and fully itemised; we will then assess it against the overall funding available for the round.

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes – infrastructure that enhances climate resilience, water security, and community wellbeing is fully eligible under the “Community Resilience” theme. Both rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures directly address the challenges you anticipate from hotter summers, aligning with the grant’s objective to support adaptive, sustainable community assets. When budgeting, please ensure you separate capital costs (tanks, structural components, installation) from ongoing maintenance expenses, as only capital expenditures are covered.

**Supporting documents we recommend preparing**  
1. **Project Narrative** – A concise description of the problem, proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Financial Statements** – A detailed line‑item budget for the tanks and shade structure, plus the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for an unincorporated group, a set of financial summaries for the past 12‑month period).  
3. **Organisational Profile** – Include a copy of the council’s auspice letter, a brief constitution or set of operating guidelines, and any relevant insurance certificates held by the council on your behalf.  
4. **Letters of Support** – At least two endorsements from local stakeholders (e.g., neighbouring businesses, community groups, or council representatives) confirming community need and support.  
5. **Risk Assessment and Management Plan** – Outline potential construction, safety, and environmental risks and how they will be mitigated.  
6. **Monitoring and Evaluation Framework** – A simple plan for tracking installation progress, usage statistics, and the broader community impact (e.g., water savings, reduced heat exposure).
