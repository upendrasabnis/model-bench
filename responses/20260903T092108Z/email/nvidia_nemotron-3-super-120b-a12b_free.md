Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the important work you are doing with your community garden. I am happy to clarify the eligibility criteria for the Community Resilience Grant and to outline the information you will need to prepare a strong application.

**Auspicing Arrangements**  
The grant program does accept applications from unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. As long as your council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will assume legal and financial responsibility for the grant funds (including insurance, reporting, and acquittal), your group meets the auspicing requirement. Please ensure the letter is on council letterhead, signed by an authorised officer, and includes the council’s Australian Business Number (ABN) and contact details.

**Grant Size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per successful applicant. Applications are assessed on the scale and impact of the proposed project, so you may request any amount within this band that aligns with the budget for your rainwater tanks and shade structure. If your total project cost exceeds $50,000, you will need to demonstrate co‑funding or in‑kind contributions for the balance.

**Eligible Costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate‑related stresses is an eligible expense. This includes the purchase and installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and associated fittings (e.g., guttering, pumps, and foundations). Ongoing operational costs (e.g., staff wages, utilities) are not covered, but you may include a modest contingency (up to 10 % of the total budget) for unforeseen installation expenses.

**Supporting Documentation**  
To substantiate your application, please prepare the following documents:

1. **Auspice Letter** – from the local council, as described above.  
2. **Project Plan** – a clear description of the rainwater tank and shade structure works, including site plans, specifications, timelines, and a risk‑management outline.  
3. **Detailed Budget** – itemised costs (quotations or supplier estimates) for tanks, foundations, plumbing, shade‑structure materials, labour, and any contingency. Indicate any council‑provided in‑kind support (e.g., site preparation) and confirm that the total requested amount falls within the $5,000–$50,000 range.  
4. **Evidence of Community Need** – brief data or testimonials showing how increasing summer temperatures affect garden usability and volunteer participation, and how the proposed infrastructure will mitigate those impacts.  
5. **Organisational Information** – a short profile of your garden (mission, volunteer structure, recent activities) and proof of the council’s auspice (e.g., minutes of a council meeting endorsing the partnership).  
6. **Financial Statements** – although your turnover is below the audit threshold, a simple income‑and‑expenditure statement for the past 12 months will help demonstrate fiscal responsibility.

Once you have assembled these items, you can submit your application via the online portal before the closing date. Should you encounter any difficulties or require further clarification on specific budget lines, please do not hesitate to contact me directly at grants@agency.gov.au or on 02 1234 5678.

We look forward to the possibility of supporting your garden’s efforts to build resilience against a changing climate.
