**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per project. Applications are assessed on the scale and impact of the proposed activities, so a request that aligns with the lower end of this range (e.g., covering the purchase and installation of rainwater tanks and a shade structure) is perfectly acceptable, provided you can demonstrate clear outcomes and value for money.

**Eligible costs – infrastructure**  
Capital works that enhance resilience to climate‑related stressors, including water‑harvesting systems and shade‑providing structures, are explicitly listed as eligible expenditure items. Therefore, the rainwater tanks and shade structure you propose would qualify, as long as the costs are reasonable, directly tied to the project, and supported by quotes or supplier estimates.
