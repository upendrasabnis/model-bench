**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from unincorporated community groups, provided they operate under the formal auspices of a local government entity that can demonstrate fiscal responsibility and liability coverage. In your case, having the council hold the relevant insurances on your behalf satisfies this requirement. The council’s involvement also serves as a reliable indicator of community support and oversight, which we view positively during the assessment process.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
We do not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum grant size; rather, we allocate funds based on the merit of the proposal, the scope of the activities, and the available budget for the round. Typical awards for infrastructure projects of the nature you describe range from $15,000 to $75,000, but applications that fall outside this band are still considered if they are well‑justified and align with the program’s objectives.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes, capital‑intensive infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category. These items directly support the resilience of community spaces by reducing reliance on municipal water supplies and mitigating the impacts of extreme heat—key priorities for the current grant round. Please ensure that any proposed purchases are clearly linked to the operational continuity of the garden and include specifications that demonstrate durability and suitability for the local climate.  

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documentation:  

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem you are addressing, the proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Financial Projections** – a detailed breakdown of all costs (including equipment, installation, and any contingency funds) alongside a simple cash‑flow statement showing your current turnover and how the grant will be integrated.  
3. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your group’s mission, volunteer base, and the council’s auspice agreement (if available).  
4. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from the council, local businesses, or community members that attest to the broader community benefit.  
5. **Insurance Certificate** – confirmation that the council holds appropriate public liability and property insurance covering the proposed works.  
6. **Site Plans or Drawings** – simple sketches indicating where the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be placed.  
7. **Evaluation Framework** – outline of how you will measure success (e.g., water usage reduction, increased garden operating days during heatwaves) and any plans for ongoing maintenance.  

We encourage you to submit a draft of your application for a preliminary review before the formal deadline. This can help identify any gaps early on and ensure that your proposal is positioned for the best possible chance of success.
