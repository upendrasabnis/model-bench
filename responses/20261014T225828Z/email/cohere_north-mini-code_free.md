Thank you for reaching out and for the opportunity to clarify the eligibility requirements for our Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate your proactive approach in gathering the necessary information before committing to a full application, and I’m happy to address each of the points you raised.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations, including unincorporated groups that operate under the formal auspice of a local government body. When an unincorporated group is sponsored by a council, the council typically acts as the primary applicant and guarantor for the grant, assuming responsibility for compliance, reporting, and any financial obligations that may arise. In practice, this means that if your council is willing to endorse the project, provide the required insurance coverage, and sign the necessary agreements, your group can proceed as an eligible applicant. We do require a formal letter of support from the council confirming its willingness to act in this capacity, as well as a copy of the council’s insurance certificate that covers the project activities.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed grant size; rather, awards are determined on the merit of the proposal, the scope of the activities, and the availability of funds for the current round. Historically, grants in this round have ranged from approximately $5,000 to $50,000, but we have approved projects both below and above these figures when the justification and budget were compelling. Your proposed infrastructure—rainwater tanks and a shade structure—falls squarely within the typical cost envelope for resilience‑focused improvements, so you should be able to tailor your budget accordingly.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes, capital‑equipment projects that enhance environmental resilience and community wellbeing are expressly eligible under the “Infrastructure and Climate Adaptation” category of the grant. Rainwater tanks directly support water‑security objectives, while a shade structure contributes to heat‑mitigation and extends the usability of the garden during extreme summer conditions. Both items must be essential to the project’s goals, reasonably priced, and documented in the project budget with itemized costs and vendor quotes.

**4. Supporting documents to prepare**  
To ensure a smooth review process, we recommend assembling the following documents (please note that the exact checklist can be found on our website, but the items below are almost always required):

- **Project Proposal Form** – a completed application form that outlines the problem, objectives, activities, timeline, and expected outcomes.  
- **Organizational Profile** – a brief description of your group’s mission, volunteer base, and the role of the council as auspice.  
- **Letter of Support from the Council** – confirming endorsement, insurance coverage, and willingness to act as fiscal sponsor.  
- **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and cash‑flow summary) demonstrating an annual turnover of roughly $40,000.  
- **Budget and Narrative** – a detailed budget that itemises equipment costs, installation, and any contingency funds, accompanied by a narrative that explains how each line item contributes to the project’s resilience goals.  
- **Vendor Quotes and Specifications** – at least three quotations for the rainwater tanks and shade structure, including technical specifications and installation details.  
- **Risk Assessment and Insurance Certificate** – a current public liability insurance certificate (provided by the council) and a brief risk‑assessment document outlining safety measures for the new infrastructure.  
- **Monitoring and Evaluation Plan** – a simple framework describing how you will measure success (e.g., water usage reduction, increased garden usage during heat events) and any reporting requirements.  
- **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, community groups, or other stakeholders that highlight the broader community benefit.

If you can provide these documents in a single PDF or folder, it will make the internal review process more efficient. Should any of the items be unavailable in their final form, please let us know as early as possible; we can discuss alternative evidence that may satisfy the eligibility criteria.
