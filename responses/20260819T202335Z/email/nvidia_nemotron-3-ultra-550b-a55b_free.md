**Auspicing Arrangements & Eligibility:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are absolutely eligible to apply. The council must be the legal applicant and sign the funding agreement on your behalf, and they must hold current public liability insurance covering the project activities. Please ensure the auspicing arrangement is documented in a written agreement (or Memorandum of Understanding) that outlines the roles, responsibilities, and financial management obligations of both parties.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (exclusive of GST). Your proposed project scope appears to fall comfortably within this range.

**Eligible Costs:** Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is an eligible expenditure category. Costs may include materials, contractor labour, necessary permits, and reasonable project management fees. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., utilities, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**
1. A signed letter of support / auspicing confirmation from the council CEO or authorised delegate.
2. The council’s current Certificate of Currency for Public Liability Insurance ($20M minimum).
3. Your group’s most recent annual financial statement (or a simple income/expenditure summary signed by your treasurer).
4. Quotes (minimum two) for the tanks and shade structure.
5. A brief project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and evidence of community need (e.g., letters of support, usage statistics).
6. If applicable, evidence of landowner consent for the infrastructure installation.

Your annual turnover of $40,000 is well within the program’s thresholds and does not affect eligibility.
