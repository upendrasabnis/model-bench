**Eligible infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances climate‑related resilience—such as water capture and storage systems, shading, cooling shelters, and related site works—is explicitly listed as an eligible expense. The rain‑water tanks, associated plumbing, and the shade structure you intend to install are therefore eligible costs, provided they are *new* or *upgrade* investments that improve the garden’s capacity to operate during extreme heat.
