**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspice arrangement with an eligible entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., is a legal entity with an ABN, holds appropriate insurances, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant). Your council’s willingness to hold insurances on your behalf is a positive indicator. You will need to provide a signed auspice agreement or letter of support from the council confirming the arrangement and their acceptance of the grant obligations.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your project scope appears to fall comfortably within this range.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures to mitigate heat impacts—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category in the program guidelines. Installation, materials, and necessary site preparation are all allowable. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water bills, maintenance) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:** In addition to the auspice agreement, you should gather:
- Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report (showing the ~$40,000 turnover)
- A simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and budget breakdown (quotes for tanks and shade structure)
- Evidence of community need/benefit (e.g., letters of support, usage statistics, photos of current site)
- Council’s public liability and workers’ compensation certificates of currency
- Your group’s governance document (constitution, rules, or terms of reference)
