First, regarding organisational eligibility: unincorporated groups that operate under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are indeed eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be willing to enter into a funding agreement on your behalf, accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant, and hold the required insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation/volunteer insurance, etc.). As your council already holds the relevant insurances, you appear well positioned in this respect. You will need to provide a signed letter from the council confirming their willingness to auspice the project, along with a copy of their current certificate of currency for public liability insurance (minimum $20 million).

Second, the grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per application. Your proposed project—involving the purchase and installation of rainwater tanks and a shade structure—falls squarely within the program’s definition of eligible infrastructure. Capital works that enhance climate resilience, water security, and the capacity of community facilities to operate during extreme heat are priority activities. Please note, however, that routine maintenance, consumables, and operational salaries are not funded.

With an annual turnover of approximately $40,000, your organisation meets the financial viability threshold. You should prepare the following supporting documents: (1) the auspicing letter and insurance certificate mentioned above; (2) a simple project plan outlining scope, timeline, risk management, and volunteer roles; (3) two written quotes for each major cost item (tanks, shade structure, installation); (4) a project budget using the template provided in the guidelines; (5) evidence of land tenure or permission to undertake works on the site; and (6) your most recent annual financial statement or treasurer’s report.
