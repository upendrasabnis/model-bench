**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant programme is designed to support community‑based projects that deliver measurable resilience benefits, and we explicitly welcome applications from unincorporated groups provided they are formally auspiced by a local government entity. In your case, the fact that your garden operates under the auspices of the council—and that the council holds the required insurances—satisfies the “organisational capacity” requirement outlined in our guidelines. The council’s involvement also serves as a reliable reference for financial oversight and accountability. Therefore, your group qualifies as an eligible applicant.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum award. Instead, we allocate funding based on the scope, impact, and cost‑effectiveness of the proposed project. Historically, awards have ranged from approximately $15,000 to $75,000, depending on the total project budget. Because your project focuses on water harvesting and climate‑adaptation infrastructure, we anticipate that a grant in the $30,000‑$45,000 range could be appropriate, assuming the total budget aligns with the scope of work you describe.

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rain‑water tanks and shade structures**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances environmental resilience and operational continuity is fully eligible under the “Climate‑Adaptation and Resource‑Efficiency” component of the grant. Rain‑water tanks, shade structures, and associated site works are considered allowable costs, provided they are directly linked to the project’s resilience objectives and are justified in your needs assessment. We do require that such capital items be detailed in the project plan, with clear specifications, cost breakdowns, and evidence of how they will mitigate the impacts of hotter summers.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documents:

1. **Project Proposal Form** – completed with all mandatory fields, including a concise description, objectives, timeline, and budget breakdown.  
2. **Needs Assessment** – a brief report outlining the current challenges (e.g., water scarcity, heat stress) and how the proposed infrastructure will address them.  
3. **Organizational Profile** – a one‑page overview of your group, its volunteer composition, and the council’s auspice agreement (if available).  
4. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements for the last fiscal year, together with a cash‑flow projection for the upcoming 12 months.  
5. **Insurance Certificate** – confirmation from the council that it holds the necessary public liability and professional indemnity coverage for the project.  
6. **Site Plans and Technical Specifications** – detailed drawings or sketches of the proposed tank and shade structure placements, along with supplier quotations or provisional cost estimates.  
7. **Community Engagement Evidence** – minutes from council or community meetings confirming support, and a stakeholder map illustrating who will benefit from the project.  
8. **Sustainability and Maintenance Plan** – a documented approach for ongoing operation, repair, and water‑usage monitoring after the grant period.
