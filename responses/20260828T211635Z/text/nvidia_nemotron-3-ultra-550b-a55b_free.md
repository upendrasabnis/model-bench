NONPROFIT GRANT WRITING BEST PRACTICES  
A COMPREHENSIVE GUIDE FROM RESEARCH TO POST‑AWARD REPORTING  

SECTION 1: UNDERSTANDING THE GRANT LANDSCAPE  
1.1 IDENTIFY YOUR ORGANIZATION’S MISSION AND FUNDING PRIORITIES  
1.2 MAP THE TYPES OF FUNDERS: FOUNDATIONS, GOVERNMENT AGENCIES, CORPORATE GIVING, INDIVIDUAL DONORS  
1.3 TRACK FUNDER CYCLES: ANNUAL, QUARTERLY, ROLLING DEADLINES  
1.4 BUILD A FUNDER DATABASE: NAME, CONTACT, FOCUS AREAS, GIVING HISTORY, APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS  
1.5 USE TOOLS: FOUNDATION DIRECTORY ONLINE, GRANTS.GOV, GUIDESTAR, LOCAL COMMUNITY FOUNDATION PORTALS  

CHECKLIST – INITIAL FUNDER RESEARCH  
[ ] LIST TOP 20 POTENTIAL FUNDERS THAT ALIGN WITH MISSION  
[ ] RECORD EACH FUNDER’S PRIOR GRANT SIZES AND GEOGRAPHIC FOCUS  
[ ] NOTE APPLICATION DEADLINES AND REQUIRED FORMATS  
[ ] IDENTIFY ANY PRE‑APPLICATION REQUIREMENTS (LOI, CONCEPT PAPER)  
[ ] ASSIGN A TEAM MEMBER TO MONITOR EACH FUNDER’S NEWSLETTER OR ALERTS  

SECTION 2: DEEP DIVE INTO FUNDER PROFILES  
2.1 READ RECENT ANNUAL REPORTS AND 990 FORMS  
2.2 ANALYZE PAST GRANTEES: PROJECT TYPES, OUTCOMES, BUDGET RANGES  
2.3 IDENTIFY FUNDER’S STRATEGIC PRIORITIES AND ANY THEMATIC INITIATIVES  
2.4 CONTACT PROGRAM OFFICERS FOR CLARIFICATION (EMAIL OR PHONE)  
2.5 DOCUMENT KEY INSIGHTS IN A ONE‑PAGE FUNDER SNAPSHOT  

EXAMPLE – FUNDER SNAPSHOT (COMMUNITY HEALTH FOUNDATION)  
MISSION: IMPROVE ACCESS TO PRIMARY CARE IN UNDERSERVED URBAN NEIGHBORHOODS  
TYPICAL GRANT SIZE: $50,000‑$150,000  
PRIORITY AREAS: TELEHEALTH, COMMUNITY HEALTH WORKERS, HEALTH LITERACY  
APPLICATION DEADLINE: MARCH 15 (ANNUAL)  
REQUIRED ATTACHMENTS: LOGIC MODEL, DETAILED BUDGET, EVALUATION PLAN  
CONTACT: JANE DOE, PROGRAM OFFICER, JANE.DOE@CHF.ORG  

SECTION 3: CRAFTING A COMPELLING NEEDS STATEMENT  
3.1 DEFINE THE PROBLEM WITH DATA: PREVALENCE, DEMOGRAPHICS, TRENDS  
3.2 USE LOCAL, STATE, AND NATIONAL STATISTICS; CITE SOURCES  
3.3 CONNECT THE PROBLEM TO YOUR ORGANIZATION’S UNIQUE POSITION  
3.4 ARTICULATE THE HUMAN IMPACT THROUGH STORIES OR CASE STUDIES  
3.5 KEEP IT CONCISE: 300‑500 WORDS MAXIMUM  

TEMPLATE – NEEDS STATEMENT STRUCTURE  
1. OPENING HOOK (ONE SENTENCE THAT CAPTURES URGENCY)  
2. QUANTITATIVE EVIDENCE (3‑4 KEY DATA POINTS)  
3. QUALITATIVE EVIDENCE (ONE PERSONAL STORY OR QUOTE)  
4. GAP ANALYSIS (WHAT CURRENT SERVICES MISS)  
5. YOUR ORGANIZATION’S READINESS (TRACK RECORD, CAPACITY)  

CHECKLIST – NEEDS STATEMENT QUALITY  
[ ] ALL STATISTICS ARE CURRENT (WITHIN 2 YEARS) AND SOURCED  
[ ] PROBLEM IS SPECIFIC TO THE TARGET POPULATION AND GEOGRAPHY  
[ ] NO JARGON; LANGUAGE ACCESSIBLE TO NON‑EXPERTS  
[ ] CLEAR LINK BETWEEN NEED AND PROPOSED SOLUTION  
[ ] WORD COUNT WITHIN FUNDER LIMIT  

SECTION 4: DEVELOPING GOALS, OBJECTIVES, AND OUTCOMES  
4.1 DISTINGUISH GOALS (BROAD, LONG‑TERM) FROM OBJECTIVES (SMART)  
4.2 WRITE OBJECTIVES USING THE SMART FRAMEWORK: SPECIFIC, MEASURABLE, ACHIEVABLE, RELEVANT, TIME‑BOUND  
4.3 ALIGN EACH OBJECTIVE WITH A MEASURABLE OUTCOME INDICATOR  
4.4 MAP OBJECTIVES TO FUNDER’S PRIORITY AREAS  

EXAMPLE – SMART OBJECTIVE  
GOAL: REDUCE CHILDHOOD OBESITY IN EASTSIDE NEIGHBORHOOD  
OBJECTIVE: BY DECEMBER 31, 2026, INCREASE THE PERCENTAGE OF CHILDREN AGED 6‑12 RECEIVING WEEKLY NUTRITION EDUCATION FROM 15% TO 45% AS MEASURED BY PROGRAM ENROLLMENT RECORDS.  

CHECKLIST – OBJECTIVE VALIDATION  
[ ] EACH OBJECTIVE HAS A CLEAR METRIC AND DATA SOURCE  
[ ] TIMEFRAME MATCHES GRANT PERIOD  
[ ] OBJECTIVE IS REALISTIC GIVEN RESOURCES AND STAFFING  
[ ] OBJECTIVE DIRECTLY SUPPORTS THE OVERALL GOAL  

SECTION 5: BUILDING A LOGIC MODEL  
5.1 COMPONENTS: INPUTS → ACTIVITIES → OUTPUTS → OUTCOMES → IMPACT  
5.2 USE A VISUAL TABLE OR FLOWCHART (ONE PAGE)  
5.3 INCLUDE ASSUMPTIONS AND EXTERNAL FACTORS  
5.4 LINK EACH OUTPUT TO A SPECIFIC OBJECTIVE  
5.5 REVISE LOGIC MODEL AFTER FUNDER FEEDBACK  

LOGIC MODEL TEMPLATE (TEXT VERSION)  
INPUTS: STAFF (2 FTE), VOLUNTEERS (10), CURRICULUM MATERIALS, FUNDING $120,000  
ACTIVITIES: DEVELOP CURRICULUM, TRAIN COMMUNITY HEALTH WORKERS, DELIVER WEEKLY WORKSHOPS  
OUTPUTS: 120 WORKSHOPS DELIVERED, 300 CHILDREN REACHED, 10 HEALTH WORKERS CERTIFIED  
SHORT‑TERM OUTCOMES: INCREASED NUTRITION KNOWLEDGE (PRE/POST TEST), IMPROVED FOOD CHOICES  
INTERMEDIATE OUTCOMES: REDUCED BMI PERCENTILE IN TARGET GROUP AFTER 12 MONTHS  
LONG‑TERM IMPACT: LOWER COMMUNITY CHILDHOOD OBESITY RATE BY 10% OVER 5 YEARS  
ASSUMPTIONS: SCHOOL PARTNERSHIPS REMAIN STABLE, FUNDING CONTINUES THROUGH YEAR 2  

CHECKLIST – LOGIC MODEL COMPLETENESS  
[ ] ALL INPUTS LISTED WITH QUANTITIES  
[ ] EVERY ACTIVITY MAPS TO AT LEAST ONE OUTPUT  
[ ] OUTPUTS ARE COUNTABLE AND TIME‑BOUND  
[ ] OUTCOMES ARE MEASURABLE AND TIED TO INDICATORS  
[ ] ASSUMPTIONS AND RISKS ARE DOCUMENTED  

SECTION 6: DESIGNING THE PROJECT BUDGET  
6.1 FOLLOW FUNDER’S BUDGET CATEGORIES (PERSONNEL, FRINGE, TRAVEL, EQUIPMENT, SUPPLIES, CONTRACTUAL, INDIRECT)  
6.2 USE REALISTIC UNIT COSTS; PROVIDE JUSTIFICATION NARRATIVE FOR EACH LINE ITEM  
6.3 INCLUDE MATCHING FUNDS OR IN‑KIND CONTRIBUTIONS IF REQUIRED  
6.4 SHOW YEAR‑BY‑YEAR BREAKDOWN FOR MULTI‑YEAR GRANTS  
6.5 ENSURE TOTAL REQUEST DOES NOT EXCEED FUNDER’S MAXIMUM  

BUDGET NARRATIVE EXAMPLE (PERSONNEL)  
PROJECT DIRECTOR (0.5 FTE) – $45,000/YEAR – OVERSEES PROGRAM DESIGN, STAFF SUPERVISION, REPORTING  
COMMUNITY HEALTH WORKER (2 FTE) – $30,000/EACH/YEAR – DELIVER WORKSHOPS, COLLECT DATA  
DATA ANALYST (0.25 FTE) – $20,000/YEAR – MANAGE DATABASE, PRODUCE QUARTERLY REPORTS  

CHECKLIST – BUDGET ACCURACY  
[ ] ALL LINE ITEMS MATCH FUNDER’S CATEGORY LIST  
[ ] FRINGE RATE CALCULATED PER ORGANIZATION POLICY  
[ ] INDIRECT COST RATE APPROVED (OR WAIVED) BY FUNDER  
[ ] NO UNAUTHORIZED EXPENSES (E.G., LOBBYING)  
[ ] BUDGET TOTALS RECONCILE WITH NARRATIVE AND LOGIC MODEL  

SECTION 7: WRITING THE PROJECT NARRATIVE  
7.1 FOLLOW FUNDER’S OUTLINE EXACTLY (SECTION HEADINGS, WORD LIMITS)  
7.2 USE ACTIVE VOICE, CLEAR HEADINGS, AND BULLET POINTS FOR READABILITY  
7.3 INTEGRATE NEEDS STATEMENT, GOALS, LOGIC MODEL, AND BUDGET INTO A COHERENT STORY  
7.4 HIGHLIGHT ORGANIZATIONAL CAPACITY: STAFF EXPERTISE, PAST SUCCESS, GOVERNANCE  
7.5 ADDRESS SUSTAINABILITY: PLAN FOR CONTINUATION AFTER GRANT ENDS  

NARRATIVE SECTION QUICK‑REFERENCE  
- EXECUTIVE SUMMARY (1 PAGE)  
- STATEMENT OF NEED (SEE SECTION 3)  
- PROJECT DESCRIPTION (GOALS, OBJECTIVES, ACTIVITIES)  
- LOGIC MODEL (VISUAL OR TEXT)  
- EVALUATION PLAN (SEE SECTION 8)  
- ORGANIZATIONAL CAPACITY  
- SUSTAINABILITY PLAN  
- BUDGET AND BUDGET NARRATIVE  

CHECKLIST – NARRATIVE COMPLETENESS  
[ ] EVERY REQUIRED SECTION PRESENT AND LABELED  
[ ] WORD COUNT PER SECTION WITHIN LIMITS  
[ ] CONSISTENT TERMINOLOGY THROUGHOUT  
[ ] ALL CLAIMS SUPPORTED BY DATA OR CITATIONS  
[ ] PROOFREAD FOR GRAMMAR, SPELLING, AND FORMATTING  

SECTION 8: DEVELOPING AN EVALUATION PLAN  
8.1 DEFINE EVALUATION QUESTIONS (PROCESS, OUTCOME, IMPACT)  
8.2 SELECT INDICATORS THAT ARE SPECIFIC, MEASURABLE, AND TIME‑BOUND  
8.3 CHOOSE DATA COLLECTION METHODS: SURVEYS, INTERVIEWS, ADMINISTRATIVE DATA, OBSERVATION  
8.4 ESTABLISH BASELINE DATA COLLECTION BEFORE PROGRAM START  
8.5 PLAN FOR DATA ANALYSIS, REPORTING FREQUENCY, AND RESPONSIBLE STAFF  

EVALUATION PLAN TEMPLATE (TEXT)  
QUESTION 1: TO WHAT EXTENT DID WORKSHOPS INCREASE NUTRITION KNOWLEDGE?  
INDICATOR: AVERAGE PRE‑POST TEST SCORE CHANGE  
METHOD: PAPER SURVEY ADMINISTERED AT FIRST AND LAST WORKSHOP  
TIMELINE: BASELINE WEEK 1, POST‑TEST WEEK 12  
RESPONSIBLE: DATA ANALYST  

QUESTION 2: DID CHILDREN’S BMI PERCENTILE DECREASE AFTER 12 MONTHS?  
INDICATOR: CHANGE IN BMI PERCENTILE FROM SCHOOL HEALTH RECORDS  
METHOD: SECURE DATA SHARING AGREEMENT WITH SCHOOL DISTRICT  
TIMELINE: BASELINE AT ENROLLMENT, FOLLOW‑UP AT 12 MONTHS  
RESPONSIBLE: PROJECT DIRECTOR  

CHECKLIST – EVALUATION READINESS  
[ ] EVALUATION QUESTIONS ALIGN WITH LOGIC MODEL OUTCOMES  
[ ] INDICATORS HAVE DEFINED DATA SOURCES AND COLLECTION TOOLS  
[ ] IRB OR ETHICAL REVIEW CONSIDERED IF HUMAN SUBJECTS INVOLVED  
[ ] DATA MANAGEMENT PLAN INCLUDES CONFIDENTIALITY AND STORAGE  
[ ] REPORTING SCHEDULE MATCHES FUNDER REQUIREMENTS  

SECTION 9: PREPARING SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS  
9.1 LETTERS OF SUPPORT / MEMORANDA OF UNDERSTANDING FROM PARTNERS  
9.2 ORGANIZATIONAL BUDGET (CURRENT YEAR) AND AUDITED FINANCIALS  
9.3 BOARD RESOLUTION AUTHORIZING APPLICATION  
9.4 IRS 501(C)(3) DETERMINATION LETTER (OR EQUIVALENT)  
9.5 KEY STAFF RESUMES OR BIOS (MAX 2 PAGES EACH)  

CHECKLIST – ATTACHMENTS READY  
[ ] ALL REQUIRED ATTACHMENTS LISTED IN FUNDER GUIDELINES  
[ ] EACH DOCUMENT NAMED PER FUNDER’S NAMING CONVENTION  
[ ] PDF FILES ARE SEARCHABLE AND NOT PASSWORD‑PROTECTED  
[ ] FILE SIZES MEET UPLOAD LIMITS  
[ ] INTERNAL REVIEW COMPLETED FOR ACCURACY  

SECTION 10: SUBMISSION STRATEGY AND TIMELINE  
10.1 CREATE A MASTER CALENDAR WITH INTERNAL DEADLINES (DRAFT, REVIEW, FINAL)  
10.2 ASSIGN LEAD WRITER, REVIEWER, AND APPROVER FOR EACH SECTION  
10.3 CONDUCT A MOCK SUBMISSION USING FUNDER’S ONLINE PORTAL (IF AVAILABLE)  
10.4 BACKUP ALL FILES IN CLOUD AND LOCAL DRIVE  
10.5 SUBMIT AT LEAST 48 HOURS BEFORE DEADLINE TO AVOID TECHNICAL ISSUES  

SAMPLE TIMELINE (12‑WEEK PRE‑DEADLINE)  
WEEK 1‑2: FUNDER RESEARCH & SNAPSHOT COMPLETION  
WEEK 3‑4: NEEDS STATEMENT, GOALS, LOGIC MODEL DRAFTS  
WEEK 5: BUDGET DRAFT & NARRATIVE OUTLINE  
WEEK 6: FULL NARRATIVE FIRST DRAFT  
WEEK 7: INTERNAL PEER REVIEW (TWO REVIEWERS)  
WEEK 8: REVISE BASED ON FEEDBACK  
WEEK 9: FINALIZE BUDGET, ATTACHMENTS, EVALUATION PLAN  
WEEK 10: FINAL PROOFREAD, FORMAT CHECK  
WEEK 11: MOCK SUBMISSION, RESOLVE PORTAL ERRORS  
WEEK 12: OFFICIAL SUBMISSION, CONFIRM RECEIPT  

CHECKLIST – SUBMISSION READINESS  
[ ] ALL SECTIONS COMPLETE AND APPROVED  
[ ] FILE NAMES AND FORMATS MATCH FUNDER SPECIFICATIONS  
[ ] CONFIRMATION EMAIL OR RECEIPT SAVED  
[ ] INTERNAL COPY ARCHIVED WITH VERSION NUMBER  
[ ] TEAM DEBRIEF SCHEDULED FOR LESSONS LEARNED  

SECTION 11: POST‑AWARD MANAGEMENT  
11.1 REVIEW GRANT AGREEMENT: REPORTING DATES, ALLOWABLE COSTS, AMENDMENT PROCESS  
11.2 SET UP GRANT TRACKING SYSTEM (SPREADSHEET OR GRANT MANAGEMENT SOFTWARE)  
11.3 ESTABLISH INTERNAL REPORTING CALENDAR (QUARTERLY, SEMI‑ANNUAL, FINAL)  
11.4 DESIGNATE A GRANT COMPLIANCE OFFICER  
11.5 COMMUNICATE REGULARLY WITH PROGRAM OFFICER (PROGRESS UPDATES, CHALLENGES)

5. BUDGET EXPENDITURES VS. PLAN (PERCENTAGE)  
6. CHALLENGES & MITIGATION ACTIONS  
7. PLANNED ACTIVITIES NEXT QUARTER  
8. SIGNATURE OF PROJECT DIRECTOR AND FINANCE OFFICER

[ ] IN‑KIND CONTRIBUTIONS DOCUMENTED WITH VALUATION METHOD  
[ ] ANY BUDGET REVISIONS SUBMITTED PER FUNDER POLICY  
[ ] FINAL REPORT SUBMITTED BY DEADLINE WITH ALL REQUIRED ATTACHMENTS
