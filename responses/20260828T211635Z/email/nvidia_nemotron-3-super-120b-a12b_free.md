**1. Auspicing arrangements**  
The Community Resilience Grant expressly permits applications from unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. As long as the council provides a formal letter of auspice confirming that it will assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds (including insurance coverage and reporting obligations), your garden will meet the eligibility criterion. Please ensure the auspice letter is on council letterhead, signed by an authorised officer, and includes the council’s ABN/ACN and the period of auspice.

**2. Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your project’s scope, a request in the $15,000‑$25,000 band would be typical, but you should align the budget with the actual cost of the rainwater tanks and shade structure.

**3. Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, solar‑powered irrigation, and related site works—is considered an eligible cost. The grant can cover the purchase and installation of equipment, necessary permits, and professional design or engineering fees. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., stall supplies, membership administration) are not fundable under this program.

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:
- A signed auspice letter from the local council (as described above).  
- A detailed project plan outlining objectives, timelines, milestones, and how the infrastructure will improve resilience to heat extremes.  
- An itemised budget with quotes (or at least three indicative quotes) for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, and any associated works.  
- Evidence of community support (e.g., letters of endorsement from residents, council representatives, or local organisations).  
- A copy of your group’s current financial statement or a simple income‑expenditure summary showing the ≈ $40,000 annual turnover.  
- Proof of the council’s insurance coverage for the garden’s activities (certificate of currency).
