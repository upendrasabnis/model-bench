First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are absolutely eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be willing to enter into a funding agreement on your behalf, hold the relevant insurances (as your council already does), and accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a formal letter of support from the council confirming their auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and evidence of their current public liability insurance.

Second, the current grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000. Given your project scope and annual turnover of approximately $40,000, your proposed budget would likely fall well within this range. Please note that we generally expect applicants to demonstrate some capacity for co-contribution, whether in-kind (volunteer labour, donated materials) or cash, though this is not a strict eligibility requirement for this round.

Third, infrastructure costs such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are considered eligible capital expenditure, provided they are directly linked to the project’s resilience outcomes—specifically, adapting to climate impacts such as heat and water scarcity. You should include itemised quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure component.

Finally, in addition to the auspicing letter and quotes, you should prepare: your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report; a simple project plan with timelines; evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site; and a brief risk management plan addressing installation and ongoing maintenance. The application form and detailed guidelines are available on our website, and I encourage you to review the assessment criteria carefully before drafting your response.
