**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and assume financial and reporting responsibility for the grant. Your council’s willingness to hold insurances and act as auspice is a positive indicator; we will require a signed letter from the council confirming this arrangement, including their ABN and agreement to the grant terms.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project scope appears to fall comfortably within this range.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly builds community resilience to climate impacts is an eligible cost category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure designed to extend safe operating hours during extreme heat both qualify, provided they are fixed assets on land where you have documented tenure (e.g., a lease, licence, or letter of support from the landowner). Quotes from licensed contractors must accompany the application.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**
1. Council auspicing letter (on letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).
2. Evidence of land tenure or permission for the infrastructure.
3. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item.
4. Your most recent annual financial statement or treasurer’s report (showing the ~$40,000 turnover).
5. A simple project plan with timeline, risk management, and volunteer capacity.
6. Letters of community support (minimum two) demonstrating broader benefit.
