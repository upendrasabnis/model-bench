Thank you for reaching out and for the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria for our Community Resilience Grant round. I’m pleased to provide the information you requested and to support your planning for a strong application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
Our grant program does accept applications from unincorporated community groups, provided they operate under the formal auspice of a local government or other recognized entity that can demonstrate fiscal responsibility and liability coverage. In your case, being auspiced by the council – which holds the necessary insurances and can act as the primary point of contact for grant funds – satisfies this requirement. However, please be aware that the council will need to sign the grant agreement and be listed as the implementing organisation on the application form.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum award. Instead, we assess each proposal against the program’s strategic priorities and the overall budget available for the round. Historically, awards have ranged from $5,000 to $50,000, but we are prepared to consider projects of any scale that demonstrate clear community benefit, sustainability, and alignment with the grant’s objectives. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should be detailed in the budget section, and we can discuss whether additional funding sources (e.g., council contributions, in‑kind donations) are required to strengthen the proposal.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures is explicitly listed as an eligible expense under the “Climate‑Resilient Community Assets” category of this grant. Both items must be directly linked to the project’s resilience goals – in your case, enhancing water security and providing cooler operating conditions during hot summers. We recommend that you include a clear justification linking each component to the anticipated climate‑adaptation outcomes.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To ensure a smooth review process, we suggest you compile the following documentation:

1. **Project plan** – a one‑page summary outlining the objectives, timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget breakdown** – itemized costs for equipment, installation, contingency, and any in‑kind contributions.  
3. **Proof of auspice** – a letter from the council confirming its support, insurance coverage, and willingness to act as the implementing body.  
4. **Organisational profile** – a brief description of your volunteer team, governance structure (even if informal), and any previous community projects.  
5. **Community engagement evidence** – sign‑up sheets, letters of support from local residents or businesses, and any existing membership data.  
6. **Site plans or sketches** – showing where the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be located.  
7. **Risk assessment and safety plan** – particularly important for the installation and ongoing use of water and shade infrastructure.  
8. **Financial statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for a small group, a simple cash flow statement) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover figure.

We recommend that you assemble these documents in a clear, concise format and upload them through our online portal once you decide to proceed with a full application. If you have any questions about the specific formatting requirements or need assistance tailoring any of the documents, please do not hesitate to contact me directly.
