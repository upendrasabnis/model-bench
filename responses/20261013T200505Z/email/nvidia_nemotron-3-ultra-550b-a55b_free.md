Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in an application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, unincorporated groups operating under the auspices of a local council are eligible to apply, provided the council is willing to act as the legal entity and accept the grant agreement on your behalf. The council must hold appropriate insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation, and professional indemnity where relevant) and be able to provide a letter of support confirming their auspicing arrangement, including their ABN and a statement that they will administer the funds in accordance with the grant guidelines. As your council already holds the relevant insurances, you appear well positioned in this regard.

Second, there is no minimum grant amount, but the maximum request for this round is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure to maintain operations during extreme heat—falls squarely within the program’s objective of building community resilience to climate impacts. Capital infrastructure of this nature is an eligible cost, provided it is permanently affixed, located on land with secure tenure (or a long‑term lease/licence), and directly supports the stated resilience outcomes. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not funded.

With an annual turnover of approximately $40,000, you meet the financial viability threshold for small community organisations. You will need to supply the following supporting documents with your application:
- Council auspicing letter (as described above)
- Evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure
- Two quotes for each major cost item (tanks, shade structure, installation)
- A simple project budget showing grant funds and any in‑kind or cash contributions
- Your most recent financial statement (or treasurer’s report) and a brief governance statement outlining how the garden is managed
- Letters of support from at least two community stakeholders (e.g., local school, neighbourhood house, or health service)

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November. I recommend starting the online form early so you can save drafts and attach documents as they are finalised. If you would like a brief phone call to walk through the budget template or discuss any aspect further, please let me know a convenient time.
