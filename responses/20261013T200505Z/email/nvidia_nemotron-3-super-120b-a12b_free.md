Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the valuable work you and your volunteers are doing to support the local community garden. I am pleased to provide the information you requested regarding the Community Resilience Grant (CRG) round that closes next month.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The CRG expressly permits applications from unincorporated community organisations that are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local government council. To qualify, you must obtain a formal letter of auspice from the council confirming that:

- The council will act as the legal and financial sponsor for the grant,  
- It will hold the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance on behalf of the project, and  
- It agrees to administer the grant funds in accordance with the grant’s terms and conditions.

If your council can provide such a letter, your group meets the auspice requirement.

**2. Grant amount range**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is **$5,000** and the maximum award is **$50,000** (GST inclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this band will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated project costs, you may wish to prepare a budget that falls comfortably within this range; many successful applicants request between $15,000 and $30,000 for similar infrastructure upgrades.

**3. Eligibility of rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Capital works that enhance climate resilience—including the installation of rainwater harvesting systems and permanent or semi‑permanent shade structures—are explicitly listed as eligible costs under the “Infrastructure Adaptation” category. The grant can cover:

- Purchase and installation of tanks, pumps, and associated plumbing,  
- Engineering design and certification fees,  
- Materials and labour for shade structures (e.g., steel frames, UV‑stable fabric, footings), and  
- Associated site preparation (e.g., ground levelling, drainage works).

Operational expenses such as ongoing maintenance or volunteer stipends are not eligible, but they may be included as in‑kind contributions in your budget narrative.

**4. Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please collate the following items:

- **Auspice letter** from the local council (on official letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).  
- **Proof of council insurance** covering public liability and volunteer activities (certificate of currency).  
- **Recent financial statements** (or a simple income‑expenditure summary) demonstrating the $40,000 annual turnover and the sources of revenue.  
- **Project plan** outlining objectives, timeline (including key milestones), and how the infrastructure will improve resilience to hot summers.  
- **Detailed budget** with itemised costs, quotes from at least two suppliers for major items (tanks, shade structure), and any in‑kind contributions (volunteer labour, donated materials).  
- **Site plan or sketches** showing proposed tank placement and shade structure layout.  
- **Risk assessment** addressing installation safety, water quality, and structural integrity.  
- **Letters of support** (optional but helpful) from community members, local schools, or other stakeholders who benefit from the garden.

Having these documents ready will streamline the assessment process and demonstrate your group’s capacity to deliver the project successfully.
