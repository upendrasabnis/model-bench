Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarity on each of the points you have raised.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as your local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be a legally constituted entity (e.g., an incorporated association, company limited by guarantee, or local government authority) that holds appropriate public liability insurance and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a signed Auspicing Agreement or a letter from the council confirming their willingness to act as auspice, along with their ABN and proof of current insurance coverage.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Given your annual turnover of approximately $40,000, please ensure your requested amount is proportionate to your organisational capacity and the project scale. Budgets that demonstrate co-contributions (cash or in-kind) are viewed favourably.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly supports community resilience is an eligible expenditure category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts align squarely with the program’s objectives of climate adaptation and sustained community food security. Eligible costs include materials, contracted labour, necessary permits, and reasonable project management fees. Ongoing maintenance costs and operational expenses (e.g., stall supplies) are not eligible.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:** In addition to the Auspicing Agreement, please gather the following for a complete application:
1. A detailed project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and measurable outcomes.
2. Itemised budget with at least two quotes for major expenditure items (tanks, shade structure).
3. Council letter confirming land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site.
4. Your group’s most recent financial statement (simple income/expenditure report is sufficient).
5. Evidence of community support (letters, petition, or survey results).
6. Certificate of Currency for the council’s Public Liability Insurance noting the project activity.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 November. I strongly recommend starting your draft in our online portal at least two weeks prior to the deadline to allow time for technical troubleshooting.
