Thank you for taking the time to get in touch and for the enthusiasm you have for enhancing your community garden’s resilience. I’m pleased to provide the following information to help you determine whether your organization meets the eligibility criteria for the upcoming round of the Community Resilience Grant.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines state that applicants must be either a registered charity or an incorporated association. However, we also accept applications from unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced (i.e., supported or managed on behalf of the group) by a municipal authority that holds the necessary insurance and holds the group’s financial accounts. In your case, because your local council provides insurance coverage and acts as the fiscal sponsor, you are likely eligible to apply as an auspiced unincorporated group. We recommend that you include a formal letter from the council confirming its auspice status in your application package; this will be a decisive factor for the eligibility review.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant program does not prescribe a strict minimum, but most successful applications fall within the $5,000 – $50,000 range. Projects that require substantial capital infrastructure—such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures—typically request amounts closer to the upper end of this band. If your total project cost is under $5,000, you may still apply, but be aware that the grant may represent a smaller proportion of overall expenses.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Both rainwater tanks and shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible capital expenditures under the “Climate‑Resilient Infrastructure” category of the grant. The key is that the items must be permanently affixed to the site, directly support year‑round garden operations, and align with the project’s resilience objectives (e.g., reducing water consumption and providing relief from extreme heat). Please ensure that the budget line items are clearly itemized and that each item includes a brief justification linking it to the grant’s goals.

**Suggested supporting documents**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend preparing the following documents:

1. **Auscice letter** from the council confirming insurance coverage and fiscal sponsorship.  
2. **Organizational profile** (including a brief description of the garden, its mission, and volunteer base).  
3. **Financial statements** for the most recent fiscal year (balance sheet, income statement, and cash flow) – even a simple profit‑and‑loss statement is acceptable given your modest turnover.  
4. **Project plan and budget** with detailed cost breakdowns for the tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary items.  
5. **Proof of insurance** (if your council can provide a copy of the policy covering the project).  
6. **Letters of support** from local businesses, community groups, or other stakeholders.  
7. **Site plans or drawings** showing where the infrastructure will be located.  
8. **Monitoring and evaluation framework** outlining how you will measure the project’s impact (e.g., water savings, increased usage during heat events).
