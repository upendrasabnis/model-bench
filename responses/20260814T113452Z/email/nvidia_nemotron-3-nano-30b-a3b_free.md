**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The CRG accepts applications from entities that are either formally incorporated or that operate under the auspices of a local government authority, provided the sponsoring council explicitly authorises the project and holds the necessary insurance and financial oversight on your behalf. Because your volunteer group is already operating under the council’s umbrella and the council maintains the required public liability and workers‑compensation cover, you meet the primary eligibility condition. However, the application must be submitted in the name of the council (or a designated council officer) and include a signed letter of support from the council confirming both the authorisation and the scope of the proposed work.  

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
For this funding round, the CRG offers grants ranging from **$5,000 to $50,000**. Projects that demonstrate a clear cost‑benefit analysis and a realistic budget are more likely to receive funding at the higher end of the scale. Your proposed investment in rain‑water tanks and a shade structure falls comfortably within this range; we recommend budgeting the full amount needed for the infrastructure (including design, procurement, installation, and a 10 % contingency) and justifying each line item.  

**3. Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Infrastructure that enhances the resilience of community‑run facilities—such as water‑capture systems, shading, or cooling installations—is expressly eligible under the CRG’s “Infrastructure and Asset Protection” stream. The key requirement is that the works must directly contribute to the site’s ability to continue operating under climate‑related stressors (e.g., heatwaves, drought). Provided you can demonstrate that the rain‑water tanks will reduce reliance on municipal water and that the shade structure will mitigate heat‑related loss of volunteer productivity, these costs are fully allowable.  

**4. Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please include the following:  

- A signed **Letter of Support** from the council confirming auspice status, insurance coverage, and endorsement of the project.  
- A **detailed project plan** (timeline, milestones, and responsible parties).  
- A **budget breakdown** that itemises the rain‑water tanks, shade structure, site preparation, and any associated professional fees, together with evidence of quotations or tenders obtained.  
- **Risk assessment and mitigation** measures that address potential delays or cost overruns.  
- **Evidence of community need**, such as usage statistics from the weekly produce stall, membership numbers, or testimonials from regular volunteers.  
- **Proof of non‑profit status** (e.g., council registration, incorporation documents if later achieved, or a copy of the council’s resolution authorising the project).
