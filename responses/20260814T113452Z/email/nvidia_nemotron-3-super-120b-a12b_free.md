Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant and for taking the time to outline your project and organisational structure. I am happy to provide the clarification you need before you proceed with a full application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant program expressly permits applications from unincorporated community groups that are formally auspiced by a local government authority. In your case, the fact that your garden operates under the auspices of the regional council, which maintains the necessary public liability and volunteer insurances, satisfies the auspice requirement. You will need to submit a signed Auspice Agreement from the council confirming their support and confirming that they will act as the legal and financial administrator for any funds awarded.

**Grant amount limits**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Projects requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be well‑aligned with the program’s intent to support modest‑scale resilience initiatives.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance climate resilience—such as the installation of rainwater harvesting tanks and shade structures—are explicitly listed as eligible expenditure items. The grant can cover the purchase, delivery, and installation of these assets, as well as associated design or engineering fees, provided that the costs are reasonable, demonstrably necessary for the project, and supported by quotes or supplier estimates.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please gather the following items:

1. **Auspice Agreement** – a signed letter or memorandum from the regional council confirming their auspice role, insurance coverage, and willingness to manage grant funds.  
2. **Project Plan** – a brief (1–2 page) description of the proposed rainwater tanks and shade structure, including site layout, anticipated benefits (e.g., water savings, temperature reduction), and a timeline for implementation.  
3. **Budget Sheet** – a detailed budget broken down by cost category (equipment, freight, installation, contingency) with at least two written quotes for each major item.  
4. **Organisational Information** – a short profile of the garden (mission, volunteer structure, membership numbers) and a summary of your annual turnover and sources of income (produce stall revenue, membership fees).  
5. **Letters of Support** (optional but recommended) – endorsements from nearby residents, the local council’s community services officer, or any partner organisations that demonstrate community backing.  
6. **Proof of Non‑profit Status** – while registration as a charity is not required, a copy of the council’s auspice letter or any existing memorandum of understanding that shows the garden operates for community benefit will suffice.
