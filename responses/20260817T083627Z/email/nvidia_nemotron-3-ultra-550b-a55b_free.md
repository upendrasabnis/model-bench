Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility details before investing time in a full application—this is exactly the kind of due diligence that strengthens submissions. I am pleased to address each of your questions in turn.

**Auspicing Arrangements:** Unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing agreement with a local council are eligible to apply, provided the council holds an Australian Business Number (ABN), maintains current public liability insurance covering the project activities, and signs the grant agreement as the legal entity. Your arrangement appears to meet these criteria. Please ensure the auspice agreement clearly outlines the council’s responsibility for financial management, reporting, and acquittal of the grant funds.

**Grant Amounts:** This round offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000 (GST-exclusive). There is no minimum project size, but the requested amount must be proportionate to the scope of work and your group’s capacity to deliver. Given your annual turnover of approximately $40,000, a request within the lower-to-mid range would generally be viewed as appropriate.

**Eligible Costs:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures—is an eligible expenditure category. Costs must be for purchase and installation only; ongoing maintenance, operational wages, or retrospective expenses are excluded. Quotes must be from licensed contractors and itemised.

**Supporting Documents:** Please prepare the following for upload with your application:
1. Signed auspice agreement on council letterhead.
2. Council’s current Certificate of Currency for public liability insurance ($20M minimum).
3. Two independent, itemised quotes for each infrastructure component.
4. A simple project plan with timeline, risk register, and volunteer capacity statement.
5. Your group’s most recent financial statement (income/expense summary).
6. Letters of support from at least two community stakeholders.

Applications close 5:00 PM AEST on 30 June. I recommend starting the draft in our SmartyGrants portal early to allow time for council sign-off. If you would like a brief phone consultation to review a draft budget, please reply with a few suitable times.

We look forward to receiving your application.
