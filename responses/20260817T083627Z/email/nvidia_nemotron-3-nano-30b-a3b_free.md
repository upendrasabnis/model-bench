Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful description of your volunteer‑run community garden. I am pleased to provide the information you requested regarding eligibility, grant limits, allowable costs, and the documentation you will need to support your application.  

**1. Eligibility of groups auspiced by a council**  
Our funding guidelines expressly include “unincorporated community groups that operate under the auspices of a local council or another registered not‑for‑profit entity.” Because your garden is formally sponsored by the council and the council holds the requisite insurance, you meet the primary eligibility criterion. However, the council’s sponsorship must be documented in a letter on official letterhead confirming their willingness to act as your auspicing body for the duration of the grant period.  

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers a single‑award range of **$10,000 to $50,000**. Projects that demonstrate a clear link to climate‑adaptation or disaster‑risk reduction are eligible for the full band, provided the applicant can match at least 20 % of the approved budget through in‑kind contributions or cash. Your projected budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure falls comfortably within this range.  

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the resilience of community facilities is an allowable expense. Specifically, the following items are covered:  

- **Rainwater harvesting systems** (including tanks, gutters, and associated plumbing)  
- **Shade structures** (e.g., pergolas, canopies, or shade cloth installations) that reduce heat exposure and improve sustainability  

These costs can be included in the project budget, provided they are directly tied to the resilience objective and are not primarily aesthetic.  

**4. Supporting documentation you should prepare**  

| Document | Purpose | Tips for preparation |
|----------|---------|----------------------|
| **Letter of auspice from the council** | Confirms council’s role as sponsor | Use official council letterhead, signed by an authorized officer, and reference the grant program. |
| **Project budget and cost breakdown** | Shows how funds will be spent | Itemise each component (tanks, fittings, shade structure, installation, monitoring). Include quotes from suppliers/contractors. |
| **Risk assessment and resilience rationale** | Demonstrates alignment with grant objectives | Outline current climate risks (e.g., heat stress, water scarcity) and how the proposed works mitigate them. |
| **Organisational profile** | Provides context for the applicant | Include a brief history, membership numbers, annual turnover ($40k), and a description of activities. |
| **Proof of insurance coverage** | Verifies risk management | Attach the council’s insurance certificate or a copy of the policy that names your group as an additional insured. |
| **Timeline and implementation plan** | Shows feasibility and milestones | Provide a month‑by‑month schedule, highlighting key deliverables and responsible parties. |
| **Letters of support (optional but recommended)** | Strengthens credibility | Obtain brief endorsements from the council’s parks department or local schools that use the garden. |

When you submit the full application, ensure all documents are compiled into a single PDF (or the format specified in the application portal) and that the file size does not exceed the limit outlined in the grant guide.
