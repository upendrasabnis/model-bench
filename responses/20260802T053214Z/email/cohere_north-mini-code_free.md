Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed information you’ve shared about your community garden. I’m pleased to provide clarification on the points you raised, and I hope this information helps you decide whether to proceed with a full application.  

**Eligibility – unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our Community Resilience Grant is designed to support projects that deliver clear public benefit and demonstrate strong community involvement. While we do require applicants to be either a registered charity or an incorporated association, we also accept applications from unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by a local government or other eligible entity. In your case, because your garden operates under the auspices of your council—and the council holds the necessary insurances on your behalf—you should meet the eligibility requirement for auspiced groups. The council’s involvement will need to be evidenced in your application (see the “Supporting Documentation” section below), but the structure you describe is permissible.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The grant programme does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award amount. Instead, funding levels are determined by the scope, impact, and budget of each proposal relative to the available funds for the round. Historically, awards for projects of similar scale have ranged from approximately $15,000 to $50,000, though this is not a fixed ceiling. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure will be evaluated against the overall budget envelope and the merit of the project.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the sustainability and resilience of community spaces is squarely within the grant’s focus areas. Rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible capital expenditures because they:  

1. Reduce water consumption and operational costs, thereby increasing the garden’s long‑term viability.  
2. Mitigate the impacts of extreme weather, aligning with the “Community Resilience” objective.  
3. Provide tangible assets that can be maintained and used by the wider community.  

Thus, the costs you have identified should be considered eligible, provided they are detailed in a clear, itemised budget and justified within the project narrative.  

**Supporting documents we recommend preparing**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest assembling the following documents (where applicable):  

1. **Auspice Agreement / Letter of Support** – an official letter from your council confirming the auspice arrangement, insurance coverage, and any logistical support they provide.  
2. **Organizational Profile** – a brief description of the garden’s mission, volunteer base, and community impact, even if you are unincorporated.  
3. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) showing the $40,000 turnover and sources of income.  
4. **Budget and Cash‑Flow Forecast** – a detailed breakdown of all project costs (including tanks, shade structure, installation, and any contingency), plus a plan for ongoing maintenance funding.  
5. **Project Plan** – a timeline, deliverables, and milestones, together with a description of how the project addresses climate resilience.  
6. **Risk Assessment and Insurance Proof** – documentation confirming that the project will be covered under the council’s insurance policy, or a copy of any additional coverage you will need.  
7. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, community groups, or other stakeholders that highlight the broader benefits of the project.  
8. **Site Plans or Drawings** – schematic layouts showing where the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be located.  

Please note that all financial documents should be clearly labelled with your organisation’s name (or the council’s, if that is the legal entity) and dated. If any of the required paperwork is not yet available, feel free to explain the situation in your application and indicate when you expect to have it ready.
