Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. We appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in detail.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under the auspices of a local council are absolutely eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body meets the program’s eligibility criteria for legal entities (e.g., holds an ABN, is not-for-profit, and holds the required insurances). Since your council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf, this arrangement satisfies our governance and risk-management requirements. You will need to submit a formal letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, their ABN, and their agreement to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant should your application be successful.

Second, the current round offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000. There is no minimum project size, but the total grant request must fall within this band. Given your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, your project appears well within the allowable range.

Third, infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as water-security installations and heat-mitigation structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible capital cost in the program guidelines. Both the rainwater tanks and the shade structure therefore qualify, provided they are fixed assets that remain the property of the auspicing council or the garden group for the useful life of the assets.

Finally, for supporting documentation you should prepare:
- Council auspicing letter (as described above)
- Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover
- Quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure item
- A simple project plan with timeline, risk register, and maintenance schedule
- Evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the site
- Letters of community support demonstrating broader benefit

Please ensure all documents are converted to PDF before uploading. The application portal opens on 1 June and closes at 5 pm AEST on 30 June. If you would like a brief pre-application phone call to walk through the budget template, please let me know a convenient time.

We look forward to receiving your application.
