**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The CRG program expressly allows applications from not‑for‑profit entities that are not incorporated as charities, provided they are auspiced by an eligible sponsor—such as a local government authority, incorporated association, or registered charity. Your arrangement, whereby the local council holds the necessary insurances and acts as the auspicing body, satisfies this requirement. In practice, the council would be listed as the legal applicant on the application form, while you would be identified as the project lead and primary contact. Please ensure that the council signs the auspice agreement template supplied in the application portal and that a copy of the council’s public liability insurance certificate is attached.

**Grant amount range**  
For the current funding cycle, the minimum award is **$5,000** and the maximum award is **$50,000** (GST exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this band will be deemed ineligible. Given your projected budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, you may wish to prepare a detailed cost breakdown that falls within these limits; if your total exceeds $50,000, consider phasing the works or seeking co‑funding from other sources to bring the CRG request into the allowable range.

**Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Capital works that enhance climate resilience—including the installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related site‑preparation works—are explicitly listed as eligible costs under the “Infrastructure Adaptation” category. Both the tanks and the shade structure qualify, provided they are new assets (not replacements of existing infrastructure) and are directly linked to the garden’s continued operation during hotter periods. Operational expenses such as stall‑running costs or membership fee processing are not covered, but you may include a modest contingency (up to 10 % of the total project cost) for unforeseen site‑works.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please have the following ready before you begin the online form:

1. **Auspice agreement** – a signed letter from the local council confirming they will act as the legal applicant, hold insurance, and accept financial responsibility for the grant funds.  
2. **Proof of council insurance** – a copy of the council’s public liability (and, if applicable, workers’ compensation) certificate covering the garden site.  
3. **Project plan** – a concise (max two‑page) description outlining the need for rainwater tanks and shade, the proposed layout, timelines, and how the works will improve resilience to extreme heat.  
4. **Detailed budget** – itemised quotes or supplier estimates for the tanks, shade structure, footings, plumbing, and any associated labour, plus a clear total that falls within the $5k–$50k band. Include a 10 % contingency line if desired.  
5. **Organisational outline** – a brief summary of the garden’s governance (e.g., volunteer committee, meeting frequency), current activities, and the $40,000 annual turnover you mentioned, to demonstrate financial viability and community support.  
6. **Letters of support** (optional but helpful) – from the council, local residents, or partner organisations endorsing the project’s benefit to the community.
