Dear Jamie,  

Thank you for reaching out and for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant. I appreciate the valuable work your volunteer‑run community garden does for the town, and I am happy to clarify the points you raised.  

**1. Eligibility of groups auspiced by a council**  
The Grant guidelines permit applications from unincorporated community groups provided they are formally auspiced by a local government authority that holds the necessary insurance and can sign the funding agreement on their behalf. In practice, this means that a council‑sponsored entity can submit an application on your behalf, provided the council’s endorsement letter is included in the submission package.  

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
For the current funding round, the grant ceiling is $25,000, and the minimum award is $2,500. Projects that demonstrate a clear public‑benefit component and a realistic budget are encouraged to apply for any amount within this range.  

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the capacity of a community facility to withstand climate‑related stresses is considered an eligible expense. Rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and associated works (such as foundations, plumbing, and protective finishes) are specifically listed under “Resilience‑related capital works” in the grant criteria. Therefore, the rainwater tanks and shade structure you propose to install would qualify as eligible costs.  

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To accompany your application you should prepare the following:  

- A signed endorsement letter from the council confirming its auspice role and confirming that the council holds the requisite insurance.  
- A detailed project budget that breaks down capital costs (e.g., tanks, shade structure) and any associated operating expenses.  
- Evidence of community need, such as demographic data or testimonials from regular users of the garden.  
- Project timelines, risk‑management plans, and any relevant quotations or tender documents for the proposed works.  
- Proof of incorporation or registration is not required, but a copy of the council’s constitution or relevant resolution authorising the auspice may be requested.
