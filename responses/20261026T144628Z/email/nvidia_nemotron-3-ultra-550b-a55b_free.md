The council must also hold the appropriate public liability and workers’ compensation insurances covering the project activities, which you have confirmed is already in place. Please ensure the council provides a formal letter of support confirming their auspice role and insurance coverage as part of your application.

Second, this grant round offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 per project. There is no minimum project budget, but the requested amount must fall within this range. Given your project scope, you should have no issue fitting within these parameters.

Third, capital infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures is considered an eligible cost, provided the assets are directly linked to building community resilience against climate impacts—exactly the rationale you have described. Eligible expenses include purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation or plumbing works. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not covered.
