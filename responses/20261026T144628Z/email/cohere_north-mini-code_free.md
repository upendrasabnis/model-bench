**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The grant programme welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. Unincorporated groups that operate under the auspices of a local council are indeed eligible, provided that the council can confirm it will act as the primary point of contact for the grant, hold any necessary insurance, and assume financial responsibility for the project if the grant is awarded. In practice, this means we will require a formal letter of support (or an official council resolution) that outlines the council’s role in overseeing the project, managing any insurance or risk‑management obligations, and, if applicable, providing matching funds or in‑kind contributions.  

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
Our guidelines do not prescribe a strict minimum award; however, we typically expect applications to request amounts that are proportionate to the scope of the proposed work. The maximum grant size for this round is $150,000, and most successful projects fall within the $30,000‑$80,000 range. If your rainwater‑tank and shade‑structure initiative aligns with the programme’s objectives, a request in the $40,000‑$60,000 band would likely be appropriate, given the projected costs of the infrastructure and any ancillary works (e.g., site preparation, plumbing compliance).  

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes, capital infrastructure that directly supports the long‑term sustainability and resilience of community assets is fully eligible. Rainwater harvesting systems, permanent shade structures, and associated site works are considered permissible expenditures, as they address climate‑adaptation goals and reduce operating costs over time. We do require that all proposed items be clearly linked to the project’s resilience objectives, have a demonstrable community benefit, and comply with all relevant building, health, and environmental regulations.  

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documentation:  

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, objectives, implementation plan, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Narrative Budget Table** – itemising all costs, with separate columns for grant request, in‑kind contributions, and any other funding sources.  
3. **Letters of Support** – from the council confirming its auspice role, and from any partner organisations, local businesses, or community members who will contribute expertise or resources.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your group’s mission, volunteer base, and operating history (even if unincorporated).  
5. **Insurance and Risk‑Management Documentation** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming coverage for the proposed works, plus any site‑specific risk assessments.  
6. **Site Plans and Technical Sketches** – indicating the location of the rainwater tanks, shade structure, and any associated infrastructure.  
7. **Financial Statements** – for the most recent fiscal year (or a simple income‑and‑expenditure summary) to corroborate the $40,000 turnover figure.  
8. **Evidence of Community Engagement** – minutes of public meetings, sign‑up sheets, or social‑media engagement metrics that demonstrate local support.  

We also advise reviewing the full grant guidelines on our website (particularly the “Eligibility” and “Allowed Costs” sections) to ensure that any additional requirements—such as environmental impact assessments or Aboriginal heritage checks—are addressed.
