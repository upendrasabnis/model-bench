Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you and your volunteers are doing to strengthen your community garden. I appreciate you taking the time to clarify eligibility before preparing a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

**1. Auspiced, unincorporated groups**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to organisations that are either incorporated not‑for‑profit entities, registered charities, or unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by an eligible sponsor such as a local government authority, a registered charity, or an incorporated peak body. Because your garden operates under the auspices of your regional council—which holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurances on your behalf—you meet the auspicing requirement and are therefore eligible to apply. You will need to provide a signed auspice agreement (or a letter of support) from the council confirming that they will act as the legal and financial sponsor for the grant, including responsibility for acquittal reporting.

**2. Grant size**  
For this funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your project scope, a request in the $15,000–$25,000 bracket would be typical, but you should tailor the budget to the actual costs of the rainwater tanks and shade structure, ensuring you remain within the ceiling.

**3. Eligible costs – infrastructure**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate‑related stresses is an expressly eligible cost category. This includes, but is not limited to, water harvesting systems (rainwater tanks, pumps, filtration), shade structures, pergolas, and associated site works (foundations, anchoring, and minor landscaping). Operational expenses such as ongoing maintenance, utilities, or staff salaries are not covered unless they are integral to the installation and commissioning phase (e.g., contractor labour for setup). Please ensure that any requested items are clearly itemised and justified in the budget narrative, linking each component to the anticipated increase in heat‑resilience and continued garden operation.

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following:

- **Auspice agreement or letter of support** from the regional council, detailing their role as legal sponsor and confirming insurance coverage.
- **Project plan** (maximum two pages) outlining the need, objectives, activities, timeline, and expected outcomes (e.g., litres of water harvested, reduction in ambient temperature, number of garden users served).
- **Detailed budget** (Excel or PDF) with itemised costs, quotes (or indicative pricing) for the rainwater tanks, shade structure, foundations, and any contractor labour, plus a brief justification for each line item.
- **Evidence of community need** – such as recent temperature records for your region, photos of the garden during extreme heat events, or letters from members/volunteers describing how heat has impacted usability.
- **Governance documents** – even though you are unincorporated, provide a copy of your garden’s terms of reference, meeting minutes showing member endorsement of the project, and a list of current office‑bearers or coordinators.
- **Financial statements** – a simple profit‑and‑loss statement for the past 12 months (showing the ~$40,000 turnover) and a bank statement confirming the council’s auspice account, if applicable.

All documents should be clearly labelled and uploaded as separate files via the online portal; combined file size should not exceed 25 MB.
