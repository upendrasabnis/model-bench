**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The CRG accepts applications from entities that are formally registered charities or not‑for‑profit organisations. However, an unincorporated group may still be eligible when it is operating under the formal auspice of a local council that holds the necessary insurance and governance arrangements on its behalf. In such cases, the council must be listed as the “nominated agency” on the application and must provide a letter of support confirming that it will manage the grant funds in accordance with the program’s requirements.  

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The current funding round offers grants ranging from **$5,000 to $25,000**. Projects that demonstrate a clear, measurable outcome and a realistic budget within this band are most likely to be successful. Your proposed investment in rainwater tanks and a shade structure appears to fall comfortably within this range.  

**3. Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Infrastructure that enhances the capacity of a community facility to respond to climate‑related stresses—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures—is explicitly listed as an eligible cost. Provided the items are directly related to improving resilience (e.g., water security during heatwaves or extreme weather), they are deemed permissible expenses under the grant’s “Infrastructure and Equipment” category.  

**4. Supporting documentation**  
To move forward, please prepare the following:  

- A signed **Letter of Support** from the council confirming its role as auspicing body and outlining its insurance coverage.  
- A concise **Project Plan** (no more than two pages) that includes objectives, timeline, and expected outcomes.  
- A **Budget Breakdown** that itemises the rainwater tanks, shade structure, associated installation costs, and any contingency items.  
- Evidence of **Financial Sustainability**, such as recent financial statements or a summary of your $40,000 annual turnover, highlighting that the project will not jeopardise existing operations.  
- **Quotes or pro‑forma invoices** from suppliers for the equipment you intend to purchase.
