**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Grant is open to “community organisations” that are not necessarily registered charities. An unincorporated group that operates under the auspices of a local council is eligible, provided the council can act as the legal beneficiary of the grant. In practice this means you’ll need to submit a signed statement from the council confirming that it will hold the grant funds and oversee the project on your behalf. This statement should include the council’s name, contact details, and the name of the council officer who will liaise with the Grants Office.

**2. Minimum and maximum award amounts**  
The award size for this round ranges from **$5,000 to $20,000**, depending on the scope of the project and the evidence of impact. Projects that demonstrate a clear community benefit and a strong plan for sustainability tend to receive the higher end of the scale. If you anticipate a cost that falls outside this band, please let us know – detectar may be able to adjust the budget.

**3. Infrastructure costs**  
Yes, infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures is fully eligible, provided you can show that the expenditure is directly related to building resilience against climate impacts. We ask that you include a detailed budget line item for each piece of infrastructure, with quotes or price estimates from reputable suppliers.
