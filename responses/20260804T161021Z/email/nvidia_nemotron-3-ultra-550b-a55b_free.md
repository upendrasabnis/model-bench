Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) holds the legal and financial responsibility for the grant. The council must be the legal applicant, sign the funding agreement, and provide evidence of appropriate insurance coverage, including public liability and workers' compensation where applicable. A signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming this arrangement will be required at the application stage.

Second, the current grant round offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 per project. There is no minimum project budget, but the grant amount requested must fall within this range. Given your project scope, it appears you would be well within these parameters.

Third, capital infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures is considered an eligible cost, provided the assets are essential to the project’s resilience outcomes, are located on land with secure tenure (or a long-term lease/licence), and comply with relevant building and planning regulations. Quotes from licensed contractors must accompany the application for any individual item exceeding $5,000.

Finally, in addition to the auspicing agreement and quotes, you should prepare: your group’s most recent financial statement (or a simple income and expenditure summary for the last financial year), a project plan with timeline and risk assessment, evidence of landholder consent for the infrastructure, and letters of community support. If your council requires a council resolution to enter the auspicing arrangement, please allow time for that process before the closing date.

I encourage you to review the full program guidelines on our website, which include the assessment criteria and a sample application form. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require further clarification as you develop your submission.
