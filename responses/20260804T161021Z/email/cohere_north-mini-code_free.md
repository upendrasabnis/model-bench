**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
Our grant programme is designed to support projects that deliver measurable community benefit, and we do accept applications from unincorporated groups provided they have a formal auspicing arrangement with a recognised entity. In your case, the fact that your garden operates under the auspices of the local council is advantageous – the council can act as the primary applicant, sign the grant agreement, and fulfil obligations such as holding the required insurance policies. If you decide to proceed, we will need a letter from the council confirming its endorsement of the project and its willingness to assume financial and legal responsibility on your behalf.

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a single fixed award; rather, funding levels are determined on the merit of the proposal and the scope of the activities proposed. Historically, successful projects have received between **$10,000 and $100,000**, although smaller awards of $5,000‑$10,000 are possible for more modest interventions. Our reviewers will assess the overall budget you present against the anticipated outcomes, so there is no strict upper ceiling, but we do expect the requested amount to be proportionate to the project’s impact and sustainability.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes – capital infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures is expressly listed as an eligible cost under the “Resilience and Sustainability” component of the grant. These assets directly address the challenges you have identified (heat‑stress mitigation and water security) and align with the programme’s objective of enhancing community capacity to cope with climate‑related pressures. We would expect the budget line items for these items to be broken down with unit costs, quantities, and installation considerations, as well as any associated labour or professional fees.

**Supporting documentation you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documents (all to be submitted as part of the online portal):

1. **Project Narrative** – a clear description of the problem, proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Detailed Budget** – itemised costs for rainwater tanks, shade structure, installation, contingency, and any ongoing maintenance fees.  
3. **Organizational Profile** – a brief constitution or set of operating guidelines for your volunteer group, plus the council’s endorsement letter.  
4. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover you mentioned.  
5. **Proof of Insurance** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming that it covers your activities and the proposed infrastructure.  
6. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from local businesses, schools, or community organisations that illustrate broader community backing.  
7. **Risk Assessment & Management Plan** – outlining how you will manage installation safety, maintenance, and potential operational risks.  
8. **Monitoring & Evaluation Framework** – a simple plan for measuring success (e.g., water usage reduction, increased garden usage during hot periods, user satisfaction surveys).

If any of these documents are not readily available, please let us know; we can provide guidance on acceptable alternatives (for example, using a cash‑basis profit‑and‑loss statement in lieu of audited accounts).
