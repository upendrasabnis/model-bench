Thank you for reaching out and for the important work you do with the community garden. I am happy to clarify the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the Community Resilience Grant so you can decide whether to proceed with a full submission.

**Auspiced groups**  
The grant programme explicitly allows unincorporated organisations to apply when they are auspiced by an eligible legal entity, such as a local council, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. Because your garden operates under the auspices of your regional council—which already holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurances—you meet this requirement. In your application you will need to provide a signed auspice agreement or a letter from the council confirming their support and acknowledging responsibility for the grant funds and reporting obligations.

**Grant size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of **$5,000** to a maximum of **$50,000** per project. There is no prescribed “average” award; the amount is determined by the scope, budget, and demonstrated need outlined in your proposal. Given your estimated project costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, you should aim to request a figure that reflects the full cost of those items, while remaining within the $50k ceiling.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience to climate impacts—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related installation labour—is considered an eligible expense. Routine maintenance, consumables (e.g., seeds, fertiliser), or general operating costs that are not tied to the specific resilience‑building activity would not be funded. Please ensure that your budget clearly separates the capital costs (eligible) from any ongoing operational expenses (ineligible).

**Supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice letter or agreement** from the local council confirming their role as the legal entity responsible for the grant.
2. **Detailed project plan** (including timelines, milestones, and a description of how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will improve resilience during hot summers).
3. **Itemised budget** with quotes or cost estimates for the tanks, shade structure, foundations, plumbing, and any contracted labour.
4. **Evidence of community support** (e.g., letters of endorsement from nearby residents, the council, or local schools) and a brief summary of your garden’s current activities and turnover (the $40,000 figure you mentioned is sufficient for this purpose).
5. **Financial statements** (or a simple income‑expenditure summary) for the past 12 months to demonstrate fiscal management, even though you are not a registered charity.
6. **Insurance certificate** showing that the council’s coverage extends to volunteer activities on the garden site.
