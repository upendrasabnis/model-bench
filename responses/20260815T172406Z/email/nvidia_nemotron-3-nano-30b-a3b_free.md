Thank you for reaching out and for the detailed information about your community garden project. I am pleased to clarify the points you raised regarding eligibility for the current Community Resilience Grant round.

**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our guidelines permit organisations that are not incorporated but are formally auspiced by a local council to apply, provided the council confirms in writing that it holds the group’s insurance, manages its financial accounts, and endorses the proposed project. The council’s endorsement must be attached to the application as a supporting document.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
For the current funding round the grant ceiling is **$25,000**, with a floor of **$2,500**. Projects that demonstrate a clear need for infrastructure – such as rainwater harvesting or shade structures – are frequently funded at the higher end of the scale, especially when they enhance climate‑adaptive capacity.

**3. Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Both rainwater tanks and shade structures are considered **eligible capital expenses** under the grant, as they directly support the garden’s resilience to extreme weather and prolonged heat. Costs must be *reasonable, necessary, and demonstrable* and must be supported by quotes, invoices, or other evidence of procurement.

**4. Recommended supporting documentation**  
To strengthen your application, please include:  

- A signed letter of endorsement from the council confirming its auspices and insurance coverage.  
- A brief project plan outlining objectives, timeline, and expected outcomes.  
- Detailed cost estimates and at least two competitive quotes for each major item (tanks, shade structure, installation).  
- Financial statements or a budget showing your $40,000 annual turnover and sources of income.  
- Risk‑management or safety documentation (e.g., site‑specific hazard assessments).  
- Evidence of community support, such as letters of endorsement from local residents or partner organisations.
