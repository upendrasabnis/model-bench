Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant  

Dear Jamie,  

Thank you for reaching out and for the interest your community garden has shown in our Community Resilience Grant programme. I’m pleased to address each of the points you raised so that you can determine whether a full application is worthwhile for your upcoming submission deadline.  

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups** – The grant guidelines stipulate that an applicant must be a legally constituted entity capable of entering into a binding grant agreement with our office. In practice, this includes both registered charities and incorporated not‑for‑profit organisations. However, we do accept applications from unincorporated groups that are **auspiced** by a municipal or regional council that holds the necessary insurance and can act as the contracting party. In your case, because your garden operates under the auspices of the local council and the council holds the relevant insurances, you should be eligible to apply, provided you submit a formal letter of auspice from the council confirming its willingness to act as the grantee. If the council prefers not to be the legal recipient, you may need to consider incorporating (e.g., as a limited by guarantee company) before the closing date.  

**Grant amount limits** – There is no minimum or maximum grant size set for this round; awards are determined on the basis of merit, alignment with programme objectives, and the proportion of total project cost that can be reasonably supported. Typical awards have ranged from $5,000 to $50,000, but applications that request amounts outside this band are still considered if they are fully justified.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs** – Capital assets such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures are expressly permitted under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” cost category. These items must be directly related to the project’s resilience objectives (e.g., reducing water consumption, mitigating heat stress) and must be clearly itemized in the budget.  

**Supporting documentation** – To strengthen your application we recommend preparing the following:  

1. **Letter of auspice** from the council confirming its role as the contracting entity and that insurance coverage will remain in place for the duration of the project.  
2. ** organisational profile** (mission, history, volunteer base, and any governing documents if your group has them).  
3. **Financial statements** for the most recent two years, including a breakdown of the $40,000 turnover and any reserves.  
4. **Project plan** outlining the installation timeline, design specifications, and expected outcomes.  
5. **Detailed budget** with line items for equipment, installation, contingency, and any matching funds you can provide.  
6. **Risk assessment and safety plan** for the construction work.  
7. **Letters of support** from local businesses, community members, or other stakeholders.  
8. **Proof of insurance** (if your auspice will provide it) or a copy of the council’s insurance certificate.  

We encourage you to review the full grant guidelines on our website, where you will also find the application portal and a checklist of required attachments.
