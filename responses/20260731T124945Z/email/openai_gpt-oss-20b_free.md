**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups sponsored by a council**  
The CRG is open to all community‑based, not‑for‑profit organisations, including unincorporated volunteer groups that receive formal support from a local authority. To be eligible you must:  

- Operate as a not‑for‑profit entity (e.g., a community association, co‑op, or a registered volunteer group).  
- Have a council‑appointed supervising body that holds the relevant insurance and is able to sign a partnership agreement.

**2. Grant amount limits**  
The CRG offers awards ranging from **$2,000 to $20,000** per project measurements. The final amount is determined by project scope, cost‑effectiveness, and the total budget you submit. Projects that include infrastructure (e.g., rainwater tanks, shade structures) typically qualify for the upper end of the range	client.

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures that improve year‑round usability—are explicitly eligible. Your proposal to install tanks and shade will be considered a core project cost, provided the equipment is for public use and the installation is undertaken by a licensed contractor or a qualified volunteer.

**4. Key supporting documents**  
To strengthen your application, please prepare the following:

1. **Council sponsorship letter** – confirming the council’s role, insurance coverage, and willingness to co‑sign the award agreement.  
2. **Financial statements** – a simple balance sheet and cash‑flow summary for the last 12 months, showing the $40,000 turnover.  
3. **Project budget** – itemised line‑items for tanks, shade structure, labour, and contingency.  
4. **Risk assessment** – a brief safety plan for installation and ongoing use.  
5. **Community impact statement** – a short narrative (≈250 words) explaining how the infrastructure will improve resilience and community engagement.  
6. **Proof of not‑for‑profit status** – a council registration certificate or a statutory declaration that the garden operates on a volunteer basis.
