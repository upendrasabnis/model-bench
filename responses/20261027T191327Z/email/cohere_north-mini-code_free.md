**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from unincorporated community groups, provided they are formally auspiced by a legal entity that can assume financial and legal responsibility for the grant funds. In your case, being “auspiced by our local council” satisfies this requirement, as the council can hold the necessary insurances and act as the primary applicant. Please note that the council’s endorsement should be documented in a formal letter of support, confirming that it will manage the grant’s financial reporting, procurement, and any required compliance matters.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award; rather, the funding level is determined by the scope, impact, and budget of the proposed project. Based on similar infrastructure initiatives, grants in the past have ranged from $15,000 to $75,000. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appear to fall comfortably within this band, though the final amount will depend on the detailed budget you submit.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Both rainwater tanks and shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible expenses under the “Sustainable Infrastructure” category of our guidelines. These items directly support the resilience of community operations during extreme weather events and align with the grant’s objective of enhancing climate‑adapted community assets. We encourage you to include detailed specifications, cost breakdowns, and justification for how each component contributes to the project’s resilience goals.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documentation:

1. **Project Narrative** – a clear description of the problem, proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget Worksheet** – itemized costs for equipment, installation, contingency, and any staff time; include unit prices and quantities.  
3. **Letter of Support from the Council** – confirming the auspice relationship, insurance coverage, and willingness to act as fiscal host.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your group’s mission, volunteer composition, and past community projects.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed statements (or, for a small operation, a simplified profit‑and‑loss statement and cash flow summary) demonstrating your $40,000 annual turnover.  
6. **Risk Assessment & Insurance Proof** – any current policies held by the council, plus a plan for safety and maintenance of the new infrastructure.  
7. **Community Engagement Evidence** – sign‑ups, meeting minutes, or promotional materials illustrating local support.

Given that the grant round closes next month, we suggest initiating the document‑gathering process promptly and aiming to submit a draft of your application to the council’s administration office at least one week before the deadline. If the council requires internal approval, we recommend coordinating with them early so that any additional signatures can be obtained without delaying your submission.
