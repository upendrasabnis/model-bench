Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the important work your community garden is doing to strengthen local resilience. I’m happy to clarify the eligibility criteria and application requirements for the upcoming Community Resilience Grant round.

**Auspice Arrangements**  
The grant program does accept applications from unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by an eligible sponsor, such as a local government council. In order for your garden to be considered eligible, the council must provide a written letter of auspice that confirms: (1) it will act as the legal and financial administrator of the grant funds, (2) it holds the necessary public liability and workers’ compensation insurance for the project, and (3) it endorses the specific activities outlined in your proposal. This letter should be signed by an authorised officer (e.g., the Council’s Community Services Manager) and included with your application.

**Grant Size**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers funding ranging from a minimum of $5,000 to a maximum of $50,000 per project. Applications are assessed on a competitive basis, and the award amount is determined by the scope, anticipated impact, and cost‑effectiveness of the proposed activities. Given your estimated turnover of $40,000, a request within the mid‑range of this band (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be appropriate, provided the budget is well justified.

**Eligible Costs**  
Infrastructure investments that directly enhance climate resilience—such as rainwater harvesting tanks, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related site works—are explicitly listed as eligible expenditures. Costs may include the purchase of materials, professional installation, and any necessary engineering or design fees. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., stall utilities, membership administration) are not covered unless they are demonstrably tied to the delivery of the resilience component of the project.

**Supporting Documentation**  
To complete a strong application, please prepare the following items:

1. **Auspice Letter** from the local council (as described above).  
2. **Project Plan** outlining objectives, activities, timeline, and measurable outcomes (e.g., litres of water captured, reduction in heat‑stress incidents).  
3. **Detailed Budget** with itemised costs, quotes or estimates for the tanks and shade structure, and a clear indication of any in‑kind contributions or volunteer labour.  
4. **Evidence of Community Support** (e.g., letters from members, minutes from garden meetings, or a short survey showing demand for the upgrades).  
5. **Organisational Information** – a brief profile of the garden, its governance structure under the council’s auspice, and confirmation of insurance coverage held by the council.  
6. **Financial Statements** (if available) or a simple income‑expenditure summary for the past 12 months to demonstrate fiscal responsibility.
