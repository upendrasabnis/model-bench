Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you are doing with your community garden. I am happy to provide the information you requested regarding the Community Resilience Grant (CRG) so you can determine whether to proceed with a full application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The CRG expressly allows applications from not‑for‑profit groups that are not incorporated, provided they operate under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible entity such as a local council, incorporated association, or registered charity. Your garden’s arrangement with the regional council satisfies this requirement, but you will need to submit a signed Auspicing Agreement (or letter of support) from the council that confirms they will hold the grant funds on your behalf, manage acquittal reporting, and maintain the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance for the duration of the project.

**Grant amount range**  
For the current funding round, the minimum grant that can be awarded is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this band will be deemed ineligible. Given your estimated annual turnover of approximately $40,000, a request in the mid‑range (e.g., $15,000–$25,000) would be competitive, provided the budget is well‑justified and demonstrates clear community benefit.

**Eligibility of the proposed infrastructure**  
Capital works that enhance resilience to climate‑related stressors—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures—are explicitly listed as eligible costs under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” category of the CRG guidelines. You may include the purchase and installation of rainwater tanks, associated plumbing, and the design/construction of a shade structure. Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., water usage, maintenance) are not covered, but you may allocate a modest portion of the grant for initial commissioning and testing if it is directly tied to the installation.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please gather the following items:

1. **Auspicing Agreement / Letter of Support** from the regional council (on council letterhead, signed by an authorised officer).  
2. **Proof of Insurance** – a copy of the council’s public liability policy that covers volunteer activities and the garden site.  
3. **Financial Overview** – a brief statement of income and expenditure for the past 12 months (including the $40,000 turnover figure) and a projected budget for the grant period.  
4. **Project Plan** – a clear description of the problem (increasing summer heat affecting garden productivity), objectives, activities, timeline, and expected outcomes (e.g., percentage reduction in water use, number of volunteer hours retained).  
5. **Quotations** – at least two written quotes for the rainwater tanks and shade structure, detailing specifications, delivery, and installation costs.  
6. **Letters of Community Support** (optional but beneficial) – from local residents, schools, or other organisations that will benefit from the garden’s continued operation.  
7. **Governance Documentation** – if available, a copy of your garden’s terms of reference, volunteer policies, or any minutes showing council endorsement.

Once these documents are compiled, you can complete the online application form available on the grant portal.
