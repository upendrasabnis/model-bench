Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarity on each of the points you have raised.

First, to address your primary eligibility question: **yes, unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are eligible to apply**, provided the auspicing organisation meets the program’s eligibility criteria (e.g., is an incorporated legal entity with an ABN, holds appropriate insurances, and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant). Your arrangement—where the council holds the relevant insurances and acts as your legal sponsor—is a common and acceptable model for this program. You will need to submit a formal letter of support from the council confirming their willingness to act as auspice, along with a copy of their Certificate of Incorporation (or equivalent), ABN details, and current public liability insurance certificate.

Regarding your **annual turnover of approximately $40,000**, this falls well within the program’s parameters for small community organisations. There is no minimum turnover requirement, and the maximum grant amount available in this round is **$50,000 (GST exclusive)**, with a minimum request of **$5,000**. Your proposed project—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure to mitigate the impacts of extreme heat—aligns directly with the program’s objective of building community resilience to climate impacts. **Capital infrastructure of this nature is an eligible cost**, provided it is permanently affixed, located on land where you have secure tenure (or a long-term lease/licence from the council), and does not constitute routine maintenance. Please note that labor costs for volunteer time cannot be claimed, but contractor quotes for supply and installation are acceptable.

**Supporting documents you should prepare** include:
1. Auspice agreement letter from the council (signed by an authorised delegate).
2. Council’s ABN, incorporation evidence, and current public liability insurance.
3. Evidence of your group’s governance (e.g., constitution, committee minutes, membership list).
4. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item (rainwater tanks, shade structure).
5. A simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and budget (template provided in the guidelines).
6. Evidence of land tenure or council permission for the infrastructure location.
7. Your most recent financial statement (even if unaudited) showing the $40,000 turnover.

I strongly recommend reviewing the full Program Guidelines and Frequently Asked Questions on our website before drafting your application, as they contain detailed assessment criteria and definitions of eligible/expenditure. The application portal opens on 1 June and closes at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June.
