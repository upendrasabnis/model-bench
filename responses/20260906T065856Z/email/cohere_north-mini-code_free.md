**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program is designed to support projects that deliver measurable community benefit, and we regularly award funding to unincorporated groups that operate under the umbrella of a local government body. In such arrangements, the council typically acts as the implementing partner, holding any required insurances, licences, or risk‑management approvals on behalf of the group. As long as the council can confirm in writing that it will be responsible for the administration of the grant, the project’s legal structure, and any necessary insurance coverage, your group will satisfy the eligibility requirement for “organizational capacity.” We do request a short letter of support from the council’s relevant department confirming this auspice relationship; this helps streamline the due‑diligence phase of the review process.  

**Grant amount – minimum and maximum**  
The Community Resilience Grant is not a fixed‑sum program; awards range from a modest $5,000 to a maximum of $150,000, depending on the scope, budget, and demonstrated community impact. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appear to fall comfortably within this band. If your total project budget is below $5,000, we would still encourage you to submit a concept note, as we have a separate micro‑grant stream that can fund smaller‑scale interventions.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is explicitly listed as an eligible cost category under the “Climate‑Resilient Community Assets” component of this grant. The program recognises that such assets increase the long‑term sustainability of community spaces, especially in the face of rising temperatures. To be considered, the costs must be directly attributable to the installation, any necessary site works (e.g., foundations, drainage), and the provision of associated safety or accessibility features. Routine consumables (e.g., water filters) or ongoing maintenance expenses are not covered.  

**Supporting documents we recommend preparing**  
1. **Project Narrative** – A concise (max 2 pages) description of the problem you are addressing, the proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Narrative Budget Table** – Itemising capital costs (tanks, shade structure, labour, permits) and a brief justification for each line item.  
3. **Letters of Support** – One from your local council confirming the auspice arrangement and insurance coverage, and additional letters from partner organisations, local businesses, or community members.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – A one‑page overview of your garden’s mission, volunteer base, current activities, and any previous projects funded by other bodies.  
5. **Financial Statements** – The most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small unincorporated group, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and cash‑flow summary for the last fiscal year).  
6. **Risk Assessment and Insurance Certificate** – A copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming that your project will be covered, together with any risk‑management plan you have in place.  
7. **Photos or Site Plans** – Visual documentation of the current site and proposed installation locations.  

We strongly advise that you compile these documents in a single, well‑organised PDF package, as our reviewers will assess completeness and clarity as part of the initial eligibility check.
