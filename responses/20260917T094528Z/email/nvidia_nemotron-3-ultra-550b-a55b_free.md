Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out to confirm eligibility before investing time in a full application, and I am pleased to address each of your questions below.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:** Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be a legally constituted entity (e.g., an incorporated association, company limited by guarantee, or local government authority) that holds appropriate public liability insurance and agrees to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant. You will need to provide a signed auspicing agreement or letter of support from the council confirming this arrangement, including their ABN and insurance details.

**Grant Amounts:** For this round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000. Your proposed project budget should fall within this range. Given your annual turnover of approximately $40,000, please ensure the requested amount is proportionate to your organisational capacity and the project scale.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:** Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible expenditure category. Rainwater tanks and a permanent shade structure to mitigate heat impacts and ensure continued operation during extreme weather events align squarely with the program’s objectives. Eligible costs include purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary site preparation or plumbing. Ongoing maintenance costs, however, are not covered.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:** In addition to the auspicing agreement, please have ready: (1) your group’s governance structure and committee contact list; (2) a simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and budget breakdown; (3) quotes from at least two suppliers for each major infrastructure item; (4) evidence of land tenure or council permission for the installation site; (5) your most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report; and (6) letters of community support demonstrating the garden’s role in local resilience.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 28 June. I encourage you to begin drafting your application in our online portal as soon as possible, as the system allows you to save progress and return later. If you would like feedback on a draft budget or project plan before submission, please feel free to email them to me at least one week prior to the deadline.
