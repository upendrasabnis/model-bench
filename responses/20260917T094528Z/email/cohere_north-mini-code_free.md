**1. Eligibility for unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program explicitly welcomes applications from unincorporated community groups that operate under the auspices of a local government entity, provided that the council can demonstrate its endorsement of the project and its responsibility for any required insurances or liabilities. Because your garden is hosted by the municipal council and the council holds the necessary insurance policies on your behalf, you satisfy this condition. The council’s formal support letter (or equivalent endorsement) should be included in the application package to confirm that the council will act as the fiscal sponsor for any grant funds awarded.  

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award amount. Instead, we assess each proposal against the program’s strategic objectives and the total cost of the proposed activities. Typical awards for infrastructure projects of the scale you describe range from $15,000 to $75,000, but we evaluate the merits of the application on a case‑by‑case basis. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should be detailed in the budget section of the application, allowing us to determine an appropriate funding level that aligns with the overall budget request.  

**3. Eligibility of infrastructure costs (rainwater tanks and shade structures)**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience and sustainability of community operations is fully eligible under the “Environmental Adaptation” component of the grant. Both rainwater harvesting systems and durable shade structures qualify, provided they:  

* Serve a clear community benefit (e.g., water security, reduced heat stress, extended growing season).  
* Are installed in accordance with local building codes and environmental regulations.  
* Include provisions for maintenance or training to ensure long‑term operability.  

We recommend attaching technical specifications, quotations, and any required permits for these items in the supporting documentation.  

**4. Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest compiling the following materials:  

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, objectives, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Council Endorsement Letter** – a formal statement from the local council confirming its support, insurance coverage, and willingness to act as fiscal host.  
3. **Budget and Financial Statements** – a detailed budget breakdown (including unit costs, quantities, and total project cost) together with the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements for the prior fiscal year (or, if none exist, a simple income‑expense summary).  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a one‑page overview of your group, its volunteer base, and any past community projects.  
5. **Quotes and Technical Specifications** – separate quotes for the rainwater tanks and shade structure, along with any engineering or installation plans.  
6. **Risk Management Plan** – an outline of how you will manage safety, maintenance, and contingency plans for the proposed infrastructure.  
7. **Evaluation Framework** – preliminary metrics (e.g., water saved, temperature reduction, increased growing days) you intend to use to measure success.  

Please note that all financial documentation should be clearly labeled with your organization’s name and the date of preparation.
