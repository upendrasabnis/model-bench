First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under the formal auspices of a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) enters into a legally binding funding agreement with us and assumes full financial and legal responsibility for the grant. The council must hold current public liability insurance and provide a letter of support confirming their willingness to auspice the project, including acceptance of reporting and acquittal obligations.

Second, the Community Resilience Grant offers funding between $5,000 and $50,000 per project. There is no requirement to request the maximum amount; applicants should apply for the genuine cost of the proposed activities. Your project budget of approximately $40,000 falls well within this range.

Third, infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat events—is explicitly listed as an eligible capital cost in the program guidelines. However, routine maintenance, operational expenses, and retrospective costs incurred before a funding agreement is executed are not eligible.

Finally, to streamline your application, please prepare the following supporting documents: (1) a signed letter from the council CEO or authorised delegate confirming auspicing arrangements and insurance coverage; (2) your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the $40,000 turnover; (3) two written quotes for each major infrastructure item; (4) a simple project plan with timeline, risk assessment, and sustainability strategy; and (5) evidence of land tenure or council permission for the proposed installations.
