Thank you for reaching out and for the opportunity to clarify the eligibility criteria for our upcoming Community Resilience Grant round. I’m pleased to confirm that your organization can indeed proceed with a full application, and I will address each of the points you raised.  

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program expressly permits unincorporated community groups provided they are formally auspiced—or “hosted”—by a local government entity that can provide the necessary legal and financial oversight. In your case, the council’s involvement, including the provision of insurance coverage, satisfies this requirement. The council’s auspice will be documented in the application’s “ organisational structure” section, and we will need a letter from the council confirming its support and the scope of its oversight.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
There is no fixed minimum or maximum grant size for this round; awards are determined on the basis of need, sustainability, and alignment with the program’s strategic outcomes. Historically, grants have ranged from approximately $15,000 to $75,000, but we assess each proposal individually. If your budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure totals within that general range, you should be able to fit comfortably within the program’s parameters.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure such as rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures is expressly listed as an eligible cost category under the “Climate‑Resilient Facilities” component of the grant. These assets must be directly related to enhancing the group’s operational resilience to climate impacts—in this instance, ensuring the garden can continue functioning during hotter summer periods. The costs should be broken down in the budget (including equipment, installation, and any necessary site works) and accompanied by quotations or preliminary designs.  

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend compiling the following documents:  

1. **Auspice Confirmation Letter** – a formal letter from your local council confirming its role as auspice, the insurance coverage it provides, and any relevant council approvals.  
2. **Organizational Profile** – a concise constitution or set of rules that outlines your group’s purpose, volunteer structure, and decision‑making processes.  
3. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple profit‑and‑loss statement and balance sheet) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover you mentioned, plus a cash‑flow projection for the upcoming year.  
4. **Project Plan and Budget** – a detailed narrative describing the rainwater tank and shade structure components, their expected benefits, a timeline with milestones, and a itemized budget with quotations.  
5. **Risk Assessment and Insurance** – a summary of any additional risk assessments you have conducted, together with proof of insurance (even if held by the council, it is useful to have a copy of the certificate).  
6. **Community Impact Statement** – evidence of community need, such as attendance figures, user surveys, or letters of support from local residents and stakeholder groups.  
7. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from the council, partner organisations, or influential community members.  

We also advise that you review the full grant guidelines on our website (Section 3 – Eligibility and Section 5 – Required Documentation) to ensure you do not overlook any supplemental requirements, such as environmental impact assessments or Aboriginal heritage clearances.
