**1. Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**

- The Grant is open to *community‑based organisations* that have a formal governance structure, even if they are not yet a registered charity.  
- An unincorporated group can apply if it can demonstrate:  
  1. A written constitution, by‑laws, or a memorandum of association that sets out how decisions are made;  
  2. Evidence of the council’s sponsorship – for example, an official council letter confirming that the garden operates under the council’s auspices and that the council holds the required insurances;  
  3. A clear line of accountability, such as a council‑appointed treasurer or an audit trail of financial transactions.  

If you do not yet have a formal constitution, we can provide a simple template that meets the Grant’s governance requirements.

**2. Minimum and maximum grant amounts**

- The standard award range is **$5,000 to $30,000**.  
- The exact amount you may receive is determined by the total project cost, the proportion of your own contribution, and the overall funding pool for the round.  
- For a project of the scale you describe (rainwater tanks +omat) we anticipate that an award of $12,000–$18,000 would be realistic, assuming a modest matching contribution of 10–15 % from your own funds or council.
