First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups auspiced by a local council are generally eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your council) is a legally constituted entity—such as a local government authority—that can enter into a funding agreement on your behalf. The council would need to provide a letter of support confirming their willingness to auspice the project, accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant funds, and maintain appropriate insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation, etc.) for the duration of the project. Your current arrangement, where the council already holds relevant insurances, is a positive indicator.

Second, the Community Resilience Grant round typically offers grants between $5,000 and $50,000. There is no strict minimum, but projects under $5,000 are rarely competitive due to the administrative burden relative to impact. The maximum request per applicant is $50,000, though exceptional multi-year projects may be considered for up to $75,000 with prior approval. Given your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, your project appears well within this range.

Third, infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience—such as rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures that enable continued operation during extreme heat—is explicitly listed as an eligible capital cost in the program guidelines. Eligible expenses include purchase and installation of tanks, pumps, plumbing, structural materials, and contractor labour. Ineligible costs include ongoing operational expenses (e.g., water bills), routine maintenance, and retrospective expenditure incurred before a funding agreement is executed.

Finally, to streamline your application, I recommend preparing the following supporting documents now:
- A signed letter of auspice from the council CEO or authorised delegate, confirming the arrangement and insurance coverage.
- Your group’s most recent financial statement (income/expenditure) or a simple profit-and-loss summary for the last financial year.
- Quotes from at least two suppliers for the tanks and shade structure (formal quotes on letterhead, itemised, and dated within the last three months).
- A brief project plan outlining key milestones, risk management (including heat safety), and how the infrastructure will benefit the wider community.
- Evidence of land tenure or permission to install infrastructure on the garden site (e.g., a lease, licence, or council resolution).
