Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for your interest in the Community Resilience Grant. I’m pleased to clarify the points you raised so you can decide whether to proceed with a full application.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our funding guidelines specifically include “organisations that are auspiced by a local government authority” as eligible applicants, provided they meet the other criteria outlined in the grant policy. Because your garden operates under the umbrella of the municipal council and the council holds the necessary insurance on your behalf, you satisfy this requirement. You will simply need to demonstrate that the council’s endorsement is current and that you have a written agreement confirming the auspicing arrangement.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers a flexible range of funding. The minimum award is **$5,000**, and the maximum is **$50,000** per project. Projects that request an amount at the lower end of the scale are often favoured when they demonstrate strong community impact and sound financial planning, while larger requests are considered when they align with the grant’s resilience objectives and show robust co‑funding or in‑kind contributions. Your proposed work—installing rainwater tanks and a shade structure—fits comfortably within the eligible cost categories and is well‑suited to a request of approximately **$12,000–$18,000**, which comfortably falls between the minimum and maximum thresholds.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Infrastructure that enhances the capacity of a community facility to withstand climate‑related stresses is explicitly eligible under the grant. Rainwater harvesting systems and shade structures are both recognised as qualifying capital works, provided they are integral to maintaining the garden’s operations during extreme weather events. You should be prepared to supply quotations, a detailed project design, and a maintenance plan to demonstrate that the infrastructure will be sustainable over the long term.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, please include the following items:

1. **Letter of endorsement** from the council confirming the auspicing relationship and outlining any in‑kind support (e.g., insurance coverage).  
2. **A project budget** that breaks down capital costs, labour, and any anticipated in‑kind contributions.  
3. **Quotes or tenders** from suppliers for the rainwater tanks and shade structure.  
4. **A risk‑assessment and resilience plan** that explains how the proposed works will mitigate climate‑related risks.  
5. **Evidence of community support**, such as letters of endorsement from local residents, schools, or other partners.  
6. **Your most recent financial statements** (or a summary of income and expenditure) to substantiate the $40,000 annual turnover figure.

Should you decide to move forward, we are happy to arrange a brief phone call or virtual meeting to walk through the application form and answer any further questions you may have. Please let us know a convenient time, and we will coordinate accordingly.
