**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from both incorporated not‑for‑profit entities and unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced (or otherwise affiliated) with a local government body. When an unincorporated group operates under a council’s auspices, the council typically acts as the primary contracting party and is responsible for meeting insurance, compliance and reporting requirements. If your garden has a formal auspice agreement with the council – or if the council can provide a letter of support confirming that it will hold the grant funding and oversee the project – your group will be considered eligible. Please ensure that the council’s involvement is documented in a way that demonstrates authority to receive and manage grant funds.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
We do not prescribe a fixed minimum or maximum grant size; awards are determined on a case‑by‑case basis according to the scope, impact and total project budget of the proposal. Historically, grant awards for community infrastructure projects have ranged from $5,000 to $50,000, but applications outside this band are certainly welcome if the justification is robust. The key is to demonstrate clear value for money and alignment with the grant’s resilience objectives.

**Eligibility of rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience and the sustainability of community operations is fully eligible. Rainwater harvesting systems and weather‑resilient shade structures both fall under our “environmental adaptation” cost category, provided they are necessary to achieve the project’s stated outcomes (e.g., ensuring year‑round production during hot summers). We do require that such capital assets be owned by the applicant (or the auspicing council) and remain in use for a minimum of three years after installation.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To streamline the review process, we recommend assembling the following materials:

1. **Project Narrative** – a concise description of the problem, objectives, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Financial Projections** – a detailed breakdown of all costs (including equipment, installation, contingency, and ongoing maintenance), plus the sources of matching funds.  
3. **Proof of Insurance** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate confirming coverage for the project; if your group will manage day‑to‑day operations, a supplemental liability waiver may be required.  
4. **Council Endorsement Letter** – an official letter from the council confirming the auspice relationship, its willingness to act as the primary grantee, and any conditions it imposes.  
5. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your garden’s membership, volunteer base, and community impact (e.g., produce distribution figures, outreach activities).  
6. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for very small groups, a simple statement of revenue and expenses) to illustrate fiscal responsibility.  
7. **Site Plans or Sketches** – visual representations of where the rainwater tanks and shade structure will be located, indicating any constraints or site‑specific considerations.  
8. **Risk Assessment and Mitigation Plan** – an outline of potential project risks (e.g., water quality, structural safety) and how they will be managed.
