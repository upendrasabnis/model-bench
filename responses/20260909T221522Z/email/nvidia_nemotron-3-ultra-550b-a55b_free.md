Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to provide clarity on each of your questions.

**Eligibility of Auspiced Groups:**  
Yes, unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible legal entity—such as a local council—are eligible to apply. The auspicing organisation must be listed as the legal applicant on the grant agreement, hold the required insurances (public liability, workers’ compensation, etc.), and accept legal and financial responsibility for the project. As your council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf, this arrangement meets our requirements provided a signed auspicing agreement is submitted with the application.

**Grant Amounts:**  
The Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Your proposed budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure falls well within this range. Please note that we require a minimum 10% co-contribution (cash or in-kind) from the applicant or auspicing body, which can include volunteer labour valued at $30 per hour.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure:**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible under the “Climate Adaptation Infrastructure” stream. Costs must be for purchase and installation only; ongoing maintenance, operational expenses, and consumables are not funded.

**Supporting Documents to Prepare:**  
To avoid delays, please gather the following:
1. Signed auspicing agreement between your group and the council (on council letterhead).
2. Council’s current Certificate of Currency for public liability insurance ($20M minimum).
3. Your group’s most recent financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
4. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item (tanks, shade structure).
5. Site plan or photos showing proposed installation locations.
6. Letters of support from at least two community stakeholders (e.g., local school, neighbourhood house).
7. A simple project plan with timeline, risk register, and volunteer management strategy.

The application portal opens 1 June and closes 30 June. I recommend drafting your responses in a Word document first, as the portal times out after 30 minutes of inactivity. If you would like a pre-submission review, please send a draft to grants@communityresilience.gov.au no later than 20 June.

Please do not hesitate to contact me if you need further guidance.
