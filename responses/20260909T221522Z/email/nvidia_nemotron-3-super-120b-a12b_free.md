Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your email and for the thoughtful work you are doing with the community garden. I appreciate you taking the time to confirm eligibility before preparing a full application, and I am happy to provide the details you requested.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to both incorporated and unincorporated organisations, provided that the applicant can demonstrate a clear governance structure and the capacity to manage grant funds. When an unincorporated group operates under the auspices of a local council—as you do—we consider the council the legal entity responsible for the grant. In such cases, the council must be listed as the “lead applicant” on the application form, and a signed letter of support or memorandum of understanding from the council outlining its role (including financial oversight, insurance coverage, and reporting responsibilities) is required. Your garden can therefore proceed with the application as long as the council agrees to act as the lead applicant and provides the necessary documentation.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum award is $5,000 and the maximum award is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your projected budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure, you should tailor your request to fall within these limits, ensuring that the total project cost (including any co‑funding or in‑kind contributions) is clearly itemised.

**Eligible costs**  
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances resilience to climate‑related stresses—such as rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, irrigation upgrades, and related site works—is explicitly listed as an eligible expense. Costs may include purchase and installation of tanks, piping, fittings, structural materials for the shade shelter, labour (if contracted), and necessary permits. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., staff salaries, routine maintenance) are not eligible unless they are directly tied to the installation and commissioning phase of the project.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
1. **Lead applicant letter** – a signed statement from the local council confirming its role as lead applicant, its insurance coverage, and its willingness to administer the grant.  
2. **Project plan** – a concise description (max 2 pages) outlining the problem, objectives, proposed activities, timeline, and expected resilience outcomes.  
3. **Detailed budget** – a spreadsheet showing all requested grant items, any co‑funding or in‑kind contributions, and quotes (or indicative pricing) for tanks, shade structure materials, and installation.  
4. **Governance evidence** – minutes or a resolution from your garden’s coordinating committee showing endorsement of the project and the auspicing arrangement.  
5. **Proof of insurance** – a copy of the council’s public liability policy covering the garden site (or a certificate of currency).  
6. **Letters of support** (optional but helpful) – from neighbouring residents, local schools, or other community groups that will benefit from the enhanced resilience of the garden.
