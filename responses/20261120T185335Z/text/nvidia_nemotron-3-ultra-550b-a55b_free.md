## 1. Philosophy & Mindset: Grants as Partnerships, Not ATMs

> **The Golden Rule:** *A grant is not a gift; it is a contract for social impact. The funder is buying outcomes, not funding your existence.*

### 1.1 Shifting the Mental Model
Most novice grant writers approach the process as **fundraising** (asking for money). Experts approach it as **business development** (selling a solution).
*   **Fundraising Mindset:** "We need $50k to keep our doors open." -> *Result: Weak needs statement, generic budget, low win rate.*
*   **Business Development Mindset:** "We have a proven, scalable model that reduces recidivism by 15%. We need $50k to pilot this in District 4." -> *Result: Compelling investment thesis, specific metrics, high win rate.*

### 1.2 The "Funder's Desk" Exercise
Before writing a single word, visualize the Program Officer (PO).
*   They have 50 applications to read in one weekend.
*   They are looking for reasons to **say "No"** (incomplete, ineligible, vague) so they can focus on the "Yes" pile.
*   **Your Job:** Make it physically impossible to say "No" due to formatting, eligibility, or clarity. Force them to evaluate you on *merit*.

### 1.3 Ethical Imperatives
*   **Mission Creep:** Never chase money that pulls you off mission. "Mission drift" destroys organizational credibility long-term.
*   **Truthfulness:** Never inflate numbers, fabricate partnerships, or promise outcomes you cannot measure.
*   **Cost Recovery:** Never submit a budget that doesn't cover true costs (including indirect/overhead) unless you have unrestricted reserves to subsidize the grant.

## 2. Phase 0: Organizational Readiness (The "Pre-Work")

> **Reality Check:** *You cannot write a competitive grant in two weeks if your infrastructure is broken. 80% of the work happens before the RFP drops.*

### 2.1 The "Grant-Ready" Audit Checklist
*Use this quarterly. If you cannot check 90% of these, pause writing and build infrastructure.*

#### Governance & Legal
- [ ] **501(c)(3) Determination Letter** (current, accessible).
- [ ] **Bylaws** updated (conflict of interest, whistleblower, document retention policies).
- [ ] **Board Roster** with affiliations, terms, giving status (100% board giving expected).
- [ ] **Articles of Incorporation** / State Charity Registration current.
- [ ] **IRS Form 990** filed on time for last 3 years (posted on GuideStar/Candid).

#### Financial Health
- [ ] **Audited Financial Statements** (or Review/Compilation per threshold) for last 2 years.
- [ ] **Current Operating Budget** (Board approved).
- [ ] **Functional Expense Allocation Policy** (how you split Program / Admin / Fundraising).
- [ ] **Indirect Cost Rate** (Negotiated NICRA, De Minimis 10%, or calculated policy).
- [ ] **Cash Flow Projection** (12-month rolling).
- [ ] **Reserves Policy** (target: 3–6 months operating expenses).

#### Program & Data
- [ ] **Logic Model / Theory of Change** documented for *every* major program.
- [ ] **Client/Participant Database** (clean, de-identified, exportable).
- [ ] **Outcome Data** (baseline + 2+ years of trend data).
- [ ] **Evidence Base** citations (internal evals + external research).
- [ ] **Curricula / Protocols** documented (manualized for replication).

#### Administrative Capacity
- [ ] **Grant Calendar** (12-month rolling, color-coded by deadline type).
- [ ] **Document Vault** (Cloud-based: 990, Audit, 501c3, Insurance, Bios, Org Chart, Policies).
- [ ] **SAM.gov / UEI Registration** active (Required for *all* federal funding).
- [ ] **Grants.gov Workspace** access configured.
- [ ] **Conflict of Interest Forms** signed annually by staff/board.
- [ ] **Record Retention / Destruction Schedule** implemented.

### 2.2 Building the "Boilerplate Library"
Stop rewriting the same paragraphs. Maintain a master document (Google Doc / SharePoint / Notion) with **approved, dated, character-counted** versions of:
1.  **Organizational History/Mission** (50w, 100w, 250w, 500w versions).
2.  **Organizational Capacity/Track Record** (Staff expertise, facilities, tech).
3.  **Key Personnel Bios** (ED, CFO, Program Dir, Evaluator – 150w & 1-page CV).
4.  **Standard Program Descriptions** (for each core program).
5.  **Demographic Service Data** (Race, ethnicity, income, geography – updated annually).
6.  **Standard Sustainability Statement** (Diversified revenue plan).
7.  **DEI / Equity Statement** (Organizational commitment + programmatic application).

## 3. Phase 1: Prospect Research – Finding the Right Fit

### 3.1 The "Alignment Triangle" Framework
A viable prospect must hit the center of three circles:
1.  **Geographic Fit:** Do they fund *your* zip code/county/state/region?
2.  **Programmatic Fit:** Do they fund *your* issue area *and* your specific intervention type (e.g., they fund "youth" but only "arts," not "sports")?
3.  **Values/Philosophy Fit:** Do they trust community-led models? Do they require evidence-based models only? Do they fund General Operating vs. Project Only?

### 3.2 Research Tools & Hierarchy of Sources
| Tier | Source | Cost | Best For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1 (Primary)** | **Funder Website / 990-PF** | Free | *Ground truth:* Guidelines, deadlines, past grantees, average grant size, board members. |
| **2 (Database)** | **Foundation Directory Online (Candid)** | $$$ | Searching by location, subject, population, grant size. "Who funds X in Y?" |
| **3 (Database)** | **GrantStation / Instrumentl / GrantWatch** | $$ | Alerts, federal/state tracking, smaller family foundations not in FDO. |
| **4 (Gov)** | **Grants.gov / SAM.gov / State Portal** | Free | Federal/State RFPs, forecasting (SAM.gov "Contract Opportunities" for sub-awards). |
| **5 (Network)** | **Peer EDs / Program Officers / Giving Circles** | Time | *Intel:* "Are they shifting focus?" "Is the PO new?" "Is the process a black box?" |

### 3.3 Mining the 990-PF (The Secret Weapon)
Download the last **3 years** of the foundation’s 990-PF (via Candid, ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, or Foundation website).
**Analyze these fields:**
*   **Part XV, Line 3a:** Grants Paid (Recipient, Address, Amount, Purpose).
*   **Pattern Recognition:**
    *   *Avg Grant Size:* If avg is $15k, don't ask for $100k.
    *   *Repeat Funding:* Do they fund same orgs yearly? (Indicates relationship-based).
    *   *Geographic Spread:* % local vs. national.
    *   *New vs. Renewal:* % first-time grantees.
    *   *Board Connections:* Cross-reference Board names with your Board/Donors.

### 3.4 The "Cold Call" Protocol (Contacting Program Officers)
**Never** submit a cold proposal to a foundation that accepts LOIs or calls without talking to them first.
**Script:**
> *"Hi [Name], I’m [Name] from [Org]. I’ve reviewed your 2023 guidelines and 990 data. We run [Program] serving [Population] in [Geography]. We have [Key Outcome Metric]. We are considering applying for the [Cycle] deadline for [Amount]. Before I invest staff time, I want to confirm: 1) Is this alignment a priority for your current cycle? 2) Are there any unwritten preferences (e.g., multi-year only, no capital) I should know? 3) Would you prefer a brief LOI or a call first?"*

**Track in CRM:** Date, Contact, Outcome, "Green/Yellow/Red" flag.

### 3.5 Prospect Tracking Spreadsheet (Master Pipeline)
| Funder | Type (Fed/State/Found/Corp) | Deadline | LOI Due? | Max Award | Avg Award | Alignment Score (1-5) | Contact/PO | Status (Research/LOI/Write/Submit/Pending/Award/Decline) | Notes/Intel |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Smith Family Fdn | Family Foundation | 3/15 | Yes (2/1) | $50k | $25k | 5 | J. Doe (PO) | Writing | Funded us 2021; prefers 2-yr grants |
| DOJ OJJDP | Federal | 6/1 | No | $750k | $400k | 4 | Grants.gov | Research | Requires 25% match; SAM reg critical |

## 4. Phase 2: Deconstructing the RFP / Guidelines

### 4.1 The "Shredding" Process
Print the RFP/Guidelines. Use three highlighters:
*   **YELLOW (Eligibility/Compliance):** Hard stops. (Due date, page limits, font, margins, required forms, match req, geographic restriction, 501c3 status).
*   **PINK (Evaluation Criteria / Scoring Rubric):** *This is your outline.* If the rubric says "Need (20pts), Approach (30pts), Capacity (20pts), Budget (15pts), Evaluation (15pts)," your narrative **must** have these exact headings in this order.
*   **GREEN (Keywords/Themes):** Words repeated often: "Equity," "Lived Experience," "Scalability," "Evidence-Based," "Trauma-Informed," "Two-Generation." **Mirror this language.**

### 4.2 Creating the Compliance Matrix (The "Bible")
Create a spreadsheet. Every row = a requirement. Assign owner + internal deadline.

| RFP Section | Requirement Detail | Location in Proposal | Responsible | Internal Due | Status | Word/Page Limit | Formatting Spec |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Eligibility | 501c3 letter | Attachment A | Ops Mgr | T-14 days | Done | N/A | PDF |
| Narrative | Needs Statement | Section A | Lead Writer | T-10 days | Drafting | 2 pages | 12pt Times, 1" margins |
| Budget | Match Requirement (20%) | Budget Narrative | CFO | T-7 days | Pending | N/A | Excel Template Provided |
| Evaluation | Logic Model | Attachment C | Evaluator | T-5 days | Outline | 1 page | Landscape, 10pt min |
| Appendices | Letters of Support (3) | Attachment D | Dev Dir | T-3 days | Requested | 1 page each | On Letterhead, Signed |

### 4.3 Decoding "Unwritten" Requirements
*   **"Letter of Inquiry (LOI) Required":** The LOI *is* the first round. Treat it with full proposal rigor.
*   **"We accept unsolicited proposals":** Usually means "We have a portal but low acceptance rate." Call first.
*   **"No Indirect Costs Allowed":** You *must* bake overhead into direct line items (e.g., "Program Manager @ 1.0 FTE" includes their share of rent/insurance).
*   **"Collaboration Encouraged":** = **Required.** Solo apps get rejected. Secure MOUs *before* writing.

## 5. Phase 3: The Narrative Core – Crafting the Case for Support

### 5.1 The Standard Narrative Architecture (The "Heinz 57" Sections)
Most proposals follow this logic flow. **Match the funder's required headings exactly.**

1.  **Executive Summary / Abstract** (Write LAST)
2.  **Statement of Need / Problem Statement**
3.  **Project Description / Methodology / Approach**
4.  **Goals, Objectives, & Outcomes (SMART)**
5.  **Logic Model / Theory of Change** (Visual + Narrative)
6.  **Evaluation Plan** (Process + Outcome)
7.  **Organizational Capacity / Key Personnel**
8.  **Sustainability / Future Funding**
9.  **Budget & Budget Narrative**

---

### 5.2 The Needs Statement: The "Hook" (Most Critical Section)
**Purpose:** Prove the problem is **Real, Urgent, Local, Solvable, and Unaddressed.**

#### The 4-Paragraph Formula
1.  **The Macro Context (National/State Data):** "National high school dropout rate is X%; cost to economy is $Y." (Cite: NCES, Census, Peer-reviewed lit).
2.  **The Micro Reality (Your Service Area):** "In County Z, the rate is 2X national avg. Only 40% of eligible youth access services." (Cite: Local Dept of Ed, Kids Count, Community Health Needs Assessment).
3.  **The Gap / Missing Piece:** "Existing providers serve only ages 14-16. No provider targets the critical 12-13 transition window using [Your Specific Model]." (This justifies *you*).
4.  **The Consequence of Inaction:** "Without intervention, 200 additional youth annually enter the juvenile justice system, costing County $5M/yr."

#### Needs Statement Checklist
- [ ] **Data is cited** (Source, Year, Link). No "studies show."
- [ ] **Data is local** (Zip code/County level preferred).
*   **Disaggregated** (Race, Gender, Income, Disability status).
- [ ] **Gap Analysis** explicit: "What exists vs. What is needed."
- [ ] **Voice of Constituent:** Include 1 short quote/anecdote (de-identified).
- [ ] **No "Kitchen Sink":** Focus on the *specific* problem this grant solves.
- [ ] **Asset-Based Framing:** "Community lacks *access to resources*, not *resilience*."

#### Concrete Example: Weak vs. Strong
> **Weak:** "Homelessness is a big problem in our city. Many people sleep on streets. Shelters are full. We need money to open a new shelter."
> **Strong:** "According to the 2023 HUD Point-in-Time Count, unsheltered homelessness in Metro County rose 22% YoY (1,240 individuals), driven primarily by a 40% loss of affordable SRO units since 2018 (County Housing Authority). While three emergency shelters operate at 110% capacity, **zero** low-barrier shelters accept individuals with active substance use disorders (SUD) or pets—two primary barriers cited by 68% of unsheltered adults in our 2022 Street Outreach Survey. This gap leaves 300+ high-acuity individuals cycling through ERs ($18k/visit avg) and jail ($140/day) rather than housing. Our Housing First model, adapted from the Pathways to Housing evidence base, is the only local intervention designed for this specific population."

### 5.3 Project Description / Methodology: The "How"
**Structure:** Chronological or Functional (Phases).
**Rule:** *Every activity must trace back to a Need and forward to an Outcome.*

#### The "Activity-Output-Outcome" Mapping Table (Insert in narrative or appendix)
| Core Activity | Frequency / Dosage | Output (Count) | Short-Term Outcome | Measurement Tool |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT) Groups | 2x/week, 90 min, 12 weeks | 4 cohorts x 10 youth = 40 youth served | Reduced depressive symptoms (PHQ-9 ↓ 5 pts) | PHQ-9 Pre/Post |
| Case Management | Weekly 1-hr sessions | 40 youth x 12 sessions = 480 hrs | Stable housing / School enrollment | HMIS / School Records |
| Family Engagement Nights | Monthly | 4 events x 20 families = 80 touchpoints | Increased family cohesion (FACES-IV) | FACES-IV Survey |

#### Writing Tips for Methodology
*   **Dosage Matters:** "We provide tutoring" is weak. "We provide 1:1 high-dosage tutoring (3x/week, 45 mins, 36 weeks) by certified teachers" is strong.
*   **Curriculum Name-Drop:** "Using *Trauma-Focused CBT (TF-CBT) protocol*" > "Using therapy."
*   **Staffing Ratios:** Explicitly state: "1 Case Manager : 15 Clients (Best Practice Standard)."
*   **Trauma-Informed / Cultural Responsiveness:** Dedicate a paragraph to *how* you deliver (language access, scheduling, physical space, staff training), not just *what*.

---

### 5.4 Goals, Objectives, & Outcomes: The SMART+E Framework
*   **Goal:** Broad, aspirational, long-term. *"Improve youth mental health in District 4."*
*   **Objective (SMART):** The *contractual* target.
    *   **S**pecific: Who, What, Where.
    *   **M**easurable: Number, Percentage, Rate.
    *   **A**chievable: Based on baseline/capacity.
    *   **R**elevant: Ties to Need & Funder Priority.
    *   **T**ime-bound: "By Month 12 / End of Grant Year."
    *   **+E (Equity):** Disaggregated target. *"Reduce PHQ-9 scores by 20% for Latinx youth (n=15) and Black youth (n=15) separately."*
*   **Outcome:** The *change* measured. *"Youth report improved coping skills."*

#### Objective Writing Workshop
| Draft Objective | Critique | SMART+E Revision |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| "Serve more youth." | Vague, no target, no outcome. | "Enroll **50 unduplicated youth** (ages 12-14) in the CBT program **by Month 6**." |
| "Improve mental health." | Not measurable. | "**70% of enrolled youth** will show a **clinically significant reduction (≥5 pts)** on the PHQ-9 **from baseline to 12-week post-test**." |
| "Help families." | Passive. | "Conduct **monthly Family Psychoeducation workshops** achieving **80% average attendance** of primary caregivers **across 12 months**." |

### 5.5 Organizational Capacity: Proving You Can Deliver
Don't just list staff. Prove **Readiness**.
**Structure:**
1.  **Track Record:** "Org has managed $2.5M in federal/state grants over 5 years with zero findings." (Cite specific grants).
2.  **Key Personnel Table:** Name, Role, %FTE on *this* grant, Qualifications (Certs, Lived Exp, Years Exp).
    *   *Tip:* If hiring, write "To Be Hired (TBH)" with Job Description attached.
3.  **Infrastructure:** Finance system (QuickBooks/Abila/Sage), HR, IT, Data/Case Mgmt System (Apricot, ETO, Salesforce), Physical Space.
4.  **Governance:** Board Finance Committee, Audit Committee, Strategic Plan dates.
5.  **Equity Capacity:** Board/Staff demographics vs. Community served. DEI Plan status.

---

### 5.6 Sustainability: Beyond "We Will Write More Grants"
Funders hate: *"We will seek other funding."*
**The 4-Pillar Sustainability Model (Required Narrative):**

1.  **Programmatic Integration:** "This pilot becomes a core line item in our FY26 Operating Budget (Board approved 3/24)."
2.  **Revenue Diversification:** Table showing current revenue mix + projected shift.
    *   *Current:* Gov 60%, Fdn 20%, Ind 10%, Earned 10%.
    *   *Post-Grant:* Gov 40%, Fdn 15%, Ind 20%, Earned (Medicaid/Fees) 25%.
3.  **Policy / Systems Change:** "We will use outcomes data to advocate for Medicaid reimbursement code [Code X] for this service."
4.  **Capacity Building:** "Grant funds 0.5 FTE Development Director to build individual giving pipeline (ROI 4:1 projected)."

## 6. Phase 4: The Logic Model & Theory of Change

### 6.1 Theory of Change (ToC) vs. Logic Model
*   **ToC (Narrative/Visual):** The *Why*. The causal pathway + Assumptions + Context. "If we do X, then Y happens *because* [mechanism], assuming [condition]."
*   **Logic Model (Table):** The *What*. The operational blueprint (Inputs -> Activities -> Outputs -> Outcomes -> Impact). Required by 95% of institutional funders.

### 6.2 The Standard Logic Model Columns (Left to Right)
| **Inputs (Resources)** | **Activities (What we do)** | **Outputs (Direct Products)** | **Short-Term Outcomes (Learning)** | **Mid-Term Outcomes (Action)** | **Long-Term Impact (Condition)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| $150k Grant<br>0.5 FTE Clinician<br>Curriculum License<br>Office Space | CBT Groups (24 sessions)<br>Case Mgmt (Weekly)<br>Family Workshops (4) | 40 youth enrolled<br>480 therapy hrs<br>80 family contacts | ↑ Coping skills (PHQ-9)<br>↑ Mental Health Literacy<br>↑ Family Communication | ↓ School disciplinary incidents<br>↑ School attendance<br>↓ ER visits | Reduced youth depression rates in District 4<br>Systemic adoption of TF-CBT in schools |

### 6.3 The "Assumptions" & "External Factors" Rows (Critical)
*Add these as footer rows or a separate column.*
*   **Assumptions (Internal):** "Staff retain certification," "Referral pipeline from schools remains open," "No major leadership turnover."
*   **External Factors (Context):** "State Medicaid policy unchanged," "School district calendar stable," "No natural disasters/pandemics."
*   **Why?** Shows sophistication. If an assumption fails, you have a pre-approved pivot rationale.

### 6.4 Visual Design Standards
*   **Landscape orientation.**
*   **One Page Max.** (Use 9pt font, tight margins).
*   **Color Code:** Inputs (Blue), Activities (Green), Outputs (Orange), Outcomes (Red), Impact (Purple).
*   **Arrows:** Show flow. Feedback loops (Evaluation -> Activities) are a bonus.

## 7. Phase 5: The Budget – Translating Program into Numbers

> **The Budget is a Narrative Document.** It tells the exact same story as the text, just in math. **Inconsistency = Rejection.**

### 7.1 Budget Structure: The Standard Categories
1.  **Personnel (Salaries & Wages)**
2.  **Fringe Benefits** (Taxes, Health, Retirement, Workers Comp)
3.  **Travel** (Local mileage, Conferences, Client transport)
4.  **Equipment** (Capitalized >$5k/unit, useful life >1yr)
5.  **Supplies** (Consumables <$5k: Office, Program, Tech <$5k)
6.  **Contractual / Consultants** (Sub-awards, Evaluators, Auditors, Trainers)
7.  **Construction / Renovation** (Rare for nonprofits, usually separate)
8.  **Other Direct Costs** (Rent, Utilities, Insurance, Printing, Client Stipends, Incentives, Software Licenses, Indirect Costs if allowed).
9.  **Total Direct Costs**
10. **Indirect Costs (F&A / Overhead)** -> **Total Project Cost**

### 7.2 Personnel: The "FTE" Discipline
**Never** list a lump sum "Staff: $50,000."
**Always** use a Personnel Detail Table:

| Position Title | Name (or TBH) | Annual Salary | % FTE on Grant | Months | Grant Request | Cost Share / Match | Total Cost |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Program Director | J. Smith | $75,000 | 50% | 12 | $37,500 | $0 | $37,500 |
| Case Manager (Bilingual) | TBH | $48,000 | 100% | 12 | $48,000 | $0 | $48,000 |
| Clinical Supervisor | Dr. Jones | $90,000 | 10% | 12 | $9,000 | $0 | $9,000 |
| **Total Personnel** | | | | | **$94,500** | **$0** | **$94,500** |

*   **% FTE:** Must match Narrative ("Case Manager provides weekly 1-hr sessions to 15 clients" = ~0.5 FTE direct service + 0.5 admin = 1.0 FTE).
*   **Salaries:** Must match your internal salary scale / 990 ranges. No "grant inflation."

### 7.3 Fringe Benefits: The Hidden Killer
*   **Calculate as % of Salary** per your audited financials / HR policy.
*   **Standard Components:** FICA (7.65%), SUTA/FUTA, Workers Comp, Health Ins (Employer share), Retirement (Match), Disability, PTO accrual.
*   **Typical Range:** 22% – 35%.
*   **Error:** Budgeting 10% fringe. Funders check this against your 990 (Part IX, Line 5-7 / Line 1). If 990 says 28%, budget 28%.

### 7.4 Indirect Costs (Overhead / F&A) – The Non-Negotiable
*   **Negotiated Rate (NICRA):** If you have a federal rate (e.g., 28% MTDC), you **must** use it for federal grants.
*   **De Minimis Rate (10% MTDC):** Available to any org *never* having a NICRA. **Elect this in writing** (Board resolution/file). Apply to **Modified Total Direct Costs (MTDC)**.
    *   *MTDC = Total Direct Costs MINUS: Equipment (>5k), Capital Expenditures, Patient Care Costs, Tuition, Subaward portion >$25k each, Rental of Facilities.*
*   **Foundation Policy:** If they cap at 10% or 15%, apply that % to their defined base (usually Total Direct or TDC - Equipment).
*   **If "No Indirect Allowed":** You **must** allocate shared costs (Rent, ED time, Audit, Insurance, IT) directly to line items via a **Cost Allocation Plan**.
    *   *Example:* Rent $24k/yr. Program uses 40% of sq ft. Budget $9,600 to "Occupancy" line item. Document methodology.

### 7.5 The Budget Narrative (Justification) – Line by Line
**Format:** Category -> Line Item -> Calculation -> Narrative Justification.
> **Personnel: Case Manager (1.0 FTE) - $48,000**
> *Calculation:* $48,000 annual salary x 1.0 FTE x 12 months.
> *Justification:* This position is essential to deliver the core CBT model requiring 1:15 caseloads. The bilingual requirement (Spanish/English) is mandated by the 65% Latinx target population (Census Tract 402). Salary aligns with Org Salary Scale Grade 4 (Range $45k-$52k) and FY23 990 reporting.

> **Supplies: Client Incentives - $2,400**
> *Calculation:* 40 youth x 12 sessions x $5 gift card/session.
> *Justification:* Evidence-based retention strategy (NIDA Clinical Trials Network Protocol). IRB approved. Tracked via signed receipt log.

### 7.6 Match / Cost Share / Leverage
*   **Cash Match:** Actual dollars spent by org/partners on *this* project.
*   **In-Kind Match:** Valued contributions (Volunteer hours @ Independent Sector rate $31.80/hr 2023, Donated space, Donated supplies).
*   **Tracking:** You **must** track match in your accounting system with same rigor as grant funds. Separate GL codes.

### 7.7 Budget Checklist (Pre-Submission)
- [ ] **Math Check:** Columns sum across (Personnel + Fringe + Travel... = Total Direct). Total Direct x Indirect Rate = Indirect. Direct + Indirect = Total.
- [ ] **Narrative Match:** Every budget line referenced in Project Description? Every staff in Narrative in Budget?
- [ ] **Funder Limits:** Salary caps? (e.g., NIH Cap $212,100). Equipment caps? Travel caps (GSA per diem)?
- [ ] **Cost Principles:** Allowable, Allocable, Reasonable, Consistent (2 CFR 200).
- [ ] **No "Miscellaneous" or "Contingency" lines.**
- [ ] **Match Commitment Letters** secured for all 3rd party match.
- [ ] **Inflation/Escalation:** Multi-year budgets: Include 3-5% COLA for Personnel/Yr 2-3.

## 8. Phase 6: Evaluation & Sustainability Planning

### 8.1 Evaluation Design: Process vs. Outcome vs. Impact
| Type | Question | Timing | Data Source | Analysis |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Process (Formative)** | *Are we doing what we said?* (Fidelity, Reach, Dose) | Monthly / Quarterly | Attendance logs, Session checklists, Staff logs | Descriptive stats (%, mean dose). CQI meetings. |
| **Outcome (Summative)** | *Did participants change?* (Knowledge, Behavior, Condition) | Pre / Post / Follow-up (3, 6, 12 mo) | Standardized Tools (PHQ-9, FACES, School Data) | Paired t-tests, Regression, Effect Sizes. |
| **Impact** | *Did community condition change?* | Longitudinal (3-5 yrs) | Population data (Census, Admin data) | Quasi-experimental (Comparison group) / RCT. |

*Most grants require Process + Short-term Outcome. Impact is for large federal/long-term.*

### 8.2 Selecting Measurement Tools (The "Gold Standard" Hierarchy)
1.  **Validated, Standardized Instruments:** PHQ-9, GAD-7, ACEs, FACES-IV, ASQ, CLASS, ERS. (Preferred).
2.  **Administrative Data:** School records (attendance, grades), HMIS, Medicaid claims, Court records. (High validity, low burden).
3.  **Custom Surveys/Tools:** Only if no standard exists. Must pilot test (Cronbach's alpha > .70).
4.  **Qualitative:** Focus groups, Interviews, Case Studies. (Context, "Why", Voice). **Code systematically** (Dedoose, NVivo, Excel thematic).
