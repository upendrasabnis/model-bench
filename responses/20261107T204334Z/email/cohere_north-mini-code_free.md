**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines explicitly state that unincorporated community groups may apply provided they operate under the formal auspices of a local government body that can provide evidence of insurance coverage, legal responsibility for assets, and the authority to enter into grant agreements. In your case, the fact that your council holds the relevant insurances on your behalf satisfies this requirement. You will, however, need to submit a letter of support (or a formal auspice agreement) from the council confirming that they will act as the primary recipient of funds and assume liability for the grant‑funded assets.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a strict minimum or maximum award. Instead, the funding level is determined by the scope and cost of the proposed activities, with typical awards ranging from $5,000 to $50,000 for projects of comparable scale. Your estimated infrastructure costs (rainwater tanks and a shade structure) fall comfortably within this range, but you are welcome to propose a budget that reflects the actual needs of the garden.

**Eligibility of infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Yes – capital infrastructure that directly enhances the resilience and sustainability of community‑based operations is fully eligible. Both rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are listed under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” cost category in our guidelines, provided they are essential to the project’s objectives, have a useful life of at least five years, and are installed on the premises owned or controlled by the applicant.

**Supporting documents you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following documents:

1. **Project Description and Business Plan** – a concise narrative outlining the problem you are addressing (e.g., water scarcity and heat stress), the proposed solution, implementation timeline, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Narrative Budget Table** – a detailed breakdown of all costs, including equipment quotations, installation fees, and any contingency.  
3. **Letter of Support/Auspice Agreement** – a formal endorsement from your council confirming insurance coverage and their willingness to act as fiscal host.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief overview of your group’s composition, volunteer base, and any previous community projects you have delivered.  
5. **Insurance Certificate** – confirmation that the council’s insurance policy extends to cover the grant‑funded assets.  
6. **Site Plans or Photographs** – visual documentation of the garden layout and current conditions to illustrate the need for the proposed infrastructure.  
7. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed financial statements (or, for a small operation, a simple income‑and‑expense summary) demonstrating your $40,000 annual turnover and the sources of revenue.
