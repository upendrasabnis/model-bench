**Auspicing Arrangements and Eligibility**
Unincorporated groups auspiced by a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your council) is a legal entity that meets our standard organisational eligibility criteria—specifically, that it holds an ABN, is not-for-profit, and maintains appropriate public liability and workers’ compensation insurances. As the council already holds the relevant insurances on your behalf, this arrangement satisfies our risk-management requirements. You will need to submit a formal letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing agreement, the council’s ABN, and its willingness to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant should your application be successful.

**Grant Amount Parameters**
For this round, the Community Resilience Grant offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 (GST exclusive). Your proposed project scope appears to fall comfortably within this band; however, please ensure your budget reflects realistic, itemised quotes for all components.

**Eligible Costs – Infrastructure**
Capital infrastructure that directly enhances community resilience is an eligible cost category. Rainwater harvesting systems and permanent shade structures are explicitly listed as eligible under the “Climate Adaptation Infrastructure” stream, provided they are fixed assets that remain the property of the auspicing organisation or the project site. Costs for site preparation, installation, and necessary plumbing or electrical work are also allowable. Ongoing maintenance, consumables, and operational salaries are not funded through this grant.

**Supporting Documentation Checklist**
To streamline your preparation, please assemble the following:
1. Council auspicing letter (as described above).
2. Current public liability and workers’ compensation certificates of currency (council’s policies).
3. Two written quotes for each major infrastructure item (tanks, shade structure, installation).
4. A simple project plan with timeline, risk register, and sustainability statement outlining how the garden will maintain the assets post-grant.
5. Your most recent annual financial statement or treasurer’s report showing the ~$40,000 turnover.
6. Evidence of community support (letters from members, partner organisations, or local MP).
7. Site plan or photographs showing proposed tank and shade structure locations.
