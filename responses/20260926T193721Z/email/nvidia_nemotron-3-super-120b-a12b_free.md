Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the thoughtful work you and your volunteers are doing to strengthen the resilience of your community garden. I appreciate you taking the time to clarify eligibility before preparing a full application, and I am happy to provide the details you requested.

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The Community Resilience Grant is open to incorporated organisations, registered charities, and unincorporated groups that are formally auspiced by an eligible sponsor, such as a local government authority, a registered charity, or an incorporated association. Because your garden operates under the auspices of your local council—and the council holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurances on your behalf—you meet the auspicing requirement. You will need to provide a letter of support from the council confirming the auspicing arrangement, the scope of their insurance coverage, and their willingness to administer any grant funds on your behalf.

**Grant amount range**  
For the current funding round, the minimum grant request is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST‑exclusive). Applications that fall outside this range will be deemed ineligible. Given your project scope, a request in the $15,000–$25,000 bracket would be typical, but you should tailor the amount to the detailed budget you prepare.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance community resilience—including the installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related site‑works—are expressly eligible. The grant can cover the purchase and installation of tanks, piping, pumps, shade sails or pergolas, associated foundations, and any necessary permits or engineering advice. Ongoing operational costs (e.g., water bills, routine maintenance) are not eligible, but you may include a modest contingency (up to 10 % of the total budget) for unforeseen site‑specific expenses.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To substantiate your application, please gather the following items:

1. **Auspicing letter** from the local council (on official letterhead) confirming the sponsorship, insurance coverage, and agreement to manage grant funds.  
2. **Project plan** outlining the objectives, timeline (including key milestones), and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will increase resilience to heat and drought.  
3. **Detailed budget** (using the grant’s budget template) itemising each cost component, quoting suppliers where possible, and showing any in‑kind contributions or volunteer labour.  
4. **Evidence of community support** – e.g., letters from regular stallholders, members, or nearby residents, and a brief summary of your weekly produce stall turnover and membership base.  
5. **Proof of turnover** – a simple profit‑and‑loss statement or bank statements showing the approximate $40,000 annual revenue you mentioned.  
6. **Risk management plan** – a short document identifying any safety or environmental risks associated with the installation and how they will be mitigated.  
7. **Governance information** – even though you are unincorporated, provide a list of the core volunteer coordinators and their roles, plus any relevant policies (e.g., child safety, volunteer induction) that your council auspice requires.

Once you have these documents assembled, you can proceed to the online application portal. Should you encounter any questions while completing the form, please do not hesitate to contact me directly at grants@agency.gov.au or on 02 XXXX XXXX.
