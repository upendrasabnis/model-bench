Subject: Re: Question about eligibility for the Community Resilience Grant

Dear Jamie,

Thank you for your enquiry regarding the Community Resilience Grant round. I appreciate you reaching out before investing time in a full application, and I am happy to address each of your questions in turn.

First, regarding your organisational structure: unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspice arrangement with a local government authority are eligible to apply, provided the auspicing body (in this case, your local council) meets the program's eligibility criteria for lead applicants—specifically, that they are a legally constituted entity with appropriate insurance coverage and financial accountability mechanisms. You will need to submit a signed auspice agreement or letter of support from the council confirming their willingness to accept legal and financial responsibility for the grant, including acquittal obligations.

Second, the current grant round offers a minimum request of $5,000 and a maximum of $50,000 per project. Given your annual turnover of approximately $40,000, your proposed budget should fall comfortably within this range, though we do assess value for money and the proportionality of the request relative to the applicant's capacity.

Third, infrastructure such as rainwater tanks and permanent shade structures is considered an eligible capital expenditure under the "Community Infrastructure and Asset Improvement" stream, provided the assets are fixed, depreciable, and directly support the project's resilience objectives. Portable or temporary items (e.g., pop-up gazebos) would not qualify, but engineered shade sails and plumbed water storage systems do.

Finally, for supporting documentation, please prepare: (1) the council auspice agreement; (2) a simple project plan with timeline and risk register; (3) two written quotes for each major capital item; (4) your most recent annual financial statement or treasurer's report; (5) evidence of land tenure or council permission for the installations; and (6) letters of community support demonstrating broader benefit.

Applications close at 5:00 pm AEST on 30 June. I encourage you to start drafting in our online portal early, as the system allows you to save progress. Please do not hesitate to contact me if you require further clarification.
