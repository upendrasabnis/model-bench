Thank you for reaching out and for the clear description of your project. I’m pleased to provide the following information to help you determine whether your organisation will be eligible for the upcoming round of the Community Resilience Grant.  

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant guidelines expressly welcome applications from unincorporated community groups that are **auspiced or sponsored by a local government entity**. The council’s involvement satisfies the requirement for a legal entity to hold insurance and other risk‑management responsibilities, and it also serves as evidence of community support. Consequently, your garden should meet the organisational‑structure criterion, provided you can supply a formal letter of auspice from the council confirming that it will act as the fiscal sponsor for any awarded funds.  

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a strict floor or ceiling; rather, awards are determined on the merit of the proposal and the scope of the requested funding. Typical grants in recent rounds have ranged from **$5,000 to $75,000**, but applications for amounts outside this band are considered if they are fully justified and aligned with the programme’s objectives. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure fall comfortably within the normal range, though we encourage you to request only the amount needed to deliver a high‑impact, sustainable solution.  

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Yes—capital infrastructure such as **rainwater harvesting systems, storage tanks, and permanent shade structures** is an eligible cost category under the “Resilience and Climate Adaptation” component of the grant. These items directly support the garden’s ability to operate during extreme heat events, which aligns with the programme’s goal of enhancing community capacity to cope with climate‑related challenges. We recommend itemising each component in your budget, providing unit costs, and citing any energy‑efficiency or water‑conservation benefits.  

**Supporting documentation you should prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest assembling the following documents:  

1. **Letter of auspice** from your local council confirming fiscal sponsorship and insurance coverage.  
2. **Organisational profile** (including a brief history, volunteer base, and current operations).  
3. **Financial statements** for the most recent fiscal year (balance sheet, income/expenditure statement, and cash flow summary). Your $40,000 turnover will be reviewed for sustainability.  
4. **Project plan** outlining objectives, timeline, deliverables, and monitoring mechanisms.  
5. **Detailed budget** with line items for tanks, shade structure, installation, and any ancillary costs (e.g., permits, contingency).  
6. **Letters of support** from community stakeholders, neighbouring organisations, or user groups.  
7. **Risk‑management and insurance documentation** (the council’s certificates are acceptable).  
8. **Environmental impact assessment** or a brief justification of how the project reduces water consumption and heat island effects.  

If you could provide these materials in a single PDF by the pre‑submission deadline (two weeks from today), we will be able to conduct a thorough review and, should your proposal be successful, guide you through the subsequent phases of funding agreement and reporting.
