**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
Our grant program welcomes applications from a broad spectrum of community‑based organisations. Unincorporated groups are indeed eligible provided that they operate under the formal auspices of a local government body that can act as the primary grantee and assume legal responsibility for the funds. In practice, this means that your council must be able to sign the grant agreement, hold any required insurance, and be accountable for the expenditure of public money. If your council is willing to serve in this capacity—and you can provide a written confirmation of that arrangement—your group will meet the eligibility criteria.

**Minimum and maximum grant amounts**  
The Community Resilience Grant does not prescribe a rigid minimum or maximum award. Instead, we assess each proposal against the overall budget available for the round and the scope of the project’s impact. Past awards have ranged from roughly $10,000 to $75,000, depending on the complexity of the infrastructure and the breadth of community benefit demonstrated. Your projected costs for rainwater tanks and a shade structure appear modest; even a grant of $15,000–$25,000 would likely cover the bulk of the work while leaving room for ancillary expenses such as site preparation, outreach, and monitoring.

**Eligibility of rainwater tanks and shade structures**  
Infrastructure that directly enhances climate resilience and the long‑term sustainability of community assets is fully eligible. Both rainwater harvesting systems and durable shade structures fall squarely within the “climate‑adaptation” and “infrastructure improvement” categories outlined in our guidelines. We particularly value proposals that demonstrate measurable outcomes—such as reduced water consumption, lower heat island effects, or increased growing season length—so be prepared to include quantitative targets where possible.

**Supporting documents to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we recommend assembling the following items:

1. **Project Narrative** – a clear description of the problem, objectives, implementation plan, and expected outcomes.  
2. **Budget and Funding Request** – a line‑item breakdown that distinguishes capital costs (tanks, structure, installation) from operating expenses.  
3. **Letters of Support** – endorsements from the council, local businesses, or community members highlighting the broader impact.  
4. **Organizational Profile** – a brief constitution or operational description, together with the council’s auspice letter confirming its role as fiscal host.  
5. **Financial Statements** – the most recent audited or reviewed accounts (or, for a small volunteer group, a simple cash‑flow summary) demonstrating the $40,000 turnover you referenced.  
6. **Insurance Confirmation** – a copy of the council’s insurance certificate that explicitly covers the activities you will be undertaking.  
7. **Site Plans or Drawings** – schematic layouts showing where the tanks and shade structure will be placed.  
8. **Monitoring and Evaluation Plan** – outline how you will track water savings, temperature reductions, and community usage.
