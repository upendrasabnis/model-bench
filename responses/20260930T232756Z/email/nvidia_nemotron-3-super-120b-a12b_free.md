Dear Jamie,

Thank you for reaching out and for the important work you and your volunteers are doing to strengthen community resilience in your regional town. I’m happy to provide the clarification you need before you begin preparing a full application for the Community Resilience Grant (CRG).

**Eligibility of unincorporated groups under council auspice**  
The CRG program expressly permits applications from not‑for‑profit organisations that are not incorporated, provided they are auspiced by an eligible legal entity—such as a local government council, incorporated association, or registered charity—that can accept the grant funds on their behalf and assume responsibility for financial reporting and acquittal. Your arrangement, where the council holds the relevant insurances and would act as the auspicing body, therefore meets the program’s eligibility criteria. Please ensure that a formal letter of support or auspice agreement from the council is included with your application; this document should outline the council’s willingness to administer the grant, confirm insurance coverage, and specify any reporting obligations.

**Grant size**  
For the current funding round, the minimum grant amount is **$5,000** and the maximum is **$50,000** (GST exclusive). Applications are assessed on a merit basis, and the amount awarded will reflect the scope, budget, and demonstrated impact of the proposed project. Your estimated budget for rainwater tanks and a shade structure should fall comfortably within this range, but we recommend providing a detailed, itemised budget to allow the assessment panel to verify cost‑effectiveness.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that enhance the resilience of community‑based facilities—including the installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade structures, and related fittings—are explicitly listed as eligible expenses under the “Infrastructure and Equipment” category of the CRG guidelines. Costs may cover purchase, delivery, installation, and any necessary professional services (e.g., engineering certification or council‑approved building approvals). Ongoing operational expenses (e.g., maintenance, utilities) are not eligible, so please separate these in your budget.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
To strengthen your application, we suggest compiling the following items:

1. **Auspice agreement or letter of support** from the local council, confirming their role as the legal entity responsible for the grant.  
2. **Project plan** (including objectives, timeline, milestones, and how the rainwater tanks and shade structure will improve resilience to extreme heat).  
3. **Detailed budget** (itemised costs for tanks, shade structure, foundations, plumbing, labor, permits, and any contingency).  
4. **Evidence of community need** (e.g., recent temperature data, records of produce stall attendance, member surveys, or letters from local residents).  
5. **Proof of current operations** (photos of the garden, copies of weekly stall receipts, membership register, or a brief annual report).  
6. **Insurance details** (copy of the council’s public liability policy covering the garden’s activities).  
7. **Governance information** (list of volunteer coordinators, meeting minutes showing project endorsement, and any relevant policies such as child safety or volunteer induction).
