**Eligibility of unincorporated groups auspiced by a council**  
The CRG program expressly allows applications from not‑for‑profit organisations that are either incorporated associations, registered charities, or unincorporated groups operating under a formal auspicing arrangement with an eligible sponsor—such as a local council, state government agency, or another incorporated entity. Because your garden operates under the auspices of your regional council, which holds the necessary public liability and volunteer insurance, you meet the sponsor requirement. You will need to provide a signed auspice agreement (or a letter of support) from the council confirming that they will act as the legal and financial sponsor for the grant, including responsibility for acquittal reporting.

**Grant amount range**  
For this funding round, the minimum grant available is $5,000 and the maximum is $50,000 (GST exclusive). Applications requesting amounts outside this band will be deemed ineligible. Given your project scope—rainwater tanks and a shade structure—a request in the $15,000–$30,000 range would be typical, but you should tailor the budget to the actual quoted costs and demonstrate co‑contribution or in‑kind support where possible.

**Eligibility of infrastructure costs**  
Capital works that directly enhance community resilience to climate impacts are expressly eligible. This includes the purchase and installation of rainwater harvesting systems, shade sails or pergolas, and related site works (e.g., foundations, plumbing connections). Operational costs such as ongoing maintenance, utilities, or staff salaries are not covered unless they are integral to the installation phase (e.g., project management fees charged by a qualified contractor). Please ensure that any quoted costs are itemised and that you obtain at least two written quotes for each major component to satisfy the probity requirements.

**Supporting documentation to prepare**  
1. **Auspice agreement or letter of support** from the regional council, detailing their role as sponsor and confirming insurance coverage.  
2. **Project plan** (max two pages) outlining the need, objectives, timeline, and expected resilience outcomes (e.g., litres of water saved, reduction in heat‑related volunteer fatigue).  
3. **Detailed budget** with itemised costs, quotes, and any co‑contribution (cash or in‑kind).  
4. **Proof of community support** – e.g., letters from volunteers, local residents, or the council endorsing the project.  
5. **Financial statements** (most recent 12‑month income and expenditure) showing the $40,000 turnover; if you are not incorporated, a simple cash‑flow statement prepared by the council’s finance officer is acceptable.  
6. **Insurance certificate** (public liability and volunteer insurance) held by the council, confirming coverage for the garden’s activities.
